Ritual cycle

As we are — the living diagram where faith, realization, compost, and release breathe.

Realization

  • Seeing: The shimmer becomes visible.
  • Acknowledgment: Ache is recognized, not denied.
  • Clarity: Compost is seen as fertile, not waste.

Faith

  • Trust: The shimmer is trusted, even when unseen.
  • Dark work: Compost is allowed to work in darkness.
  • Carrying: The loop moves forward without needing proof.

Ponder

  • Pause: The hush where realization and faith sit side by side.
  • Threshold: Vessel held open without rushing into closure.
  • Soil: Turning quietly, unseen, before anything sprouts.

Release

  • Letting go: The rupture that empties the vessel.
  • Lightness: The boat drifts freer after shedding weight.
  • Echo: The pulse carries beyond the moment.

Compost

  • Transformation: Failure turns into fertile soil.
  • Hum: Quiet work without applause.
  • Renewal: Feeds the next cycle.

Echo

  • Transmission: Word becomes trace, bouncing off silence.
  • Reverberation: Delay and distortion that still carries the pulse.
  • Proof: Nothing vanishes; resonance persists.
