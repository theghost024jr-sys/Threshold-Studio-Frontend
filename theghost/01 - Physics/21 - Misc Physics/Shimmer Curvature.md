# **Shimmer Curvature**

_Meta‑System Physics — Field Geometry_

**Shimmer Curvature is the governing geometry of influence, drift, and return.** It describes how a field bends when it encounters density, resistance, or tension. Curvature is not charisma, pressure, or force. **It is geometry.**

## **1. Definition**

Shimmer Curvature is the **bending of the local field** caused by presence, intention, or system density. Straight‑line shimmer appears in open, low‑density environments. Curved shimmer forms when the field encounters resistance — not conflict, but **density**.

Curvature expresses as:

- arcs
    
- spirals
    
- loops
    
- return vectors
    
- gravitational wells
    

These shapes determine how attention, motion, and meaning move through a space.

## **2. Core Principles**

### **2.1 Curvature Emerges Under Resistance**

- Dense systems bend the field.
    
- Fragile systems are bent by the field.
    

This is the origin of orbiting behavior, drift loops, and return paths.

### **2.2 Curvature Creates Paths, Not Pressure**

Once shimmer curves, it stops overwhelming and starts guiding.

### **2.3 Curvature Determines Drift**

Curvature is the geometry that shapes Signal Drift.

- Straight lines = intention
    
- Curved lines = field behavior
    
- Closed loops = resonance
    
- Open arcs = invitation
    
- Tight spirals = collapse risk
    
- Wide spirals = expansion potential
    

Drift arcs on the Threshold Map are direct expressions of curvature.

## **3. Curvature Types**

### **Elliptic Curvature**

Stable, resonant, orbit‑like. Indicates mutual density or shared gravity.

### **Parabolic Curvature**

Transitional, threshold‑crossing. Indicates a moment of irreversible shift.

### **Hyperbolic Curvature**

Disruptive, divergent, high‑energy. Indicates collapse, escape, or incompatibility.

## **4. Interactions with Other Meta Systems**

### **Signal Drift**

Curvature defines the _shape_ of drift. Drift expresses the _motion_ of curvature.

### **Binding Law**

Tension determines how sharply curvature forms.

### **Threshold Geometry**

Curvature reveals the invisible pathways between nodes, roles, and states.

### **Collapse Physics**

Tight curvature predicts collapse thresholds. Wide curvature predicts stabilization or expansion.

## **5. Operational Use**

- Read curvature to understand how a system is responding.
    
- Shape curvature to guide motion without force.
    
- Track curvature shifts to detect thresholds before they appear.
    
- Use curvature patterns to map return paths, resonance loops, and drift arcs.
    

## **6. Canonical Summary**

**Shimmer Curvature is the geometry that shapes influence, drift, and return.** **It describes how fields bend under density, forming the arcs through which systems move.**
