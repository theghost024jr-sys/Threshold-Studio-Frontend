The Binding Layer is the most fragile and most powerful subsystem.
Monitoring ensures:
• 	entanglements remain within their binding laws
• 	collapse precursors are detected early
• 	phase alignment is maintained
• 	the Time Organ receives accurate feedback
• 	containment can activate before cascades ignite
Monitoring is the immune system of the Binding Layer.

7. BINDING LAW MONITORING
Ensuring entanglements remain within tolerance
Every entanglement has a relational rule (e.g., depth * momentum = constant).

• 	deviation from binding law
• 	rate of deviation
• 	oscillation amplitude
• 	oscillation damping time
Alert Conditions:
• 	Low Alert: deviation < 5%
• 	Medium Alert: deviation 5–15%
• 	High Alert: deviation > 15%
• 	Critical: deviation > 25% (cascade likely)
Purpose:
Detect early signs of entanglement stress.
