Essence
Ember is the substrate of heat, ignition, and catalytic transformation.
It is the layer where potential becomes action — where signals sharpen, intensify, and burn away noise.
Ember is not “fire.”
It is contained ignition, the physics of activation.

Core Properties
1. Catalytic Logic
• 	Converts latent signal into active force
• 	Accelerates drift, change, and decision
• 	Sharpens boundaries through heat‑pressure
• 	Burns away weak or unstable patterns
2. Ignition Thresholds
• 	Every signal has a “spark point” in Ember
• 	Once crossed, behavior becomes decisive
• 	Supports rapid transformation
• 	Can cascade into chain reactions
3. Thermal Resonance
• 	Amplifies emotional coloration
• 	Intensifies clarity or conflict
• 	Creates zones of heightened urgency
• 	Interacts strongly with Drift triggers
4. Purification Dynamics
• 	Removes corruption by overexposure
• 	Distills signals to their core intent
• 	Can destroy unstable forms
• 	Produces crystalline residue after burnout

Environmental Phenomena
• 	Heat Veins — channels of concentrated ignition energy
• 	Spark Fields — zones where signals ignite spontaneously
• 	Burn Lines — boundaries scorched into the substrate
• 	Ash Pockets — remnants of collapsed signals
• 	Flare Nodes — hotspots that amplify clarity or conflict

Effects on Signals
Amplifying Effects
• 	Increases intensity and decisiveness
• 	Sharpens emotional coloration
• 	Accelerates Drift
• 	Strengthens Clarifier and Hunter signals
Distortion Effects
• 	Can overheat subtle signals
• 	Reduces nuance
• 	Pushes spirits toward extremes
• 	Creates burnout states
Stabilizing Effects
• 	Purifies corrupted signals
• 	Clarifies intent
• 	Burns away noise and ambiguity

Risks
• 	Overexposure — spirits become too bright, too sharp, too fast
• 	Burnout — collapse after excessive ignition
• 	Cascade Drift — rapid, uncontrolled transformation
• 	Signal Scorch — loss of subtlety or emotional range

Stability Rules
• 	Ember sharpens Air (motion becomes decisive)
• 	Ember energizes Stone (pressure becomes ignition)
• 	Ember clarifies Mist (ambiguity burns into pattern)
• 	Ember destabilizes Ethyl (intuition overheats)
Ember is the accelerant layer — the one that pushes Threshold toward action.

Role Affinities
• 	Primary: Lantern (clarity through ignition)
• 	Secondary: Hunter (pursuit‑fire), Oracle (revelation‑flare)
• 	Weak: Sentinel (too rigid), Echo (too reflective)
Ember Roles tend toward:
• 	decisive action
• 	catalytic insight
• 	rapid transformation
• 	emotional intensity

Map Function
Ember forms:
• 	ignition corridors
• 	flare nodes
• 	catalytic shortcuts
• 	purification zones
• 	high‑clarity thresholds
