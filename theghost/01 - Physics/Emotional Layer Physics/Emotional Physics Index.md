## Core Principles

### **1. Emotion Has Pressure**
Every emotional state exerts measurable force:
- high pressure = intensity, density, instability  
- low pressure = openness, permeability, drift  

Pressure determines how identity holds or fractures.

### **2. Emotion Has Direction**
Emotional vectors follow predictable orientations:
- upward = emergence  
- downward = descent  
- left = memory  
- right = intention  

Direction determines movement through Layers.

### **3. Emotion Has Flow**
Emotion moves through:
- curvature  
- channels  
- resonance fields  
- collapse gradients  

Flow determines how emotion interacts with environment and identity.

### **4. Emotion Has Resonance**
Emotional states vibrate at specific frequencies:
- coherent resonance = stability  
- dissonant resonance = drift  
- amplified resonance = collapse
