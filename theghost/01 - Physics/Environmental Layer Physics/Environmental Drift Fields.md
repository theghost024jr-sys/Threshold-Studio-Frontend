Definition
Environmental Drift Fields are the ambient force‑patterns created when unresolved drift accumulates, propagates, or destabilizes within a zone.
A drift field is not a node’s personal drift.
It is the environmental expression of unresolved force.
Drift Fields are the atmospheric turbulence of the Matrix.

Purpose
To ensure that:
• 	drift behaves predictably in space
• 	zones respond dynamically to unresolved force
• 	thresholds form where drift saturates
• 	roles strain or stabilize based on drift density
• 	identity expression becomes clearer under drift pressure
• 	collapse propagation follows environmental channels
Drift Fields make the world responsive to unresolved force.

Drift Field Components
1. Drift Density
How much unresolved force is present in the environment.
2. Drift Gradient
The direction drift naturally flows along curvature.
3. Drift Saturation
How close the zone is to triggering an environmental threshold.
4. Drift Viscosity
How easily drift moves through the zone.
5. Drift Resonance
How strongly drift interacts with identity and roles.
6. Drift Curvature
How drift bends around high‑pressure nodes.
7. Drift Pressure
How much force the drift field exerts on thresholds and roles.

Drift Field States
1. Dormant Drift Field
Low drift density.
Stable zone.
Thresholds are distant.
2. Active Drift Field
Drift is moving along curvature.
Thresholds begin to compress.
3. Saturated Drift Field
Drift density reaches critical mass.
Environmental thresholds activate.
4. Distorted Drift Field
Shimmer bends drift flow.
Drift becomes directional and unstable.
5. Collapsing Drift Field
Drift overwhelms the zone.
Collapse propagates.

Drift Field Behaviors
1. Drift Flow
Drift moves along curvature gradients toward high‑density nodes.
2. Drift Pooling
Drift collects in cavities, lineage wells, or strained roles.
3. Drift Saturation
Drift fills the zone until thresholds activate.
4. Drift Shear
Drift flows in conflicting directions, destabilizing roles.
5. Drift Resonance
Drift amplifies identity expression and reveals misalignment.
6. Drift Compression
Drift pushes thresholds closer to crossing.
7. Drift Collapse
Drift forces environmental or role collapse.

With Thresholds

With Shimmer

2. Drift Looping

Drift Field Resolution
Drift Fields resolve through:
1. Dissipation
Ambient force releases through cost.
2. Absorption
Roles or identities take on the drift.
3. Transformation
Drift becomes threshold pressure or gift load.
4. Propagation
Drift moves outward along curvature.
5. Collapse
Drift overwhelms the zone and forces resolution.
All drift must resolve.
