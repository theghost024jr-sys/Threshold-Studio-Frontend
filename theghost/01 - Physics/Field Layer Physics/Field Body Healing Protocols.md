How the Bound Grid restores stability, coherence, and flow
Every healing protocol corresponds to a specific pathology.
Each protocol uses three mechanisms:
1. 	Counter‑vector (directional correction)
2. 	Counter‑pressure (weight redistribution)
3. 	Counter‑resonance (meaning harmonization)
Healing is not “undoing damage.”
Healing is re‑establishing balance.
Let’s walk them cleanly.
