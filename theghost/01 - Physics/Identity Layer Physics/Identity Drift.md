00 Meta Systems — Identity Cluster / Signal Cluster
Identity Drift describes how an entity’s core identity bends, shifts, or destabilizes under emotional, environmental, or relational pressure.
Where Role Identity Drift describes how a role changes, Identity Drift describes how the self beneath the role begins to move.
Identity Drift is the earliest indicator that a system is approaching instability, transformation, or collapse.

Purpose
Identity Drift defines:
• 	how identity responds to pressure
• 	how emotional states alter self‑perception
• 	how species resonance shapes behavior
• 	how environmental layers bend identity
• 	how signals distort internal meaning
• 	how drift leads to role misalignment
• 	how collapse or transformation begins
It is the root‑level physics of Threshold’s beings.

Primary Drift Modes
1. Soft Drift
Minor shifts in identity expression.
• 	subtle emotional changes
• 	increased sensitivity
• 	mild distortion
• 	early warning state
Common in Air and Earth environments.

2. Resonant Drift
Identity aligns with an external field.
• 	species resonance
• 	emotional contagion
• 	environmental attunement
• 	role amplification
Common in Ember and Ethyl environments.

3. Displacement Drift
Identity shifts toward a neighboring archetype.
• 	temporary identity borrowing
• 	mimicry
• 	adaptive behavior
Common in Mist and Ember–Mist boundary zones.

4. Fracture Drift
Identity splits into partial expressions.
• 	contradictory impulses
• 	unstable behavior
• 	memory distortion
Common in Stone environments or under high emotional load.

5. Inversion Drift
Identity flips into its shadow form.
• 	self‑negation
• 	role inversion
• 	collapse precursors
Common when signals distort or resonance loops overload.

Identity Inputs
Identity Drift is shaped by:
Species
• 	sensory bias
• 	emotional resonance
• 	instinctive patterns
Elemental Alignment
• 	environmental curvature
• 	pressure vectors
Signal Sensitivity
• 	susceptibility to distortion
• 	drift acceleration
Memory / Lineage
• 	resonance loops
• 	inherited patterns
Emotional State
• 	charge
• 	fatigue
• 	volatility
Identity Drift is the sum of all these forces.

Environmental Influence
Each Layer pushes identity in predictable directions:
• 	Air: dissipation, soft drift
• 	Earth: grounding, slow drift
• 	Ember: intensification, resonant drift
• 	Mist: ambiguity, displacement drift
• 	Stone: rigidity, fracture drift
• 	Ethyl: symbolic bleed‑through, surreal drift
This is where identity becomes environmentally legible.

Drift Thresholds
Identity enters Drift when:
• 	emotional load exceeds capacity
• 	resonance loops amplify instability
• 	signals distort or invert
• 	species sensitivity spikes
• 	environmental curvature bends meaning
Identity Drift is the pre‑collapse state.

Downstream Effects
Identity Drift leads to:
• 	Role Identity Drift
• 	Signal Distortion
• 	Resonance Loop amplification
• 	Species‑Role misalignment
• 	Collapse Signals
• 	Threshold Events
Identity Drift is the root cause of many downstream instabilities.
