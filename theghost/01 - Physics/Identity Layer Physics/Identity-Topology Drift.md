**10. TOPOLOGY DRIFT FAILURE MODES**

When topology drift misfires:

**1. Drift Lock**

Drift remains stuck on one link (e.g., Gatherer → Holder).

**2. Drift Loop**

Drift cycles between two roles without progressing (e.g., Holder ↔ Releaser).

**3. Drift Cascade**

Drift bursts across multiple links uncontrollably (collapse storms).

**4. Drift Reversal**

Drift moves opposite the phase direction (e.g., upward drift in Collapse).

**5. Drift Fragmentation**

Multiple micro‑drift paths form instead of one coherent route.

Failure modes reveal **network‑level instability**.
