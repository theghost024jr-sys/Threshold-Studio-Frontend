II. Cluster Purpose
-------------------

Each cluster answers a different question:

- Field: how space behaves.
- Pressure: how tension accumulates and releases.
- Drift: how signals move when not steered.
- Collapse: how systems reorganize under failure.
- Resonance: how stability and coherence form.
- Migration: how things move between regions/Flowers.
- Identity: how self persists and fractures.
- Environmental: how spaces contribute and influence.
- Elemental: how each Layer’s physics expresses itself.
