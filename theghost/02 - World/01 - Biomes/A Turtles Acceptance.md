- It accepts that the player’s speed created turbulence.
- It accepts that the path slowed it down because the timing was misaligned.
- It accepts that this is simply how the biome works.

There is no blame.  
No frustration.  
No shame.  
Just the physics of timing spoken plainly.

The turtle’s acceptance is a mirror for the player’s pace.
