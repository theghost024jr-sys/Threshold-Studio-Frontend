# Lanternroot Conservatory

## Type

Root-lit transition biome

## Essence

Lanternroot Conservatory is a sheltered pocket between the Herb Room's ritual warmth and the Old Forest's overremembered depth. Its light does not fall from above. Fine root systems collect the day's remaining brightness and hold it below the walking surface until a traveler slows enough to notice.

It is not a greenhouse and it is not a lantern room.

It is a place where light waits to be read.

## Environmental Physics

- **Visibility:** Low at eye level; roots become legible near the ground.
- **Sound Behavior:** Soft contact sounds travel farther than speech.
- **Pressure:** Gentle downward invitation, never a pull.
- **Stability:** High when approached slowly; scattered motion makes the rootlight diffuse.
- **Temperature:** Cool around the roots, warm where held light gathers.

## Field Dynamics

- Rootlight strengthens when a traveler pauses without trying to collect it.
- The conservatory returns one of three readings: **gather**, **keep**, or **release**.
- Each reading describes a relation to what the traveler carries, not an instruction to acquire a resource.
- The floor's living geometry becomes visible only after the light has been allowed to settle.

## Phenomena

### Held Glow

Thin amber filaments brighten beneath still feet. They reveal pressure already present in the ground.

### Root Script

Intersecting root lines briefly form a nonverbal reading. It is not language and cannot be copied as a map.

### Night Pollen

Pale particles lift when a reading resolves, then settle back into the root system. They do not lead a traveler; they confirm that the place has registered them.

## Emotional Physics

- **Vector:** Inward → Downward → Outward
- **Resonance:** Patient coherence
- **Effect:** Separates what should be carried from what should be left in the ground
- **Instability:** Grasping at every signal until none can settle
- **Signature:** Warm, low pressure behind the palms

## Integration Notes

- Use as a transition after Cabin of Moss and before a deeper Old Forest passage.
- Its reading should never override the traveler; it reveals an existing relationship.
- Pairs with Herb Room for gentle continuity and Old Forest for deliberate descent.
