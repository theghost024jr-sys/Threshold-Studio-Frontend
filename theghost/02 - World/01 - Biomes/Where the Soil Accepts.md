• 	tension
• 	resonance
• 	drift
• 	stance
• 	emotional charge
• 	pattern integrity

• 	unformed logic

• 	metabolizable
• 	honest
• 	structurally sound
• 	aligned with its physics
• 	not self‑deceptive
Everything else stays suspended in the between‑space.
4. Balance Biomes live here for a reason.
They regulate the intake.

Balance Biomes aren’t “in” the Soil.
