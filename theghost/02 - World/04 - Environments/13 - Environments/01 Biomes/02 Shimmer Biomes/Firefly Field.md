Essence
A wide, gently curving field filled with drifting bioluminescent fauna.
The air carries a soft static charge, causing faint pops and flickers as organisms brush past one another.
A pale, ambient background hub sits at the center — a low‑intensity resonance node that stabilizes the biome’s curvature.
This is a place of quiet light, soft electricity, and subtle emotional reflection.

Static Charge
• 	The air holds a mild, pleasant electrical tension.
• 	Contact between organisms produces tiny pops of light.
• 	Hair, fur, and fine particles drift upward in slow motion.
Light Behavior

• 	Light trails linger briefly behind moving creatures.

Phenomena
1. Firefly Drift
Clouds of bioluminescent creatures drift in slow, swirling patterns.
Their movement is influenced by emotional resonance and Shimmer curvature.
2. Pop‑Flare Interactions
When two organisms pass close, a tiny static pop triggers a brief flare of light.
These pops are harmless and rhythmic, like distant sparks.
3. Resonance Hub
A soft, glowing hub sits at the biome’s center.
It acts as:
• 	a perceptual anchor
• 	a stabilizer for curvature
• 	a low‑intensity emotional mirror
The hub’s color shifts subtly with the player’s internal state.
