1. Drift Threads
Thin, shimmering currents that tug lightly at the player’s stance.
Not directional — more like emotional wind.
2. Softfall Regions
Pockets where gravity forgets what it’s doing.
You don’t float — you pause.
3. Resonance Puddles
Shallow pools of sound.
Step in one and it plays a tone that matches your internal tension.
4. Whorl Seeds
Tiny spirals of particulate that try to form Fibonacci patterns but keep failing.
They’re adorable and slightly tragic.
5. Petal Shadows
Not shadows cast by Fib Flowers — shadows of possible Fib Flowers that don’t exist yet.
6. Static Lace
Filigree-like patterns of charged dust that cling to anything passing through.
They reveal movement you didn’t know you made.
7. The Quiet Tilt
A region where the whole world feels like it’s leaning 3 degrees to the left.
It’s not — but your stance is.
8. Echo Knots
Places where sound gets tangled.
You hear your own footsteps five seconds later, but only if you meant them.
9. The Between-Bloom Hum
A low vibration that only appears when two Fib Flowers are resonating in harmony.
Players can’t cause it — only witness it.
10. Unclaimed Light
Light that hasn’t decided what to illuminate yet.
It drifts like pollen.
