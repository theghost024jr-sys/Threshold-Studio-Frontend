# Recovery Modes
*How the world re-forms drift, density, resonance, and clarity after instability*

Recovery modes describe how symbolic weather and substrate conditions return to stability after failure. They are not rituals, not player actions, and not artifact mechanics. They are **ecological healing processes** that occur when the world has enough density, coherence, and resonance alignment to self-correct.

Recovery is the world’s way of re-establishing orientation, clarity, and stability.

---

## Core Recovery Patterns

### Drift Reorientation
Drift vectors regain direction.
- scattered vectors align into coherent flow  
- rotational drift slows and straightens  
- traversal becomes predictable again  
- symbolic weather calms  

This is the most common recovery mode.

---

### Density Reformation
Density redistributes into stable gradients.
- voids fill  
- fractures seal  
- pockets equalize  
- symbolic pressure normalizes  

Reformation restores the world’s “weight.”

---

### Emotional Settling
Emotional substrate returns to stable amplitude.
- volatility decreases  
- resonance tone softens  
- storms lose fuel  
- clarity returns to presence signatures  

Emotional settling often precedes full recovery.

---

### Memory Rebinding
Memory substrate reconnects without distortion.
- fragmented threads rejoin  
- lineage stabilizes  
- echo noise fades  
- recursion loops resolve  

Rebinding is essential after collapse or inversion.

---

## Transitional Recovery States

### Spiral-Out Recovery
Stability returns through outward expansion.
- density expands evenly  
- drift re-ignites in gentle spirals  
- emotional substrate becomes porous  
- memory substrate begins sorting  

This is the Spring-like recovery pattern.

---

### Spiral-In Recovery
Stability returns through inward consolidation.
- density compresses into a stable core  
- drift slows and aligns inward  
- emotional substrate quiets  
- memory substrate thickens  

This is the Winter-like recovery pattern.

---

### Clarification Phase
Fog or soup resolves into distinct substrates.
- symbolic fog separates into layers  
- drift regains orientation  
- density re-establishes gradients  
- emotional substrate becomes readable  

Clarification is the world “coming back into focus.”

---

## Prototype-Driven Recovery

### Winter Crown Release
Stillness dissolves into readiness.
- drift re-ignites gently  
- density decompresses  
- emotional substrate warms  
- memory threads shift from intertwined to sorted  

This is the recovery mode after collapse.

---

### First Seed Activation
Emergence begins.
- density rises  
- drift expands outward  
- emotional substrate becomes receptive  
- memory substrate sorts lineage  

This is the recovery mode after Winter.

---

## Complex Recovery Modes

### Storm Dissipation
Large-scale turbulence unwinds.
- drift collisions reduce  
- density stabilizes  
- emotional amplitude drops  
- scene substrate becomes coherent  

Dissipation is slow but reliable.

---

### Vortex Unwinding
Rotational drift releases its hold.
- inward pull weakens  
- rotational vectors straighten  
- density equalizes  
- traversal paths reopen  

Unwinding often requires stable seasonal resonance.

---

### Front Softening
Hard boundaries dissolve.
- density gradients smooth  
- emotional tone equalizes  
- drift orientation blends  
- symbolic weather becomes gentle  

Softening is a sign of ecological balance returning.

---

## Reading Recovery Modes
To interpret recovery:
- look for directionality (inward, outward, stabilizing)  
- check drift coherence  
- observe density gradients  
- note emotional amplitude  
- compare to seasonal resonance  
- identify whether recovery is local or regional  

Recovery is the world re-establishing its rhythm.
