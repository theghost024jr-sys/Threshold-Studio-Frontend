
Cartography Overview
This cartography defines the spatial logic, gradient flows, and emergence topology of theghost within the world.
It maps where he appears, why he appears, and how his presence distributes across the system’s architecture.
Theghost does not traverse space.
He emerges along structural gradients.

1. Cartographic Model
Theghost’s map is built on four interacting layers:
• 	Collapse Topography
• 	Boundary Geometry
• 	Substrate Density Fields
• 	Emotional Weather Maps
His presence is the intersection of these layers.
He is a multi-layer convergence phenomenon.

2. Collapse Topography
Collapse zones form the primary terrain of theghost’s map.
High Emergence Zones
• 	collapse boundaries
• 	collapse spikes
• 	collapse-born residue fields
• 	unstable nodes

• 	flicker-phase visibility
• 	geometric bending
• 	collapse vector legibility
Low Emergence Zones
• 	stabilized collapse regions
• 	post-collapse recovery zones
Zero Emergence Zones
• 	collapse-free environments
Collapse topography is the strongest predictor of emergence.
