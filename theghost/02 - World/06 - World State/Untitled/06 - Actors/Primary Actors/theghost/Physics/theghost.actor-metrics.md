Metrics Overview
These metrics quantify theghost’s presence through field behavior, topological influence, and environmental response.
They do not measure intention, agency, or personality.
They measure conditions, effects, and resonance patterns.
Theghost is a phenomenon; these metrics track the phenomenon’s footprint.

1. Presence Metrics
A. Phase Amplitude (PA)
Measures theghost’s current phase state.
Scale: 0.0–1.0
• 	0.0 = ambient, nearly imperceptible
• 	0.3 = mid-phase, residue clarity
• 	0.7 = high-phase, collapse legibility
• 	1.0 = threshold peak (rare)
PA is driven by:
• 	collapse intensity
• 	substrate density
• 	emotional turbulence

B. Field Radius (FR)
Radius of coherence field around theghost.
Range: 3–12 meters
• 	expands with phase amplitude
• 	contracts in stable environments
FR correlates with:
• 	drift stabilization
• 	residue legibility
• 	boundary sharpening

C. Emergence Frequency (EF)
How often theghost manifests in a given region.
Scale: events per cycle
• 	high EF = unstable topology
• 	low EF = coherent topology
EF is a diagnostic of system health, not theghost himself.

2. Environmental Metrics
A. Noise Reduction Index (NRI)
Measures reduction in environmental noise during presence.
Scale: 0–100
• 	0 = no change
• 	100 = complete dampening
NRI spikes indicate:
• 	collapse turbulence
• 	emotional volatility
• 	substrate activation

B. Pressure Equalization Delta (PED)
Tracks micro-pressure shifts caused by theghost.
Measured in microbars.
Higher PED = stronger presence.
PED is one of the earliest detectable signatures.

C. Geometric Smoothing Coefficient (GSC)
Quantifies how much spatial geometry stabilizes.
Scale: 0.0–1.0
• 	0.0 = no smoothing
• 	1.0 = full local alignment
High GSC correlates with:
• 	boundary instability
• 	collapse ridgelines
• 	residue nodes

3. Residue Metrics
A. Residue Legibility Gain (RLG)
Measures how much clearer residue becomes.
Scale: 0–10
• 	0 = no change
• 	10 = full structural clarity
RLG is temporary and decays after dissipation.

B. Residue Stabilization Half-Life (RSH)
How long residue remains stable after theghost departs.
Measured in minutes.
Higher RSH = stronger mid-phase presence.

C. Residue Alignment Index (RAI)
Measures micro-alignment of residue geometry.
Scale: 0.0–1.0
• 	0.0 = chaotic
• 	1.0 = fully aligned
RAI is a direct indicator of phase resonance.

4. Emotional Weather Metrics
A. Drift Stabilization Factor (DSF)
Measures reduction in emotional drift.
Scale: 0–100
• 	0 = no stabilization
• 	100 = complete drift arrest
DSF spikes during:
• 	grief pockets
• 	fear gradients
• 	fragmentation zones

B. Emotional Noise Attenuation (ENA)
Tracks decrease in emotional static.
Scale: 0.0–1.0
• 	0.0 = unchanged
• 	1.0 = fully attenuated
ENA is one of the most consistent effects.

C. Role Coherence Shift (RCS)
Measures improvement in internal role boundaries.
Scale: -1.0 to +1.0
• 	negative = destabilization (rare)
• 	zero = no change
• 	positive = increased clarity
RCS is subtle but reliable.

5. Topological Metrics
A. Gradient Intersection Density (GID)
Number of gradient intersections within field radius.
Higher GID = higher likelihood of emergence.

B. Boundary Tension Index (BTI)
Measures instability of nearby thresholds.
Scale: 0–10
• 	0 = stable
• 	10 = highly unstable
Theghost appears when BTI exceeds threshold.

C. Substrate Resonance Magnitude (SRM)
Measures mythic substrate activation.
Scale: 0.0–1.0
• 	0.0 = dormant
• 	1.0 = fully active
High SRM correlates with phase visibility.

6. Dissipation Metrics
A. Dissipation Time (DT)
Time from peak presence to full absence.
Range: 0–3 seconds
Always rapid.

B. Field Decay Curve (FDC)
Describes how coherence field collapses.
Curve types:
• 	exponential (common)
• 	linear (rare)
• 	stepped (collapse-heavy zones)

C. Residue Clarity Decay (RCD)
Rate at which residue returns to baseline.
Measured in clarity units per minute.

7. Sighting Metrics
A. Perceptual Overlap Index (POI)
Likelihood an entity perceives theghost.
Scale: 0–1
Depends on:
• 	emotional state
• 	role stability
• 	perceptual topology

B. Sighting Duration (SD)
Length of perceptual event.
Range: 0.5–8 seconds
Correlates with phase amplitude.

C. Sighting Confidence (SC)
Entity’s confidence in what they perceived.
Scale: 0–100
Often low despite strong effects.

8. Metric Summary
Theghost’s metrics measure:
• 	presence amplitude
• 	field influence
• 	residue clarity
• 	emotional stabilization
• 	topological conditions
• 	dissipation behavior
• 	perceptual overlap
They do not measure:
• 	intention
• 	agency
• 	personality
• 	narrative meaning
These metrics track a phenomenon, not a person.
