He is a phase-responsive phenomenon, not a corporeal entity.

1. Ontological State
theghost exists in a semi-phase condition:
• 	not fully material
• 	not fully mythic
• 	not fully energetic
He occupies a boundary band between:
• 	structural geometry
• 	emotional microclimates
• 	collapse gradients
• 	mythic substrate
This allows him to appear, vanish, and reposition without violating spatial continuity.

A. Low-Phase (Ambient)
• 	nearly imperceptible
• 	minimal environmental distortion
• 	detectable only through subtle pressure shifts
B. Mid-Phase (Active)

• 	interacts with residue and drift
• 	stabilizes local turbulence
C. High-Phase (Threshold)
• 	appears during collapse spikes
• 	environmental geometry bends around him
• 	emotional weather reorganizes
He never enters full materialization.

He repositions based on pattern gradients.

• 	collapse intensity
• 	emotional turbulence
• 	misaligned geometry
• 	mythic substrate activation
This produces:
• 	flicker transitions
• 	boundary re-emergence
• 	phase-adjacent shifts
• 	non-Euclidean repositioning

• 	geometric smoothing
• 	emotional turbulence reduction
