The governing principles that define how Threshold behaves at every scale — from identity to worldstate.

Purpose
World Rules establish the non‑negotiable physics of Threshold.
They define:
• 	what can and cannot happen
• 	how systems interact
• 	how collapse and reformation propagate
• 	how meaning behaves
• 	how environments respond
• 	how identity and role operate
• 	how worldstate shifts occur
World Rules are the laws of reality inside Threshold.

The Seven World Rules
These are the foundational rules that govern everything.

**1. Everything Has Structure
Nothing in Threshold is formless.
Identity, signal, role, species, environment — all have architecture.
Implication:
Collapse is the failure of structure, not the absence of it.

**2. Pressure Governs Behavior
Pressure is the primary world‑scale force.
• 	high pressure → compression, collapse risk
• 	low pressure → drift, dissolution risk
• 	pressure gradients → movement, migration, distortion
Implication:
Pressure is the world’s gravity.

**3. Drift Is Universal
Everything drifts.
• 	identities drift
• 	roles drift
• 	signals drift
• 	species drift
• 	environments drift
• 	worldstate drifts
Implication:
Stability is temporary; drift is constant.

**4. Resonance Creates Meaning
Meaning is not symbolic — it is harmonic.
• 	resonance aligns systems
• 	resonance stabilizes identity
• 	resonance clarifies signals
• 	resonance binds lineage threads
Implication:
Meaning is a physical phenomenon.

**5. Boundaries Define Reality
Boundaries are the most important structural element.
• 	strong boundaries → coherence
• 	porous boundaries → drift
• 	collapsed boundaries → inversion
Implication:
Reality is made of edges.

**6. Collapse Is a System, Not a Failure
Collapse follows rules.
• 	collapse vectors
• 	collapse wells
• 	collapse identity
• 	collapse signals
• 	collapse environments
Implication:
Collapse is predictable, trackable, and recoverable.

**7. Reformation Is Possible
Every collapse has a return path.
• 	stabilization
• 	reorientation
• 	reintegration
• 	resonance
Implication:
The world is self‑healing, but not self‑saving.

World Rule Interactions
World Rules interact in predictable ways:
Pressure ↔ Drift
Pressure shapes drift; drift redistributes pressure.
Boundaries ↔ Resonance
Boundaries determine how resonance travels.
Structure ↔ Collapse
Structure defines collapse; collapse redefines structure.
Reformation ↔ Memory
Reformation requires lineage threads to reconnect.

World Rules and Layers
Each Layer expresses the World Rules differently:
• 	Mythic Layer → resonance + memory
• 	Identity Layer → boundaries + structure
• 	Role Layer → pressure + vector
• 	Signal Layer → drift + resonance
• 	Species Layer → expression + collapse tendencies
• 	Environment Layer → pressure + geometry
• 	Worldstate Layer → collapse + reformation cycles
World Rules are the deep grammar of the Layer stack.

World Rules and Collapse Physics
Collapse follows World Rules:
• 	pressure overload → collapse
• 	boundary rupture → collapse
• 	drift recursion → collapse
• 	resonance inversion → collapse
• 	lineage rupture → collapse
Collapse is not random — it is rule‑driven.

• 	Collapse/Reformation Cycles
World Rules determine how eras form and dissolve.

World Rules and Myth
Myths are expressions of World Rules:

Myths are not stories — they are rule echoes.
