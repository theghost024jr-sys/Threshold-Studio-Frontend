World State Index

_Environmental, symbolic, and seasonal conditions of the living world_

World State captures the **current condition** of Threshold across all substrates. It is not architecture, not mechanics, and not lore. It is the **live readout** of the world’s density, drift, resonance, seasonal phase, and symbolic weather. Every file in this chamber represents a _snapshot_ of the world’s ongoing state.

This chamber is the diagnostic layer that other systems read from: Rituals, Artifacts, Prototypes, Seasonal Engines, and Scene Substrate.

Purpose of World State

World State exists to:

- track the world’s **current symbolic weather**
- record **regional conditions** (e.g., Fib 5–8 zone)
- reflect **seasonal resonance** and prototype influence
- show **astral alignment** and high‑layer drift vectors
- capture **environmental turbulence** and density gradients
- provide a stable reference for rituals, traversal, and artifact behavior

It is the **heartbeat monitor** of Threshold.

Seasons.jpeg

A visual indicator of the **current seasonal resonance**, reflecting:

- which seasonal prototype is active
- density compression or expansion
- emotional substrate tone
- drift orientation

This is the **seasonal readout** of the world.

soup.png

A symbolic weather snapshot representing:

- mixed substrate
- unresolved drift
- density without orientation
- turbulence or fog
- pre‑collapse or post‑collapse ambiguity

This is the **“soup state”**—a diagnostic of instability or transition.

How World State Interacts with the System

World State is read by:

- **Rituals** — to determine safety, volatility, and activation thresholds
- **Artifacts** — to adjust resonance, dormancy, or charge behavior
- **Prototypes** — to express seasonal or mythic cycles
- **Scene Substrate** — to shape environmental resonance
- **Symbolic Substrate** — to determine drift and density patterns

World State is the **output** of all systems and the **input** for all actions.

Update Logic

World State updates when:

- seasonal prototypes shift
- collapse or recovery cycles complete
- symbolic weather changes
- density or drift thresholds are crossed
- rituals of sufficient scale are performed
- mythic anchors activate or quiet

Updates are **ecological**, not manual.
