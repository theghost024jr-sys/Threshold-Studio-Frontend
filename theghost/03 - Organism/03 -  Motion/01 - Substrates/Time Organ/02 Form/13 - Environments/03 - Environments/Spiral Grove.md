8. Description
A circular grove where the trees grow in a slow, perfect spiral.
The air hums with a soft, rhythmic distortion.
Footsteps echo in loops — you hear yourself walking a moment before you take the step.
Shadows curve inward.
The world feels like it’s breathing in a slow, hypnotic rhythm.
The Spiral Grove is not hostile.
It is entrancing — a place where the world’s curvature becomes music.
Stay too long, and you begin to move in time with it.
