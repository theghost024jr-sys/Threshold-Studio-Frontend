Essence
A Warm Pocket is a localized zone of emotional heat, a small area where the environment softens, slows, and becomes unexpectedly gentle. It forms where resonance, memory, and shelter overlap. Warm Pockets are not created intentionally; they accumulate around moments of care, rest, or quiet recognition.
They are the system’s way of saying:
“Stay here a moment.”
Environmental Physics
• 	Temperature: Noticeably warmer than surroundings
• 	Pressure: Low, soft, enveloping
• 	Visibility: Slightly golden or muted, depending on environment
• 	Stability: Medium; persists as long as emotional coherence remains
• 	Size: Typically 3–12 feet across
Field Dynamics
• 	Sound becomes rounder, less sharp
• 	Movement slows without feeling restricted
• 	Emotional resonance thickens, like warm air
• 	Identity stabilizes temporarily
Phenomena
1. Heat Bloom
A gentle rise in warmth triggered by:
• 	Relief
• 	Recognition
• 	Shared presence
• 	Emotional exhale
2. Softfall
Particles (dust, pollen, snow, ash) fall more slowly inside the Pocket.
Time feels slightly stretched.
3. Memory Glow
Past moments of safety or tenderness surface lightly, without overwhelm.
4. Boundary Blur
Edges of the Pocket shimmer or soften, making it feel larger than it is.
Flora & Fauna
• 	Mosslights: Soft, glowing moss that thrives in emotional warmth
• 	Pocket Mice: Tiny creatures drawn to stable heat zones
• 	Resting Deer: Often pause in Warm Pockets during drift
• 	Glowflies: Hover in slow, looping patterns
Emotional Physics
• 	Vector: Inward → Settle
• 	Resonance: Gentle coherence
• 	Effect: Calms drift, stabilizes identity, encourages rest
• 	Instability: Overstaying can create emotional stagnation
• 	Signature: A soft, chest-centered warmth
Role Interaction
• 	Guide Roles: Use Warm Pockets as regrouping points
• 	Trickster Roles: Avoid them; warmth dulls their edge
• 	Healer Roles: Amplify the Pocket’s stability
• 	Wanderer Roles: Risk lingering too long, losing momentum
Drift Behavior
• 	Early Drift: Warm Pocket forms spontaneously around the drifter
• 	Mid Drift: Pocket expands, creating a temporary safe zone
• 	Late Drift: Pocket collapses suddenly, leaving emotional chill
• 	Collapse Mode: Heat inversion — warmth turns to sharp cold
Mythic Fragment

They say Warm Pockets appear
where someone once rested
without fear.

The air remembers the shape
of a body at ease,
and holds that shape
for anyone who needs it.

Integration Notes
• 	Use Warm Pockets as emotional rest points in tense or high-pressure environments
• 	Ideal for scenes of vulnerability, reconnection, or quiet revelation
• 	Pairs naturally with Meadow, Branch, and Spiral Field
• 	Can be used as a counterpoint to Cold Hollows or Drift Zones
• 	Excellent for signaling a shift from tension to softness
