🌫️ **MIST LAYER — The Balloon Room (Architecture Seed)**

**Atmosphere**

A dim, pale room where every breath you take sends a faint ripple through the fog. Balloons drift at waist height, lightly glowing from within. When you inflate one, the mist inside it swirls like ink in water.

Everything is soft, slow, and slightly unstable.

🎈 **Core Mechanic: Breath → Shape → Meaning**

**1. Inflate a balloon**

- You breathe into a balloon.
- The balloon expands, but the _mist inside_ forms a vague symbol.
- The symbol is never crisp — it’s a suggestion.

**2. Twist the balloon**

- Twisting doesn’t create animals.
- Instead, each twist “locks” a part of the mist into a new configuration.
- You’re sculpting **conceptual shapes**, not physical ones.

**3. The goal**

Create a balloon “animal” that matches a **mathematical symbol, diagram, or structure** — but interpreted through Mist logic.

Examples:

- A balloon twisted into a loop that stabilizes into the **∞** symbol.
- A balloon with three locked segments that settle into a **triangle** diagram.
- A balloon whose mist forms a **Venn diagram** when held at a certain angle.
- A balloon that, when gently squeezed, reveals a **Fibonacci spiral** in the fog.

The puzzle is not precision — it’s **perception**.

🎈 **Puzzle Logic (Mist‑style)**

**1. The mist resists clarity**

If you twist too fast, the symbol dissolves.  
If you twist too forcefully, the balloon deflates.  
If you pause and breathe slowly, the symbol sharpens.

**2. The room reacts to your interpretation**

When you _think_ you see a symbol, the room subtly confirms it:

- A faint chime
- A shift in the fog
- A soft pulse of light

Mist rewards **recognition**, not correctness.

**3. Each balloon is a “phrase” in disguise**

Just like the phrase‑rearrangement puzzles, each balloon is a scrambled idea.  
Your job is to **reorder the segments** until the idea becomes legible.

A balloon with segments labeled (in mist‑glyphs):

- curve
- join
- repeat
- open

…might become a **spiral** when arranged in the right order.

🎈 **Example Puzzle Seed**

You inflate a balloon. Inside the mist forms four drifting fragments:

- **loop**
- **touch**
- **again**
- **soft**

Twisting the balloon rearranges the fragments.  
Only one ordering stabilizes the symbol:

**“soft loop again touch” → becomes the ∞ symbol**  
because the player interprets it as:  
**“softly loop again until the ends touch.”**

That’s pure Mist.
