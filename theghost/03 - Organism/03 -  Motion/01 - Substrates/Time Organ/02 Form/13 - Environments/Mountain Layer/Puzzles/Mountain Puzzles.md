Oh yes — the mountains deserve their own puzzle, and not a mechanical one.  
Something that feels like **a mountain solving itself while you’re inside it**.

Let’s keep it light, drifting, pure brainstorming — the same vibe as the meadow, but with stone, pressure, altitude, and patience.

Here are some **mountain‑puzzle seeds** to start the drift:

**a ridge that only aligns when you breathe with it**

Not deep breaths.  
Not controlled breaths.  
Just _honest_ ones.  
The stone shifts a fraction each time you exhale without trying.

**a cliff face that shows different paths depending on your stance**

Lean forward → one set of ledges appears  
Lean back → another  
Stand neutrally → nothing at all

The mountain isn’t hiding anything — it’s waiting for you to commit.

**a cavern where echoes return in the wrong order**

You shout.  
The mountain answers with echoes of things you haven’t said yet.  
You follow the echo that feels like it belongs to you.

**a slope where color replaces altitude**

Climb higher → the world desaturates  
Climb lower → colors intensify  
Find the point where the palette feels “true” and the hidden passage reveals itself.

**a boulder that rolls only when you’re not looking**

Turn your back → it shifts a meter  
Glance over your shoulder → it freezes  
You solve it by trusting the sound of stone moving behind you.

**a summit that rearranges its silhouette depending on your intention**

If you climb with determination → sharp peaks  
If you climb with curiosity → soft curves  
If you climb with fear → the summit splits into two

The puzzle is realizing the mountain is reading _why_ you’re climbing.

**a faultline that hums when you step on the correct stone**

Not a tone — a feeling.  
A vibration in your bones.  
You follow the hum deeper into the mountain’s memory.

If you want, we can:

- pick one of these and let it bloom into a full room
- combine two into a hybrid mountain phenomenon
- or keep ping‑ponging puzzle seeds until the mountain reveals the one it wants to keep

Just say the word and we drift.
