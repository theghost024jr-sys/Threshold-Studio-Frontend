# Plasma‑Bloom Meadow

## Overview
A field of Threshold flowers that behave like living plasma filaments.  
Color, movement, and attention all shape the way the meadow responds to you — and the way **you** respond to it.

---

## The Flowers
- Not botanical; more like soft light wearing the idea of a flower.  
- They don’t sway from wind — they sway from **your color‑signature**.  
- Each blossom emits a thin plasma thread that reaches toward motion, hesitation, or curiosity.  
- When two flowers “agree,” a tiny lightning‑bridge arcs between them in a color you can’t name.

---

## Your Color
- Your hue isn’t fixed.  
- It shifts as you move through the meadow.  
- Flowers can change you first; sometimes you change them.  
- The color‑conversation is constant, gentle, and never symmetrical.

---

## The Plasma
- Filaments leap toward your hand like excited static.  
- They brighten when you linger, dim when you rush.  
- Hovering changes the hue more than touching.  
- The air feels tuned, like a quiet instrument waiting for a player.

---

## The Butterflies
- They don’t land — they **tune**.  
- Wings flicker with impossible colors that ripple through the field.  
- Passing near you shifts your hue, sometimes subtly, sometimes dramatically.  
- When a butterfly chooses a flower, both flare into a shared, unnameable color.

---

## The Puzzle (Unstated)
The meadow isn’t solved.  
It simply **solves itself around you**, adjusting color, plasma, and motion in a continuous negotiation of mood and presence.

You’re not the player.  
You’re the variable.
