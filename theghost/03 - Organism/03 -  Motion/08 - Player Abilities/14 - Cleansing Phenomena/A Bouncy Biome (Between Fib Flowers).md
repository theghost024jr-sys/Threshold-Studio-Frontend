# Bouncy Biome (Inter‑Fib Medium)

The Bouncy Biome is a resonance‑elastic environment that forms in the space between [[Fib Flowers]].  
It is the physical expression of the inter‑fib medium’s elasticity: a region where motion rebounds, pressure oscillates, and stance becomes spring‑loaded.

The biome is not playful.  
It is **elastic physics made visible**.

## Definition
The Bouncy Biome is:
- an elastic resonance field  
- a drift‑responsive rebound zone  
- a temporal oscillation pocket (see [[Temporal Substrate]])  
- a saturation‑buffer between Fib Flowers  
- a biome where collapse cannot settle

It is the inter‑fib medium at maximum elasticity.

## Environmental Behavior
Inside the biome:
- the ground has a soft rebound  
- drift currents bounce instead of flow  
- echoes ricochet in slow arcs (see [[03 - Organism/03 -  Motion/08 - Player Abilities/14 - Cleansing Phenomena/Environmental Phenomena/The Echo]])  
- [[Time Particles]] oscillate like springs  
- the air has a faint upward push

Everything wants to return to center.

## Player Experience
The player:
- takes slightly springy steps  
- feels stance “load” before releasing  
- hears soft boing‑like resonance in the Drift Layer  
- sees motion overshoot before settling  
- experiences micro‑delays in directional pull

The biome is not silly — it is *responsive*.

## Inter‑Fib Logic
Because it sits between Fib Flowers:
- each bounce is a negotiation between petals  
- resonance travels along Fibonacci ratios  
- saturation increases with repeated motion  
- drift becomes rhythmic instead of chaotic  
- collapse vectors are absorbed and redistributed

The biome is a living buffer.

## Fauna
Creatures adapted to the biome include:
- **Spring‑Hoppers** — drift‑sensitive animals that store rebound  
- **Elastic Wisps** — particles that stretch before snapping back  
- **Bounce‑Snakes** — serpents that coil and uncoil like springs  
- **The Rebound Guide** — a being who interprets oscillation

The Rebound Guide is not a companion.  
It is a **timing interpreter**.

## Rebound Guide Behavior
The Rebound Guide:
- appears when oscillation peaks  
- moves in arcs, not lines  
- pauses at saturation nodes  
- senses when the biome is “overloaded”  
- leads the player to the next stable rhythm

It reads timing, not direction.

## Collapse Interaction
Collapse behaves strangely here:
- collapse vectors stretch before dissolving  
- residue forms loops instead of fragments  
- collapse cannot propagate  
- the biome “absorbs” structural failure

The biome is a collapse cushion.

## Notes
The Bouncy Biome is the inter‑fib medium laughing quietly.  
It is the world practicing elasticity.
