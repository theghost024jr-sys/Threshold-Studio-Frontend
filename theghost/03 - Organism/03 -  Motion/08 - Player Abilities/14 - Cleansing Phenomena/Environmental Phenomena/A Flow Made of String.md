The void as a string‑fabric flow

A flag made of loose threads doesn’t need wind to shift.

That gives the void:

- texture
- quiet motion

If you flick one end of a string‑flag, the vibration travels across the threads.  
But if you’re standing far away, you can’t tell:

- where it started
- whether it was intentional
- whether it was internal or external
