🌑 Core Identity

- molten geometry
- shifting planes
- gravitational anomalies
- echo‑selves that hammer reality into form

🔥 Environmental Logic

The Forge is built on three rules:

- **Contradiction creates heat.**  
    The more conflicted you are, the hotter the biome becomes.
- **Clarity creates shape.**  
    When you know what you want, the world cools and solidifies.
- **Movement creates tools.**  
    Every step you take generates a new implement—tongs, hammers, chisels—made of your own intention.

This biome is not passive.  
It is **reactive**, **loud**, **alive**, and **massive**.
