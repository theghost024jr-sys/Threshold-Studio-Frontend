# Hourglass of Velin

The Hourglass of Velin is a temporal‑attunement artifact that measures **weight**, not time.  
It reveals the density of unresolved motion in the bearer and the surrounding environment.

The hourglass does not count seconds.  
It counts silence.

## Form
- double‑walled glass containing reactive mist  
- oxidized silver frame with node‑map filigree  
- asymmetrical caps echoing the [[Hourglass Glyph]]  
- base etched with low‑frequency shimmer lines  
- mist shifts color and density in response to stance

The hourglass is carried, not worn.

## Function
The Hourglass interacts with:
- the [[Temporal Substrate]]  
- the [[02 - Scaffolding/00 - Meta Systems/18 - Drift/Drift Layer]]  
- [[Time Particles]]  
- the [[Wind (Attunement System)|Wind]]  
- the bearer’s stance and unresolved weight

Its primary functions:
- **Weight Reading** — reveals accumulated emotional or narrative weight  
- **Mist Opaquing** — signals threshold saturation  
- **Echo Mode** — activates when paired with the [[Signal Mark]]  
- **Drift Trigger** — opens a Drift Sequence when the mist fully stills

## Behavioral Logic
The hourglass:
- thickens its mist in high‑drift zones  
- clears in zones of rest (see [[West — Comfort / Rest]])  
- vibrates faintly near collapse vectors (see [[02 - Scaffolding/03 - Archives/Reference/01 - Core Concept Essays/Collapse]])  
- aligns with directional temperament fields (see [[Directional Map]])  
- amplifies the hum of the [[Choice Layer]]

It is a diagnostic artifact, not a tool.

## Echo Mode
When paired with the Signal Mark:
- past dialogue fragments surface  
- micro‑memory becomes audible  
- the Drift Layer becomes semi‑permeable  
- the bearer hears what was almost said

Echo Mode is a reading state, not a communication channel.

## Drift Sequence Trigger
When the mist becomes fully opaque:
- Velin appears  
- she places a book beside the hourglass  
- the book opens into a playable memory  
- the player enters a **Drift Sequence**

The hourglass is the threshold key.
