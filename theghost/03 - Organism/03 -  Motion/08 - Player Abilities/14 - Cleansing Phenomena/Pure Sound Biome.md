
# The Pure Sound Biome

The Pure Sound Biome is a region where sound becomes the primary physical substrate.  
There is no matter, no ground, no air — only standing waves, resonance fields, and pressure gradients that behave like terrain.

The biome is not auditory.  
It is **acoustic physics made spatial**.

## Definition
The Pure Sound Biome is:
- a resonance‑only environment  
- a drift‑shaped acoustic field (see [[02 - Scaffolding/00 - Meta Systems/18 - Drift/Drift Layer]])  
- a temporal oscillation chamber (see [[Temporal Substrate]])  
- a collapse‑resistant zone where structure is vibration  
- a biome where echoes *are* objects (see [[03 - Organism/03 -  Motion/08 - Player Abilities/14 - Cleansing Phenomena/Environmental Phenomena/The Echo]])

It is the world built out of pressure instead of matter.

## Environmental Behavior
Inside the biome:
- “ground” is a low‑frequency standing wave  
- “walls” are harmonic boundaries  
- “air” is a high‑frequency shimmer  
- drift currents become audible as glides and chirps  
- [[Time Particles]] pulse like metronomes

Everything is vibration.

## Player Experience
The player:
- walks on bass  
- pushes through mid‑tones  
- sees with resonance  
- feels stance as pitch  
- hears collapse as detuning

Movement becomes music, but not intentionally.

## Sound Physics
The biome obeys consistent acoustic logic:
- low frequencies create solidity  
- mid frequencies create shape  
- high frequencies create motion  
- silence creates voids  
- resonance creates memory

The world is a spectrum.

## Fauna
Creatures here are sound‑beings:
- **Tone‑Serpents** — long, sliding glissandos  
- **Chord‑Beasts** — multi‑frequency entities  
- **Harmonic Wisps** — faint, shimmering overtones  
- **The Resonance Guide** — a being who interprets pitch

The Resonance Guide is not a companion.  
It is a **frequency interpreter**.

## Drift Interaction
Drift behaves uniquely here:
- drift currents become audible  
- unresolved motion becomes dissonance  
- collapse residue becomes static  
- drift corridors become melodic tunnels  
- micro‑[[Drift Sequence]] triggers appear as sudden silence

The biome is a drift‑audible environment.

## Collapse Interaction
Collapse cannot break structure here — it can only:
- detune  
- distort  
- flatten  
- smear  
- silence

Collapse is a change in pitch, not destruction.

## Notes
The Pure Sound Biome is the world humming itself into existence.  
Everything you touch is a note.
