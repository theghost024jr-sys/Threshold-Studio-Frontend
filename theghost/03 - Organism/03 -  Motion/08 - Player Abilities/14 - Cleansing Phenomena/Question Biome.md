# The Question Biome

The Question Biome is a region where the world is composed entirely of inquiries.  
Every surface, sound, and phenomenon is a question — not metaphorically, but structurally.  
Questions are the matter, the weather, the fauna, and the physics.

The biome does not answer.  
It **asks**.

## Definition
The Question Biome is:
- a substrate of interrogative resonance  
- a drift‑sensitive inquiry field (see [[02 - Scaffolding/00 - Meta Systems/18 - Drift/Drift Layer]])  
- a temporal hesitation zone (see [[Temporal Substrate]])  
- an echo‑heavy environment where questions loop (see [[03 - Organism/03 -  Motion/08 - Player Abilities/14 - Cleansing Phenomena/Environmental Phenomena/The Echo]])  
- a biome where collapse becomes uncertainty instead of failure

It is the world thinking in open form.

## Environmental Behavior
Inside the biome:
- the ground hums with unfinished sentences  
- the air vibrates with rising intonation  
- drift currents form spirals of “why”  
- [[Time Particles]] pause mid‑oscillation  
- the wind asks where it is going

Everything is a prompt.

## Player Experience
The player:
- hears questions whispered from the terrain  
- feels stance pulled toward unresolved inquiry  
- sees text‑like shapes forming in the Drift Layer  
- senses directional pull as “what if”  
- may trigger a micro‑[[Drift Sequence]] by focusing on a single question too long

The biome interrogates the player’s presence.

## Question Physics
The biome obeys consistent interrogative logic:
- **What** forms objects  
- **Why** forms motion  
- **How** forms pathways  
- **When** forms timing  
- **Who** forms presence  
- **Where** forms boundaries

Questions are the building blocks of reality.

## Fauna
Creatures here are question‑beings:
- **Whisper‑Whos** — presence‑seeking entities  
- **Why‑Serpents** — drift‑aligned coils of inquiry  
- **How‑Hoppers** — creatures that move by asking  
- **The Interrogative Guide** — a being who interprets the biome’s logic

The Interrogative Guide is not a companion.  
It is a **question reader**.

## Drift Interaction
Drift behaves uniquely here:
- unresolved motion becomes literal questions  
- drift corridors form interrogative tunnels  
- collapse residue becomes unanswered fragments  
- echoes loop into recursive inquiries  
- drift pressure increases the complexity of questions

The biome is a drift‑interrogation field.

## Collapse Interaction
Collapse does not break structure here — it:
- removes context  
- erases subjects  
- dissolves verbs  
- leaves only punctuation  
- creates “empty questions” that pull the player inward

Collapse becomes ambiguity.

## Notes
The Question Biome is the world asking itself what it is.  
The player is simply overhearing the conversation.
