# The Resolution Biome

The Resolution Biome is a region where questions and answers fuse into a single structural phenomenon.  
It is not compromise, balance, or synthesis — it is the world entering a state where inquiry and conclusion are indistinguishable.

The biome does not ask or declare.  
It **resolves**.

## Definition
The Resolution Biome is:
- a coherence field between the [[Question Biome]] and the [[Answer Biome]]  
- a temporal convergence zone (see [[Temporal Substrate]])  
- a drift‑neutral environment where motion completes  
- a collapse‑immune region where structure holds  
- a resonance state where meaning becomes shape

It is the world finishing its own sentence.

## Environmental Behavior
Inside the biome:
- the ground feels both open and complete  
- the air carries a soft, harmonic certainty  
- drift currents settle into smooth arcs  
- [[Time Particles]] align into stable spirals  
- echoes resolve into single, clear tones (see [[03 - Organism/03 -  Motion/08 - Player Abilities/14 - Cleansing Phenomena/Environmental Phenomena/The Echo]])

Everything feels inevitable and newly discovered at the same time.

## Player Experience
The player:
- feels stance settle into a stable interpretation  
- hears both the question and the answer at once  
- sees text‑like shapes that complete themselves  
- senses directional pull as clarity  
- may trigger a **Resolution Moment** — a micro‑state of perfect coherence

The biome is not comforting.  
It is **final without being closed**.

## Resolution Physics
The biome obeys consistent resolution logic:
- **Thus** forms objects  
- **As a result** forms motion  
- **Hereafter** forms pathways  
- **Already** forms timing  
- **It becomes** forms presence  
- **This is where** forms boundaries

Resolution is the building block of reality here.

## Fauna
Creatures here are resolution‑beings:
- **Thus‑Wings** — birds that glide in perfect arcs  
- **Already‑Serpents** — snakes that move as if they’ve already arrived  
- **Hence‑Walkers** — beings that step into completed motion  
- **The Resolute Guide** — an entity who interprets convergence

The Resolute Guide is not a companion.  
It is a **completion reader**.

## Drift Interaction
Drift behaves uniquely here:
- unresolved motion completes itself  
- drift corridors straighten and soften  
- collapse residue dissolves into clarity  
- echoes finalize into single tones  
- drift pressure drops to zero

The biome is a drift‑end state.

## Collapse Interaction
Collapse cannot exist here — it:
- resolves  
- integrates  
- completes  
- stabilizes  
- disappears

Collapse becomes coherence.

## Notes
The Resolution Biome is the world realizing what it meant all along.  
The player is simply present for the moment of understanding.
