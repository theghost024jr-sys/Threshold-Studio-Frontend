# Astral Alignment
*How the astral layer reflects, amplifies, or contradicts world state*

Astral alignment describes how the high‑layer drift vectors, constellations, and resonance patterns in the astral field align with the four substrates and the current seasonal prototype. The astral layer is the world’s long‑range orientation system: it shows where the world is pointing, how stable it is, and what mythic forces are active.

Astral alignment is the highest‑altitude indicator of world state.

---

## What the Astral Layer Represents
The astral layer expresses:
- long‑range drift orientation  
- resonance constellations  
- mythic alignment patterns  
- high‑layer symbolic weather  
- seasonal resonance at altitude  
- prototype influence on the upper field  

Astral alignment is the “sky‑level” coherence of the world.

---

## Fully Aligned Astral State
*Constellations, drift vectors, and resonance patterns match the active season.*

Characteristics:
- constellations form stable shapes  
- drift vectors point in a unified direction  
- brightness matches emotional amplitude  
- no distortions or gaps  
- astral motion is smooth  

Effects:
- world state becomes predictable  
- symbolic weather stabilizes  
- rituals become safer  
- prototypes express cleanly  

Full alignment is most common in Winter and early Spring.

---

## Partial Astral Alignment
*Most astral patterns match the season, but one element diverges.*

Common divergences:
- drift vectors slightly off-angle  
- constellation brightness mismatched  
- resonance tone too sharp or too soft  
- minor distortions in star paths  

Effects:
- mild turbulence  
- fronts and eddies form  
- emotional substrate becomes sensitive  
- artifacts enter low-amplitude mode  

Partial alignment is normal during transitions.

---

## Astral Misalignment
*Constellations contradict the season or substrate behavior.*

Characteristics:
- drift vectors scatter  
- constellations distort or break  
- brightness becomes uneven  
- astral motion becomes jerky or rotational  

Effects:
- storms become likely  
- fog spreads  
- memory substrate becomes noisy  
- traversal becomes unpredictable  

Misalignment is a precursor to collapse or inversion.

---

## Full Astral Misalignment
*The astral layer breaks coherence entirely.*

Characteristics:
- constellations dissolve  
- drift vectors collide  
- brightness flickers or dims  
- astral field rotates or folds  

Effects:
- vortex chains  
- storm cascades  
- resonance snap  
- mythic distortion  

Full misalignment is the world’s most unstable astral state.

---

## Seasonal Astral Patterns

### Winter
- constellations tighten  
- brightness lowers  
- drift vectors curve inward  
- astral motion slows  
Alignment: **high**  
Winter Crown stabilizes the astral field.

### Spring
- constellations open  
- brightness rises  
- drift vectors spiral outward  
- astral motion becomes gentle  
Alignment: **medium-high**  
First Seed reorients the astral layer.

### Summer
- constellations flare  
- brightness peaks  
- drift vectors accelerate  
- astral motion becomes sharp  
Alignment: **medium-low**  
Expansion Prototype increases astral volatility.

### Autumn
- constellations soften  
- brightness warms  
- drift vectors collapse inward  
- astral motion unwinds  
Alignment: **medium**  
Shedding Prototype cools the astral field.

---

## Astral Alignment During Transitions

### Winter → Spring
- constellations brighten  
- drift vectors straighten  
- astral motion shifts from curved → spiral-out  

### Spring → Summer
- constellations flare  
- drift vectors accelerate  
- astral motion becomes high-frequency  

### Summer → Autumn
- constellations dim  
- drift vectors collapse inward  
- astral motion unwinds  

### Autumn → Winter
- constellations tighten  
- drift vectors slow  
- astral motion becomes curved and inward  

Transitions are visible first in the astral layer.

---

## Astral Failure Modes

### Constellation Break
Star patterns fracture or dissolve.

### Vector Scatter
Drift vectors lose orientation.

### Astral Flicker
Brightness becomes unstable.

### Rotational Field
Astral layer begins spinning.

These failures appear in **Stars.md** before they appear in the substrates.

---

## Astral Recovery Modes

### Reorientation
Drift vectors realign.

### Reformation
Constellations reassemble.

### Brightness Stabilization
Astral tone returns to baseline.

### Motion Smoothing
Astral movement becomes coherent.

Recovery begins in the astral layer when the world stabilizes.

---

## Reading Astral Alignment
To interpret astral alignment:
- observe constellation shape  
- check brightness tone  
- read drift vector direction  
- watch for distortions or gaps  
- compare to seasonal resonance  
- identify prototype influence  

The astral layer is the world’s long-range compass.
