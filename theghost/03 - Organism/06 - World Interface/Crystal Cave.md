**Giant Crystal Cave → Threshold Biome Seed**

**Working Title: The Lumen Vault**

We start with the real place, then mutate it.

**1. The Real-World Core**

The Giant Crystal Cave in Naica, Mexico is:

- unbearably hot
- filled with massive selenite crystals
- lethal without protection
- geologically impossible-looking

That’s our anchor.

**2. What We Keep**

We keep:

- **scale** (crystals the size of buildings)
- **heat** (but not normal heat)
- **impossible geometry** (crystals growing in directions that shouldn’t be stable)
- **the sense of trespassing into something ancient**

These are the bones.

**3. What We Change**

Here’s where we adjust it — heavily.

**A. The heat is not thermal**

Instead of temperature, the “heat” becomes:

**Cognitive Overload Pressure**  
A field that:

- overheats thoughts
- melts intentions
- causes memory drift
- forces players to simplify their thinking

You can’t think complex thoughts inside the Lumen Vault.  
Your mind becomes geometric.

This is the first mutation.

**B. The crystals are not mineral**

They’re **frozen decisions**.

Each crystal is:

- a choice someone didn’t make
- a path that collapsed
- a timeline that calcified
- a possibility that became inert

Touching one gives you a flash of the decision it represents.

This is the second mutation.

**C. The cave is not underground**

It exists in a **fold** — a pocket of space where:

- gravity is optional
- orientation is negotiable
- “down” is a suggestion

The cave can be entered from multiple layers, but it never sits _in_ a layer.

This is the third mutation.

**4. The Biome Identity**

**The Lumen Vault**

_A biome where abandoned choices crystallize into impossible structures, and the air itself simplifies the mind into geometric clarity._

This is the adjusted version — still recognizable, but now Threshold-native.
