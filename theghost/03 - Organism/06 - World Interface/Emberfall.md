## 01 — Overview  
Emberfall is a **heat‑biased region** where the local field expresses through **slow‑burn gradients**, **ember‑phase drift**, and **pressure‑anchored resonance**.  
It is not a fire biome; it is a **stability‑through‑glow environment**, where collapse is delayed by distributed warmth.

The region’s physics are shaped by:  
- [[Ember Myth]] — the archetypal narrative root  
- [[Ember]] — the elemental behavior pattern  
- [[Layer Pressure Map]] — Emberfall sits between two pressure ridges  
- [[Resonance Architecture]] — the region amplifies low‑frequency coherence
