# Worldstate Density Map
*How density behaves across astral, symbolic, emotional, memory, and scene layers*

The Worldstate Density Map describes how density forms, compresses, expands, fractures, disperses, and reforms across all layers of the world. Density is the “mass” of symbolic reality: the weight of meaning, the thickness of memory, the pressure of emotional resonance, and the solidity of scene structure.

Density determines how stable or volatile the world feels at any moment.

---

## Density Across Layers

### Astral Layer
Density appears as:
- constellation clustering  
- brightness concentration  
- resonance thickness  
- astral pressure zones  

Astral density is the world’s high-altitude pressure field.

---

### Symbolic Substrate
Density expresses as:
- symbolic mass  
- pressure gradients  
- fronts and boundaries  
- collapse or expansion  

Symbolic density is the world’s structural weight.

---

### Emotional Substrate
Density expresses as:
- emotional heaviness or lightness  
- amplitude pressure  
- resonance saturation  
- volatility thresholds  

Emotional density determines how charged the world feels.

---

### Memory Substrate
Density expresses as:
- thread thickness  
- lineage weight  
- recursion depth  
- dormancy or activation  

Memory density is the world’s continuity and inertia.

---

### Scene Substrate
Density expresses as:
- spatial solidity  
- threshold rigidity  
- environmental pressure  
- physical resonance  

Scene density determines how “thick” or “thin” the world feels physically.

---

## Density States

### Compressed Density
- high pressure  
- low drift  
- thick memory  
- quiet emotional substrate  
- rigid scene thresholds  

This is the Winter Crown state.

---

### Rising Density
- expanding pressure  
- outward drift  
- porous emotional substrate  
- sorting memory  
- softening scene  

This is the First Seed / Spring state.

---

### Peak Density
- maximum saturation  
- accelerated drift  
- volatile emotional substrate  
- passive memory  
- dynamic scene  

This is the Summer Expansion state.

---

### Falling Density
- dispersal  
- inward drift  
- stabilizing emotional substrate  
- active memory  
- cooling scene  

This is the Autumn Shedding state.

---

## Density Failure Modes

### Density Fracture
Density splits into incompatible regions.
- symbolic fault lines  
- emotional dissonance  
- memory fragmentation  
- spatial instability  

Fracture is a sign of deep ecological stress.

---

### Density Void
Density collapses to near-zero.
- drift becomes chaotic  
- emotional substrate destabilizes  
- memory substrate loses continuity  
- scene becomes hollow  

Voids often precede collapse.

---

### Over-Saturation
Density exceeds tolerance.
- emotional substrate spikes  
- symbolic storms form  
- memory substrate becomes overloaded  
- scene substrate distorts  

Over-saturation is the Summer failure mode.

---

### Density Inversion
Density flips from high → low or low → high too quickly.
- symbolic weather reverses  
- emotional amplitude snaps  
- memory threads tear or fuse  
- scene thresholds warp  

Inversion is rare but dangerous.

---

## Density Recovery Modes

### Reformation
Density redistributes into stable gradients.
- voids fill  
- fractures seal  
- pressure normalizes  
- symbolic weather calms  

Reformation is the most common recovery.

---

### Compression Recovery
Density collapses gently inward.
- emotional substrate quiets  
- memory intertwines  
- scene stabilizes  

This is the Winter-like recovery pattern.

---

### Expansion Recovery
Density expands gently outward.
- emotional substrate warms  
- memory sorts  
- scene softens  

This is the Spring-like recovery pattern.

---

### Clarification
Density separates cleanly from drift.
- fog clears  
- soup resolves  
- symbolic weather becomes readable  
- emotional tone stabilizes  

Clarification is the world regaining focus.

---

## Cross-Layer Density Mapping

### Astral → Symbolic
Astral pressure shapes symbolic density.

### Symbolic → Emotional
Symbolic density determines emotional heaviness.

### Emotional → Memory
Emotional density influences memory weight.

### Memory → Scene
Memory density shapes spatial solidity.

### Scene → Astral (feedback)
Scene density anchors astral pressure zones.

Density is a loop, not a one-way cascade.

To interpret density across layers:
- observe astral clustering  
- check symbolic pressure gradients
