# Seasonal Resonance
*The resonance tone, density behavior, drift orientation, and emotional quality of each season*

Seasonal resonance describes the energetic signature of each season as expressed through the symbolic, emotional, memory, and scene substrates. Seasons in Threshold are not calendar periods—they are **resonance states** shaped by prototypes, density behavior, drift orientation, and symbolic weather.

Each season has a distinct resonance tone that influences world state, artifact behavior, ritual safety, and substrate dynamics.

---

## Winter Resonance
*Stillness, compression, memory density.*

Winter expresses:
- near‑zero drift  
- compressed density  
- quiet emotional substrate  
- intertwined memory substrate  
- low‑volatility resonance  
- inward, curved signal patterns  

Winter resonance is governed by the **Winter Crown**, which holds the world in a dormant, stable state. Symbolic weather becomes soft, slow, and low‑amplitude.

Winter is the season of **holding**.

---

## Spring Resonance
*Emergence, sorting, outward spirals.*

Spring expresses:
- drift re‑igniting in outward spirals  
- density rising and expanding  
- emotional substrate becoming porous  
- memory substrate sorting lineage  
- resonance brightening and widening  
- gentle turbulence forming  

Spring resonance is governed by the **First Seed**, which initiates emergence and re‑orientation. Symbolic weather becomes active but not volatile.

Spring is the season of **beginning**.

---

## Summer Resonance
*Amplitude, pressure, peak density.*

Summer expresses:
- accelerated drift  
- density reaching peak saturation  
- emotional substrate becoming volatile  
- resonance amplitude increasing  
- artifacts entering high‑charge states  
- symbolic weather intensifying  

Summer resonance will be governed by the **Expansion Prototype** (future file). Symbolic weather becomes bright, loud, and high‑frequency.

Summer is the season of **expression**.

---

## Autumn Resonance
*Release, dispersal, inward collapse.*

Autumn expresses:
- drift collapsing inward  
- density falling and dispersing  
- emotional substrate stabilizing  
- memory substrate activating  
- resonance softening and lowering  
- symbolic weather unwinding  

Autumn resonance will be governed by the **Shedding Prototype** (future file). Symbolic weather becomes cooling, settling, and inward‑moving.

Autumn is the season of **letting go**.

---

## Resonance Transitions
Seasonal resonance shifts when:
- prototypes activate or dissolve  
- drift changes direction  
- density crosses thresholds  
- emotional substrate changes tone  
- symbolic weather stabilizes or destabilizes  

Transitions are ecological, not scheduled.

---

## Reading Seasonal Resonance
To interpret seasonal resonance:
- observe drift orientation (inward, outward, zero)  
- check density behavior (compressed, rising, peak, falling)  
- read emotional substrate tone (quiet, porous, volatile, stabilizing)  
- examine symbolic weather (stillness, spirals, storms, unwinding)  
- identify which prototype is active  

Seasonal resonance is the world’s long‑wave emotional and structural rhythm.
