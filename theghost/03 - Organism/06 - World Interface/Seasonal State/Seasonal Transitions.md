        # Seasonal Transitions
*How the world moves from one seasonal prototype to the next*

Seasonal transitions describe how Threshold shifts between Winter, Spring, Summer, and Autumn at the substrate level. These transitions are not calendar events. They are ecological changes driven by prototypes, symbolic weather, drift orientation, density behavior, and emotional substrate tone.

A seasonal transition is the world changing its internal stance.

---

## Winter → Spring
*Stillness dissolves into emergence.*

This transition occurs when:
- drift re-ignites in gentle outward spirals  
- density decompresses from compressed → rising  
- emotional substrate warms and becomes porous  
- memory threads shift from intertwined → sorted  
- Winter Crown releases its hold  
- First Seed activates  

Symbolic weather signs:
- fog clarifies  
- stillness pockets dissolve  
- spiral-out patterns appear  
- density gradients soften  

This is the recovery phase after collapse.

---

## Spring → Summer
*Emergence expands into amplitude.*

This transition occurs when:
- drift accelerates  
- density rises toward peak saturation  
- emotional substrate becomes more volatile  
- resonance amplitude increases  
- generative spirals widen  
- the Summer Expansion Prototype begins expressing  

Symbolic weather signs:
- fronts sharpen  
- eddies multiply  
- storms become possible  
- resonance becomes bright and high-frequency  

This is the world entering full expression.

---

## Summer → Autumn
*Amplitude releases into dispersal.*

This transition occurs when:
- drift collapses inward  
- density begins to fall  
- emotional substrate stabilizes  
- artifacts discharge stored charge  
- the Autumn Shedding Prototype activates  
- symbolic pressure drops  

Symbolic weather signs:
- storms dissipate  
- vortices unwind  
- fronts soften  
- spiral-in patterns appear  

This is the world letting go of accumulated resonance.

---

## Autumn → Winter
*Dispersal settles into stillness.*

This transition occurs when:
- drift slows toward zero  
- density compresses  
- emotional substrate quiets  
- memory substrate thickens  
- symbolic weather loses turbulence  
- Winter Crown begins forming its stillness field  

Symbolic weather signs:
- fog returns  
- gradients flatten  
- turbulence collapses  
- density pockets merge  

This is the world preparing for dormancy.

---

## Transitional Failure Modes
Seasonal transitions can fail when:
- drift re-ignites too early  
- density collapses unevenly  
- emotional substrate spikes  
- symbolic weather inverts  
- prototypes misalign  

Failure modes include:
- seasonal inversion  
- resonance snap  
- fog spread  
- storm cascade  
- stillness break  

These failures appear in world state snapshots.

---

## Transitional Recovery Modes
When transitions stabilize:
- drift reorients  
- density reforms  
- emotional substrate settles  
- memory substrate rebinds  
- symbolic weather clarifies  

Recovery modes include:
- spiral-out recovery  
- spiral-in recovery  
- clarification phase  
- storm dissipation  
- vortex unwinding  

These modes signal that the seasonal shift is completing.

---

## Reading Seasonal Transitions
To interpret a seasonal transition:
- identify the active prototype  
- observe drift direction (inward, outward, zero)  
- check density behavior (rising, falling, compressed)  
- read emotional substrate tone (quiet, porous, volatile)  
- examine symbolic weather patterns (spirals, fog, storms)  
- compare to previous world state snapshots  

A transition is complete when all substrates align with the new season.
