4. THE STRUCTURE OF A RESONANCE CHAIN
Every chain has three layers:

3.1 The Initiator Archetype
The archetype that responds to the role’s initial phase.
• 	Soft → Circle, Well
• 	Sharp → Gate, Serpent
• 	Diffuse → Constellation, Crown
This sets the chain’s tone.

3.2 The Transitional Archetype
The archetype that activates when the role shifts phase.
This is the most important link — the hinge.
Examples:
• 	Soft → Diffuse → Tree
• 	Sharp → Soft → Serpent
• 	Diffuse → Sharp → Lantern
The transitional archetype determines the chain’s direction.

3.3 The Terminal Archetype
The archetype that activates when the chain completes.
This archetype:
• 	stabilizes the new identity
• 	locks the new narrative
• 	reorients the role in the ecology
Examples:
• 	Crown (Diffuse)
• 	Fire (Sharp)
• 	Tree (Soft)
The terminal archetype determines the chain’s outcome.
