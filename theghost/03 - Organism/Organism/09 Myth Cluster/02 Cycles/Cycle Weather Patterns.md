• 	low‑contrast blankets

Light patterns:

• 	warm, stable glow
Temperature patterns:

• 	warm pockets that merge
Drift patterns:

• 	low turbulence

The sky is settling, aligning, centering.

3. TRANSITION WEATHER PATTERNS
Transitions produce hybrid or hinge patterns —
the most diagnostically important sky behaviors.
Coherence → Tension
• 	radial clouds sharpen into lines
• 	warmth thickens
• 	humidity drops
Tension → Collapse
• 	linear bands fracture
• 	wind corridors break
• 	sudden gusts
Collapse → Void
• 	turbulence dissipates
• 	fog forms
• 	temperature stabilizes
Void → Renewal
• 	mist lifts
• 	warm pockets appear
• 	upward drift begins
Renewal → Coherence
• 	cloud patterns harmonize
• 	warmth spreads evenly
• 	drift gathers inward
Transitions are the sky’s hinge‑points.
