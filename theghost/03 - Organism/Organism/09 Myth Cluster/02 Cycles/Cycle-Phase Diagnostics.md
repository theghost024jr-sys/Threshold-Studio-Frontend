1. PURPOSE OF CYCLE‑PHASE DIAGNOSTICS
Cycle‑Phase Diagnostics answers three essential questions:
• 	What phase am I in?
• 	What phase is this environment in?
• 	What phase is this system/entity/story in?
It is not prediction.
It is recognition.
The Cycle is always present.
Diagnostics reveal where you are inside it.

2. THE FIVE PHASES — CORE DIAGNOSTIC SIGNATURES
Each phase has a distinct diagnostic fingerprint.

These signatures are the foundation of all diagnostics.

3. DIAGNOSTIC LAYER 1 — SOMATIC INDICATORS
The body is the fastest diagnostic instrument.
Coherence (Circle)
• 	breath deepens
• 	shoulders soften inward
• 	sense of “returning to center”
Tension (Gate)
• 	breath holds naturally
• 	micro‑tightening in diaphragm
• 	sense of “too much”
Collapse (Crown)
• 	spontaneous exhale
• 	muscles release
• 	sense of “letting go”
Void (Tree)
• 	breath quiets
• 	weight drops
• 	sense of “nothing required”
Renewal (Lantern)
• 	subtle upward lift
• 	warmth in sternum
• 	sense of “something wants to begin”
Somatic diagnostics are immediate and reliable.

8. PHASE TRANSITION DIAGNOSTICS
Transitions have their own signatures.
Circle → Gate
• 	coherence becomes heavy
• 	inward drift becomes linear
• 	pressure begins to form
Gate → Crown
• 	pressure peaks
• 	drift becomes turbulent
• 	structures begin to soften
Crown → Tree
• 	collapse exhausts
• 	drift settles downward
• 	stillness spreads
Tree → Lantern
• 	warmth returns

• 	early forms shimmer
Lantern → Circle
• 	renewal stabilizes
• 	patterns re‑align
• 	coherence returns
Transitions are the most important diagnostic moments.

10. RAPID DIAGNOSTIC CHECKLIST
A quick, five‑question diagnostic tool:
