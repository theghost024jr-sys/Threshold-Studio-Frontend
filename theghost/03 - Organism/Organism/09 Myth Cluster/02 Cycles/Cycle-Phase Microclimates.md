• 	low variance
Humidity:
• 	moderate
• 	evenly distributed
Air movement:
• 	gentle inward drift
• 	centripetal micro‑currents
Light:
• 	soft, low‑contrast
• 	radial diffusion
Resonance:
• 	harmonic, low‑variance hum
Drift:
• 	inward gathering
• 	low turbulence

Creates pockets of stability and alignment.
Transition signal:

• 	evaporative pockets
Air movement:
• 	linear, directional
• 	upward or downward funnels
Light:
• 	high contrast
• 	sharp edges

Drift:
• 	compressed into lines
• 	high density

Transition signal:

• 	erratic gusts

• 	diffuse
• 	unstable gradients
• 	flickering shadows

Drift:

• 	turbulence

3. MICROCLIMATE TRANSITION BEHAVIORS
Microclimates are the earliest indicators of phase transitions.
Coherence → Tension
• 	warmth thickens
• 	air becomes directional
• 	humidity drops
Tension → Collapse
• 	vertical gradients break
• 	gusts appear
• 	light destabilizes
Collapse → Void
• 	turbulence dissipates
• 	temperature stabilizes
• 	air stills
Void → Renewal
• 	warm pockets appear
• 	humidity lifts
• 	upward drift begins
Renewal → Coherence
• 	warmth spreads evenly
• 	light stabilizes
• 	drift gathers inward
Microclimate transitions are precise and diagnostic.
