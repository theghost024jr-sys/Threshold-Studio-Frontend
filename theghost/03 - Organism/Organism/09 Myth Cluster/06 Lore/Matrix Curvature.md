Definition
Matrix Curvature is the bending of the Field caused by force density, identity resonance, drift load, threshold pressure, and environmental distortion.
It determines how vectors move, how roles stabilize or collapse, how drift propagates, and how the shimmer manifests.
Curvature is the geometry of force.
It is not metaphorical.
It is the literal shape of the system.

Purpose
To ensure that:
• 	force has spatial consequences
• 	high‑density nodes reshape the Field
• 	thresholds form along curved lines
• 	drift follows predictable paths
• 	zones distort coherently
• 	collapse propagates along curvature
• 	the Matrix remains structurally interpretable
Curvature is the world’s physics engine.

Sources of Curvature
Matrix curvature arises from five primary sources:
1. Force Density
High‑density nodes bend the Matrix.
This is the root cause of the shimmer.
2. Drift Load
Accumulated unresolved force creates gravitational wells.
3. Threshold Pressure
Approaching thresholds warps local geometry.
4. Identity Resonance
Strong identity patterns create directional curvature.
5. Zone Signature
Environmental force fields shape the ambient geometry.
Curvature is the sum of all these distortions.
