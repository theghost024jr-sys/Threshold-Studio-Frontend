# Mythic Substrate Index

The Mythic Substrate is the foundational soil of Threshold.  
It contains the symbolic, emotional, and atmospheric structures that all entities, roles, signals, and environments grow from.

---

## Purpose
To define and organize the underlying mythic physics:
- emotional cartography  
- narrative substrate  
- symbolic diagrams  
- resonance fields  
- whisper maps  
- pressure geometries  
- collapse-born meaning structures  

These artifacts do not describe the world.  
They **are** the world’s soil.
