Myth Development Stages
Each myth moves through four canonical stages:
1. Seed
The myth exists as a fragment, image, or atmospheric pressure.
2. Form
The myth gains structure — characters, motifs, physics, or emotional logic.
3. Integration
The myth connects to other myths, environments, roles, or lineage threads.
4. Canon
The myth is fully placed in the Narrative Substrate with stable connective tissue.
