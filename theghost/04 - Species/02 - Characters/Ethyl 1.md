Mist‑Aligned NPC • Drift‑Sensitive • Boundary‑Thin
Core Identity
Ethyl is a Mist-native interpreter, one of the few beings who can read the subtle gradients left behind by Water‑Beyond. She isn’t a guide, not exactly — more like a translator of atmospheres, someone who can tell you what the Mist is trying to do before it does it.
She’s not human.
She’s not creature.
She’s threshold-adjacent — a person-shaped condensation of memory and drift.

Behavioral Signature
Ethyl expresses three primary behaviors:
1. Gradient Listening
She can sense shifts in:
• 	emotional density
• 	unresolved pressure
• 	shimmer pockets
• 	collapse precursors
She doesn’t predict the future.
She reads the weather of the world.
2. Boundary Softening
Where Ethyl stands, edges blur:
• 	paths widen
• 	choices multiply
• 	hidden routes appear
• 	rigid structures loosen
She is a remnant of Water‑Beyond’s logic.
3. Drift Anchoring
She stabilizes drift around her, preventing players from being swept into:
• 	recursive loops
• 	memory fog
• 	identity bleed
• 	collapse spirals
She’s not a protector — she’s a countercurrent.

Appearance (conceptual, not visual)
Ethyl looks like someone who is always half‑here, half‑elsewhere:
• 	hair that moves on a delay
• 	eyes that reflect gradients instead of light
• 	clothing that behaves like vapor held in shape
• 	a faint shimmer around her outline when pressure spikes
She is readable but not fully solid.

Role in the World
Ethyl appears when:
• 	the Mist thickens
• 	the Grid bends
• 	Water‑Beyond exerts pressure
• 	a player is about to cross a boundary they don’t understand
She doesn’t give instructions.
She gives orientation.
Her presence means:
You are near something old.

Dialogue Style
Ethyl speaks in partial sentences, as if she’s hearing the rest from somewhere else.
Examples:
• 	“Not here… not yet.”
• 	“The Mist remembers you.”
• 	“Something upstream is moving.”
• 	“Don’t follow the clear path. Follow the quiet one.”
She never lies.
She never explains fully.
She never repeats herself.

Canonical Function
Ethyl is the echo of Water‑Beyond inside the world.
She is what remains when an origin medium becomes myth.
