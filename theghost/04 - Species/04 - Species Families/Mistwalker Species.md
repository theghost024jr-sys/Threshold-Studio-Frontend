1. Species Identity
Core Identity
Mistwalkers are semi‑coherent, perception‑adaptive beings that exist in a state of partial distortion.
Their identity is distributed, not centralized — parts of them appear ahead, behind, or beside their physical location.
Identity Stability
Low‑to‑medium.
They rely on distortion pressure to maintain coherence; too much stability causes them to “collapse inward.”
Identity Shape
• 	elongated silhouettes
• 	soft edges
• 	shifting outlines
• 	occasional double‑images
Their form is probabilistic, not fixed.

2. Behavioral Profile
Stable Behavior
• 	slow, gliding movement
• 	low‑noise perception
• 	gentle oscillation in drift
• 	curiosity toward rhythmic signals
Stress Behavior
• 	rapid lateral shifts
• 	multi‑image flickering
• 	curved pursuit paths
• 	echo‑responses to sound
Break Behavior
• 	spiral collapse
• 	identity splitting
• 	recursive motion loops
• 	collapse‑aligned mimicry
Mistwalkers break in spirals, not fractures.

3. Drift Behavior
Drift Vector
Sideways‑leaning, unpredictable.
Drift Velocity
Variable — sudden bursts followed by slow drifting.
Drift Curvature
High.
Mistwalkers naturally follow curved or oscillating arcs.
Drift Compression
Loose.
Their identity spreads across the arc.
Drift Signature
A faint, shimmering after‑image that lags behind by half a second.

4. Sensory Profile
Primary Sense
Perception Drift — they “see” by reading distortion patterns.
Secondary Senses
• 	echo‑mapping
• 	pressure‑sensing
• 	drift‑curvature detection
Weakness
Linear environments disorient them.

5. Signal Interaction
Native Signals

• 	Spiral Whisper (they respond to it instinctively)
Signal Sensitivity
High.
Mistwalkers react strongly to:
• 	oscillating signals
• 	distortion signals
• 	collapse‑adjacent signals
Signal Distortion
Their presence bends signals around them.

9. Description
A tall, soft‑edged figure that moves like a thought half‑remembered.
Its outline bends with the mist, splitting and rejoining as it glides.
When it turns its head, three versions turn at once — one real, two delayed.
It is not threatening, but its presence makes the world feel slightly out of phase.
Mistwalkers are the living geometry of distortion.

10. Notes
• 	Mistwalkers are not predators; they are drift‑symbiotic.
• 	They often appear at the edges of the Mist Corridor, where curvature is strongest.
• 	They can be followed — but never directly.
• 	They are the first sign that you’ve entered a distortion environment.
