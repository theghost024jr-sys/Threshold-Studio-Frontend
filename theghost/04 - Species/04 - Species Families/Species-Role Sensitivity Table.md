How each species family responds to the influence, presence, or expression of each Archetypal Role.

Sensitivity Levels
• 	Low — minimal behavioral or signal response
• 	Moderate — consistent but manageable influence
• 	High — strong behavioral or signal shift
• 	Critical — destabilizing; can trigger drift or collapse

Table


Species-by-Species Notes

• 	High to Seer — Seer resonance amplifies Owl pattern fixation.
• 	High to Flame — Flame intensity disrupts Owl stillness.
Crow

Interpretation Logic
Species sensitivity is shaped by:
1. Signal Type
• 	Stillness (Owl) destabilizes under Flame
• 	Interruption (Crow) destabilizes under Flame and Strider
• 	Resonance (Treeform) destabilizes under Harbor
• 	Drift (Mistborne) destabilizes under Seer and Strider
• 	Weight (Stonekin) destabilizes under Anchor
• 	Wave (Tidecall) destabilizes under Bridge and Harbor
2. Environmental Affinity
Species react differently depending on where the interaction occurs.
3. Role Pressure
Roles exert directional pressure that can stabilize or destabilize species.
4. Lineage Threads
Some species share lineage resonance with certain roles.
