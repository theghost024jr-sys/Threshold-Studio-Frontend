[[Signals]]
A canonical directory of all species families, variants, and their relational architecture within Threshold.

Purpose
This index provides a unified map of every species family in Threshold and their:
• 	Variants
• 	Native environments
• 	Signal patterns
• 	Role affinities
• 	Behavioral signatures
• 	Lineage threads
• 	Associated artifacts
It serves as the entry point for navigating the entire Species subsystem.
