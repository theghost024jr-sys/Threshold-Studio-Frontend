1. Emotional Weather
Names should feel like pressure, movement, or atmosphere, not biology.
Examples:
• 	Owl
• 	Mistborne
• 	Stonekin
• 	Tidecall
These aren’t animals — they’re expressions.

2. Pattern + Force
A species name usually combines:
• 	a pattern (shape, behavior, or movement)
• 	a force (pressure, resonance, drift, weight, wave)
Examples:
• 	Crow → boundary‑breaker
• 	Treeform → growth‑pattern
• 	Mistborne → drift‑pattern
• 	Stonekin → weight‑pattern

3. Mythic Simplicity
Names should be:
• 	short
• 	intuitive
• 	archetypal
• 	emotionally legible
Threshold species names are felt before they’re understood.
