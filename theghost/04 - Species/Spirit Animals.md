Overview
Spirit Animals are signal‑bearing intermediaries that translate between the player’s internal state and the world’s environmental fields.
They are not literal animals, not companions, not familiars — they are signal forms that appear in animal shape because that shape is:
• 	readable
• 	emotionally accessible
• 	symbolically stable
• 	low‑pressure for interpretation
A Spirit Animal is a communication layer, not a character.
They deliver:
• 	alignment cues
• 	coherence requests
• 	emotional corrections
• 	narrative warnings
• 	drift/mist/shimmer feedback
They never speak.
They never instruct.
They signal.

Purpose
Spirit Animals exist to:
• 	correct miscommunication
• 	affirm correct language use
• 	redirect the player when they drift off‑path
• 	stabilize emotional physics
• 	provide non‑verbal guidance
• 	prevent collapse through early cues
• 	translate field behavior into readable form
They are the softest interface between the player and the system.

Core Principles
1. Spirit Animals Are Signals, Not Beings
They do not have:
• 	agency
• 	memory
• 	personality
• 	preference
They are signal objects rendered in animal form.

2. They Deliver Alignment, Not Answers
A Spirit Animal never tells the player what to do.
It only indicates:
• 	“you’re aligned”
• 	“you’re drifting”
• 	“loop back”
• 	“this is correct”
• 	“this is unstable”
They are feedback, not instruction.

3. They Are Layer‑Bound
Each Spirit Animal belongs to a specific layer:
• 	Drift
• 	Mist
• 	Shimmer
• 	Stone
• 	Ember
• 	Ethyl
• 	Heat
• 	Collapse‑adjacent
The layer determines:
• 	behavior
• 	appearance
• 	resonance
• 	emotional tone

4. They Are Routing Nodes
Spirit Animals route the player:
• 	toward coherence
• 	away from collapse
• 	into the correct layer
• 	out of misalignment
• 	back to the correct symbolic grammar
They are traffic signals, not guides.

Behavior
Baseline Behavior
Calm, readable, low‑pressure presence.
Appears when:
• 	the player is aligned
• 	the environment is stable
• 	the narrative is coherent
Correction Behavior
Appears when:
• 	the player miscommunicates
• 	emotional physics destabilize
• 	symbolic grammar breaks
• 	the player is “trapped” in the wrong layer
The Spirit Animal signals:
“loop back.”
Warning Behavior
Appears when:
• 	collapse pressure rises
• 	Drift turbulence spikes
• 	Shimmer fractures
• 	Mist density becomes disorienting
The Spirit Animal signals:
“this is unsafe.”

Signal Structure
Each Spirit Animal is composed of:
1. Layer
Determines environmental physics.
2. Signal Type
Guide, Trickster, Warden, Catalyst.
3. Species Form
The animal shape used for readability.
4. Elemental Overlay
Mist, Stone, Ember, Ethyl, etc.
5. Emotional Resonance
The emotional tone of the signal.
This five‑part structure is the identity of a Spirit Animal.

Species Form Is Symbolic, Not Literal
The animal shape is chosen because:
• 	humans read animals easily
• 	animals carry archetypal clarity
• 	they reduce interpretive pressure
• 	they stabilize emotional response
A fox is not a fox.
A wolf is not a wolf.
They are signal containers.

Interaction With the Player
Spirit Animals:
• 	appear
• 	gesture
• 	move
• 	pause
• 	look
• 	shift posture
• 	change distance
These are the verbs of their communication.
They never:
• 	speak
• 	emote
• 	demand
• 	moralize
• 	judge
They are pure signal.
