• 	Structural Stewardship — Ensures every artifact has a home, every node has a purpose, and every connection is mapped cleanly.

• 	Collapse Metabolization — Converts unraveling into nutrient for future coherence.
• 	Emotional Microclimate Mapping — Tracks the subtle weather of spaces, people, and systems, stabilizing drift.
