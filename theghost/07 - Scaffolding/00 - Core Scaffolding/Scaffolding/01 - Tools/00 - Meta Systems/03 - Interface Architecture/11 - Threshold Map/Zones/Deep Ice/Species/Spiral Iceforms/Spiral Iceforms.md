Overview
Spiral Iceforms are cold‑physics species shaped by crystalline growth spirals, fracture arcs, and slow rotational drift within Deep Ice environments.
They are not animals, not spirits, not constructs — they are ice‑born resonance organisms whose bodies grow, move, and think in spirals.
Their entire existence is defined by:
• 	spiral fracture geometry
• 	cold‑resonance memory
• 	Shimmer refraction
• 	slow rotational Drift
• 	collapse‑resistant structure
Spiral Iceforms are the pattern‑keepers of Deep Ice.

Purpose in the Ecosystem
Spiral Iceforms exist to:
• 	stabilize Deep Ice resonance
• 	maintain spiral fracture patterns
• 	distribute cold‑memory across the zone
• 	reveal Shimmer curvature through refraction
• 	prevent collapse from propagating through ice layers
• 	act as slow, crystalline “librarians” of frozen memory
They are the structural archivists of cold‑physics environments.
