Essence
The Lantern is the archetype of illumination, clarity, and guidance through uncertainty.
It lights the path, stabilizes drift, and reveals what is obscured.
Function
• 	Illuminates hidden paths
• 	Clarifies emotional or signal confusion
• 	Stabilizes drift in others
• 	Acts as a beacon in chaotic environments
Signal Tendencies
• 	Strong affinity for Guide
• 	Secondary affinity for Catalyst
Layer Resonance
• 	Air (clarity)
• 	Ember (ignition)
Drift Behavior
• 	Under pressure: Lantern → Hunter (illumination becomes pursuit)
• 	Under collapse: Lantern → Sentinel (light becomes rigidity)
Shadow Expression
• 	Burnout
• 	Overexposure
• 	Harsh clarity
Species Notes
Species associated with light, sound, or high perception often express Lantern roles.
Overlay Interactions
• 	Ember‑Lantern = wildfire clarity
• 	Air‑Lantern = sky beacon
• 	Stone‑Lantern = lighthouse
