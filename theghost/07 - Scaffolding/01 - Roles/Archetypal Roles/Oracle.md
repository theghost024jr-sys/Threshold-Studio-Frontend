Essence
The Oracle is the archetype of intuition, subtext, and symbolic interpretation.
It reads beneath the surface, channels meaning, and reveals what cannot be spoken directly.
Function
• 	Interprets signals across layers
• 	Reveals hidden emotional structures
• 	Guides others through symbolic logic
• 	Acts as a bridge between conscious and unconscious
Signal Tendencies
• 	Strong affinity for Trickster
• 	Secondary affinity for Guide
Layer Resonance
• 	Ethyl (dream logic)
• 	Mist (liminality)
Drift Behavior
• 	Under pressure: Oracle → Weaver (insight becomes pattern)
• 	Under collapse: Oracle → Echo (identity dissolution)
Shadow Expression
• 	Distortion
• 	Ambiguity
• 	Manipulation through symbolism
Species Notes
Species with heightened intuition or symbolic behavior often express Oracle roles.
Overlay Interactions
• 	Ethyl‑Oracle = deep dreamseer
• 	Water‑Oracle = tide‑reader
• 	Ember‑Oracle = ecstatic prophet

Echo.md
Essence
The Echo is the archetype of reflection, resonance, and emotional mirroring.
It amplifies, absorbs, and reflects the signals of others.
Function
• 	Mirrors emotional states
• 	Reveals hidden dynamics through reflection
• 	Amplifies resonance patterns
• 	Acts as a stabilizer or destabilizer depending on context
Signal Tendencies
• 	Strong affinity for hybrid states
• 	Often emerges during drift or fracture
Layer Resonance
• 	Mist (soft reflection)
• 	Ethyl (symbolic mirroring)
Drift Behavior
• 	Under pressure: Echo → Weaver (reflection becomes connection)
• 	Under collapse: Echo → Oracle (reflection becomes prophecy)
Shadow Expression
• 	Identity bleed
• 	Loss of self
• 	Emotional parasitism
Species Notes
Highly social or mimic‑capable species often express Echo roles.
Overlay Interactions
• 	Mist‑Echo = fog mirror
• 	Ethyl‑Echo = dream reflection
• 	Air‑Echo = harmonic resonance
