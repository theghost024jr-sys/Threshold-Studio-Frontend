Essence
The Weaver is the archetype of connection, pattern, and relational intelligence.
It binds meaning, forms networks, and reveals the threads between things.
Function
• 	Creates coherence
• 	Forms alliances and structures of support
• 	Detects patterns across layers
• 	Stabilizes drift through connection
Signal Tendencies
• 	Strong affinity for Guide
• 	Secondary affinity for Catalyst
Layer Resonance
• 	Air (pattern clarity)
• 	Mist (soft connection)
Drift Behavior
• 	Under pressure: Weaver → Oracle (patterns become insight)
• 	Under collapse: Weaver → Echo (identity bleed)
Shadow Expression
• 	Over‑entanglement
• 	Loss of boundaries
• 	Emotional over‑identification
Species Notes
Social or migratory species often express Weaver roles.
Overlay Interactions
• 	Water‑Weaver = fluid networker
• 	Ember‑Weaver = spark‑binder
• 	Stone‑Weaver = structural architect
