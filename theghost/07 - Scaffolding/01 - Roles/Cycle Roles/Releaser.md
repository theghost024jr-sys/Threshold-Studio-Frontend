# Releaser

## Core role

The Releaser is the entity, event, or condition that **lets pressure leave the system without destroying it**. It is the moment where accumulated tension, contradiction, or memory is allowed to move, instead of being forced to stay.

## Function in the world

- **Pressure valve:** Prevents collapse by giving built‑up forces somewhere to go.  
- **Transition agent:** Marks the boundary between one state and the next (world state, character state, environment state).  
- **Memory discharger:** Converts stored contradiction into motion, story, or change.  
- **Cycle pivot:** Often sits at the turning point of a cycle (from peak tension to release).

## Behaviors

- **Selective release:** It never releases *everything*—it chooses what can safely move.  
- **Directional flow:** Release is not random; it has a vector (toward a person, place, myth, or system).  
- **Cost:** Every release has a price—someone pays, something is lost, or a new constraint appears.  
- **Signal:** A Releaser is always preceded by a signal (omens, glitches, emotional spikes, environmental anomalies).

## Types of Releasers

- **Environmental Releaser:** Storms, quakes, floods, eruptions, seasonal shifts.  
- **Organism Releaser:** Confessions, betrayals, sacrifices, transformations.  
- **Mythic Releaser:** Prophecies fulfilled, curses broken, oaths revoked.  
- **Systemic Releaser:** Laws repealed, protocols overridden, thresholds opened.

## Placement in cycles

- **Tension Cycle:** The Releaser sits just after maximum tension.  
- **Collapse Cycle:** The Releaser can soften or redirect collapse.  
- **Renewal Cycle:** The Releaser clears residue so renewal can begin.  
- **Void Cycle:** The Releaser opens access to the void without total erasure.

## Design questions

- **What pressure is being released?**  
- **Who or what acts as the Releaser?**  
- **What is the cost of release?**  
- **Where does the released energy go?**  
- **What new state becomes possible afterward?**
