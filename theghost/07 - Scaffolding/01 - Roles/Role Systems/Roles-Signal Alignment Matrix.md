Overview
This matrix defines how each Archetypal Role interacts with the major Signal Types.
Signals are behavioral vectors; Roles are behavioral archetypes.
Alignment determines:
• 	clarity
• 	stability
• 	drift likelihood
• 	shadow expression
• 	elemental resonance
• 	species‑role compatibility
Signals that align strongly with a Role amplify it.
Signals that misalign destabilize it.

Matrix Key
Strong Alignment — reinforces the Role’s core function
Secondary Alignment — supports but does not define
Neutral — minimal effect
Destabilizing — increases drift or shadow expression
Volatile — high drift risk, collapse pressure

Role‑by‑Role Breakdown
Sentinel
• 	Strong: Warden, Anchor
• 	Secondary: Guide
• 	Destabilizing: Trickster
• 	Volatile: Echo loops (feedback recursion)
Sentinel thrives on stability signals and collapses under recursive or chaotic ones.

Weaver
• 	Strong: Guide, Catalyst
• 	Secondary: Warden
• 	Destabilizing: Trickster
• 	Volatile: Hunter‑vector signals (over‑pursuit of threads)
Weaver needs relational clarity; pursuit signals overstretch it.

Hunter
• 	Strong: Catalyst, Seeker
• 	Secondary: Guide
• 	Destabilizing: Echo
• 	Volatile: Lantern‑burst signals (over‑illumination)
Hunter thrives on motion and collapses under reflection overload.

Oracle
• 	Strong: Seeker, Echo
• 	Secondary: Catalyst
• 	Destabilizing: Warden
• 	Volatile: Ember‑type spikes (signal overload)
Oracle needs subtlety; rigid or explosive signals fracture it.

Echo
• 	Strong: Echo, Resonance
• 	Secondary: Seeker
• 	Destabilizing: Catalyst
• 	Volatile: Trickster (identity destabilization)
Echo thrives on subtlety; chaotic signals dissolve its center.

Lantern
• 	Strong: Guide, Illumination
• 	Secondary: Catalyst
• 	Destabilizing: Echo
• 	Volatile: Seeker (over‑illumination collapse)
Lantern needs clarity; pursuit signals burn it out.

Usage Notes
1. Drift Prediction
Signal patterns can push a Role along the wheel or across it.
2. Species Compatibility
Species with strong signal tendencies will naturally gravitate toward certain Roles.
3. Overlay Tinting
Elemental overlays modify how signals express within a Role.
4. Map Placement
Signal‑role alignment determines where a spirit stabilizes on the Threshold Map.
