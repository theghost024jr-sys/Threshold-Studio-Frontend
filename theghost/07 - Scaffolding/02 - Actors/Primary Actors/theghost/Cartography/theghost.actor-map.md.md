Map Overview
This map defines the spatial logic, appearance zones, and behavioral topology of theghost within the world.
It is not a location map but a field‑based distribution model showing where theghost can manifest and how his presence interacts with environmental conditions.
Theghost does not travel; he emerges where conditions align.

1. Spatial Distribution
theghost appears only in zones with:
• 	collapse gradients
• 	emotional turbulence
• 	mythic substrate activation
• 	residue density
• 	boundary instability
• 	misaligned geometry
He is absent from:
• 	stable centers
• 	sealed systems
• 	high‑coherence regions
• 	narrative hubs
• 	areas with no substrate activity
His map is gradient‑based, not coordinate‑based.
