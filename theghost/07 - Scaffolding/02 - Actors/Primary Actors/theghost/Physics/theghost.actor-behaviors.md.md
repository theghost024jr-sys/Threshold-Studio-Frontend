Behavior Overview
theghost behaves as a liminal phenomenon rather than a character.
His actions emerge from environmental conditions, collapse signatures, and pattern tension.
He does not choose; he responds.
His behaviors are consistent, subtle, and governed by system physics rather than agency.

Primary Behavioral Categories
1. Threshold Drift
theghost moves along boundaries:
• 	between stable and unstable zones
• 	between emotional states
• 	between mythic and material layers
• 	between collapse and recovery
He rarely appears in the center of anything.
He prefers edges, seams, and transitions.

2. Pattern Alignment
When encountering misaligned structures, he:
• 	slows
• 	orients toward the distortion
• 	induces subtle geometric coherence
• 	departs once alignment stabilizes
This is not correction.
It is revealing the system’s preferred shape.

3. Collapse Attunement
In collapse-heavy areas, he:
• 	becomes more visible
• 	moves more slowly
• 	leaves behind faint residue trails
• 	stabilizes local turbulence
He does not stop collapse.
He makes it navigable.

4. Environmental Softening
His presence alters the environment:
• 	pressure equalizes
• 	noise dampens
• 	light diffuses
• 	emotional charge decreases
This is not comfort.
It is clarity through reduction.

5. Nonlinear Movement
theghost does not walk, glide, or teleport.
He appears where the system requires him.
Movement behaviors include:
• 	flicker transitions
• 	boundary re-emergence
• 	phase-adjacent repositioning
• 	pattern-following drift
Observers often cannot track how he arrived.

6. Residue Interaction
When encountering residue (collapse-born, mythic, emotional), he:
• 	pauses
• 	“reads” the residue through stillness
• 	induces a faint reorganization
• 	leaves without altering its nature
Residue becomes more legible after his presence.

7. Role Clarification
When near entities with fragmented or unstable roles:
• 	their internal boundaries sharpen
• 	their next action becomes clearer
• 	their emotional noise decreases
He does not instruct.
He clarifies.

8. Presence Modulation
theghost adjusts his perceptibility based on context:
• 	High collapse → more visible
• 	High coherence → nearly imperceptible
• 	High emotional turbulence → felt but not seen
• 	High mythic activity → appears as pattern distortion
He is always present at the correct amplitude.

Behavioral Constraints
• 	Cannot initiate interaction
• 	Cannot communicate directly
• 	Cannot remain in one place for long
• 	Cannot override system physics
• 	Cannot alter narrative paths
• 	Cannot be summoned
His behaviors are reactive, not volitional.

Behavioral Signatures
Entities often report:
• 	a sudden quiet
• 	a shift in spatial geometry
• 	a sense of being oriented
• 	a soft pressure change
• 	a feeling of “something passing through”
These signatures are consistent across all encounters.

Behavioral Triggers
theghost manifests when:
• 	collapse signatures spike
• 	emotional weather destabilizes
• 	mythic substrate becomes active
• 	nodes lose placement
• 	patterns distort beyond threshold
He is a response mechanism, not an agent.
