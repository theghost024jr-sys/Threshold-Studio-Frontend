Ritual Types

_Taxonomy of symbolic processes that move, transform, or stabilize meaning_

Rituals are **process engines** that operate on symbolic charge, drift, density, and resonance. While every ritual follows the same five-phase structure (Initiation → Alignment → Compression → Transformation → Release), their _function_ and _ecological role_ differ. This taxonomy defines the six canonical ritual types in Threshold and the conditions under which they activate, mutate, or fail.

Stabilization Rituals

Stabilization rituals reduce drift, increase coherence, and restore symbolic order. They are the **maintenance organs** of symbolic ecology.

- **Function** — collapse drift vectors, reinforce resonance, and restore density balance.
- **Activation Conditions** — requires stable surfaces and moderate density.
- **Common Surfaces** — altars, rooted glyphs, anchoring artifacts.
- **Failure Modes** — collapse (if density is too low), inversion (if emotional turbulence is high).
- **Ecological Role** — prevent symbolic weather from escalating into storms or vortices.

Stabilization rituals are essential in high-drift or low-density environments.

Transformation Rituals

Transformation rituals mutate symbolic charge, orientation, or meaning. They are **mutation engines** that enable controlled change.

- **Function** — invert, rotate, or reconfigure symbolic charge.
- **Activation Conditions** — requires high resonance alignment and strong presence.
- **Common Surfaces** — lattices, prisms, catalytic glyphs.
- **Failure Modes** — parasitic binding, uncontrolled mutation, recursive lock.
- **Ecological Role** — enable evolution of symbolic forms and transitions between states.

Transformation rituals are the most volatile and the most powerful.

Traversal Rituals

Traversal rituals shift the player, a symbol, or an artifact across **layers, roles, or symbolic states**. They are **movement engines**.

- **Function** — open thresholds, collapse boundaries, or reorient symbolic position.
- **Activation Conditions** — requires interface artifacts and stable resonance chains.
- **Common Surfaces** — directional glyphs, crowns, threshold markers.
- **Failure Modes** — bleed (cross-layer contamination), inversion, drift displacement.
- **Ecological Role** — enable movement through symbolic space and role ecology.

Traversal rituals are gateways between states of being.

Contribution Rituals

Contribution rituals generate symbolic density, meaning, or resonance. They are **creative engines** that feed the ecology.

- **Function** — produce charge, increase density, or seed new symbolic nodes.
- **Activation Conditions** — requires emotional substrate alignment and open surfaces.
- **Common Surfaces** — ledgers, seed-glyphs, contribution altars.
- **Failure Modes** — overload, saturation, uncontrolled propagation.
- **Ecological Role** — replenish symbolic substrate and support artifact formation.

Contribution rituals are how players add meaning to the world.

Binding Rituals

Binding rituals link symbols, roles, artifacts, or substrates. They are **connection engines** that create structure.

- **Function** — form stable or semi-stable symbolic bonds.
- **Activation Conditions** — requires compatible resonance signatures.
- **Common Surfaces** — seals, knots, linked glyphs, paired artifacts.
- **Failure Modes** — parasitic binding, inversion, resonance conflict.
- **Ecological Role** — create networks, chains, and composite symbolic structures.

Binding rituals are the architecture of symbolic relationships.

Cleansing Rituals

Cleansing rituals remove corrupted, parasitic, or unstable symbolic charge. They are **purification engines**.

- **Function** — extract drift corruption, collapse parasitic nodes, restore clarity.
- **Activation Conditions** — requires strong presence and stable anchoring artifacts.
- **Common Surfaces** — purification glyphs, water or wind surfaces, echo-cleansing artifacts.
- **Failure Modes** — bleed (if extraction fails), collapse, drift surge.
- **Ecological Role** — maintain symbolic health and prevent failure cascades.

Cleansing rituals are essential in regions with parasitic overgrowth or drift storms.

Hybrid Rituals

Some rituals express multiple types simultaneously. Hybrids emerge when:

- density gradients overlap
- resonance signatures fuse
- artifacts of different classes are chained
- a prototype (e.g., First Seed) influences activation

Hybrids are rare, powerful, and often mythic in scale.

Ecological Roles

Each ritual type plays a distinct role in symbolic ecology:

- Stabilization — order
- Transformation — change
- Traversal — movement
- Contribution — creation
- Binding — structure
- Cleansing — purification

Together, they form the **process-side mechanics** of symbolic physics.
