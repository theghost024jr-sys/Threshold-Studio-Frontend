Ritual Alter Shape

_A dynamic geometric ritual that transforms symbolic charge by shifting the ritual surface itself_

Ritual Alter Shape is a **transformation ritual** built around a mutable surface. Instead of performing the ritual _on_ a shape, the ritual _is_ the act of changing the shape. The geometry of the surface determines how symbolic charge is compressed, reoriented, or inverted. As the shape shifts, the ritual’s resonance signature shifts with it.

This ritual is a **shape‑logic engine**: it uses geometry as the mechanism of transformation.

Core Function

Alter Shape transforms symbolic charge by:

- changing the geometry of the ritual surface
- reorienting drift vectors according to new shape boundaries
- collapsing and expanding density in controlled pulses
- shifting resonance signatures as the shape transitions
- enabling multi‑state symbolic mutation

It is one of the most direct ways to alter meaning‑physics through form.

Activation Conditions

Because the ritual surface itself changes, activation requires:

- **Shape Integrity** — the initial geometry must be stable.
- **Resonance Flexibility** — the participant’s presence signature must tolerate rapid shifts.
- **Density Buffer** — enough symbolic density must be available to absorb shape transitions.
- **Artifact Support** — at least one artifact must anchor the transformation (glyph, lattice, crown, or seed).

If any condition is unstable, the ritual becomes volatile.

Ritual Phases

Alter Shape expresses the five canonical phases through **geometric transitions**.

Initiation

The ritual begins with a stable base shape (circle, square, triangle, spiral).

Alignment

The participant’s resonance synchronizes with the geometry’s symbolic orientation.

Compression

The shape contracts, folds, or tightens, pulling charge inward.

Transformation

The shape shifts—rotates, unfolds, splits, or reconfigures—changing the orientation of symbolic charge.

Release

The new shape stabilizes and emits a transformed resonance pattern.

The transformation phase is the most volatile and the most powerful.

Shape Logic

Each geometry expresses a different symbolic function. Alter Shape moves _between_ them.

- **Circle → Triangle** — stability to transformation
- **Triangle → Square** — volatility to anchoring
- **Square → Spiral** — structure to mutation
- **Spiral → Circle** — mutation to coherence
- **Line → Knot** — direction to binding
- **Knot → Line** — binding to release

The ritual’s meaning is encoded in the **sequence** of shapes, not any single form.

Surfaces

Alter Shape uses a **mutable ritual surface**, which may be:

- a flexible glyph plate

- a segmented altar surface
- a wearable surface that shifts geometry
- a projected symbolic membrane

The surface must be able to change form without breaking resonance continuity.
