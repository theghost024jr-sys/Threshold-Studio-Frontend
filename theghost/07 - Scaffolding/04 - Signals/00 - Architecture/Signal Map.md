# Signal Map
Mode: intake, pattern recognition, routing.

## Functions

- Identifies pattern, intensity, and layer resonance
- Routes signals to the correct transformation layer
- Acts as the system’s “attention filter”

## When to use
- When something arises and you don’t know where it belongs
- When a signal feels mixed or unclear
- When choosing a transformation pathway

## Outputs

- signal classification
