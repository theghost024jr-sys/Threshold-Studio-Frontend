# Branches Signal
*An environmental recursion signal expressed through growth, divergence, and threshold‑splitting*

The Branches Signal is an environmental behavior where the world expresses recursive divergence. It appears when thresholds, paths, or symbolic structures begin to split, fork, or multiply. Branches are not plants — they are **recursion geometry made visible**.

This signal marks the point where a single orientation becomes multiple possible futures.

---

## Essence
Branches express:
- divergence  
- recursive unfolding  
- multi‑path orientation  
- threshold splitting  
- symbolic growth  

Branches show where the world is **opening**, **multiplying**, or **testing directionality**.

---

## Geometry
The geometry of Branches is:
- forked vectors  
- recursive splits  
- mirrored or asymmetric branching  
- layered divergence  
- upward or outward growth patterns  

The geometry always begins from a **root** — the anchor point where the world commits to branching.

## Behavior
Branches behave by:
- creating multiple traversal options  
- splitting symbolic drift into parallel paths  
- increasing local density variation  
- generating mild turbulence at the split  
- amplifying emotional “choice pressure”  
- activating memory substrate around lineage and possibility  

Branches are not chaotic — they are **structured divergence**.

---

## Substrate Expression

### Symbolic Substrate
- drift splits into two or more vectors  
- symbolic weather becomes directional  
- fronts may form along each branch  

### Emotional Substrate
- amplitude rises slightly  
- emotional tone becomes anticipatory  
- resonance becomes outward and searching  

### Memory Substrate
- lineage threads fork  
- recursion becomes shallow but active  
- memory becomes possibility‑oriented  

### Scene Substrate
- thresholds divide  
- spatial gradients widen  
- traversal becomes multi‑path  

Branches are a **scene‑level choice architecture**.

---

## Worldstate Interactions

### With Drift
Branches appear when drift is:
- outward  
- rising  
- searching  
- reorienting  

They rarely appear during inward collapse.

### With Density
Branches form when density is:
- rising (Spring)  
- peak but stable (early Summer)  

They dissolve when density compresses (Winter).

### With Pressure
Branches increase local pressure slightly but do not destabilize it.

### With Seasonal Resonance
- Spring: common  
- Summer: directional  
- Autumn: rare  
- Winter: dormant  

Branches are a **Spring‑dominant signal**.

---

## Reading the Branches Signal
To interpret Branches:
- identify the root (origin point)  
- count the number of splits  
- observe drift direction along each branch  
- check emotional amplitude at the fork  
- compare to density gradients  

Branches show **where the world is choosing**.
