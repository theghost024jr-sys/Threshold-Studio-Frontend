Predictive models of how entangled collapse spreads through the system
Cascade Simulation Maps (CSMs) are non‑spatial diagrams that show how collapse jumps across entanglements. They don’t follow geography — they follow binding laws.
Each map simulates:
• 	Trigger point
• 	Binding activation sequence
• 	Phase misalignment
• 	Propagation pattern
• 	Containment opportunities
• 	Failure endpoints
There are four canonical simulation maps, each corresponding to a cascade type.

1. MIRROR CASCADE MAP
Collapse spreads as paired opposites
Trigger Node:
Shatter Zone (Fracture Bloom)
Propagation Path:
2. 	Shatter Zone → Stone Basin (QE‑003)
3. 	Stone Basin → Depth Chamber (QE‑102)
4. 	Depth Chamber → Quiet Grove (QE‑001)
5. 	Quiet Grove → Plains (QE‑001)
Pattern:

[Fracture Bloom]
      ↓
[Pressure Cascade]
      ↓
[Recursive Collapse]
      ↓
[Quiet Lock] ↔ [Drift Runaway]

Interpretation:
The system fractures, then overloads, then deepens too far, then locks and skims simultaneously.
Containment Window:
Between steps 2 and 3 — Stone Basin diffusion can break the chain.

2. COUNTERWEIGHT INVERSION MAP
Collapse spreads as polarity flips
Trigger Node:
Quiet Grove (Quiet Lock)
Propagation Path:
3. 	Quiet Grove → Plains (QE‑001)
4. 	Plains → Wind Channel (QE‑005)
5. 	Wind Channel → High Branch (QE‑005)
6. 	High Branch → Momentum Chamber (QE‑101)
Pattern:

[Quiet Lock]
      ↓
[Drift Runaway]
      ↓
[Vector Shear]
      ↓
[Drift Runaway+]
      ↓
[Linear Collapse]

Interpretation:
Stillness becomes runaway motion, which becomes shear, which becomes collapse.
Containment Window:
Step 2 — stabilizing Plains prevents Wind Channel shear.

3. PHASE MISMATCH MAP
Collapse spreads through temporal misalignment
Trigger Node:
Recursive Hollow (Recursive Collapse)
Propagation Path:
4. 	Recursive Hollow → Grove (QE‑004)
5. 	Grove → Depth Chamber (QE‑102)
6. 	Depth Chamber → Stone Basin (QE‑102)
7. 	Stone Basin → Elasticity Chamber (QE‑103)
Pattern:

[Recursive Collapse]
      ↓
[Quiet Lock]
      ↓
[Recursive Collapse+]
      ↓
[Pressure Cascade]
      ↓
[Elasticity Failure]
Interpretation:
Loops tighten, anchors overload, depth collapses inward, pressure spikes, elasticity fails.
Containment Window:
Step 1 — Grove can absorb the recursion if stillness is intact.

4. RIGIDITY CASCADE MAP

6. 	Deep Ice → Drift Corridors (indirect)
7. 	Drift Corridors → Pressure Mesh
8. 	Pressure Mesh → Resonance Web

[Frozen Collapse]
      ↓
[Freeze-Twist Lock]
      ↓
[Corridor Seizure]
      ↓
[Pressure Hardening]
      ↓
[Resonance Crystallization]
      ↓
[Linear Collapse]

Interpretation:
Flexibility dies, cold spreads, corridors seize, pressure hardens, meaning crystallizes, momentum snaps.
Containment Window:
Step 1 — Thaw-Flow Recovery can stop the entire cascade.

5. COMPOUND CASCADE MAP
When multiple entanglements fire at once
This is the catastrophic simulation.
Trigger Node:
Recursive Hollow (under high load)
Propagation Path:
6. 	Recursive Hollow → Grove (QE‑004)
7. 	Recursive Hollow → Plains (QE‑001)
8. 	Recursive Hollow → Deep Ice (QE‑103)
9. 	Grove → Depth Chamber (QE‑102)
10. 	Plains → Wind Channel (QE‑005)
11. 	Deep Ice → Elasticity Chamber (QE‑103)
12. 	Elasticity Chamber → Momentum Chamber (QE‑101)
Pattern:

          [Recursive Collapse]
          ↙       ↓       ↘
 [Quiet Lock] [Drift Runaway] [Freeze-Twist Lock]
        ↓            ↓               ↓
[Recursive+]   [Vector Shear]   [Elasticity Failure]
        ↘           ↓               ↙
           [Temporal Heartbreak]

Interpretation:
Three collapse modes ignite simultaneously and converge on the Time Organ.
Containment Window:
Only one: Elasticity Chamber must survive.
If elasticity holds, the system can recover.
If elasticity fails, the system enters total temporal lock.
