## Purpose

The Craving Map tracks what the system (or being) **pulls toward** when under pressure. It reveals:

- latent desires
    
- stability-seeking behaviors
    
- collapse-avoidance patterns
    
- resonance-seeking signals
    
- identity exposure zones
    

This canvas is not a mood tracker. It is a **structural diagnostic tool** that shows how craving behaves under drift, collapse, and distortion.

---

## Structure

The Craving Map contains:

- **Craving Vectors** — directional pulls toward environments, roles, or signals
    
- **Collapse Triggers** — conditions that activate craving
    
- **Resonance Anchors** — nodes that stabilize or fulfill craving
    
- **Identity Exposure Zones** — where craving reveals the self
    
- **Signal Emission Patterns** — how craving propagates
    

---

## Craving Vectors

Each craving is mapped as a vector:

- **Direction** — what the system moves toward
    
- **Intensity** — how strong the pull is
    
- **Origin Point** — what triggered the craving
    
- **Environmental Bias** — which environments amplify or suppress it
    

Example:

```
Craving: Solitude
Direction: Cold Spiral Zone
Intensity: High
Origin: Collapse adjacency in Spiral Grove
Bias: Amplified by Mirage Plain, suppressed by Quiet Grove
```

## Collapse Triggers

Craving emerges when stability fails. Triggers include:

- identity distortion
    
- role inversion
    
- signal interference
    
- drift overload
    
- field compression
    

Each trigger is mapped to its corresponding craving vector.

---

## Resonance Anchors

These are nodes that stabilize craving:

- environments that fulfill the pull
    
- roles that align with the craving
    
- signals that soothe or complete the loop
    

Anchors are not solutions — they are **structural matches**.
