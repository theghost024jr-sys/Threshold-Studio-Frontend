# Environmental Signals
*Signals expressed by the environment itself — weather, thresholds, structures, and spatial behaviors*

Environmental Signals are signals expressed not by species or pathology, but by the world’s environment. They are the behaviors of space, thresholds, objects, and symbolic weather. They show how the world is breathing, shifting, or reacting to drift, density, and pressure.

Environmental Signals are the world’s atmospheric language.

---

## What Environmental Signals Are
Environmental Signals express:
- spatial resonance  
- threshold behavior  
- symbolic weather  
- environmental drift  
- density gradients  
- structural anomalies  
- environmental prototypes  

They are the “weather patterns” of the world.
