Purpose
Recovery Physics defines the laws that govern how a destabilized identity becomes coherent again.
It is not healing, reversal, or restoration.
It is a forward‑moving physical process that transforms collapse residue into stable identity.
Recovery Physics governs:
• 	how collapse becomes inert
• 	how drift stabilizes
• 	how identity re‑coheres
• 	how signals shift from collapse to recovery
• 	how environments support stabilization
• 	how roles re‑align after destabilization
This is the physics substrate beneath all recovery behavior.

1. The Three Laws of Recovery Physics
Recovery Physics is built on three universal laws.

1.1 The Law of Forward Coherence
Recovery never returns identity to a previous state.
It always moves identity forward into a new stable configuration.
Implications:
• 	collapse residue is integrated, not erased
• 	identity shape changes permanently
• 	recovery is directional, not cyclical
This is the foundational law.

1.2 The Law of Drift Stabilization
Recovery begins when drift momentum drops below the collapse threshold.
Implications:
• 	drift curvature straightens
• 	drift velocity decreases
• 	drift becomes rhythmic or linear
• 	collapse contagion halts
Drift stabilization is the trigger condition for recovery.

1.3 The Law of Boundary Reformation
Recovery completes when identity boundaries re‑form and can hold motion.
Implications:
• 	collapse becomes inert
• 	roles re‑align
• 	signals normalize
• 	environments re‑open
• 	forward motion becomes possible
Identity must be able to contain itself before recovery ends.

2. Recovery States
Recovery Physics defines three physical states that identity moves through.

2.1 Inert Collapse State
Collapse has stopped spreading but is still active internally.
Characteristics:
• 	drift is slow
• 	identity is fragile
• 	collapse residue is present
• 	signals are muted
This is the entry point of recovery.

2.2 Re‑Coherence State
Identity reorganizes into a new stable shape.
Characteristics:
• 	drift becomes rhythmic
• 	collapse residue integrates
• 	emotional noise reduces
• 	role switching becomes deliberate
This is the active phase of recovery.

2.3 Forward State
Identity resumes motion in a stable configuration.
Characteristics:
• 	drift is linear or patterned
• 	collapse is inert
• 	signals normalize
• 	roles re‑engage
This is the completion of recovery.

3. Recovery Forces
Recovery Physics is driven by four stabilizing forces.

3.1 Compression Force
Identity compresses into coherence.
Aligned with:
• 	Ethyl

• 	Deep Quiet

3.2 Memory Force
Identity reconnects with continuity.
Aligned with:

• 	old forests

3.3 Heat‑Balance Force
Identity warms and becomes flexible.
Aligned with:

• 	warm pockets

3.4 Pattern Force
Identity regains interpretive capacity.
Aligned with:
• 	Clearline
• 	Scholar/Listener roles

4. Recovery Vectors
Recovery moves identity along three canonical vectors.

4.1 Stabilization Vector
Collapse → Inert Collapse
• 	drift slows
• 	collapse halts
• 	boundaries re‑form

4.2 Re‑Coherence Vector
Inert Collapse → Re‑Coherence
• 	identity reorganizes
• 	collapse residue integrates
• 	signals clarify

4.3 Forward Vector
Re‑Coherence → Forward Motion
• 	drift resumes
• 	roles re‑engage
• 	environments re‑open
These vectors define the shape of recovery.

5. Recovery Interference
Recovery can be disrupted by:
• 	spiral drift
• 	distortion pressure
• 	emotional overload
• 	collapse signals
• 	role opposition
• 	unstable environments
Interference does not reset recovery — it delays it.

6. Recovery Completion Conditions
Recovery is complete when:
• 	identity is coherent
• 	drift is stable
• 	collapse is inert
• 	signals normalize
• 	roles re‑align
• 	environment re‑opens
Recovery ends when forward motion is possible without collapse.
