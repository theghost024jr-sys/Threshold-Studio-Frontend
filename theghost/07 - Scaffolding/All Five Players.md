# ROOT CARDS — FIVE PLAYERS, FIVE ATTRIBUTES EACH
### Proto‑Proto Edition (v0.0)

---

## PLAYER ONE — The Proximity Connector
**Theme:** linking PNG.md pairs using closeness + memory

1. **Click Target**  
   You may click any **PNG.md pair** that is visually close to another PNG.md on the canvas.

2. **Action**  
   When you click a PNG.md, you may attempt to **connect it** to the nearby PNG.md.

3. **Creation**  
   You do **not** create anything new.

4. **Linking**  
   You may create **one link** between the two PNG.md files *only if* you can **remember** the second PNG.md without looking again.

5. **Limiter**  
   You may only link PNG.md files that are **spatially close**, **remembered**, and **unchanged** during your attempt.

---

## PLAYER TWO — The Blank Filler
**Theme:** emptiness → meaning

1. **Click Target**  
   You may click any **blank `.md` file** (zero content).

2. **Action**  
   You may add **one sentence** to that blank `.md`.

3. **Creation**  
   You do **not** create new nodes.

4. **Linking**  
   You may **not** add links.

5. **Limiter**  
   You may only act on `.md` files that are **still blank** at the moment you click.

---

## PLAYER THREE — The Biome Spawner
**Theme:** images → biomes

1. **Click Target**  
   You may click any **image PNG** on the canvas.

2. **Action**  
   When you click an image, you may **declare a biome** out loud.

3. **Creation**  
   You may create **one biome node** (`.md`) with that name.

4. **Linking**  
   You may link the biome node **to the clicked PNG**.

5. **Limiter**  
   You may not link the biome to anything else or edit it after creation.

---

## PLAYER FOUR — The Layer Binder
**Theme:** attaching meaning to layers

1. **Click Target**  
   You may click any **Layer node** (Mist, Stone, Ember, etc.).

2. **Action**  
   When you click a Layer, you may **select one existing `.md` file**.

3. **Creation**  
   You do **not** create new nodes.

4. **Linking**  
   You may link the selected `.md` **to the Layer**.

5. **Limiter**  
   You may only attach `.md` files that are **not blank**, **not biomes**, and **not PNG.md pairs**.

---

## PLAYER FIVE — The Meaning Connector
**Theme:** `.md` ↔ `.md` relationships

1. **Click Target**  
   You may click any **two `.md` files** that already contain content.

2. **Action**  
   When you click the first `.md`, you may **declare a connection** to the second.

3. **Creation**  
   You do **not** create new nodes.

4. **Linking**  
   You may create **one link** between the two `.md` files.

5. **Limiter**  
   You may only link `.md` files that have content and have not been linked by you in the last 60 seconds.
