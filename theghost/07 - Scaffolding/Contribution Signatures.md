VII. Signature Interaction
--------------------------

Signatures interact through:
- resonance  
- interference  
- amplification  
- cancellation  
- drift coupling  

Compatible signatures strengthen each other.  
Incompatible signatures distort or cancel.

Links: [[Identity Resonance]], [[Drift Field]]


VIII. Signature Failure Modes
-----------------------------

A Contribution Signature can fail through:
- identity slippage  
- drift misalignment  
- boundary rupture  
- collapse interference  
- residue overload  

Failure leads to contribution collapse or contribution inversion.

Links: [[Failure Cascade]], [[Collapse Drift]]
