**2. THE FIVE ECOLOGICAL STATES OF THE CYCLE**

Each phase produces a distinct ecological condition.

_The ecology of gathering and stability_

**Environmental behavior:**

- inward‑curving paths
- radial flora growth
- stable microclimates

**Species behavior:**

- clustering
- cooperative foraging
- territorial stabilization

**Drift behavior:**

- gathering drift
- low turbulence

**Resonance behavior:**

- harmonic, low‑variance hum

**Ecological function:**

**Drift behavior:**

**Drift behavior:**

**Species behavior:**

**Drift behavior:**

**5. ECOLOGICAL FEEDBACK LOOPS**
