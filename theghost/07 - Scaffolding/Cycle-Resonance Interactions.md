**3. HOW THE CYCLE SHAPES RESONANCE**

**A. Coherence → Harmonic Resonance**

Coherence produces a **stable, low‑variance hum**.

Effects:

- aligns structures
- reduces drift turbulence
- strengthens clustering
- stabilizes identity

Environmental result:  
Clear, predictable resonance fields.

**B. Tension → Sharp Resonance**

Tension produces **narrow‑band, high‑contrast resonance**.

Effects:

- reveals strain
- amplifies pressure corridors
- increases sensitivity
- destabilizes rigid structures

Environmental result:  
Resonance beams, pressure spikes, brittle zones.

**C. Collapse → Diffuse Resonance**

Collapse produces **unstable, multi‑directional resonance**.

Effects:

- disorients drift
- destabilizes structures
- amplifies turbulence
- accelerates dissolution

Environmental result:  
Resonance clouds, chaotic gradients.

**D. Void → Muted Resonance**

Void produces **low‑frequency, dampened resonance**.

Effects:

- reduces activity
- lowers metabolic load
- stabilizes collapse residue
- prepares for renewal

Environmental result:  
Quiet resonance basins.

**E. Renewal → Flickering Resonance**

Renewal produces **rising, flickering resonance**.

Effects:

- ignites early forms
- attracts spark drift
- organizes upward movement
- re‑establishes coherence

Environmental result:  
Shimmering resonance nodes.

**4. HOW RESONANCE SHAPES THE CYCLE**

Resonance is not passive.  
It actively influences the Cycle’s timing and behavior.

**A. Resonance Stability → Phase Duration**

- **Stable resonance** prolongs Coherence and Void.
- **Unstable resonance** accelerates Tension and Collapse.
- **Rising resonance** accelerates Renewal.

Resonance stability is the **clock** of the Cycle.

**B. Resonance Amplitude → Transition Violence**

Amplitude determines how violently a phase transitions.

- high amplitude → sharp transitions
- low amplitude → soft transitions

Amplitude is the **volume** of the Cycle.

**C. Resonance Directionality → Phase Clarity**

Directionality determines how clearly a phase expresses.

- harmonic → clear Coherence
- narrow → clear Tension
- diffuse → clear Collapse
- muted → clear Void
- flickering → clear Renewal

Directionality is the **signal** of the Cycle.

**D. Resonance Interference → Hybrid Phases**

Interference creates hybrid states:

- harmonic + sharp → Circle–Gate
- sharp + diffuse → Gate–Crown
- diffuse + muted → Crown–Tree
- muted + flicker → Tree–Lantern
- flicker + harmonic → Lantern–Circle

Interference is the **phase‑blending mechanism**.

**5. CYCLE–RESONANCE FEEDBACK LOOPS**

Cycle and resonance reinforce each other.

**Positive feedback (amplifying):**

- Coherence harmonizes → deeper Coherence
- Tension sharpens → deeper Tension
- Collapse diffuses → deeper Collapse

**Negative feedback (balancing):**

- Collapse reduces Tension
- Void reduces Collapse
- Renewal reduces Void

Feedback loops determine the **shape** of each cycle.

**Tension → Collapse**

**Collapse → Void**

**Void → Renewal**

Resonance is the **earliest indicator** of phase change.
