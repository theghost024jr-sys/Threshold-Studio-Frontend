Definition
Invariants are the non‑negotiable laws that stabilize the Field and constrain how vectors operate.
They define what the system always preserves, regardless of context, role, or state.
An invariant cannot be violated without collapse.
It is the backbone of the System of Force.

Purpose
To ensure:
• 	coherence
• 	stability
• 	conservation
• 	interpretive closure
• 	predictable consequence
Invariants prevent the system from fragmenting or contradicting itself.

Core Invariants
1. Cost
Nothing occurs without cost.
Every action, gift, drift, or crossing requires an expenditure or transfer of force.
2. Transformation
Every interaction changes something.
No motion leaves the system unchanged.
3. Identity
A thing remains itself through change.
Transformation modifies state, not essence.
4. Consequence
Every action produces an outcome.
No action is inert or without effect.
5. Resolution
All drift, imbalance, or tension must resolve.
Unresolved force cannot persist indefinitely.
6. Conservation
Meaning, motion, and force cannot disappear.
They must propagate, transform, or resolve.
7. Reciprocity
Force moves in loops, not lines.
Every giving creates a return path.

Properties of Invariants
Universal
Invariants apply to every artifact, vector, and node in the system.
Non‑negotiable
They cannot be overridden, bypassed, or selectively ignored.
Stabilizing
They prevent runaway force, infinite drift, or incoherent motion.
Interlocking
Each invariant reinforces the others.
Cost supports consequence.
Consequence supports resolution.
Resolution supports conservation.
Interpretive
Invariants determine how the system interprets actions and states.

Function
Invariants:
• 	stabilize the Field
• 	constrain vector behavior
• 	maintain coherence across the Matrix
• 	ensure predictable outcomes
• 	preserve the integrity of roles, gifts, thresholds, and drift
• 	prevent contradictions
• 	guarantee closure
Without invariants, the system would collapse into arbitrary motion.
With invariants, the system becomes a coherent engine.

Relationship to Other Meta Files
• 	Binding Law → declares universal participation
• 	Field → provides the environment
• 	Vectors → generate motion
• 	Matrix of Force → maps all interactions
• 	Invariants → stabilize and constrain all motion
Invariants are the laws that keep the Matrix from tearing itself apart.
