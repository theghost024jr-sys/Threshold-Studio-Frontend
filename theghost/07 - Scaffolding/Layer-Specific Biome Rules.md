## Overview

Each Layer in Threshold has distinct environmental physics, symbolic density, and emotional drift behaviors. Biomes placed within these Layers must conform to the Layer’s phenomenological constraints. This document outlines the canonical rules for biome placement, behavior, and integration within each Layer.

---

## 01 Substrate Layer

- **Biome Types**: Foundational, memory-rich, slow-drift environments.
    
- **Allowed Biomes**: The Soil, Root Basin, Memory Grove.
    
- **Rules**:
    
    - Must anchor symbolic memory.
        
    - Drift speed must be low.
        
    - Collapse adjacency is permitted but must be stabilized.
        

## 02 Species Layer

- **Biome Types**: Ecological, migratory, interaction-heavy environments.
    
- **Allowed Biomes**: Marsh, Forest, Ruins.
    
- **Rules**:
    
    - Must support species drift and role expression.
        
    - Emotional physics must be balanced.
        
    - Signal density must be moderate.
        

## 03 Environment Layer

- **Biome Types**: Atmospheric, pressure-bearing, emotionally resonant environments.
    
- **Allowed Biomes**: Firefly Field, High Altitude Layer, Collapse Edge.
    
- **Rules**:
    
    - Must express pressure physics.
        
    - Emotional saturation must be mapped.
        
    - Signal interactions must be traceable.
        

## 04 Identity Layer

- **Biome Types**: Symbolic, stance-reactive, memory-anchored environments.
    
- **Allowed Biomes**: Mirror Basin, Identity Spiral, Fractured Field.
    
- **Rules**:
    
    - Must respond to player stance.
        
    - Identity geometry must be coherent.
        
    - Role bias must be declared.
        

## 05 Signal Layer

- **Biome Types**: Signal-emitting, mirage-prone, drift-sensitive environments.
    
- **Allowed Biomes**: Mirage Field, Signal Core, Residual Zone.
    
- **Rules**:
    
    - Must emit or absorb signal.
        
    - Compression and residue must be mapped.
        
    - Spiral behavior must be declared.
        

## 06 Emotional Layer

- **Biome Types**: Emotionally saturated, collapse-adjacent, memory-reactive environments.
    
- **Allowed Biomes**: Emotional Core, Compression Zone, Cold Column.
    
- **Rules**:
    
    - Must declare emotional physics.
        
    - Drift behavior must be stable or fractured.
        
    - Collapse adjacency must be intentional.
        

---

## Integration Notes

- Biomes may appear in multiple Layers if their physics are multi-domain.
    
- Each biome must declare its **Layer Affinity** in its .md file.
    
- Cross-links between biomes and Layer Indexes must be dotted unless physics inheritance is declared.
    

## Placement Protocol

- Place biome .md files under `03 Environments/LayerName/`.
    
- Canvas nodes must reflect Layer geometry.
    
- Index files must be updated with new biome entries.
