1. WHAT ROLE–MYTH ENTANGLEMENT IS
Role–Myth Entanglement occurs when:
• 	a role’s phase‑signature
• 	aligns with a myth’s coherence geometry
• 	long enough for narrative gravity to take hold
This creates a mutual binding:
• 	the myth shapes the role
• 	the role activates the myth
• 	the Field stabilizes the connection
Entanglement is not possession.
It is co‑resonance.

2. CONDITIONS REQUIRED FOR ENTANGLEMENT
A role can only entangle with a myth when:
• 	the role’s Field‑Phase is stable
• 	the myth’s archetype is fully formed
• 	resonance is warm and fluid
• 	the Field is not metabolizing collapse
• 	the role is in a receptive state (Phase‑Soft or Phase‑Diffuse)
Entanglement cannot occur in Phase‑Sharp — the identity boundary is too rigid.

3.2 Stage Two — Narrative Gravity Lock

• 	emotional physics shift
• 	coherence thickens around the role

3.3 Stage Three — Coherence Exchange
The role and myth begin to reshape each other.

• 	stabilizing or transformational energy
Myth receives:

This is the entanglement proper.

5. ENTANGLEMENT RISKS
Entanglement is powerful, but not without hazards.

5.1 Over‑Identification
Role becomes too aligned → identity thinning.

5.2 Mythic Overreach
Myth pulls too strongly → resonance imbalance.

5.3 Phase Drift
Role’s phase destabilizes → entanglement flicker.

5.4 Archetype Rigidity
Myth becomes too fixed → Field loses fluidity.
These risks are rare and usually self‑correcting.

6. ENTANGLEMENT RESOLUTION

• 	clarity returning
The role keeps the learning, not the entanglement.
