**Tier 3 — Phase‑Competent**

_Able to shift phase deliberately_

- stabilizes local turbulence

**Primary lesson:**  
_Phase is choice._

**Tier 4 — Phase‑Fluent**

_Phase becomes a tool, not a state_

**Primary lesson:**  
_Phase is language._

_Identity and phase become one continuous behavior_

**Characteristics:**

- mythic entanglement becomes co‑creative
- Field‑Rhythm Intelligence becomes perceptible

This is the apex of Role‑Phase Evolution.

Each cycle deepens the role’s identity and expands its phase fluency.
