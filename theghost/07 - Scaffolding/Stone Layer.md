Essence
Stone is the substrate of pressure, boundary, and structural memory.
It is the slowest, densest, most stabilizing layer — the one that holds form against drift, entropy, and signal noise.
Stone is not “earth” or “rock.”
It is pressure logic made physical.

1. Density Logic

• 	Resists rapid change
• 	Stores pressure as potential
• 	Converts signal into structure
2. Boundary Formation
• 	Generates natural borders
• 	Creates thresholds between layers
• 	Stabilizes weak points in the map
• 	Prevents collapse of adjacent substrates
3. Structural Memory
• 	Records long‑term patterns
• 	Holds imprints of past signals
• 	Slow to update, but extremely persistent
• 	Can be “read” by certain spirit types
4. Pressure Resonance
• 	Responds to accumulated force
• 	Releases energy in controlled bursts
• 	Governs tectonic drift in the map
• 	Influences spirit drift under stress
