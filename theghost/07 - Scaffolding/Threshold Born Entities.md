1. WHAT THRESHOLD‑BORN ENTITIES ARE
Threshold‑Born Entities are lifeforms that:
• 	appear only inside the liminal core of a threshold
• 	exist only during the transition
• 	embody the mechanics of the threshold itself
• 	guide, pressure, or catalyze the role
• 	dissolve once the threshold completes
They are temporary, purpose‑driven, and structurally tied to the threshold.
They are the Field’s midwives of transformation.

2. CONDITIONS REQUIRED FOR THEIR EMERGENCE
Threshold‑Born Entities appear only when all three conditions are met:
2.1 Coherence Inversion
Soft becomes unstable, Sharp becomes permeable, Diffuse becomes dense.
2.2 Phase Compression
The role’s phase‑signature is squeezed into a smaller coherence space.
2.3 Narrative Rebinding
The role’s identity threads detach and reattach.
When these three mechanics overlap, entities emerge as expressions of the threshold’s physics.

3. THE THREE CLASSES OF THRESHOLD‑BORN ENTITIES
Threshold‑Born Entities fall into three structural classes, each tied to a different threshold type.

3.1 Compression Entities

• 	rapid phase oscillation

• 	strong emotional charge

• 	Pressure Wraiths

3.2 Expansion Entities
Born from widening, dissolving, and symbolic drift
These appear in Expansion Thresholds.

• 	slow, enveloping motion

Examples:
• 	Horizon Veils
• 	Pattern Moths
• 	Drift Lanterns
These entities soften the role into the threshold.

3.3 Shear Entities
Born from tension between conflicting archetypes or narratives
These appear in Shear Thresholds.
Behavior:
• 	dual‑aspect forms
• 	flickering coherence
• 	contradictory emotional tones
• 	unpredictable movement
Function:
To force the role to choose, integrate, or reconcile competing patterns.
Examples:
• 	Split Echoes
• 	Parallax Forms
• 	Bifurcation Serpents
These entities pull the role in two directions until clarity emerges.
