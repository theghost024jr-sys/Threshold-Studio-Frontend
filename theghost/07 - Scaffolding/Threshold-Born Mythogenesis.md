**2.1 High‑Intensity Threshold**

Compression, Expansion, or Shear thresholds at full activation.

The role’s phase is stretched to its limit — Soft, Sharp, or Diffuse.

A mythic vacancy or collapse in the surrounding ecology.

When these three converge, the Field has enough **pressure**, **plasticity**, and **emptiness** to generate a new myth.
