- If they fear collapse → the raft looks fragile.
- If they crave direction → the raft aligns with a path.
- If they’re torn → the raft sits neutral, waiting.
- If they’re ready → the raft becomes unmistakably solid.

The raft is not a test.  
It’s a **translation** of the player’s internal state into physical form.
