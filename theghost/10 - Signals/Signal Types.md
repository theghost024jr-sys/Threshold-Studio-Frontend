# **Signal Types**

**Color:** _Identity Pink (#FF4DA6)_ **Category:** `06 - Mind` **Role:** Defines the four fundamental behavioral signals that shape perception, action, drift, and alignment across all layers.

Signal Types are the **behavioral primitives** of the Threshold substrate. They are not personalities, roles, or archetypes — they are **base‑state signal emissions** that determine how an entity _moves through cognition_.

Every entity, environment, and system expresses a **primary**, **secondary**, and **latent** Signal Type.

# **The Four Canonical Signal Types**

**Essence:** Orientation, coherence, forward‑vector **Behavioral Signature:**

- stabilizes attention
    
- reduces drift
    
- increases clarity
    
- creates navigable pathways

# **Signal Type Mechanics**

## **Primary Signal**

The dominant behavioral emission. Determines **default cognitive posture**.

## **Secondary Signal**

Modulates the primary. Determines **how** the primary expresses under load.

## **Latent Signal**

Only emerges under:

- drift
    
- pressure
    
- collapse
    
- threshold events
    

Latent signals reveal **true alignment**.

# **Interaction With Layers**

Signal Types express differently depending on substrate:

- **Air** → clarity, orientation
    
- **Mist** → ambiguity, inversion
    
- **Stone** → structure, resistance
    
- **Ember** → ignition, acceleration
    
- **Ethyl** → dissolution, recombination
    
- **Earth** → grounding, stabilization

# **Interaction With Drift**

Signal Types determine **how drift begins**, **how it spreads**, and **how it resolves**.

- Guide drift → over‑clarity, rigidity
    
- Trickster drift → fragmentation, incoherence
    
- Warden drift → over‑containment, stagnation
    
- Catalyst drift → runaway escalation
