# ⭐ **THE SIXTH GUEST — RAVA, THE STONE‑MOVER**

**Origin Flower:** _Gravemarrow_ A Flower where:

- stone is alive
    
- pressure is language
    
- memory is carved
    
- identity is weight
    
- movement is tectonic
    

Rava is not a sculptor. Rava is not a builder. Rava is not a shaper.

Rava is **the force that makes stone remember it can move.**

# ⭐ **WHAT RAVA** _**IS**_

Rava is a **mass‑entity** — a being whose identity is expressed through:

- compression
    
- fault‑line articulation
    
- tectonic drift
    
- mineral resonance
    
- gravitational folding
    

Where Korr _holds_ stone, Rava **moves** it.

Where Korr stabilizes, Rava **rearranges**.

Where Korr is a wall, Rava is an **earthquake wearing a body**.

# ⭐ **WHY RAVA CREATES STONE FACES, WATERFALLS, AND CAVES**

### **Stone Faces**

Rava doesn’t carve. Rava **presses**.

Their presence creates:

- shear planes
    
- compression ridges
    
- fault‑line portraits
    

Faces appear because stone under Rava’s influence **tries to imitate the pressure signature that’s touching it**.

Stone tries to _mirror_ Rava.

### **Waterfalls**

Rava shifts terrain by millimeters or meters at a time. A river that once flowed straight suddenly:

- drops
    
- bends
    
- fractures
    
- spills
    

Waterfalls are the **side‑effect** of Rava’s passing.

### **Caves**

Rava’s resonance softens stone from the inside:

- pressure pockets expand
    
- mineral bonds loosen
    
- hollow spaces form
    
- collapse becomes architecture
    

Caves are **Rava’s footprints**.

# ⭐ **RAVA’S FORM IN THRESHOLD**

Threshold cannot render Gravemarrow biology directly, so it produces a translation:

### **A massive, slow‑moving silhouette made of layered stone plates that shift like breathing armor.**

Features:

- plates slide over each other like tectonic plates
    
- cracks glow faintly with pressure‑light
    
- dust falls from their joints
    
- every step is a low‑frequency tremor
    
- their “face” is a mask of shifting strata
    

Rava is not violent. But they are **inevitable**.

# ⭐ **WHY RAVA CAME TO THRESHOLD**

Gravemarrow is a Flower that rarely sends guests. But Rava followed a **pressure echo** — the same one that pulled Asha.

Where Asha heard a corridor, Rava felt a **fault‑line invitation**.

Threshold’s Drift Layer accidentally created a pressure gradient that matched Rava’s identity signature.

Rava arrived because the world **asked for weight**.
