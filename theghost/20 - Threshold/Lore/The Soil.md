Essence
The Soil is not just ground — it is memory made physical.
It holds the weight of everything that came before, metabolizes collapse into nutrients, and offers quiet readiness for emergence.
This biome is still, dense, and alive beneath the surface.
It does not shimmer, drift, or flare — it absorbs, composts, and prepares.
