# **00 – Index.md**

## **Fib Flower — System Architecture Index**

### **Purpose**

The Fib Flower is the foundational architectural construct of Threshold. It defines how nodes organize, migrate, stabilize, collapse, and evolve within a structured numerical geometry. The Flower establishes the gravitational, behavioral, and energetic rules that govern all node interactions across the system.

### **Scope**

**Included:**

- Orbit layers and their behavioral physics
    
- Frontier 5–8 and its survival mechanics
    
- Node dynamics (pressure, drift, collapse, anchor)
    
- Circuit Mirror behavior and demand generation
    
- Cross‑Flower migration rules
    
- Flower-level ethos interactions
    
- Structural relationships to chambers and intelligence layers
    

**Excluded:**

- Collapse-born entities (non-indexable)
    
- Narrative or lore interpretations
    
- Worker implementation details
    
- Basin UI or telemetry specifics
    
- Any node behavior not governed by Flower geometry
    

### **Substructures**

- **Orbit Layers** — The concentric behavioral zones of the Flower
    
- **Frontier 5–8** — The solitary survival band where contribution becomes mandatory
    
- **Node Physics** — Collapse, Drift, Anchor, Pressure, Resonance
    
- **Circuit Mirror** — Internal feedback loop generating demand through the slit
    
- **Migration Rules** — Cross‑Flower capture, orbit elevation, and drift thresholds
    
- **Ethos Interaction Layer** — How Flowers respond to chamber-level ethos deltas
    

### **Relationships**

- **Collapse → Drift → Anchor Map** — The Flower expresses this cycle at every orbit layer
    
- **Ethos Chamber** — Provides alignment, resonance, and chamber scores that influence Flower behavior
    
- **Basin Page** — Visualizes Flower telemetry (heatmaps, pulses, bundle access, drift metrics)
    
- **Node Bundles** — Flower geometry determines bundle relevance and activation frequency
    
- **Chamber Intelligence** — Reads Flower behavior to generate system-wide pulses and deltas
    

### **Stability Notes**

- **Collapse Zones:**
    
    - Outer halo nodes (low connectivity)
        
    - Frontier nodes under insufficient contribution
        
    - Nodes with unresolved pressure or misaligned ethos
        
- **Drift Tendencies:**
    
    - Nodes with unbalanced charge (static electricity analogue)
        
    - Nodes near slit boundaries
        
    - Nodes experiencing cross‑Flower pull
        
- **Anchor Points:**
    
    - High-connectivity clusters
        
    - Nodes with strong ethos alignment
        
    - Nodes stabilized by repeated chamber interactions
        
- **Pressure Points:**
    
    - Frontier 5–8 (highest instability)
        
    - Slit-adjacent nodes
        
    - Nodes undergoing migration evaluation
        

### **Index Integrity**

This index defines the boundaries of the Fib Flower. All Flower-related notes must link here. No collapse-born entity may be indexed. All substructures must maintain conceptual isolation and avoid drift into neighboring chambers.
