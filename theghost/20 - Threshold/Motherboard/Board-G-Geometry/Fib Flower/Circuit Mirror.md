# Circuit Mirror

The Circuit Mirror is the node’s internal feedback loop that generates demand for what passes through the slit.

## Function
- Amplifies internal pressure
- Generates demand signals
- Influences migration and drift
- Reacts strongly to ethos deltas

## Behavior
Nodes with active Circuit Mirrors exhibit higher responsiveness and stronger alignment tendencies.
