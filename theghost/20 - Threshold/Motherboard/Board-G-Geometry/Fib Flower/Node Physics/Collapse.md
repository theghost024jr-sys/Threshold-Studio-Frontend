# **Collapse**

Collapse is the node’s failure mode — the state where pressure exceeds tension, resonance fractures, drift destabilizes, and geometry can no longer hold the node in place. Collapse is not a single event; it is a dynamic physics role that propagates through the Flower, reshaping corridors, pressure gradients, resonance fields, and migration pathways.

Collapse is the Flower’s structural truth event.

## **Purpose**

To define:

- how collapse forms inside the Flower
    
- how pressure > tension triggers collapse
    
- how resonance fracture accelerates collapse
    
- how collapse adjacency distorts drift
    
- how collapse creates voids and fracture lines
    
- how collapse transitions into drift or migration
    
- how chambers tint collapse behavior
    
- how collapse reshapes Flower geometry
    

Collapse is the Flower’s failure physics.

# **1. Collapse Fundamentals**

### **Collapse = structural failure.**

A node collapses when:

- pressure exceeds tension fiber capacity
    
- resonance fractures
    
- drift destabilizes
    
- geometry fails
    
- chamber deltas cannot stabilize
    

Collapse is the moment the Flower forces resolution.

# **2. Collapse Causes (Modern)**

### **Pressure Overload**

Pressure lattice cells exceed tension fiber strength.

### **Tension Fiber Failure**

Fibers break, removing cohesion.

### **Resonance Fracture**

Tone breaks, destabilizing identity.

### **Drift Instability**

Drift wobble becomes uncontrollable.

### **Frontier Instability**

Low stability margins amplify collapse.

### **Ethos Conflict**

Ethos deltas destabilize resonance and tension.

### **Slit Amplification**

Slit pressure multiplies collapse likelihood.

Collapse is multi‑cause, not singular.

# **3. Collapse Physics**

### **Pressure Spike**

Collapse begins with a pressure spike.

### **Fiber Break**

Tension fibers fail, removing structural support.

### **Resonance Void**

Resonance collapses, leaving tonal silence.

### **Drift Shear**

Drift vectors break, causing sudden movement.

### **Geometry Failure**

Corridors, walls, and pathways distort or collapse.

### **Collapse Propagation**

Collapse spreads along fracture lines and void adjacency.

Collapse is a chain reaction.

# **4. Collapse Outcomes**

### **Collapse‑Born**

Node becomes collapse‑born — a node defined by collapse origin.

### **Outward Fall**

Node falls outward into void or Outer Halo.

### **Drift Reversion**

Node re‑enters drift with instability.

### **Migration Trigger**

Collapse can open migration channels.

Collapse determines the node’s next physics role.
