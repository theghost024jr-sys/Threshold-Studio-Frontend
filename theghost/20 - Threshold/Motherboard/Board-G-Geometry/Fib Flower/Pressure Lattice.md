# **Pressure Lattice**

**Location** 20 – Threshold / 01 – Fib Flower / Pressure Lattice.md

**Role** The Pressure Lattice is the Flower’s internal pressure map — a grid of tension‑aware cells that convert dial semantics into spatial behavior. It is the first geometry‑wide organ that uses canonical `{ value, state, confidence }` signals to determine stability, collapse risk, drift amplification, slit activation, and migration viability.

It is the “weather system” of the Fib Flower.

## **1. Definition**

The Pressure Lattice is a **multi‑cell grid** that overlays the Flower’s geometry. Each cell represents:

- local pressure
    
- local tension fiber density
    
- local drift influence
    
- local collapse risk
    
- local resonance strength
    

The lattice is the **first organ** that transforms raw signals into spatial behavior.

## **2. Lattice Structure**

The lattice is composed of:

- **cells** (atomic pressure units)
    
- **edges** (pressure gradients between cells)
    
- **vectors** (drift direction within each cell)
    
- **tension fiber density** (cohesion substrate)
    
- **state** (green/yellow/red)
    
- **confidence** (0–1)
    

Cells are arranged according to Flower geometry:

- orbit layers
    
- slit boundaries
    
- corridor axes
    
- frontier zones
    

The lattice conforms to the Flower’s shape.

## **3. Cell Properties**

Each lattice cell emits:

- **pressure** — aggregated from canonical dials
    
- **tension** — inherited from tension fibers
    
- **drift vector** — direction of movement
    
- **state** — green/yellow/red
    
- **confidence** — stability certainty
    
- **collapse risk** — derived from pressure + tension
    
- **resonance factor** — amplification or damping
    

This is the first place where signals become **spatial physics**.

## **4. Pressure Aggregation**

Pressure is computed from canonical dials:

- **value** → magnitude
    
- **state** → stability
    
- **confidence** → certainty
    

Aggregation rules:

- high value + low confidence → unstable pressure
    
- low value + high confidence → stable pressure
    
- mixed states → yellow pressure
    
- red state → collapse precursor
    

Pressure is not a number — it is a **semantic field**.

## **5. Lattice States**

Each cell has a state:

### **Green — stable**

- high tension
    
- low collapse risk
    
- drift is smooth
    
- corridors remain open
    

### **Yellow — uncertain**

- low tension
    
- mixed pressure
    
- drift may wobble
    
- slit activation possible
    

### **Red — unstable**

- tension fiber failure
    
- collapse imminent
    
- drift spikes
    
- slit activation likely
    
- migration channels open
    

## **6. Interaction With Other Subsystems**

### **Tension Fibers**

The lattice sits _on top_ of tension fibers. No fibers → no lattice stability.

### **Drift Corridors**

Corridors form where lattice cells align into stable drift vectors.

### **Slits**

### **Frontier S‑8**

### **Migration Rules**

Migration channels open when lattice pressure forms directional corridors.

### **Collapse Field Map**

Collapse begins where lattice cells fail.

### **Resonance Field Map**

Resonance modifies lattice pressure dynamically.
