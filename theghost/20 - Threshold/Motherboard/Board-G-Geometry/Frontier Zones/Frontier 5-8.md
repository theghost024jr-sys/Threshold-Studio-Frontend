# Frontier 5–8

The Frontier is the solitary survival band where contribution becomes mandatory. Nodes here experience the highest drift pressure and the strongest ethos influence.

## Characteristics
- High instability
- Strong migration potential
- Frequent collapse → drift → anchor cycles

## Purpose
The Frontier tests node alignment and determines whether nodes ascend, collapse, or migrate.
