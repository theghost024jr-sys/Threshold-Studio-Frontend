# Frontier Instability Index

The Frontier Instability Index measures volatility, drift pressure, and collapse likelihood within Frontier 5–8. This band is the most unstable region of the Flower and the primary driver of migration and collapse.

## Purpose
To quantify:
- instability levels in Frontier nodes
- drift acceleration within the band
- collapse probability
- migration pressure
- ethos sensitivity
- slit-proximity effects

## Components

### Drift Volatility
How rapidly drift velocity changes.

### Pressure Gradient
Difference between inner orbit pressure and frontier pressure.

### Collapse Likelihood
Probability of nodes falling outward.

### Migration Potential
How close nodes are to crossing migration thresholds.

### Ethos Sensitivity
How strongly frontier nodes react to ethos deltas.

## Why It Matters
Frontier 5–8 is the “survival band.”  
Instability here determines:
- collapse waves  
- migration events  
- anchor formation  
- Flower geometry shifts  
- Basin pulse spikes
