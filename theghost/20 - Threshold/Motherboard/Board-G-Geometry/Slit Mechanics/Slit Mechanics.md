# **Slit Mechanics**

The slit is the Flower’s primary instability organ — a geometric structure where pressure, drift, resonance, and tension fibers converge. It amplifies drift, fractures resonance, destabilizes geometry, and creates the initial conditions for migration. The slit is not a passive opening; it is an active pressure engine.

## **Purpose**

To define:

- how the slit interacts with the Pressure Lattice
    
- how tension fiber failure creates slit activation
    
- how drift accelerates inside slit geometry
    
- how resonance bends, fractures, or amplifies
    
- how migration channels emerge from slit flow
    
- how the Circuit Mirror intensifies demand signals
    
- how collapse precursors form around slit boundaries
    

## **Core Slit Behaviors**

### **1. Pressure Amplification**

The slit multiplies incoming pressure. Cells entering the slit region experience:

- increased pressure magnitude
    
- reduced tension fiber cohesion
    
- higher collapse probability
    
- directional pressure gradients
    

This is the **primary driver** of slit instability.

### **2. Drift Acceleration**

Drift velocity increases sharply inside the slit. Small imbalances produce large movement changes.

Drift inside the slit is:

- curved
    
- accelerated
    
- unstable
    
- sensitive to resonance distortion
    

This is why nodes “snap” toward migration channels.

### **3. Tension Fiber Failure**

Slit activation occurs when tension fibers drop below threshold.

Fiber failure causes:

- geometry loosening
    
- pressure leakage
    
- corridor formation
    
- collapse precursors
    

The slit is the **first place** tension fibers break.

### **4. Resonance Distortion**

Resonance fields warp inside the slit.

Effects include:

- tone bending
    
- resonance fracture
    
- amplification spikes
    
- directional resonance flow
    

This distortion shapes migration direction.

### **5. Migration Channel Formation**

When pressure, drift, and resonance align, the slit forms a **migration channel**.

Channels emerge when:

- pressure > tension
    
- drift vector aligns with slit axis
    
- resonance fracture occurs
    
- collapse precursors activate
    

This is the **gateway** to cross‑Flower migration.

### **6. Circuit Mirror Interaction**

Demand signals intensify when passing through the slit.

The Circuit Mirror:

- amplifies demand
    
- increases drift pressure
    
- accelerates migration likelihood
    
- destabilizes slit-adjacent cells
    

This is the feedback loop that makes the slit volatile.

## **Why It Matters**

The slit determines:

- drift acceleration
    
- collapse onset
    
- migration likelihood
    
- pressure gradient formation
    
- Frontier instability
    
- corridor activation
    
- bundle behavior
    
- Basin telemetry spikes
    

It is the **turbulence core** of the Flower.
