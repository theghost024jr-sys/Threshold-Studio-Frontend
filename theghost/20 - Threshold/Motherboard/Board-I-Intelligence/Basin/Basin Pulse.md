# Basin Pulse

The Basin Pulse is the temporal signal of Threshold — the rhythmic output generated by chamber deltas, Flower geometry shifts, bundle activation, and node pressure changes. It is the Basin’s heartbeat, revealing *when* the system is changing.

## Purpose
To provide a time-based reading of:
- Ethos shifts
- Drift acceleration
- Collapse onset
- Anchor formation
- Bundle activation waves
- Frontier instability
- Chamber resonance

## What It Represents

### Chamber Deltas
Ethos changes create visible pulse spikes, showing moments of narrative or structural tension.

### Drift Pressure
Rising drift produces pulse acceleration, indicating nodes approaching migration thresholds.

### Collapse Signals
Collapse zones generate sharp downward pulses, marking instability or structural failure.

### Anchor Formation
Stable clusters create smoothing waves, showing stabilization and alignment.

### Bundle Activation
Bundles create flicker-like micro pulses, revealing intelligence-layer activity.

## Why It Matters
The Pulse is the Basin’s temporal intelligence layer.  
It shows *when* Threshold is shifting — not just *where*.  
It is essential for diagnosing:
- instability
- resonance
- migration
- collapse
- chamber influence
- Flower geometry changes
