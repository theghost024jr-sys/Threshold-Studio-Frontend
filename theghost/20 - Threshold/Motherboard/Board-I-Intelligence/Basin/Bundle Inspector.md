# Bundle Inspector

The Bundle Inspector is the Basin’s lens into node bundle behavior. It reveals which bundles activate, how often, under what conditions, and how they relate to Flower geometry.

## Purpose
To provide real-time visibility into:
- Bundle activation frequency
- Bundle relevance based on Flower orbit
- Chamber-driven activation changes
- Drift and pressure correlations
- Ethos-influenced bundle shifts

## What It Shows
- Active bundles (live)
- Dormant bundles
- High-frequency bundles
- Frontier-reactive bundles
- Ethos-sensitive bundles
- Migration-triggered bundles

## Why It Matters
Bundles are the “payload” of node behavior.  
Understanding them is essential for:
- Chamber intelligence
- Drift prediction
- Anchor formation analysis
- Frontier stability evaluation
- Basin pulse interpretation
