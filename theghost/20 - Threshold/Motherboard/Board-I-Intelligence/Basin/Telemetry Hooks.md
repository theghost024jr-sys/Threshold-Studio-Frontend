# Telemetry Hooks

Telemetry Hooks are the Basin’s attachment points into Threshold’s runtime behavior. They collect signals from Flowers, chambers, bundles, and node physics, allowing the Basin to interpret the system’s internal state.

## Purpose
To provide a structured interface for capturing:
- Drift pressure changes
- Collapse onset signals
- Anchor formation events
- Ethos deltas
- Bundle activation pulses
- Frontier instability
- Slit-adjacent imbalance
- Migration threshold crossings

Telemetry Hooks turn raw behavior into readable intelligence.

## Types of Hooks

### Drift Hooks
Capture drift velocity, acceleration, and pressure changes.  
Used by Drift Metrics and Pulse.

### Collapse Hooks
Detect collapse onset, halo formation, and structural failure.  
Feed into Heatmap and Pulse.

### Anchor Hooks
Identify stabilization events and cluster formation.  
Used by Resonance and Anchor Chamber.

### Ethos Hooks
Capture chamber-driven deltas and alignment shifts.  
Feed into Pulse and Heatmap.

### Bundle Hooks
Track bundle activation frequency and relevance.  
Used by Bundle Inspector and Node Bundles.

### Frontier Hooks
Monitor instability in Frontier 5–8.  
Feed into Drift Metrics and Migration Rules.

### Slit Hooks
Detect slit-adjacent drift sensitivity and imbalance.  
Used by Drift Metrics and Pressure.

## Why It Matters
Telemetry Hooks are the Basin’s sensory organs.  
Without them, the Basin cannot:
- read Flower geometry  
- detect chamber influence  
- measure drift  
- predict collapse  
- track bundles  
- interpret resonance  
- understand migration  

They are the foundation of Threshold’s runtime intelligence.
