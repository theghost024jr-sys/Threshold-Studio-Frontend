# **Ethos Chamber**

The Ethos Chamber is the Flower’s identity‑tinting organ. It does not generate physics — it _colors_ physics. Ethos modifies pressure, drift, collapse, resonance, tension fibers, migration likelihood, and chamber behavior. Ethos is the Flower’s emotional‑intent layer, shaping how nodes behave and how geometry responds.

Ethos Chamber is the Flower’s identity engine.

## **Purpose**

To define:

- how ethos deltas tint physics roles
    
- how ethos stabilizes or destabilizes pressure
    
- how ethos bends drift and corridor direction
    
- how ethos strengthens or weakens tension fibers
    
- how ethos amplifies or dampens collapse
    
- how ethos shapes resonance tone
    
- how ethos influences migration likelihood
    
- how ethos interacts with other chambers
    

Ethos Chamber is the behavioral layer that colors all physics.

Fast drift → ethos curvature Slow drift → ethos smoothing

### **Chamber Signals**

Collapse Chamber → failure tint Anchor Chamber → stability tint Migration Chamber → direction tint
