# Ethos Delta Map

The Ethos Delta Map tracks how changes in chamber ethos propagate through the Flower. Ethos deltas are the primary force that reshapes node behavior, drift pressure, resonance, and anchor formation.

## Purpose
To map:
- where ethos deltas originate
- how they travel through orbit layers
- which nodes are affected first
- how deltas alter drift, collapse, and anchor behavior
- how Frontier 5–8 responds to ethos shifts

## Delta Types

### Positive Ethos Delta
Increases resonance, stabilizes clusters, strengthens anchor formation.

### Negative Ethos Delta
Raises drift pressure, destabilizes nodes, increases collapse likelihood.

### Mixed Ethos Delta
Creates unpredictable behavior; often triggers Frontier instability.

## Propagation Pathways
Ethos deltas move through:
- Orbit Layers (core → mid → frontier → halo)
- Chamber Intelligence outputs
- Basin telemetry (Pulse + Heatmap)
- Node physics (Drift, Pressure, Resonance)

## Why It Matters
Ethos deltas are the “weather system” of Threshold.  
They determine:
- stability  
- collapse  
- migration  
- resonance  
- alignment  
- Flower geometry shifts
