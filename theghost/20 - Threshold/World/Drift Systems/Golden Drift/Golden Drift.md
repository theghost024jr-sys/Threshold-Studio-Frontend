Overview
Golden Drift is a rare, high‑coherence Drift phenomenon characterized by warm resonance, stable motion, and luminous particulate flow.
It appears when:
• 	Drift currents synchronize
• 	emotional fields settle
• 	Shimmer curvature aligns
• 	collapse pressure is low
• 	symbolic density is high but stable
Golden Drift is the safest and most navigable Drift state in Threshold.

Purpose
Golden Drift exists to:
• 	stabilize travel
• 	reveal safe paths
• 	amplify clarity
• 	reduce collapse risk
• 	support emotional recovery
• 	enhance symbolic interpretation
It is the healing and orientation state of Drift.

Core Properties
1. Warm Resonance Field
Golden Drift emits a soft, warm resonance that:
• 	calms emotional turbulence
• 	reduces fear and overwhelm
• 	increases perceptual clarity
• 	stabilizes symbolic interpretation
It is the opposite of Drift turbulence.

2. Luminous Particulate Flow
Golden Drift contains fine, glowing particles that:
• 	trace motion
• 	reveal micro‑currents
• 	highlight safe trajectories
• 	illuminate Shimmer arcs
These particles are not light — they are resonance dust.

3. High Coherence
Golden Drift is extremely stable:
• 	no turbulence
• 	no shear fields
• 	no collapse drag
• 	no emotional spikes
It is the most predictable Drift state.

4. Emotional Softening
Golden Drift gently lowers emotional intensity:
• 	grief → soft heaviness
• 	fear → warmth
• 	overwhelm → clarity
• 	anticipation → steady lift
It is a regulation field.
