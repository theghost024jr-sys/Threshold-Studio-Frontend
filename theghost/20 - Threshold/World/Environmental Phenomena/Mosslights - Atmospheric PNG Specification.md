Purpose
A single visual anchor for the Threshold Map that communicates:
• 	calm
• 	safety
• 	Drift‑Shimmer boundary
• 	low symbolic density
• 	soft resonance
• 	environmental gentleness
This PNG is not a literal representation.
It’s a mood glyph for the world.

Visual Qualities
1. Light Behavior
• 	soft, low‑intensity glow
• 	no hard edges
• 	bioluminescent, but not neon
• 	light diffuses into Mist
• 	slight shimmer, but not reflective
The glow should feel like breathing, not shining.

2. Color Palette
• 	deep greens
• 	soft teals
• 	muted blues
• 	faint gold or white highlights
Avoid:
• 	saturated colors
• 	Ember reds/oranges
• 	Crown whites
• 	Stone grays
Mosslights live in the Drift‑Shimmer gradient, not the extremes.

3. Texture
• 	velvety
• 	mosslike but not botanical
• 	slightly luminescent fibers
• 	organic clustering
Think:
“bioluminescent moss meets symbolic resonance.”

4. Environment Context
The PNG should imply:
• 	a forest floor
• 	a Drift‑Shimmer boundary
• 	soft Mist in the background
• 	faint Shimmer curvature in the air
But none of this should be explicit.
It should feel like a hint, not a scene.

5. Composition
• 	close‑up or mid‑range
• 	Mosslights clustered in a small patch
• 	background softly blurred
• 	light gradients leading the eye inward
This keeps the PNG atmospheric rather than illustrative.

What the PNG should not be
• 	not a diagram
• 	not a symbol
• 	not a logo
• 	not a species
• 	not a glowing mushroom
• 	not a fantasy plant
• 	not a sci‑fi object
Mosslights are ambient world behavior, not entities.

Where this PNG goes

It becomes the visual anchor for the phenomenon.
