Mosslights — Interaction File
Overview
Mosslights are low‑intensity bioluminescent environmental organisms that stabilize Drift‑Shimmer boundary zones. Their interactions are subtle but foundational: they smooth turbulence, diffuse symbolic density, and provide soft navigational cues.
They do not “act” in the way species or substrates do — they respond and influence.
This file maps their interactions across Threshold’s systems.

1. Layer Interactions
01 Mist — Perception & Ambiguity
• 	Mosslights soften perceptual noise in Mist‑heavy zones
• 	Their glow reduces disorientation
• 	Mist thickens their light, making it diffuse and dreamlike
Effect:
• 	gentler transitions
• 	reduced perceptual drift

02 Stone — Stability & Structure
• 	Stone anchors Mosslight clusters
• 	Mosslights grow strongest where Stone stability meets Drift motion
• 	Stone suppresses overgrowth
Effect:
• 	stable, grounded glow
• 	reliable navigational cues

03 Ember — Activation & Catalysis
• 	Ember heat weakens Mosslights
• 	High Ember density causes dimming or retreat
• 	Mosslights avoid ignition sites
Effect:
• 	Mosslights mark “cool” paths away from Ember activity

04 Ethyl — Flow & Coherence
• 	Ethyl currents distribute Mosslight spores
• 	Flow increases their spread
• 	Ethyl enhances their rhythmic pulsing
Effect:
• 	Mosslights appear in gentle streams and flow corridors

05 Drift — Motion & Helical Dynamics
• 	Drift turbulence shapes Mosslight growth patterns
• 	Mosslights stabilize low‑intensity Drift
• 	High Drift shears them apart
Effect:
• 	Mosslights indicate safe Drift zones
• 	They disappear in chaotic Drift

06 Shimmer — Symbolic Density
• 	Shimmer curvature feeds Mosslight bioluminescence
• 	Mosslights absorb excess symbolic density
• 	They glow brightest in low‑density Shimmer pools
Effect:
• 	Mosslights act as symbolic diffusers
• 	They prevent resonance overload

07 Crown — Integration & Sovereignty
• 	Crown‑layer influence is minimal
• 	Mosslights dim in high‑order cognitive zones
• 	They avoid Crown structures
Effect:
• 	Mosslights mark “low‑cognition” safe spaces

Summary
Mosslights are gentle stabilizers, symbolic diffusers, and environmental guides.
They interact with nearly every system in Threshold, but always softly — never intrusively.
They are the world’s way of saying:
“This place is safe. You can breathe here.”
