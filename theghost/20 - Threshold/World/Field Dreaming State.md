Overview
The Field Dreaming State is the condition in which environmental fields — Drift, Mist, Shimmer, Stone, Ember, Ethyl, Heat, and Collapse — enter a low‑activity, high‑sensitivity mode.
It is not sleep, not consciousness, not intention.
It is the ambient dreaming of the world, expressed through:
• 	soft resonance
• 	symbolic drift
• 	emotional echoing
• 	narrative murmuring
• 	low‑density pattern formation
The Field Dreaming State is the world’s way of processing, cooling, and redistributing meaning.

Purpose
The Field Dreaming State exists to:
• 	release accumulated symbolic density
• 	diffuse emotional charge
• 	stabilize Drift turbulence
• 	soften Shimmer curvature
• 	reset narrative pressure
• 	allow collapse residue to dissipate
• 	prepare the environment for new resonance
It is the restorative cycle of the world.

Core Properties
1. Low Activity, High Sensitivity
Fields move less, but feel more.
This creates:
• 	gentle Drift currents
• 	soft Mist veils
• 	dim Shimmer halos
• 	quiet Stone resonance
• 	low Ember flicker
• 	slow Ethyl flow
The world becomes receptive.

2. Symbolic Drift
Meaning loosens and drifts:
• 	symbols blur
• 	echoes stretch
• 	silhouettes soften
• 	narrative fragments float
This is the dreaming motion of the substrate.

3. Emotional Echoing
Emotional residue becomes:
• 	faint
• 	rhythmic
• 	repetitive
• 	non‑directive
The environment “echoes” without amplifying.

4. Narrative Murmuring
Narrative substrate enters a whisper state:
• 	soft omens
• 	half‑formed arcs
• 	drifting motifs
• 	quiet mythic residue
Nothing resolves.
Everything drifts.

Behavior

Deep Dreaming

• 	collapse recovery
• 	emotional exhaustion

Interrupted Dreaming
Triggered by:
• 	sudden emotional spikes
• 	Shimmer fractures
• 	Heat surges
• 	collapse fronts
Fields snap out of dreaming abruptly.
