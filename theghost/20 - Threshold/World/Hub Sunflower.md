Overview
The Hub Sunflower is a central resonance structure used to organize multi‑path systems, radial environments, and symbolic navigation nodes.
It is not a plant, not a species, not an artifact — it is a hub geometry, a spatial‑symbolic form that distributes motion, meaning, and emotional physics outward in a radial pattern.
The Hub Sunflower expresses:
• 	radial coherence
• 	distributed flow
• 	multi‑path orientation
• 	soft narrative gravity
• 	stable emotional warmth
It is one of the most stable hub‑forms in Threshold.

Core Properties
1. Radial Geometry
The Hub Sunflower is defined by:
• 	a central resonance core
• 	multiple outward‑facing petals (paths)
• 	soft curvature arcs
• 	distributed Drift flow
• 	Shimmer‑aligned symmetry
This geometry creates equal access to all connected paths.

2. Emotional Warmth Field
The hub generates a low‑intensity Heat field:
• 	warm
• 	steady
• 	grounding
• 	non‑directive
This warmth stabilizes travelers and reduces collapse risk.

3. Narrative Soft‑Center
The center of the hub holds:
• 	low narrative pressure
• 	high symbolic receptivity
• 	soft mythic residue
This makes the hub ideal for:
• 	orientation
• 	decision points
• 	emotional reset
• 	Whisper Cartography calibration
