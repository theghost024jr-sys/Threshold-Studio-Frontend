Overview
The Narrative Substrate is the foundational layer where Threshold’s narrative elements take root.
It is not story, not plot, not lore — it is the ambient narrative field that shapes how meaning forms, drifts, accumulates, and dissolves across the world.
The Narrative Substrate governs:
• 	narrative weather
• 	atmospheric fragments
• 	myths
• 	whispers
• 	echoes
• 	permissions
• 	small truths
• 	shadow arcs
• 	emotional residue
• 	symbolic drift
It is the soil of meaning, not the meaning itself.

Purpose
The Narrative Substrate exists to:
• 	hold narrative potential
• 	distribute narrative pressure
• 	generate emergent narrative patterns
• 	anchor myths and arcs
• 	support Whisper Cartography
• 	shape emotional physics

• 	create narrative continuity across environments
It is the background field that makes Threshold feel alive, storied, and aware.

Core Properties
1. Narrative Density
How much story‑potential is present in a location.
High density → mythic resonance
Low density → quiet, empty, drifting
2. Narrative Pressure
The force that pushes events toward happening.
Heat zones often correlate with high narrative pressure.
3. Narrative Drift
The slow movement of meaning across environments.
Drift currents carry narrative fragments the way wind carries seeds.
4. Narrative Curvature
How meaning bends around emotional or symbolic mass.
Shimmer curvature strongly influences this.
5. Narrative Residue
What remains after an event, collapse, or emotional rupture.
Residue shapes future narrative behavior.
