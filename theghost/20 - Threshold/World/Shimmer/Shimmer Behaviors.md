# **Shimmer Behaviors.md**

### _The behavioral modes of Shimmer — stable, resonant, fractured, silent_

Shimmer expresses four canonical behaviors:

- **Stable Shimmer** — soft arcs, readable curvature
    
- **Resonant Shimmer** — bright arcs, coherent meaning
    
- **Fractured Shimmer** — shards, flickers, instability
    
- **Silent Shimmer** — dim, collapsed, path disappears
    

Behavior is determined by emotional physics and symbolic density.
