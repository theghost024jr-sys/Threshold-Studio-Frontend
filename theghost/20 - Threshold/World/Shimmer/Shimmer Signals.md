# **Shimmer Signals.md**

### _Signals emitted by Shimmer curvature — glints, pulses, halos, fractures_

Signals include:

- **Glints** — direction hints
    
- **Halos** — interpretive potential
    
- **Pulses** — emotional resonance
    
- **Fractures** — collapse risk
    

Signals are the **UI** of Shimmer.
