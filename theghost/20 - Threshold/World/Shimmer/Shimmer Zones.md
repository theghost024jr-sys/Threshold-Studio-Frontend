# **Shimmer Zones.md**

### _The spatial‑interpretive zones of Shimmer — Fib1, Fib2, Fib3_

Shimmer has three canonical zones:

- **Fib1 Zone** — shallow meaning, soft curvature
    
- **Fib2 Zone** — active meaning, branching arcs
    
- **Fib3 Zone** — deep meaning, symbolic recursion
    

Zones determine path density and interpretive difficulty.
