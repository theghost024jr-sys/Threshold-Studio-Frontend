Overview
Whisper Cartography uses listening methods rather than drawing methods. Each technique is a way of perceiving the world’s subtle signals and translating them into spatial intuition.
Below are the canonical methods.

1. Drift Listening
The cartographer attunes to Drift currents:
• 	turbulence
• 	spirals
• 	helical motion
• 	pockets of stillness
Drift reveals movement paths and emotional gradients.

2. Shimmer Reading
The cartographer observes Shimmer curvature:
• 	reflections
• 	symbolic density
• 	resonance patterns
• 	curvature distortions
Shimmer reveals meaning pockets and symbolic hotspots.

3. Mosslight Following
Mosslights act as soft guides:
• 	steady glow → safe zone
• 	pulsing → stable path
• 	dimming → turbulence
• 	extinguishing → collapse
Mosslights reveal safe navigation routes.

4. Mist Density Sensing
Mist reveals:
• 	ambiguity
• 	emotional softness
• 	perceptual thresholds
• 	hidden transitions
Mist density maps liminal zones.

5. Stone Echo Mapping
Stone emits resonance echoes:
• 	deep echoes → stability
• 	shallow echoes → fragility
• 	fractured echoes → collapse scars
Stone reveals structural integrity of the environment.

6. Ethyl Flow Tracking
Ethyl currents show:
• 	flow lines
• 	natural movement paths
• 	resonance distribution
Ethyl reveals environmental coherence.

7. Emotional Physics Listening
The cartographer senses:
• 	calm
• 	fear
• 	grief
• 	curiosity
• 	overwhelm
• 	hope
Emotional physics reveal the world’s mood, which is part of the map.

8. Soft Anchoring
Instead of fixed points, Whisper Cartographers use:
• 	resonance anchors
• 	emotional anchors
• 	symbolic anchors
These are not coordinates — they are felt locations.
