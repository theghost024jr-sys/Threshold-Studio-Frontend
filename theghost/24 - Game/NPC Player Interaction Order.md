1. Sensory Mirroring
NPC reacts to the player’s presence before the player acts.
• 	NPC gaze, breath, posture shift
• 	Spirit animal behavior echoes the player’s state
• 	NPC proximity adjusts based on player drift or resonance
Why first:
This is the earliest signal — the NPC feels the player before anything is said or done.

2. Behavioral Echo
NPC begins to mirror or resist the player’s actions.
• 	If the player is generous, NPC softens
• 	If the player is erratic, NPC becomes cautious
• 	If the player is intentional, NPC becomes cooperative
Why second:
Behavioral feedback teaches the player how their actions shape the social field.

3. Role Strain or Alignment
NPC role begins to shift based on the player’s role expression.
• 	Guardian player stabilizes NPC witness
• 	Leader player strains NPC caretaker
• 	Wanderer player destabilizes NPC guardian
Why third:
Role physics emerge only after behavior is patterned — this is the first structural interaction.

4. Identity Resonance
NPC identity begins to surface or distort based on the player’s emotional physics.
• 	Player clarity reveals NPC lineage
• 	Player collapse triggers NPC mask failure
• 	Player integration invites NPC transformation
Why fourth:
Identity is relational — it only emerges when roles and behaviors have stabilized or strained.

5. Threshold Crossing
NPC crosses an internal threshold because of sustained player pressure.
• 	NPC collapses
• 	NPC transforms
• 	NPC reveals
• 	NPC withdraws
• 	NPC inverts
Why fifth:
Threshold crossing is the moment the NPC stops adapting and starts changing.

6. Drift Redistribution
NPC sheds or absorbs drift based on the player’s state.
• 	NPC takes on unresolved force
• 	NPC releases burden into the environment
• 	NPC stabilizes the player through presence
Why sixth:
This is the energetic exchange — the NPC becomes a pressure vessel or a release valve.

7. Role Reconfiguration
NPC adopts a new role in response to the player’s transformation.
• 	Guardian becomes witness
• 	Witness becomes leader
• 	Caretaker becomes wanderer
Why seventh:
This is the systemic outcome — the NPC’s role in the world changes because the player changed.

8. Identity Integration
NPC identity stabilizes in the presence of a coherent player.
• 	NPC becomes more themselves
• 	NPC gains new affordances
• 	NPC becomes mythically louder
• 	NPC becomes a fixed point in the Field
Why eighth:
This is the final state — the NPC is no longer reactive, but transformed.

The Full Sequence (One Line Each)
1. 	Sensory Mirroring → NPC feels the player
2. 	Behavioral Echo → NPC reacts to player actions
3. 	Role Strain/Alignment → NPC role shifts
4. 	Identity Resonance → NPC identity surfaces
5. 	Threshold Crossing → NPC changes state
6. 	Drift Redistribution → NPC absorbs or sheds force
7. 	Role Reconfiguration → NPC adopts new role
8. 	Identity Integration → NPC becomes more themselves.
