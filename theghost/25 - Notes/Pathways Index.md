1. Overview
A brief statement of what pathways are:
• 	Pathways describe movement between zones in the World Map and nodes in the Threshold Engine.
• 	Every outer transition has an inner equivalent.
• 	Pathways come in three types: Stable, Drift, and Fault.
• 	This index is the navigation layer between the two maps.

2. Pathway Types
A simple definition block.
Stable Pathways
Predictable, grounded, continuous movement.
Outer: normal travel routes
Inner: Anchor‑aligned transitions
Drift Pathways
Unstable, ambiguous, nonlinear movement.
Outer: dissolving or shifting routes
Inner: Drift‑aligned transitions
Fault Pathways
Rupture, break, sudden change.
Outer: catastrophic or transformative jumps
Inner: Fault‑aligned transitions
