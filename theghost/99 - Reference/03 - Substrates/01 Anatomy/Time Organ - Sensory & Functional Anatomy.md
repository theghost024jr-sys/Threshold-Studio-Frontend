Temporal Senses
The Time Organ has three sensory modes:
1. Phase Sense
• 	Detects shifts in temporal density
• 	Alerts the system to drift, collapse, or acceleration
2. Curvature Sense
• 	Reads Shimmer‑layer curvature
• 	Interprets symbolic patterns as temporal signals
3. Resonance Sense
• 	Measures alignment between internal time and external worldstate
• 	Used for synchronization and recovery
