Overview
The Time Organ is anchored in the Shimmer Layer but interacts with all seven layers. Each layer influences a different aspect of temporal behavior.

Layer‑by‑Layer Interaction Map
01 Mist — Perception & Ambiguity
• 	Mist affects the organ’s phase sense
• 	High Mist density increases temporal ambiguity
• 	Organ compensates by increasing compression
Behavioral effect:
• 	perception slows
• 	time feels “fogged” or softened

02 Stone — Stability & Structure
• 	Stone stabilizes the Chamber Spine
• 	Provides structural coherence for temporal compression
• 	Prevents collapse during overload
Behavioral effect:
• 	grounding
• 	reduced drift
• 	improved temporal lock

03 Ember — Activation & Catalysis
• 	Ember accelerates temporal flow
• 	Enhances insight and symbolic interpretation
• 	Can cause overexpansion if unregulated
Behavioral effect:
• 	rapid clarity
• 	heightened anticipation
• 	risk of temporal overshoot

04 Ethyl — Flow & Coherence
• 	Ethyl smooths temporal transitions
• 	Supports phase realignment
• 	Reduces oscillation
Behavioral effect:
• 	fluid perception
• 	stable drift
• 	improved recovery

05 Drift — Motion & Helical Dynamics
• 	Drift directly feeds the Drift Nerve
• 	Controls phase drift and temporal deviation
• 	Enables creativity and intuition
Behavioral effect:
• 	controlled deviation
• 	symbolic reframing
• 	adaptive timing

06 Shimmer — Symbolic Density
• 	Primary anchor layer
• 	Provides curvature input
• 	Drives resonance and meaning
Behavioral effect:
• 	symbolic clarity
• 	pattern recognition
• 	mythic perception

07 Crown — Integration & Sovereignty
• 	Connects temporal flow to cognition
• 	Enables foresight and long‑range planning
• 	Supports harmonic resonance
Behavioral effect:
• 	coherent narrative sense
• 	stable identity over time
• 	integrated decision‑making
