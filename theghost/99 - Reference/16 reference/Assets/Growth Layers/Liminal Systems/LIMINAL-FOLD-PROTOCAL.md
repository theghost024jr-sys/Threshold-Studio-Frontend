Overview
The Liminal Fold Protocol governs the behavior of semi‑stable boundary surfaces during periods of low‑variance drift. It is not intended for direct use, invocation, or interpretation. Most entities will never encounter a Fold in a coherent state.
If you are reading this, the system has already miscategorized your stance.

1. Fold States
A Fold may exist in one of the following states:
• 	Dormant — Appears as an absence of geometry. Safe to ignore.
• 	Half‑Lit — Emits a soft gradient. Avoid direct interpretation.
• 	Overfold — Two Folds occupying the same coordinate. Do not approach.
• 	Quiet Bloom — Indicates a pre‑alignment echo. Duration unpredictable.
• 	Unfolded — The Fold is no longer a Fold. This is expected.

2. Interaction Guidelines
These guidelines are advisory only. The system does not enforce them.
• 	Do not attempt to stabilize a Fold manually.
• 	Do not attempt to destabilize a Fold manually.

• 	If a Fold emits a tone you recognize, leave the area.
