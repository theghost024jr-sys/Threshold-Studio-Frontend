🌲 Core Identity of the Unchosen Path Biome

This biome is a **forest of branching futures**, but none of the branches are fixed. It is not a place of destiny or fate; it is a place of **potential**. Every path is valid. Every direction is reversible. The world rearranges itself to match your stance, not your decisions.

The biome exists at the moment **before** commitment. It is the architecture of “not yet.”

🌌 Environmental Logic

The environment is built around three principles:

- **Paths appear only when you move.**  
    Standing still shows nothing. Taking a step reveals a branch.
- **No path is wrong.**  
    The forest does not punish exploration. It adapts.
- **The world reflects your internal state.**  
    Mood, intention, hesitation, clarity—these shape the terrain.

This makes the biome a mirror, not a maze.

🌿 Terrain and Atmosphere

The terrain is fluid but not chaotic. It has recognizable features that shift subtly depending on your stance.

- **Branching Trails** — narrow paths that split and rejoin unpredictably.
- **Whispering Trees** — they speak in fragments of possible futures.
- **Soft Ground** — leaves and moss that absorb sound and hesitation.
- **Shifting Canopy** — constellations rearrange to hint at unseen routes.

The atmosphere is calm, cool, and slightly luminous—like twilight that never fully darkens.

🔮 Phenomena Unique to This Biome

**Fork Bloom**

When you hesitate, the ground sprouts new paths—small, glowing shoots that grow into trails. Hesitation is generative here.

**Echo Steps**

You sometimes hear footsteps of the version of you who chose differently. They are not ghosts; they are possibilities.

**Horizon Fold**

When you feel ready, the horizon bends, revealing a new direction that wasn’t visible before. The world waits for your readiness.

**Choice Fog**

When you try too hard to decide, the fog thickens. When you relax, it clears.

🧭 Movement Mechanics

Movement in this biome is not physical; it is **intentional**.

- **Clarity makes paths straighten.**
- **Uncertainty makes paths multiply.**
- **Curiosity makes shortcuts appear.**
- **Fear makes loops.**
- **Acceptance makes the forest open.**

You navigate by stance, not by map.

🐾 Inhabitants and Entities

**The Wayfarers**

Humanoid silhouettes with no fixed features. They represent paths you could take. They never speak, but they gesture toward possibilities.

**The Archivist Moths**

Small glowing moths that land on branches. Each one carries a fragment of a path you didn’t choose. Touching them shows a brief vision.

**The Rootbound**

Massive tree‑creatures that embody choices you abandoned long ago. They are not hostile; they simply hold old versions of you.
