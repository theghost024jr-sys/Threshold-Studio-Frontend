Overview
Mist Layer 1 is the surface expression of Mist physics — the gentlest form of perceptual softening and boundary blur.
It is not disorienting, not concealing, not dangerous.
It is the introductory layer of Mist: light, breathable, and interpretively soft.
Mist 1 governs:
• 	mild ambiguity
• 	softening of edges
• 	emotional diffusion
• 	perceptual drift
• 	early threshold cues
It is the layer most species can enter without training.

Core Properties
1. Light Ambiguity
Edges soften slightly.
Shapes blur just enough to feel dreamlike but not confusing.
2. Emotional Diffusion
Mist 1 gently spreads emotional charge across the environment.
This creates:
• 	calm
• 	softness
• 	reduced tension
• 	mild introspection
3. Perceptual Drift
Perception becomes:
• 	slower
• 	gentler
• 	less rigid
• 	more interpretive
This is the layer where Whisper Cartography becomes easier.
4. Threshold Sensitivity
Mist 1 gathers around:
• 	transitions
• 	liminal spaces
• 	emotional boundaries
• 	narrative edges
It signals “something is shifting.”

Behavior
Baseline State
Thin, breathable mist.
Barely noticeable unless you’re paying attention.
Veil State
Edges blur slightly more.
Used by the environment to soften transitions.
Pulse State
Triggered by emotional resonance.
Mist thickens and thins rhythmically.
Drift‑Coupled State
Mist moves with Drift 1 currents, creating soft directional cues.

Environmental Interactions
With Drift
Drift 1 motion shapes Mist 1 into:
• 	veils
• 	ribbons
• 	soft spirals
This is the foundation of Whisper Cartography.
With Shimmer
Shimmer reflections become diffused, creating:
• 	soft glows
• 	blurred curvature
• 	gentle symbolic halos
With Stone
Stone anchors Mist 1, creating pockets of clarity.
With Ember
Ember heat thins Mist 1, creating temporary clearings.
With Ethyl
Ethyl flow distributes Mist 1 evenly, increasing coherence.

Species Perception
Most species perceive Mist 1 through:
• 	softened edges
• 	gentle emotional shifts
• 	slowed perception
• 	Mosslight diffusion
• 	Shimmer halos
Mist‑aligned species perceive it as a comforting field.

Navigation Use

It is the training layer for deeper Mist states.

Mist 1 vs Mist 2
