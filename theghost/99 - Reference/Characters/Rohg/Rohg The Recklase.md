**Form:** Honey Badger  
**Layer:** Earth  
**Function:** Leads the confused back to the Hub

## Essence

## Role
Rohg appears when someone is lost, disoriented, or drifting away from the Hub.  
He forces orientation back into place through reckless, chaotic intervention.

Actually snaps the drinker back to the Hub and clears confusion.
