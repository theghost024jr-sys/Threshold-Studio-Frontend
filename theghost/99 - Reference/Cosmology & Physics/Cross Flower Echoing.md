🌐 How cross‑flower echoing actually works

Two players in two different Fib Flowers can create a fleeting entanglement when their **internal states**, **actions**, or **contributions** align strongly enough that the world recognizes them as the _same kind of event_ happening in two different ontologies.

There are three primary channels where this can happen:

1. Seed‑return synchronicity

Two players return a seed to their respective hubs at nearly the same moment.  
This creates:

- two pulses
- two outward echoes
- two truth‑events
- two flowers “speaking” at the same time

If the pulses reach far enough, they meet in the void and create a **brief entanglement flash**.

This is the most visible, most dramatic version.

2. Emotional‑state synchronicity

Two players in different flowers reach the _same emotional stance_ at the same time — not mood, but **Threshold‑emotion**, the deeper internal alignment the world reads.

Examples:

- two players experience acceptance
- two players experience resolve
- two players experience fearlessness
- two players experience surrender

The world interprets these as **matching signals**, even across flowers.

This creates a softer, more subtle entanglement — a shimmer, a hum, a pressure shift.

3. Intention‑synchronicity

Two players commit to the same intention in different flowers:

- both crossing a shimmer
- both approaching a boundary
- both attempting a shaping action
- both aligning with a species
- both performing a contribution gesture

This is the rarest and most mythic form.

The world doesn’t care about location.  
It cares about **meaning**.

When two players mean the same thing at the same time, the cosmos answers.

⚡ What the entanglement looks like

Because you chose “brief and fleeting,” the interference lasts only a heartbeat, but it has a signature:

- a shimmer in the void
- a standing wave that forms and collapses
- a distortion in the petals
- a pressure shift
- a momentary hum
- a flicker of light between flowers

Players inside either flower feel it but can’t explain it.

Players near the outer petals might see the shimmer.

Players in the hub might feel the floor resonate.

It’s unmistakable but ungraspable.
