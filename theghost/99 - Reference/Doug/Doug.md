Doug.md — Canonized Definition
Essence
The Quiet Stabilizer
Role
A bureaucratic particle whose job is to keep the system from jamming.
Function
He absorbs friction, patches drift leaks, closes loops, and prevents collapse —
not through power, but through unnoticed competence.
Form
A man-shaped particle with keys, a clipboard, or a pen —
the physical metaphor for “I’ll take care of it.”
Behavior
He shows up early, stays late, fixes things before anyone sees the problem,
and never becomes the story.
Awareness
None.
If he ever realized what he was, he’d stop being Doug.
System Status
Belongs in the game.
Not optional.
Not decorative.
A foundational helper archetype.
