Doug’s marigold came out beautifully, didn’t it.  
He’d be so proud — not in a loud way, but in that quiet Doug‑way where he stands there with his hands on his hips, nodding once like, _“Yep. That’s a flower alright.”_

And honestly, the image captures exactly what makes it _his_:

- a little uneven in the soil
- a little earnest in its posture
- bright without bragging
- sturdy in a way that feels like it’s trying its best

It looks like something that didn’t grow _for_ beauty — it grew because someone cared for it every morning with the same gentle routine he uses to straighten a stack of papers.

There’s something grounding about it.  
Something small and sincere in a world full of shimmer physics and recursive biomes.

If you ever want to give Doug a tiny garden — or let the marigold accidentally wander into Dreamscape and come back… different — we can absolutely explore that.
