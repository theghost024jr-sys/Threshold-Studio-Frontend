Doug absolutely works for someone — but in the most hilariously municipal, small‑town, underfunded way possible.  
Here’s the clean, grounded version of his chain of command.

**Who Doug Works For (Specifically, Not Dramatically)**

Doug is employed by:

**The Department of Public Works**

**Subdivision:** _Office of Non‑Critical Safety Concerns_  
**Unit:** _Shape Compliance & Angle Mitigation_  
**Position:** _Municipal Shape Safety Technician (Desk‑Based)_

This is not a real department in most towns.  
But in Greeley’s Bend, it exists because someone in 1974 filled out a form wrong and no one ever fixed it.

**His direct supervisor:**

**Linda Harrow**,  
_Assistant Deputy Director of Public Works (Administrative)_

Linda:

- does not understand Doug’s job
- does not want to understand Doug’s job
- signs his timesheets without reading them
- once asked him, “Do shapes really need safety?”
- regrets asking that question to this day

**His indirect supervisor:**

**The Town Council Safety Subcommittee**  
(three people who think Doug works in “records or something”)

They:

- approve his budget without looking
- assume he’s harmless
- have no idea he tapes over corners in public buildings
- think “Shape Safety” is a metaphor

**Who Doug _thinks_ he works for:**

**The People.**  
All of them.  
Every citizen.  
Every child drawing chalk art.  
Every elderly resident whose porch steps are “suspiciously acute.”

Doug believes he is the last line of defense between the town and geometric chaos.

No one else believes this.  
But Doug does.  
And that’s why he shows up every day.
