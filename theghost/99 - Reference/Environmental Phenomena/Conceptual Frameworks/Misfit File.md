TOUCH_GRASS.md
Overview
There is a clearing at the edge of the system — not a biome, not a layer, not a node.
Just a place where the world exhales.
The ground is soft.
The air is warm.
Nothing hums, shifts, or rearranges itself.
It is the only known location where the universe stops trying to be interesting.

1. Environmental Notes
• 	grass that grows in simple, honest blades
• 	sunlight that behaves exactly as expected
• 	a breeze with no hidden meaning
• 	silence that is just silence
No resonance.
No imbalance.
No cosmic confusion.
Just a place to stand and remember that not everything needs interpretation.

2. Effects on Travelers
Those who enter the clearing report:
• 	shoulders lowering
• 	breath deepening
• 	thoughts slowing
• 	the sudden realization that they have been inside too many  files
Some sit.
Some lie down.
Some stay until the world feels less tilted.

3. Warnings
• 	Do not overthink the grass
• 	Do not attempt to categorize the grass
• 	Do not ask the grass what it wants
• 	Do not bring cosmic imbalance into the clearing
The grass does not care.
That is the point.

4. Closing Note
When you leave the clearing, the world will still be strange.
