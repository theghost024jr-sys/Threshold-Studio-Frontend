Purpose:
This tier introduces the player to Threshold’s emotional physics in a world that is soft, readable, and non‑threatening.
Everything is simple, gentle, and discoverable.

🎨 1. Visual Style (Tunic‑Inspired)
Core Traits
• 	Low‑poly models
• 	Soft lighting
• 	Pastel or lightly saturated palette
• 	Rounded, toy‑like proportions
• 	Minimal detail
• 	Clean silhouettes
• 	Gentle gradients (not flat, not complex)
Why this works
• 	It’s approachable
• 	It’s emotionally safe
• 	It’s readable
• 	It’s easy for open‑source contributors
• 	It sets the stage for later complexity
Environmental Look
• 	Simple trees, rocks, and terrain
• 	Soft shadows
• 	Light fog or bloom
• 	No symbolic geometry yet
• 	No layered motion
This is the “dreamlike onboarding” band.

🧩 2. Puzzle Structure (Tunic + Zelda)
Puzzle Philosophy
• 	Environmental, not mechanical
• 	Discoverable, not forced
• 	Gentle, not punishing
• 	Symbolic hints, not explicit instructions
Puzzle Types
• 	Pathfinding
• 	Light environmental clues
• 	Simple switches or triggers
• 	Spatial relationships
• 	“Notice this shape” puzzles
• 	Soft pattern recognition
Puzzle Tone
• 	Calm
• 	Curious
• 	Inviting
• 	Non‑threatening
Why this fits Fib 1–3
The player is learning:
• 	how to notice
• 	how to drift
• 	how to align
• 	how to read emotional cues
Not how to “solve” things under pressure.

🎮 3. Interaction & Movement (Abzû‑Lite)
Movement
• 	Soft drift
• 	Gentle acceleration
• 	Smooth turning
• 	No sharp edges
• 	No precision required
Interaction
• 	Proximity‑based
• 	Simple presence responses
• 	No complex timing
• 	No combo inputs
• 	No pressure
Spirit Animal Behavior
• 	Simple idle loops
• 	Soft bobbing
• 	Minimal personality
• 	No symbolic gestures yet
• 	Basic color pulses
This is the “learning to exist in the world” tier.

🌈 4. Color Grammar (Journey‑Soft)
Color Behavior
• 	Slow fades
• 	Gentle transitions
• 	Soft gradients
• 	No dramatic shifts
Emotional Mapping
• 	Warm = safety
• 	Cool = drift
• 	Neutral = threshold state
Why this works
It teaches the player that color = emotion, but in a soft, non‑overwhelming way.

🌬️ 5. Motion Grammar (Simple, Rhythmic, Sinker‑Lite)
Idle Motion
• 	Small vertical bob
• 	Tiny rotation
• 	Soft scale pulse
• 	Slow blink cycle
Interaction Motion
• 	Small snap‑in
• 	Gentle overshoot
• 	Quick settle
Environmental Motion
• 	Very subtle
• 	Light wind sway
• 	Soft water ripple
• 	Minimal particle use
This is the “motion as breath” tier.

🧩 6. Why Fib 1–3 Works as the Foundation
1. It’s simple enough for anyone to contribute
Open‑source friendly.
2. It’s emotionally safe
Perfect for onboarding.
3. It’s visually readable
No overwhelm.
4. It’s expandable
You can layer complexity on top of it.
5. It matches the Fibonacci growth curve
1–3 = simple
3–5 = expressive
5–8 = symbolic
