Fib 1–3 → Fib 3–5: the liquid‑shimmer reveal

This is the **Stargate / The Abyss** moment — a membrane that looks harmless until you touch it, and then the world changes its rules.

What this reveal expresses in Threshold

- The world has been hiding its true physics.
- The player has been in a “training ontology.”
- The environment has been soft, symbolic, readable.
- The shimmer is the **boundary of truth**.
- Crossing it activates the real mechanics: drift, pressure, shaping.

Emotional tone

- curiosity
- apprehension
- awe
- “I shouldn’t be doing this… but I have to.”
- the sense of stepping into something alive

How the reveal behaves

- The surface ripples like liquid.
- The air thickens.
- Colors distort.
- Sound drops out or muffles.
- The membrane reacts to proximity.
- Touching it creates a moment of resistance.
- Passing through is a single, decisive instant.

How Godot renders it

- shader‑based refraction
- depth‑based distortion
- color‑shift gradient
- low‑frequency rumble or silence
- camera easing into drift physics
- environment re‑lighting
- entity behavior switching to 3–5 logic

This is the **first wow moment** — the world reveals its second layer.

This is **charged space**, like:

What this reveal expresses in Threshold

- The world is no longer guiding you.
- You are entering the frontier.
- Survival requires contribution.
- The environment is now an active force.

Emotional tone

- mythic awe

- Light fractures.
- Snow‑storm‑like particles streak toward the player.
- The world becomes high‑contrast and vast.
- Pressure fields distort the camera.

- additive lightning‑like pulses

They form a **three‑act revelation structure**:

1. **Fib 1–3**  
    “This is a gentle, symbolic world.”
2. **Fib 3–5**  
    “No — this world has physics, drift, pressure, agency.”
3. **Fib 5–8**  
    “No — this world is ancient, dangerous, mythic, alive.”

Each reveal is a **rupture**, not a transition.  
Each reveal is a **truth event**, not a level change.  
Each reveal is a **shift in ontology**, not aesthetics.

And the first player to discover each reveal becomes the one who brings that truth back to the hub — which is exactly the social ignition Threshold is built for.
