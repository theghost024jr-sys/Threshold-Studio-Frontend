A Threshold Biome File

1. Biome Overview
Layer Affinity: Unplaced Fold (accessible from Shimmer, Soil, and Crown)
Biome Type: Crystallized Decision Field
Primary Phenomenon: Cognitive Overload Pressure (“mind‑heat”)
Signature Feature: Massive crystalline structures formed from abandoned choices
The Lumen Vault is a cavernous fold where unmade decisions accumulate and solidify into impossible crystalline architecture. The air is thick with cognitive heat — not temperature, but a pressure that simplifies thought into geometric clarity. Complex intentions melt. Only clean vectors remain.

2. Environmental Physics
2.1 Cognitive Heat
• 	Not thermal; does not burn skin.
• 	Overheats thoughts, forcing them into simple shapes.
• 	Multistep reasoning collapses into single‑vector impulses.
• 	Emotional nuance flattens into clean angles.
Effect:
Visitors become temporarily “geometric,” thinking in triangles, lines, and arcs.

2.2 Crystallized Decisions
Every crystal is a collapsed possibility — a choice someone almost made.
Properties:
• 	Touching one gives a flash of the unchosen path.
• 	Breaking one releases a brief, disorienting burst of “decision static.”
• 	Growing patterns follow emotional geometry, not mineral logic.
• 	Crystals can “hum” when nearby decisions are forming elsewhere.

2.3 Spatial Fold Geometry
The Vault is not underground.
It exists in a fold, where:
• 	gravity is optional
• 	orientation drifts
• 	“down” is a suggestion
• 	paths loop without repeating
The Vault can be entered from multiple layers but belongs to none.

3. Flora & Fauna
3.1 Thought‑Lichen
A soft, pale growth on crystal surfaces.
Feeds on discarded micro‑decisions (hesitations, doubts).
Glows faintly when someone is about to choose something important.

3.2 Vector Moths
Paper‑thin creatures made of light.
They follow the cleanest thought‑vector in the room.
If someone’s mind becomes too geometric, the moths orbit them like a halo.

3.3 The Archivist Worm
A blind, translucent creature that burrows through crystal.
Consumes old decisions and excretes “clarity dust.”
Harmless unless startled — then it emits a pulse that erases the last 3 seconds of memory.

4. Emotional Logic
4.1 Simplification Field
The Vault rewards:
• 	clarity
• 	decisiveness
• 	single‑vector thinking
It punishes:
• 	hesitation
• 	emotional complexity
• 	layered intentions
Visitors often leave with a temporary sense of clean certainty, which fades after a few hours.

4.2 Decision Echoes
Every unmade choice leaves a faint emotional residue.
These echoes can:
• 	whisper
• 	hum
• 	vibrate
• 	distort nearby crystals
Some echoes are strong enough to form Decision Phantoms — brief illusions of paths not taken.

5. Hazards
5.1 Over‑Simplification
Staying too long can flatten a person’s emotional range.
They may become:
• 	overly decisive
• 	blunt
• 	literal
• 	unable to hold contradictions
Recovery is slow but complete.

5.2 Crystal Collapse
If too many decisions are made in rapid succession, the Vault “reshapes,” causing:
• 	crystal avalanches
• 	spatial folds
• 	temporary loss of orientation

5.3 The Clarity Drop
A sudden plunge in cognitive heat.
Visitors regain full complexity instantly, often mid‑action.
This can be disorienting or dangerous.

6. Interaction with the Rounded Absurd Corner
The Vault touches the Rounded Absurd Corner through a Fractal Contact Event.
Effects:
• 	Some crystals become curved (impossible in normal Vault physics).
• 	Decision echoes become playful instead of solemn.
• 	Vector Moths behave unpredictably, following emotional vectors instead of logical ones.
• 	Fold geometry becomes “soft,” allowing sideways exits into absurd regions.
This is the only place where a crystallized decision can become a joke without losing its weight.

7. Known Entrances
• 	A shimmer fracture in the lower Shimmer Layer
• 	A soil‑level sinkhole that isn’t always present
• 	A crown‑layer reflection that becomes a doorway when ignored
Entrances appear and disappear based on local decision density.

8. Notes for Field Researchers
• 	Do not attempt complex planning inside the Vault.
• 	Avoid touching more than one crystal per hour.
• 	If Vector Moths begin orbiting you, simplify your thoughts immediately.
• 	If you hear a low hum, a Decision Phantom is forming — do not follow it.
• 	If the Archivist Worm surfaces, remain calm and still.
