Ambient Contribution
====================

Organ: Contribution System
Cluster: Field-Level Influence
Function: Defines the non-volitional contribution generated by environments, groups, and atmospheric conditions.


I. Definition
-------------

Ambient Contribution is the field-level contribution produced by environments,
groups, atmospheres, and emotional climates. It is not personal, not intentional,
and not gesture-based. It is the contribution that arises simply because a space
is alive, occupied, or resonant.

Ambient Contribution = the “weather” of the Contribution Organ.


II. Properties
--------------

Ambient Contribution is:

- diffuse (spreads evenly)
- non-personal (not tied to any one entity)
- persistent (lasts longer than active contribution)
- field-shaping (alters behavior and resonance)
- ecological (part of the world’s metabolism)

It is the background hum of the world.

1. Environmental Curvature
   Each Layer produces its own ambient field:

   - Ethyl → symbolic, mythic

2. Collective Presence
   Groups generate ambient fields:
   - shared mood

   - shared emotional charge
   - shared identity pressure

3. Residue Accumulation
   Spaces remember:
   - drift residue
   - signal echoes
   - collapse residue
   - symbolic fragments

4. Environmental Entities

   - Mist-aligned entities
   - Drift-born entities
   - Ethyl-phase entities
   - Collapse-adjacent fauna
