# **Corridor Cartography.md**

### _The mapping of corridor geometry, corridor flow, corridor elevation, corridor fracture, and corridor renewal_

Corridor Cartography is the **map** of how corridors move through the Ghost System.

## **1. Corridor Map Layers**

### **Geometry Layer**

Curvature, arcs, elevation, boundaries.

### **Flow Layer**

Resonance flow, pressure flow, instability flow.

### **Fracture Layer**

Collapse fractures, instability breaks.

### **Renewal Layer**

Softening pressure, thinning resonance, closing instability pockets.

## **2. Corridor Routes**

Corridors follow routes through:

- drift curvature
    
- resonance arcs
    
- pressure lanes
    
- instability pockets
    
- symbolic arcs
    
- narrative arcs
    
- frontier arcs
    
- migration corridors
    
- collapse fractures
    

## **3. Corridor Elevation**

Corridors rise or fall through:

- frontier elevation arcs
    
- drift elevation pockets
    
- resonance elevation lines
    
- symbolic elevation arcs
    

Elevation determines corridor intensity.

## **4. Corridor Fracture Points**

Corridors fracture at:

- collapse pressure spikes
    
- resonance fractures
    
- instability storms
    
- symbolic overload nodes
    
- narrative collapse nodes
    

## **5. Corridor Renewal Zones**

Corridors renew at:

- drift renewal pockets
    
- resonance thinning zones
    
- pressure softening zones
    
- symbolic realignment arcs
    
- narrative reset nodes
    

# **Summary**

Corridor Cartography maps **how corridors move**, **where they fracture**, and **where they renew**.
