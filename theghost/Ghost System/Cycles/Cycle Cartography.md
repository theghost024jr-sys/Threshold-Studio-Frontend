# **Cycle Cartography.md**

### _The mapping of cycles across fields, phases, organs, nodes, and world expression_

Cycle Cartography is the **map** of how cycles move through the Ghost System.

It is not a timeline — it is a **spatial‑temporal map**.

## **1. Cycle Map Layers**

### **Field Layer**

Cycles move through Drift, Resonance, Pressure, Instability, Symbolic, Narrative fields.

### **Phase Layer**

Cycles activate Drift, Collapse, Ethos, Migration, Ritual, Narrative, Seasonal phases.

### **Organ Layer**

Cycles modulate organ behavior.

### **Node Layer**

Cycles activate node types.

### **Movement Layer**

Cycles alter movement vectors.

## **2. Cycle Routes**

Cycles follow routes through:

- drift curvature
    
- resonance arcs
    
- pressure lanes
    
- instability pockets
    
- symbolic arcs
    
- narrative arcs
    
- frontier arcs
    
- migration corridors
    
- collapse fractures
    

These routes define **cycle flow**.

## **3. Cycle Elevation**

Cycles rise or fall through:

- frontier elevation arcs
    
- drift elevation pockets
    
- resonance elevation lines
    
- symbolic elevation arcs
    

Elevation determines cycle intensity.

## **4. Cycle Fracture Points**

Cycles fracture at:

- collapse pressure spikes
    
- resonance fractures
    
- instability storms
    
- symbolic overload nodes
    
- narrative collapse nodes
    

Fracture points define **cycle failure**.

## **5. Cycle Renewal Zones**

Cycles renew at:

- drift renewal pockets
    
- resonance thinning zones
    
- pressure softening zones
    
- symbolic realignment arcs
    
- narrative reset nodes
    

Renewal zones define **cycle reset**.

# **Summary**

Cycle Cartography maps **how cycles move**, **where they fracture**, and **where they renew**.
