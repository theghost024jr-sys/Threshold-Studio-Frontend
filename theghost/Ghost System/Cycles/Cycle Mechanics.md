# **Cycle Mechanics.md**

### _The physics, logic, and transformation rules that govern all cycles inside the Ghost System_

Cycle Mechanics define **how cycles operate** — how they begin, evolve, saturate, fracture, collapse, renew, and propagate through fields, phases, organs, and movement.

Cycles are **temporal physics**.

## **1. Cycle Drivers**

Cycles are driven by:

- drift curvature
    
- resonance density
    
- pressure gradients
    
- instability pockets
    
- symbolic meaning
    
- narrative meaning
    
- seasonal modulation
    

These drivers determine cycle speed, intensity, and failure modes.

## **2. Cycle Dynamics**

### **Bloom → Arc → Saturation → Collapse → Renewal**

Every cycle follows this universal dynamic:

1. **Bloom** — initial activation
    
2. **Arc** — rising coherence
    
3. **Saturation** — peak density
    
4. **Collapse** — structural failure
    
5. **Renewal** — reset and reformation
    

This applies to Drift, Collapse, Ethos, Migration, Ritual, Narrative, and Seasonal cycles.

## **3. Cycle Modulation**

Cycles are modulated by:

- **Field Conditions** Drift, Resonance, Pressure, Instability
    
- **Phase Conditions** Drift Phase, Collapse Phase, Ethos Phase, Migration Phase, Ritual Phase
    
- **Organ Conditions** Drift Organ, Resonance Organ, Pressure Organ, Instability Organ, Symbolic Organ, Narrative Organ
    
- **Node Conditions** Harmonic, Pressure, Instability, Symbolic, Narrative nodes
    

## **4. Cycle Failure Modes**

Cycles fail through:

- resonance fracture
    
- pressure spike
    
- instability storm
    
- symbolic overload
    
- narrative collapse
    
- drift dissolution
    

See: [[Cycle Phases]] for collapse‑adjacent transitions.

## **5. Cycle Renewal Mechanics**

Renewal occurs when:

- pressure gradients soften
    
- resonance density thins
    
- instability pockets close
    
- symbolic arcs realign
    
- narrative pulses reset
    

Renewal is the **cycle reset state**.

# **Summary**

Cycle Mechanics define **how cycles behave**, **how they fail**, and **how they renew**.
