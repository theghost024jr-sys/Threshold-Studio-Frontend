### _A being whose perception is tuned to drift curvature, turbulence pockets, and harmonic motion fields_

A Drift‑Walker is not a role, class, or profession. It is a **condition of perception** — a way of seeing and moving through Threshold’s Drift Field that ordinary beings cannot access.

A Drift‑Walker does not “walk through drift.” They **walk drift itself**.

They read:

- turbulence pockets
    
- pressure lanes
