Drift Sequence
==============

A Drift Sequence is a **navigable corridor of unresolved motion** formed when
the Drift Layer becomes permeable and the Temporal Substrate folds into a
stable seam. It is not dream, memory, or hallucination — it is **physics**.

A Drift Sequence is the world remembering motion that never completed.


I. Definition
-------------

A Drift Sequence is:
- a stabilized pocket of unresolved motion  
- a temporal fold anchored by the Temporal Substrate  
- a corridor shaped by drift pressure  
- a memory-adjacent environment  
- a non-editable, navigable state  

A Drift Sequence is **readable**, not **rewritable**.


II. Formation Conditions
------------------------

A Drift Sequence forms when:

1. **Drift Pressure exceeds local stability**  
2. **Temporal Substrate enters Seam Mode**  
3. **Collapse residue becomes navigable**  
4. **Directional Pulls activate**  
5. **Identity stance aligns with drift curvature**

Formation is **threshold-based**, not gradual.


III. Structural Components
--------------------------

Every Drift Sequence contains:

1. **Corridor Spine**  
   The main path of unresolved motion.

2. **Memory Rooms**  
   Fragments of what almost happened.

3. **Pressure Nodes**  
   Emotional or narrative tension points.

4. **Directional Pulls**  
   N/S/E/W temperament shaping the route.

5. **Particle Clusters**  
   Micro-agents interpreting the sequence.

6. **Seam Walls**  
   Temporal boundaries preventing collapse.


IV. Sequence Behavior
---------------------

Inside a Drift Sequence, the player can:
- walk  
- observe  
- listen  
- follow pressure  
- stabilize or destabilize the corridor  

The player cannot:
- rewrite events  
- force outcomes  
- break the sequence  
- alter the past  

A Drift Sequence is a **mirror**, not a stage.


V. Sequence Logic
-----------------

A Drift Sequence follows a predictable rhythm:

1. **Entry**  
   Seam opens; corridor forms.

2. **Orientation**  
   Directional pull becomes clear.

3. **Traversal**  
   Player follows drift currents.

4. **Confrontation**  
   Pressure node resolves.

5. **Release**  
   Corridor dissolves.

6. **Return**  
   Player exits with reduced weight.

This rhythm is governed by the Temporal Substrate.


VI. Drift Sequence Types
------------------------

1. **Soft Drift Sequence**  
   - low pressure  
   - gentle curvature  
   - emotional tint

2. **Turbulent Drift Sequence**  
   - high drift shear  
   - instability spirals  
   - collapse adjacency

3. **Symbolic Drift Sequence**  
   - archetypal curvature  
   - mythic residue  
   - Ethyl influence

4. **Collapse Drift Sequence**  
   - brittle boundaries  
   - collapse residue  
   - reconstitution triggers


VII. Failure Modes
------------------

1. **Sequence Saturation**  
   Pressure overwhelms seam stability.

2. **Sequence Inversion**  
   Meaning flips; corridor reverses.

3. **Sequence Fracture**  
   Corridor splits into multiple unstable paths.

4. **Sequence Collapse**  
   Drift shear exceeds seam integrity.


VIII. Exit Conditions
---------------------

A Drift Sequence ends when:
- the corridor collapses  
- the final pressure node resolves  
- the mist clears  
- the substrate stabilizes  
- identity stance shifts out of drift alignment  

Exit is always a **return**, never a rupture.


IX. Summary
-----------

A Drift Sequence is:
- unresolved motion  
- stabilized memory-adjacency  
- drift physics made navigable  
- temporal substrate in seam mode  
- collapse residue in corridor form  

It is the world remembering itself.
