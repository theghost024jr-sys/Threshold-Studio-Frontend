Mistreach
=========

Mistreach is the **boundary‑violation event** where a system reaches into the
wrong layer, wrong identity, wrong phase, or wrong pattern. It is the physics
of **incorrect reach** — the precursor to collapse, distortion, and instability.


I. Definition
-------------

Mistreach occurs when:
- a reach vector extends into a domain it cannot support  
- identity misaligns with environment  
- pressure forces a reach beyond structural capacity  
- drift curvature pulls a reach off its correct path  

Mistreach is not collapse.  
Mistreach is the **misalignment act that *causes* collapse**.


II. Components of Mistreach
---------------------------

Every Mistreach event contains four mechanical elements:

1. **Reach Vector**  
   The direction, intention, or identity extension that initiates the event.

2. **Boundary Violation Point**  
   Where the reach crosses into a domain it cannot structurally support.

3. **Distortion Bloom**  
   The Field’s reaction — swirl, warp, pressure spike, drift agitation.

4. **Collapse Trigger Line**  
   The threshold beyond which the system fails.


III. Causes of Mistreach
------------------------

Mistreach arises from:

- identity overextension  
- pressure misalignment  
- drift curvature overload  
- resonance failure  
- symbolic inversion  
- lineage thread misbinding  
- environmental rejection  

It is always a **misfit between intention and physics**.


IV. Classes of Mistreach
------------------------

1. **Soft Mistreach**  
   Minor misalignment; turbulence without collapse.

2. **Sharp Mistreach**  
   High‑precision violation; directional snap; collapse risk.

3. **Diffuse Mistreach**  
   Unfocused multi‑vector reach; resonance smear.

4. **Hybrid Mistreach**  
   Oscillating sharp + diffuse; unpredictable collapse vectors.

5. **Inversion Mistreach**  
   Reach flips into its opposite; collapse ignition likely.


V. Mistreach Behaviors
----------------------

Mistreach produces predictable physics:

- **Drift Shear** — drift layers tear in opposite directions  
- **Resonance Break** — alignment collapses into distortion  
- **Pressure Spike** — Threshold Pressure surges at violation point  
- **Boundary Tear** — identity or environment boundaries rupture  
- **Collapse Coupling** — collapse vectors attach to the reach vector  


VI. Mistreach vs Collapse
-------------------------

Mistreach is **the cause**.  
Collapse is **the consequence**.

Mistreach → Collapse when:
- pressure exceeds capacity  
- resonance cannot stabilize  
- drift shear becomes directional  
- boundary tear reaches critical length  

Mistreach can be corrected.  
Collapse cannot.


VII. Mistreach Recovery
-----------------------

Recovery requires:

1. **Reach Retraction**  
   Pulling the identity vector back into its correct domain.

2. **Boundary Resealing**  
   Stabilizing the tear through reformation.

3. **Drift Clearing**  
   Removing turbulence and shear.

4. **Signal Re‑tuning**  
   Correcting inversion or distortion.

5. **Lineage Rebinding**  
   Reconnecting origin threads to restore coherence.

Recovery ends in **Identity Resonance** or **Neutral Stability**.


VIII. Indicators of Mistreach
-----------------------------

Early warnings include:

- pressure spikes  
- silhouette distortion  
- resonance collapse  
- drift thickening  
- temporal jitter  
- directional inversion  
- identity turbulence
