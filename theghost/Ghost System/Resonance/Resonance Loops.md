Resonance Loops
===============

Resonance Loops are **recursive amplification patterns** formed when a signal,
identity, role, or emotional state re-enters its own field and returns with
greater intensity, clarity, distortion, or collapse pressure.

A Resonance Loop is not repetition —  
it is **recursive meaning**.


I. Definition
-------------

A Resonance Loop occurs when:

1. A source emits a signal.  
2. The environment shapes the signal.  
3. The signal returns to the source.  
4. The source reacts, amplifies, or distorts.  
5. The loop repeats until stability or collapse.

Resonance Loops are the **self-organizing engine** of Threshold.


II. Loop Components
-------------------

Every Resonance Loop contains:

1. **Source**  
   Identity, role, species, emotional state, or signal.

2. **Field**  
   The environment shaping the loop (Mist, Ember, Ethyl, Earthbind, Threshold).

3. **Return Path**  
   The mechanism by which meaning re-enters its origin.

4. **Amplification Pattern**  
   How the loop strengthens, distorts, or collapses.

5. **Loop Outcome**  
   Stability, momentum, instability, collapse, or renewal.


III. Loop Types
---------------

1. **Stabilizing Loops**  
   Meaning returns clearer.  
   - reduces drift  
   - strengthens identity  
   - aligns roles  
   - common in Earthbind, Air, Stone

2. **Amplification Loops**  
   Meaning returns stronger.  
   - emotional intensification  
   - role overexpression  
   - species resonance spikes  
   - common in Ember, Ethyl

3. **Distortion Loops**  
   Meaning returns bent or fragmented.  
   - semantic noise  
   - drift acceleration  
   - instability spirals  
   - common in Mist, Ember

4. **Collapse Loops**  
   Meaning returns with destructive force.  
   - identity fracture  
   - role inversion  
   - collapse adjacency  
   - common in Stone, Threshold Basin

5. **Restorative Loops**  
   Meaning returns with healing force.  
   - emotional repair  
   - role realignment  
   - species-role harmony  
   - common in Healer species


IV. Loop Mechanics
------------------

A Resonance Loop requires:

1. **A Source**  
   Identity, role, species, or emotional state.

2. **A Field**  
   Environmental layer, emotional pressure, symbolic context.

3. **A Return Path**  
   Drift arc, pressure gradient, symbolic curvature, or identity tether.

The **shape** of the loop determines its effect.


V. Environmental Influence
--------------------------

Each Layer shapes loops differently:

- **Air** — clarity, dissipation, long loops  
- **Earthbind** — grounding, slow loops  
- **Ember** — intensity, volatility, short loops  
- **Mist** — ambiguity, echoing, recursive loops  
- **Stone** — rigidity, fracture-risk loops  
- **Ethyl** — symbolic bleed-through, mythic loops  


VI. Role Interaction
--------------------

Roles generate characteristic loops:

- **Guide** — stabilizing + restorative  
- **Keeper** — stabilizing + grounding  
- **Witness** — amplification + echoing  
- **Trickster** — distortion + inversion  
- **Maker** — amplification + constructive  
- **Warden** — collapse-risk under pressure  
- **Healer** — restorative + integrative  

This forms the backbone of **Role Drift** and **Role Dynamics**.


VII. Species Interaction
------------------------

Species amplify or dampen loops based on:

- sensory attunement  
- emotional resonance  
- elemental alignment  
- role sensitivity  

This is where the **Species–Role Sense Table** plugs in.


VIII. Loop Failure Modes
------------------------

1. **Loop Saturation**  
   Amplification exceeds stability.

2. **Loop Inversion**  
   Meaning flips into its opposite.

3. **Loop Fragmentation**  
   Loop breaks into multiple unstable sub-loops.

4. **Loop Collapse**  
   Loop becomes destructive and triggers collapse physics.


IX. Loop Stabilization
----------------------

Loops stabilize when:

- pressure releases  
- drift clarity activates  
- resonance enters (1R)  
- return paths reopen  
- memory scars reorganize  

Stabilization is **reformation**, not reversal.


X. Indicators
-------------

Resonance Loops reveal:

- pressure level  
- drift curvature  
- collapse adjacency  
- identity stability  
- role alignment  
- species resonance  


XI. Summary
-----------

Resonance Loops =  
recursive meaning + amplification pattern + environmental shaping + identity reaction

They are the **feedback architecture** of the world.
