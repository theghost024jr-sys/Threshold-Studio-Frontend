About the Falls

Tall beyond measure, the Drift Falls mark the threshold between two worlds. Luminous Drift energy infuses every drop, glowing brightest at the crest and fading into heavy mist below. Thick roots grip the stone walls. The shallow pool at the base holds a silence that feels earned. Natural stone shelves descend southward, carving the first steps into the Forest biome.
