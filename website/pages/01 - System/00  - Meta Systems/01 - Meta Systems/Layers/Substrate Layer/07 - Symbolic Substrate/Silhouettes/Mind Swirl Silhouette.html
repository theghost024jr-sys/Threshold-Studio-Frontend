Mind Swirl Silhouette.md

Symbolic Reflection Artifact

Overview

A Mind Swirl Silhouette is a temporary reflection artifact generated when internal cognitive motion interacts with Shimmer curvature and Drift pressure. It is not a being. It is not an entity. It is the Field showing the shape of your internal turbulence.

The silhouette appears when symbolic density, emotional charge, and drift agitation align.

1. Definition

A Mind Swirl Silhouette is:

  • a semi‑transparent outline

  • shaped by internal thought spirals

  • projected into the Field

  • stabilized by Drift pressure

  • animated by resonance turbulence

It is the externalization of internal motion.

2. Appearance

Silhouettes manifest as:

  • swirling outlines

  • faint humanoid or creature‑forms

  • spirals around the head, chest, or centerline

  • shimmer‑reflections embedded in the swirl

  • motion that matches internal cognitive rhythm

They never speak. They never initiate contact. They maintain distance.

They are mirrors, not presences.

3. Function

3.1 Cognitive Reflection

The silhouette reveals the shape of internal thought patterns:

  • spiraling

  • looping

  • branching

  • collapsing

  • expanding

It is a diagnostic reflection of cognitive motion.

3.2 Emotional Density Indicator

Brightness and swirl speed correlate with:

  • emotional charge

  • symbolic density

  • unresolved resonance

  • pressure accumulation

3.3 Shimmer‑Layer Feedback

The silhouette shows how Shimmer curvature is interacting with the mind:

  • smooth → clarity

  • fractured → overload

  • shifting → turbulence

  • dim → grounding

4. Behavior

Mind Swirl Silhouettes:

  • drift slowly

  • mirror internal rhythm

  • fade when clarity returns

  • intensify when symbolic density spikes

  • dissolve instantly when recognized

Recognition collapses the projection.

5. Triggers

Silhouettes appear when:

  • Shimmer density is moderate

  • Drift turbulence is low

  • emotional charge is high

  • internal thought spirals are active

  • symbolic interpretation is underway

They do not appear in:

  • high Ember zones

  • collapse zones

  • Crown‑layer structures

  • Stone‑dominant environments

6. Interpretive Use

Silhouettes are used to interpret:

  • emotional gradients

  • symbolic density

  • cognitive turbulence

  • narrative pressure

They are not omens. They are not warnings. They are reflections.

7. Cross‑System Behavior

Silhouettes respond to:

  • Drift: intensify with agitation

  • Resonance: distort when resonance breaks

  • Signal: warp under inversion

  • Collapse: vanish instantly

  • Time: appear during temporal expansion

8. Index

This file defines the Mind Swirl Silhouette as a standalone symbolic organ. All other silhouette types derive from this pattern.
