Overview Species shape how a Role expresses itself in the world. A Sentinel‑spirit in a migratory species behaves differently from a Sentinel‑spirit in a burrowing species. A Weaver in a hive species is not the same as a Weaver in a solitary predator. Species determine: • how a Role manifests • what behaviors it defaults to • how it handles drift • how it expresses shadow • how overlays tint the embodiment Roles are the why. Species are the how. This table maps the six Archetypal Roles across common species archetypes.

Species Archetype Key To keep this system universal, species are grouped into functional archetypes: • Burrowers — grounded, territorial, pressure‑sensitive • Fliers — aerial, perceptive, pattern‑oriented • Pack Hunters — coordinated, pursuit‑driven • Solitary Predators — focused, territorial, high‑precision • Herd / Flock Species — relational, responsive, drift‑sensitive • Aquatic Species — fluid, reflective, resonance‑oriented • Hive Species — networked, distributed intelligence • Nocturnal Species — intuitive, subtle, shadow‑aware You can expand or refine these later as your Species Architecture grows.
