Overview Different species place different pressures on Roles. Some species amplify a Role’s strengths. Some species destabilize it. Some species push drift. Some species trigger shadow. This table maps how each species archetype affects each Role’s stability. Sensitivity levels: • Low Sensitivity — species strongly supports the Role • Moderate Sensitivity — species neither stabilizes nor destabilizes • High Sensitivity — species increases drift likelihood • Critical Sensitivity — species triggers shadow or collapse patterns

Species Archetypes • Burrowers — grounded, pressure‑readers • Fliers — perceptive, pattern‑oriented • Pack Hunters — coordinated, pursuit‑driven • Solitary Predators — focused, territorial • Herd / Flock Species — relational, drift‑responsive • Aquatic Species — reflective, resonance‑oriented • Hive Species — networked, distributed intelligence • Nocturnal Species — intuitive, shadow‑aware

Species–Role Sensitivity Matrix

Interpretation Notes Sentinel • Most stable in Burrowers, Hive species, and Solitary Predators • Most sensitive in Fliers and Herd species (too much motion or relational pressure) Weaver • Strongly supported by Fliers, Herd species, and Hive species • Most sensitive in Solitary Predators (lack of relational substrate) Hunter • Strongly supported by Pack Hunters, Solitary Predators, Fliers, Nocturnal species • Most sensitive in Herd species (pursuit destabilizes group dynamics) Oracle • Strongly supported by Aquatic, Hive, Nocturnal, Fliers • Most sensitive in Pack Hunters (too much motion, not enough subtlety) Echo • Strongly supported by Aquatic, Herd, Hive, Nocturnal • Most sensitive in Pack Hunters and Solitary Predators (too much directional pressure) Lantern • Strongly supported by Fliers, Herd, Aquatic, Nocturnal • Most sensitive in Solitary Predators (illumination collapses into over‑focus)
