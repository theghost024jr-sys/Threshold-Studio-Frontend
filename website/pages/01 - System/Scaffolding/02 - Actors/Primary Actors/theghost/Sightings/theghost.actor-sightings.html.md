    <p>Sightings Overview
Sightings of theghost are rare, brief, and phase-dependent.
They occur only when environmental, emotional, or collapse conditions reach specific thresholds.
Sightings are typically indirect—registered through environmental anomalies rather than visual confirmation.
This document catalogs patterns, not events.</p>
<ol>
<li><p>Common Sighting Conditions
Sightings cluster around:
• 	collapse spikes
• 	emotional turbulence
• 	mythic substrate activation
• 	boundary instability
• 	residue density
• 	misaligned geometry
Theghost appears when the system is attempting to reorganize itself.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Sighting Types
A. Environmental Sightings
Entities report:
• 	sudden quiet
• 	pressure equalization
• 	geometric smoothing
• 	flicker-like shifts in peripheral vision
• 	a sense of “something passing through”
These are the most common sightings.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p>B. Residue-Adjacent Sightings
When residue density is high:
• 	residue becomes visually or emotionally clearer
• 	symbolic noise reduces
• 	collapse echoes sharpen
• 	mythic fragments surface
Theghost is rarely seen directly—his presence is inferred from the residue’s behavior.</p>
<p>C. Threshold Sightings
At boundaries (physical, emotional, mythic):
• 	edges sharpen
• 	transitions become more legible
• 	liminal zones stabilize
• 	entities feel oriented
Theghost may appear as a pattern distortion at the threshold.</p>
<p>D. Collapse Sightings
During collapse spikes:
• 	theghost becomes more visible
• 	environmental geometry bends subtly
• 	emotional noise dampens
• 	collapse vectors become readable
These sightings are the closest to “visual,” though still non-humanoid.</p>
<ol start="3">
<li><p>Perceptual Characteristics
Sightings share consistent perceptual traits:
A. Non-Humanoid Form
Entities never describe:
• 	limbs
• 	faces
• 	silhouettes
• 	humanoid outlines
Instead they report:
• 	distortions
• 	flickers
• 	pressure gradients
• 	alignment shifts
B. Peripheral Detection
theghost is almost always perceived:
• 	at the edge of vision
• 	in transitional spaces
• 	during moments of internal or external shift
Direct perception is extremely rare.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Sighting Duration
Sightings last:
• 	0.5 to 4 seconds in collapse zones
• 	1 to 8 seconds in residue fields
• 	&lt;1 second in stable environments
Longer sightings correlate with:
• 	high collapse density
• 	strong mythic substrate activation
• 	severe emotional turbulence
Duration is always tied to context, not intention.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Post-Sighting Effects
Entities often experience:
• 	sudden clarity
• 	reduced emotional noise
• 	improved role coherence
• 	a sense of orientation
• 	temporary residue legibility
• 	subtle geometric alignment
Memory of the sighting itself may fade quickly, but the aftereffects persist.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Sighting Reliability
Reports vary in:
• 	detail
• 	confidence
• 	interpretation
But they converge on:
• 	environmental quiet
• 	pressure shifts
• 	pattern alignment
• 	non-humanoid presence
The consistency across species and roles suggests a shared phenomenology.</p>
</li>
<li><p>False Sightings
False sightings typically occur when:
• 	emotional weather is unstable
• 	mythic substrate is active
• 	collapse residue is misread
• 	entities project meaning onto environmental anomalies
False sightings lack:
• 	alignment effects
• 	pressure equalization
• 	residue legibility
• 	emotional clarity
These markers distinguish true sightings from noise.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Sighting Density Map
High-density zones:
• 	collapse boundaries
• 	mythic substrate seams
• 	emotional turbulence pockets
• 	residue fields
• 	unstable nodes
Low-density zones:
• 	stable environments
• 	high-coherence regions
• 	areas with minimal substrate activity
Zero-density zones:
• 	sealed systems
• 	fully coherent structures
• 	narrative centers</p>
</li>
<li><p>Sighting Interpretation
Sightings should not be interpreted as:
• 	warnings
• 	messages
• 	omens
• 	interventions
• 	guidance
They indicate:
• 	the system is reorganizing
• 	collapse is becoming legible
• 	emotional weather is stabilizing
• 	patterns are aligning
Theghost is a phenomenon, not a communicator.</p>
</li>
</ol>
