[[Ember Layer]]

A heat‑logic environment — not fire, not flame, but structured warmth. Everything here is built from glowing filaments that pulse like slow breathing. Where the Spiral deals in delay and echo, the Lattice deals in immediacy and imprint. Core Physics • Every action leaves a heat signature that persists for minutes • Motion creates lattice trails — temporary pathways others can follow • Emotional intensity changes the color of the filaments • Stillness cools the environment; agitation heats it • The Lattice “remembers” patterns of movement and replays them faintly Environmental Behavior • Warmth is not comfort — it’s information • The Lattice brightens when someone is truthful • It dims when someone withholds • It flares when someone lies • It collapses into ash‑dust if overwhelmed by contradiction Sensory Profile • Air feels thick, like walking through warm silk • Surfaces glow from within, not from light hitting them • Footsteps leave ember‑prints that fade slowly • Voices distort slightly, as if passing through heated metal Role Bias This environment favors: • Interpreters • Pattern‑readers • Those who can stay emotionally coherent • Those who move with intention rather than impulse It punishes: • Deception • Hesitation • Emotional fragmentation Collapse Mode If the Lattice is pushed too hard: • Filaments snap • Heat signatures scatter • The environment “goes dark” • Navigation becomes impossible until it cools and resets
