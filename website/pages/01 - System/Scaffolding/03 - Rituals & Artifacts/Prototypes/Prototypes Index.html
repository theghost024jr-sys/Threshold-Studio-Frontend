Prototypes Index

Mythic-scale organs that define generative, dormant, and ecological cycles

Prototypes are mythic-scale symbolic organs that shape the deepest ecological rhythms of Threshold. They are not artifacts, not rituals, and not environmental nodes. They are archetypal engines that express the world’s foundational cycles: emergence, stillness, mutation, lineage, and transformation.

Each prototype influences entire regions, seasons, or substrate layers. They operate slowly, powerfully, and often invisibly, forming the mythic backbone of the system.

This index defines the prototype cluster and provides direct links to each prototype’s canonical file.

Purpose of Prototypes

Prototypes exist to:

  • anchor the world’s largest symbolic cycles
  • regulate collapse, recovery, and emergence
  • shape seasonal resonance and density patterns
  • influence artifact behavior at ecological scale
  • govern mythic fragments and lineage structures
  • provide stable archetypes for player interaction

They are the deep organs of the system.

Prototype Categories

Prototypes fall into three broad categories:

  • Generative Prototypes — create new symbolic forms, density, or lineage (e.g., First Seed).
  • Dormant-State Prototypes — stabilize collapse, stillness, and memory density (e.g., Winter Crown).
  • Transitional Prototypes — govern movement between ecological states (future prototypes).

Each category expresses a different phase of the world’s breath.

Ecological Roles

Prototypes shape the ecology at the highest level:

  • Winter Crown — holds stillness, prevents premature activation, stabilizes memory.
  • First Seed — initiates emergence, growth, and generative cycles.
  • Future Prototypes — will govern mutation, traversal, or mythic convergence.

Together, they form the mythic cycle engine of Threshold.

Substrate Influence

Prototypes interact with all four substrates:

  • Symbolic — density, drift, rooting, collapse
  • Emotional — volatility, amplitude, resonance tone
  • Memory — lineage, recursion, mythic continuity
  • Scene — environmental resonance, spatial thresholds

Their influence is slow, large-scale, and foundational.

Relationship to Rituals & Artifacts

Prototypes are not rituals or artifacts, but they:

  • shape when rituals are safe or dangerous
  • determine artifact dormancy or activation
  • influence seasonal resonance
  • govern collapse and recovery cycles
  • anchor mythic fragments

They are the context in which rituals and artifacts operate.
