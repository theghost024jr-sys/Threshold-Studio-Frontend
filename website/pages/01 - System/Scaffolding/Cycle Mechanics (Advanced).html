Positive curvature (Coherence → Tension)

  • inward pull strengthens
  • pressure stacks
  • resonance tightens

Neutral curvature (Void)

  • no directional bias
  • drift sinks
  • resonance flattens

Negative curvature (Collapse)

  • outward bursts
  • drift scatters
  • resonance destabilizes

Curvature determines phase stability.

High curvature → fast transitions.
Low curvature → long phases.
