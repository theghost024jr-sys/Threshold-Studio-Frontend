The five functional identities that express the Cycle — the operational roles that enact Coherence, Tension, Collapse, Void, and Renewal across environments, systems, and beings These are not the mythic archetypes (Circle, Gate, Crown, Tree, Lantern). These are the functional roles — the operational behaviors that any entity, system, or environment can take on when expressing a phase of the Cycle. Where the mythic roles are cosmological, the Cycle Roles are mechanical. Below is the clean, canonical Cycle Roles.md.

  1. WHAT CYCLE ROLES ARE Cycle Roles are the five functional identities that emerge whenever the Cycle expresses itself through: • environments • species • systems • structures • drift patterns • resonance fields • individuals • mythic entities A Cycle Role is not a personality. It is not a job. It is not a narrative archetype. It is a mode of operation — a way the world behaves when a phase is active.

  2. THE FIVE CYCLE ROLES Each role corresponds to a phase of the Cycle and expresses its mechanics.

A. The Gatherer (Coherence Role) The one who collects, organizes, stabilizes Function: Bring scattered elements into alignment. Behavioral signatures: • organizes information or resources • creates structure • stabilizes environments • reduces noise • strengthens coherence Environmental expression: Paths curve inward; drift gathers; resonance harmonizes. Failure mode: Over‑gathering → saturation → triggers Tension. Mythic correspondence: Circle.

B. The Holder (Tension Role) The one who bears weight, maintains pressure, sustains the threshold Function: Maintain necessary pressure until truth emerges. Behavioral signatures: • braces • contains • intensifies • maintains boundaries • reveals strain Environmental expression: Narrowing corridors; vertical drift; sharp resonance. Failure mode: Overpressure → fracture → triggers Collapse. Mythic correspondence: Gate.

C. The Releaser (Collapse Role) The one who dissolves, unbinds, and lets go Function: Release what can no longer be held. Behavioral signatures: • dissolves rigid structures • scatters accumulated weight • unbinds identities • breaks stagnation • redistributes drift Environmental expression: Scatter drift; softening structures; volatile resonance. Failure mode: Runaway dissolution → destabilizes Void. Mythic correspondence: Crown.

D. The Restorative (Void Role) The one who rests, neutralizes, and holds stillness Function: Return the system to neutrality. Behavioral signatures: • quiets activity • suspends processes • absorbs residue • reduces resonance • restores baseline Environmental expression: Still air; muted sound; downward drift. Failure mode: Deep Void saturation → prevents Renewal. Mythic correspondence: Tree.

E. The Initiator (Renewal Role) The one who sparks, awakens, and begins Function: Ignite new forms and guide early emergence. Behavioral signatures: • introduces new patterns • sparks curiosity • re‑forms structure • lifts resonance • organizes upward drift Environmental expression: Warm pockets; sprouts; flickering resonance. Failure mode: Premature ignition → unstable Coherence. Mythic correspondence: Lantern.

  1. HOW CYCLE ROLES MOVE Cycle Roles follow the same sequence as the Cycle: Gatherer → Holder → Releaser → Restorative → Initiator → Gatherer But roles can: • accelerate • stall • reverse • overlap • hybridize depending on environmental and systemic conditions.

  2. HYBRID CYCLE ROLES Some states express two roles simultaneously. Gatherer–Holder (Coherence + Tension) Stable but tightening. Holder–Releaser (Tension + Collapse) Volatile, fracture‑prone. Releaser–Restorative (Collapse + Void) Softening into stillness. Restorative–Initiator (Void + Renewal) Thawing void. Initiator–Gatherer (Renewal + Coherence) Early coherence forming. Hybrid roles are crucial for diagnostics and navigation.
