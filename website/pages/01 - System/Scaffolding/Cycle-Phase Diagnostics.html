  2. THE FIVE PHASES — CORE DIAGNOSTIC SIGNATURES Each phase has a distinct diagnostic fingerprint.

These signatures are the foundation of all diagnostics.

  1. DIAGNOSTIC LAYER 1 — SOMATIC INDICATORS The body is the fastest diagnostic instrument. Coherence (Circle) • breath deepens • shoulders soften inward • sense of “returning to center” Tension (Gate) • breath holds naturally • micro‑tightening in diaphragm • sense of “too much” Collapse (Crown) • spontaneous exhale • muscles release • sense of “letting go” Void (Tree) • breath quiets • weight drops • sense of “nothing required” Renewal (Lantern) • subtle upward lift • warmth in sternum • sense of “something wants to begin” Somatic diagnostics are immediate and reliable.

  2. DIAGNOSTIC LAYER 2 — ENVIRONMENTAL INDICATORS The environment expresses the Cycle physically. Coherence (Circle) • inward‑curving paths • radial flora patterns • stable temperature pockets Tension (Gate) • narrowing corridors • vertical pressure • directional wind Collapse (Crown) • scattering debris • softening structures • unpredictable drift Void (Tree) • still air • muted sound • dormant flora Renewal (Lantern) • warm pockets • early sprouts • upward drift spirals Environmental diagnostics reveal the phase of place.

  3. DIAGNOSTIC LAYER 3 — STRUCTURAL INDICATORS Systems, organizations, and narratives also move through the Cycle. Coherence (Circle) • clear roles • stable patterns • strong alignment Tension (Gate) • bottlenecks • over‑definition • rising pressure Collapse (Crown) • fragmentation • loss of structure • rapid unbinding Void (Tree) • pause in activity • minimal demands • neutral drift Renewal (Lantern) • new ideas • early prototypes • re‑forming structure Structural diagnostics reveal the phase of systems.

  4. DIAGNOSTIC LAYER 4 — BEHAVIORAL INDICATORS Entities and roles express the Cycle through behavior. Coherence (Circle) • organizing • gathering • stabilizing Tension (Gate) • bracing • over‑holding • intensifying Collapse (Crown) • releasing • dissolving • scattering Void (Tree) • resting • withdrawing • neutralizing Renewal (Lantern) • initiating • exploring • re‑engaging Behavioral diagnostics reveal the phase of beings.

  5. DIAGNOSTIC LAYER 5 — RESONANCE INDICATORS Resonance is the most subtle but most accurate diagnostic layer. Coherence (Circle) • harmonic, low‑variance hum Tension (Gate) • sharp, directional resonance Collapse (Crown) • diffuse, unstable resonance Void (Tree) • muted, low‑frequency resonance Renewal (Lantern) • flickering, rising resonance Resonance diagnostics reveal the phase of meaning itself.

  6. PHASE TRANSITION DIAGNOSTICS Transitions have their own signatures. Circle → Gate • coherence becomes heavy • inward drift becomes linear • pressure begins to form Gate → Crown • pressure peaks • drift becomes turbulent • structures begin to soften Crown → Tree • collapse exhausts • drift settles downward • stillness spreads Tree → Lantern • warmth returns • upward drift flickers • early forms shimmer Lantern → Circle • renewal stabilizes • patterns re‑align • coherence returns Transitions are the most important diagnostic moments.

  7. HYBRID PHASE DIAGNOSTICS Some states express two phases at once. Circle + Gate • stable but tightening • early pressure Gate + Crown • volatile • fracture imminent Crown + Tree • softening into stillness Tree + Lantern • thawing void Lantern + Circle • early coherence Hybrid diagnostics are essential for navigation.

  8. RAPID DIAGNOSTIC CHECKLIST A quick, five‑question diagnostic tool:

  9. Is the environment gathering or scattering?

  10. Is the body tightening or releasing?

  11. Is the structure stabilizing or dissolving?

  12. Is the resonance rising, falling, or still?

  13. Is drift moving inward, outward, upward, or downward?

The answers reveal the phase.
