The foundational condition from which Threshold emerges — the pre‑architectural state that gives rise to Layers, systems, and meaning.

Purpose Genesis defines the origin logic of Threshold. Not the first event, not the first myth — the pre‑event state that makes events possible. Genesis explains: • what existed before structure • how structure first formed • why Layers emerged • how meaning became possible • how collapse and reformation became the world’s breathing rhythm Genesis is the zero‑point architecture.

The Four Pre‑Architectural Conditions Before Threshold had Layers, systems, or substrate, it had four conditions — not things, not forces, but tendencies.

  1. Differentiation The tendency for sameness to become distinct. Outcome: The first boundary.

  2. Drift The tendency for distinction to move. Outcome: The first vector.

  3. Resonance The tendency for movement to align. Outcome: The first harmonic.

  4. Memory The tendency for alignment to persist. Outcome: The first thread. These four tendencies are the proto‑forms of Identity, Role, Signal, and Lineage.

Genesis Architecture Genesis produces three structural consequences:

  1. The Emergence of Boundary Differentiation creates the first inside/outside distinction. This is the seed of Identity.

  2. The Emergence of Direction Drift creates the first orientation. This is the seed of Role.

  3. The Emergence of Meaning Resonance + Memory create the first pattern that persists. This is the seed of Signal and Lineage.
