Overview Layer Index 1 is the primary reference spine for the entire Layer System. It defines the seven layers, their ordering, their core functions, and their structural relationships. This index is the entry point for all layer‑related navigation, physics, and system behavior. It does not describe physics, behaviors, or collapse interactions — those live in their own files. This file simply establishes what the layers are and how they relate.

The Seven Layers

  1. Drift (Layer 1) • Motion field • Micro‑currents, turbulence, flow • Primary driver of movement and subtle behavior
  2. Mist (Layer 2) • Softening field • Diffusion, blur, emotional haze • Governs permeability and boundary softness
  3. Shimmer (Layer 3) • Meaning field • Curvature, refraction, symbolic arcs • Governs interpretation and resonance
  4. Stone (Layer 4) • Stability field • Weight, grounding, structure • Governs anchoring and resistance
  5. Ember (Layer 5) • Ignition field • Heat, spark, activation • Governs intensity and catalytic behavior
  6. Ethyl (Layer 6) • Distribution field • Spread, flow, equalization • Governs dispersal and systemic balance
  7. Heat (Layer 7) • Emotional field • Charge, escalation, volatility • Governs emotional physics and intensity gradients

Layer Ordering Logic The layers are ordered by: • subtle → dense • motion → meaning → structure → ignition → distribution → emotion • low‑pressure → high‑pressure • low symbolic density → high symbolic density This ordering is structural, not thematic.

Layer Relationships Vertical Relationships Each layer influences the one above and below it: • Drift softens into Mist • Mist refracts into Shimmer • Shimmer stabilizes into Stone • Stone ignites into Ember • Ember distributes into Ethyl • Ethyl intensifies into Heat Cross‑Layer Interactions Cross‑layer interactions occur when: • Drift meets Shimmer → motion‑meaning arcs • Mist meets Stone → soft‑structure gradients • Ember meets Heat → ignition‑emotion spikes • Ethyl meets Drift → distributed motion These interactions are defined in Layer Physics Index.md.

Layer Identity Rules A layer is defined by:

  1. Field Behavior
  2. Emotional Physics
  3. Symbolic Density
  4. Collapse Response
  5. Environmental Expression If an element does not express all five, it is not a layer.
