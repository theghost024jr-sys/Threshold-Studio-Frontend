What is the next entity in Threshold that should inherit or interact with this Mirage?

To keep the system coherent, the pivot needs to land on something that can hold the Mirage without collapsing its physics. That gives us a few structurally clean categories.

Species Pivot

A species pivot means the Mirage becomes part of a creature’s biology, cognition, or survival mechanic. The cleanest candidates are species that already deal with:

  • boundary instability
  • memory distortion
  • heat‑logic or catalytic behavior
  • recursive perception

This would turn the Mirage into a trait, a defense mechanism, or a communication mode.

Layer Pivot

A layer pivot means the Mirage becomes a cross‑layer phenomenon, altering how that layer behaves under heat or recursion. Good fits include:

  • Mist Layer — dissolution is native here, so the Mirage becomes a counterfeit version of Mist physics.
  • Stone Layer — the Mirage tests endurance instead of form.
  • Threshold Layer — the Mirage becomes a transition test rather than a dissolution test.

This would turn the Mirage into a layer‑wide rule.

Substrate Pivot

A substrate pivot means the Mirage becomes part of the physics spine — a rule that governs how signals behave.

Possible substrate anchors:

  • Symbolic Substrate — the Mirage becomes a distortion of meaning.
  • Narrative Substrate — the Mirage becomes a false ending or false collapse.
  • Emotional Substrate — the Mirage becomes a heat‑triggered emotional unmaking.

This would turn the Mirage into a law.

Idea / Concept Pivot

A conceptual pivot means the Mirage attaches to a core idea in the world, such as:

  • Alignment
  • Identity
  • Memory
  • Transition
  • Presence

This would turn the Mirage into a philosophical mechanic that characters and layers must interpret.
