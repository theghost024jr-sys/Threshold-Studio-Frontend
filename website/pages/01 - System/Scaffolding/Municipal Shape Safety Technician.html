Municipal Shape Safety Technician (MSST) A totally unnecessary but extremely committed local government role. A Municipal Shape Safety Technician is a low‑level town employee whose entire job is to: Core Duties • Monitor public spaces for unauthorized geometric shapes • Enforce local shape‑related ordinances (even if nobody remembers passing them) • Issue warnings for “suspicious angles” • Apply corrective tape to overly pointy corners • Confiscate contraband protractors, rulers, or pizza slices • File incident reports with titles like “Acute Angle Sighting, 3:14 PM” Qualifications • Zero formal geometry knowledge • A reflective vest • A clipboard • The ability to say “Not on my watch” with confidence • A deep, misplaced sense of civic duty Authority Level • None • But they act like they have a lot Typical Behavior • Overreacts to chalk drawings • Misidentifies squares as “stealth triangles” • Believes triangles are “trying to get in” • Thinks a parallelogram is “a rectangle that’s up to something” Why the Town Keeps Them • They’re harmless • They’re enthusiastic • And honestly, it’s easier to let them do it than explain geometry
