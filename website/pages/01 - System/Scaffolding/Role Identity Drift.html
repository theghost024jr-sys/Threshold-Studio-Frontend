00 Meta Systems — Roles Layer / Identity Cluster Role Identity Drift describes how a Role’s expression bends, warps, or shifts when the underlying identity of the actor (species, emotional state, environmental layer, or personal resonance) exerts pressure on the Role’s canonical form. It is the mechanism by which Roles become overexpressed, underexpressed, misaligned, or inverted under strain. Identity Drift is not failure — it is the adaptive behavior of a Role under real conditions.

Purpose Role Identity Drift defines: • how Roles change under emotional or environmental pressure • how identity influences Role expression • how drift leads to misalignment or collapse • how Roles stabilize or destabilize in different Layers • how Species amplify or dampen Role behavior • how Signals distort Role meaning This file is the bridge between Identity, Roles, and Signal Physics.

Primary Drift Modes

  1. Overexpression Drift The Role becomes exaggerated. • Guide becomes authoritarian • Witness becomes intrusive • Maker becomes obsessive • Trickster becomes destabilizing Driven by: Ember, Ethyl, high emotional charge.

  2. Underexpression Drift The Role collapses inward. • Keeper becomes avoidant • Warden becomes passive • Healer becomes depleted Driven by: Earth, Stone, emotional fatigue.

  3. Inversion Drift The Role flips into its shadow form. • Guide → Misleader • Witness → Absorber • Maker → Destroyer • Trickster → Saboteur Driven by: Mist, Ember–Mist boundary zones, identity fracture.

  4. Displacement Drift The Role shifts into a neighboring archetype. • Maker → Guide • Witness → Healer • Trickster → Maker Driven by: environmental mismatch, species resonance, relational pressure.

  5. Fragmentation Drift The Role splits into partial expressions. • inconsistent behavior • contradictory signals • unstable identity Driven by: Stone, high distortion, collapse precursors.

Identity Influence Identity determines how a Role drifts: • Species → sensory bias, emotional resonance • Elemental Alignment → environmental curvature • Signal Sensitivity → drift susceptibility • Emotional State → pressure vectors • History / Memory → resonance loops Identity Drift is the personal physics of a Role.

Environmental Influence Each Layer pushes Roles in predictable directions: • Air: clarity, underexpression • Earth: grounding, slow drift • Ember: intensity, overexpression • Mist: ambiguity, inversion • Stone: rigidity, fragmentation • Ethyl: symbolic bleed‑through, surreal drift This is where Role behavior becomes predictable.

Drift Thresholds A Role enters Drift when: • emotional load exceeds capacity • species sensitivity spikes • environmental curvature bends meaning • resonance loops amplify instability • signals distort or invert Drift is the early warning system for Role Collapse.

Role Collapse (Downstream) If Drift is unaddressed, Roles may: • invert fully • fracture • merge with neighboring roles • lose identity coherence • trigger threshold events This is where Collapse Signals activate.
