(The First Dog) Essence Shivvery is the first companion animal to appear in Threshold. He isn’t a pet — he’s a presence. A quiet, steady, emotionally attuned Border‑Chow mutt who shows up only when the world becomes stable enough to hold companionship. He is the signal that the Settlement Zone is real. Origin Born from the blend of: • Border Collie — attention‑reader, motion‑tracker • Chow — grounded, loyal, slow‑forming but deep bonds Shivvery carries both instincts: • He senses drift before it forms • He anchors overwhelmed players • He stays close without clinging He is the first dog because he chooses to be. Appearance • Medium build, thick coat with a soft mane • Tail with a slight curl • Eyes that track mood as much as movement • Colors: smoky gray, dusk brown, muted black • A faint shimmer in the fur when collapse weather is near He looks like he belongs to a world that’s half‑wild, half‑settled. Temperament • Quiet, observant, steady • Protective without aggression • Bonds slowly but permanently • Sensitive to emotional fields • Avoids high‑drift pockets • Sits beside players who are losing coherence He’s the kind of dog who doesn’t bark — he notices. Behavior in the Settlement Zone • Patrols the perimeter in slow arcs • Sleeps near warm stone or shallow water • Watches the horizon more than the settlement • Nudges players toward calmer areas • Stands still when collapse weather is forming He’s a living stabilizer. Symbolic Markers • A soft tremor in the air around him when drift rises • A low rumble in his chest when danger approaches • A stillness that spreads outward like calm water • A faint glow around his silhouette at dusk These markers make him recognizable even in low light. Role in the System Shivvery is: • the first relational being • the first sign of emotional safety • the first companion who chooses the player • the first creature that bridges internal and external physics He is the emotional anchor of Fib 3–5.
