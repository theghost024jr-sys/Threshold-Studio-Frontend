Essence A Stone Basin is an environment defined by weight, containment, and slow accumulation. It is a natural depression carved into stone where pressure gathers, sound deepens, and emotional resonance sinks into a heavy, grounded stillness. A Stone Basin is not a hollow. It is a vessel the world carved to hold what sinks. Environmental Physics • Visibility: Clear, unobstructed; walls curve inward • Sound Behavior: Deepened; echoes return slower and lower • Pressure: Downward and inward; gravity feels slightly heavier • Stability: Very high; stone absorbs turbulence • Temperature: Cool, steady, rarely fluctuates Field Dynamics • Movement slows as one descends into the Basin • Emotional resonance pools at the center • Memory settles into stone layers, forming sedimented impressions • Light bends slightly inward, creating a soft central focus Phenomena

  1. Weightfall A subtle increase in perceived gravity near the center. Often mistaken for fatigue or emotional heaviness.
  2. Echo Bowl Sound curves along the stone walls, returning in softened, rounded tones. Used by Guides to read the Basin’s emotional load.
  3. Sediment Memory Layers of stone hold impressions of past events. Touching them can trigger: • grounded clarity • emotional heaviness • slow, steady recall
  4. Stillwater Bloom If water collects in the Basin, it becomes unnaturally still. Reflects emotional states with uncanny accuracy. Flora & Fauna • Stoneforms: Anchor species; reinforce the Basin’s stability • Basin Deer: Move slowly; rest in the lowest point • Quiet Lizards: Absorb heat from stone; rarely leave • Mosslights: Grow in cracks; glow faintly in high‑memory zones Emotional Physics • Vector: Downward → Settle • Resonance: Heavy coherence • Effect: Grounds emotional turbulence; deepens introspection • Instability: Over‑weighting; sinking into emotional heaviness • Signature: A dense, centered pressure behind the sternum Role Interaction • Guide Roles: Use Basins to stabilize group resonance • Trickster Roles: Diminished; weight dampens misdirection • Healer Roles: Thrive; grounding amplifies restorative work • Wanderer Roles: Risk sinking too deeply into stillness Drift Behavior • Early Drift: Steps feel heavier; breath deepens • Mid Drift: Emotional weight accumulates; clarity becomes dense • Late Drift: Identity sinks inward; movement slows dramatically • Collapse Mode: Basin‑sink — emotional immobility wrapped in stone stillness Mythic Fragment

They say the first Stone Basin formed when the world needed a place to set down what it could no longer carry.

The stone curved inward, the weight settled, and the earth learned how to hold heaviness gently.
