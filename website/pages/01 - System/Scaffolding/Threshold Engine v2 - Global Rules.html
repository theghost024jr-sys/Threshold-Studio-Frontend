THRESHOLD ENGINE v2 — GLOBAL RULES

1. RULE OF COHERENCE

Nothing in Threshold may contradict the global logic. Local systems may be complex, emergent, or unpredictable — but never incoherent.

2. RULE OF CAUSALITY

Every state change must have a cause. No spontaneous events. No narrative overrides. No “because the engine said so.”

3. RULE OF ISOLATION

Subsystems cannot mutate each other’s internals. All interaction must occur through defined interfaces. No hidden channels. No backdoors.

4. RULE OF TRACEABILITY

Every subsystem must expose its state and transitions. Nothing is allowed to be opaque. If it changes, it must be observable.

5. RULE OF STABILITY

Threshold cannot enter undefined or contradictory states. If a subsystem fails, the substrate stabilizes — it does not collapse.
