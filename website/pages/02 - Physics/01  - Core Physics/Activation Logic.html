[[Invariant00 – Cohesion Under Pressure]]

Definition Activation Logic defines the conditions under which vectors begin operating within the Field. It determines when force enters motion, which vectors engage, and how the Consequence Engine interprets the event. Activation is not random. Activation is causal.

Purpose To ensure that: • vectors only fire when appropriate • the system does not activate without cause • motion is always interpretable • consequences arise from real events • the Field remains stable until acted upon Activation Logic prevents noise and guarantees meaningful motion.

Activation Conditions A vector activates when one or more of the following conditions occur:

  1. Invocation A role, artifact, or system is explicitly called into action. Examples: • a role is assumed • a lineage is invoked • a gift is initiated • a threshold is approached Invocation is deliberate activation.

  2. Contact Two or more forces, roles, or artifacts interact. Examples: • giver meets receiver • role meets zone • lineage meets drift • threshold meets actor Contact activates relational vectors.

  3. Imbalance A state becomes unstable or unresolved. Examples: • neglect • tension • unmet obligation • unresolved drift Imbalance activates Drift and Resolution vectors.

  4. Transfer Value, attention, or force moves from one node to another. Examples: • giving • receiving • exchanging • yielding Transfer activates Reciprocity and Gift vectors.

  5. Crossing A boundary, threshold, or state change occurs. Examples: • entering a new zone • shifting roles • transitioning states • completing a cycle Crossing activates Threshold vectors.

  6. Observation A node becomes aware of another node or force. Examples: • recognition • acknowledgment • witnessing Observation activates Lineage and Identity vectors.

Activation Sequence When an activation condition is met, the system follows this sequence:

  1. Detect Event The Field registers the activation condition.
  2. Identify Vector(s) Determine which vectors are relevant to the event.
  3. Apply Invariants Stabilize and constrain the vector’s behavior.
  4. Generate Motion The Consequence Engine produces the appropriate outcome.
  5. Update Matrix The Matrix of Force records the new state.
  6. Resolve or Propagate Motion either resolves or becomes new input for further activation.

Activation Properties Causal Activation requires a real event. Nothing fires without cause. Contextual Activation depends on the state of the Field, roles, and zones. Deterministic Given the same conditions, activation produces the same result. Conserved Activation always leads to motion that must resolve or propagate. Non‑spontaneous Vectors do not activate on their own.

Function Activation Logic: • prevents arbitrary motion • ensures vectors fire only when appropriate • maintains coherence across the Field • drives the Consequence Engine • keeps the Matrix dynamic but stable • ensures every action has a clear cause This is the ignition system of the System of Force.
