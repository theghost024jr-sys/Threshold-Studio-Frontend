[[Invariant00 – Cohesion Under Pressure]]

Invariant Anchor: This module expresses how [module] behaves when cohesion is under pressure.

[[Resonance Loops]]

A collapse is not a single event. It is a progression with distinct thresholds. Each threshold has its own physics, its own signals, and its own recovery logic. Think of it as a four‑stage descent with a fifth-stage fork.

  1. Drift Definition: The system begins to deviate from its intended pattern, but the deviation is still correctable without cost. Characteristics: • Minor inconsistencies • Latency, hesitation, misalignment • Early warning signals • No structural damage yet Recovery: A single corrective action restores alignment.

  2. Distortion Definition: The deviation now alters the pattern. The system is still functional, but the distortion is self-reinforcing. Characteristics: • Feedback loops start to warp • Roles misfire • Gifts invert or stall • Emotional physics begin to skew Recovery: Requires intentional intervention. Cost is introduced: time, energy, or relational repair.

  3. Collapse Definition: The system can no longer maintain its intended form. Function persists only in fragments. Characteristics: • Core logic breaks • Signals become unreliable • Behaviors become reactive instead of generative • The system cannot self-correct Recovery: Requires external stabilization or a reset. Cost is significant.

  4. Rupture Definition: The system’s architecture fails. The collapse becomes identity-level rather than functional. Characteristics: • Roles dissolve • Gifts invert into their shadows • Boundaries fail • The system becomes unsafe to itself or others Recovery: Requires containment, reconstruction, or replacement. Cost is existential.

  5. Fork: Renewal or Fragmentation Once rupture occurs, the system cannot return to its previous state. It must choose one of two paths: A. Renewal • A new architecture is built • Old patterns are composted • The system re-emerges with new physics B. Fragmentation • The system splits • Subsystems operate independently • Coherence is lost • Future collapse becomes more likely This fork is the true end-state of collapse sequencing.

The Full Sequence (Compact Form)

  1. Drift → harmless deviation
  2. Distortion → pattern warps
  3. Collapse → function breaks
  4. Rupture → identity breaks
  5. Fork → renewal or fragmentation This is the universal pattern. Every system follows it — biological, emotional, architectural, relational, mechanical, mythic.
