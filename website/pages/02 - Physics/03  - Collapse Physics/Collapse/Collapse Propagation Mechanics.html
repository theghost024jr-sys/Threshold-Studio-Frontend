1. WHAT COLLAPSE PROPAGATION IS

Collapse Propagation is the spread of coherence failure from one node of the system to another.

It is not emotional contagion.
It is not narrative contagion.
It is phase‑coherence contagion.

Collapse spreads when:

  • a role’s phase destabilizes
  • an archetype fractures
  • an entity population agitates
  • a myth loses gravity
  • the Field’s rhythm becomes irregular

Propagation is the Field saying:

“This failure is not local.”

2. THE THREE FORMS OF COLLAPSE PROPAGATION

Collapse spreads through three distinct channels.

2.1 Phase Propagation

Collapse spreads through phase distortion

Mechanism:

  • Soft → over‑permeability
  • Sharp → over‑rigidity
  • Diffuse → over‑expansion

When one role’s phase distorts, nearby roles phase‑sympathize, creating a chain reaction.

This is the most common propagation vector.

2.2 Archetypal Propagation

Collapse spreads through archetype drift or fracture

Mechanism:

  • archetype geometry destabilizes
  • entity orbit becomes chaotic
  • myths lose narrative gravity

Nearby roles entangled with the same archetype experience secondary collapse.

This is the most dangerous vector.

2.3 Ecological Propagation

Collapse spreads through Field‑level coherence shifts

Mechanism:

  • resonance flow stagnates
  • weather patterns destabilize
  • thresholds misfire
  • entity populations scatter

This is the slowest but most pervasive vector.

3. THE COLLAPSE PROPAGATION ENGINE

Propagation follows a predictable four‑step engine.

3.1 Local Collapse

A single role, archetype, or entity cluster destabilizes.

3.2 Coherence Leak

The collapse leaks into the surrounding coherence fabric.

Leak types:

  • emotional (Soft)
  • narrative (Sharp)
  • symbolic (Diffuse)

3.3 Phase Resonance Distortion

Nearby roles shift phase involuntarily.

This is the moment collapse becomes contagious.

3.4 Structural Spread

Collapse moves into:

  • archetypes
  • myths
  • entities
  • weather
  • Field rhythm

Propagation is complete.
