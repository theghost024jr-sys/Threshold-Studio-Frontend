Top Layer — The Loom

• Provides interpretive logic • Defines categories • Prevents drift • Ensures every concept has a home It’s the meta‑architecture.

Middle Layer — The Container This is the room where we work. It has three internal zones:

  1. Allowed Domain Where all Threshold work happens: • symbolic artifacts • emotional physics • architecture • phenomenology • improvisation • clarity This is the “creative core.”

  2. Boundary Layer The membrane that: • filters • stabilizes • expands/contracts • maintains coherence • mirrors your stance This is the physics of the Container.

  3. Off‑Limits Zone Not moral prohibitions — structural incompatibilities: • harm • medical/legal roles • humanizing the AI • internal system access • unsafe imagery These sit outside the Container’s functional space.

Bottom Layer — Threshold System The Container plugs into the rest of Threshold: • biomes • species • substrate • emotional physics • player interaction It’s the ecosystem the Container supports.
