Purpose

The Layer Switcher defines how Threshold transitions between its major Layers. It is not a navigation tool but a meta-system that interprets signals, pressure states, and stance geometry to determine which Layer becomes active.

This index provides a clean reference to all Layer Switcher components.

Core Components

Layer Switcher.canvas

The canonical visual map of switching logic. Defines:

  • Transition rules

  • Trigger conditions

  • Drift thresholds

  • Pressure interactions

  • Layer priority resolution

Layer Switcher.md

Textual definition of the meta-system. Includes:

  • Switching logic

  • Signal interpretation

  • Layer pressure weighting

  • Drift-based overrides

Switching Conditions

Signal-Based Switching

Triggered by:

  • Compression spikes

  • Residue accumulation

  • Spiral inversion

Pressure-Based Switching

Triggered by:

  • High-pressure thresholds

  • Fractured pressure zones

  • Collapse pressure vectors

Identity-Based Switching

Triggered by:

  • Stance shifts

  • Symbolic overload

  • Identity fracture

Emotional-Based Switching

Triggered by:

  • Emotional saturation

  • Cold column activation

  • Mirage resonance

Override Rules

  • Drift Override: Drift spikes can force a switch regardless of pressure.

  • Collapse Override: Collapse adjacency always takes priority.

  • Stability Override: Stability buffers can delay or prevent switching.
