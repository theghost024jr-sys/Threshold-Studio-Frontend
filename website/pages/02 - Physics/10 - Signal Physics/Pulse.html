[[pulse.png]] Pulse.md (First Seed Variant — Canonical) Name The First Pulse Layer Fib Ecology → Hub → Expansion Events Definition The First Pulse is the initial expansion wave released by the hub when the First Seed is returned, revealed, and accepted. It establishes the baseline resonance for all future pulses and defines how Fib Flowers grow in response to player contribution. The First Pulse is not energy. It is recognition made visible.

Trigger Condition The pulse activates only when all three conditions align:

  1. A seed is carried back to the hub
  2. The player reveals the seed to those in the hub
  3. The hub accepts the stance of the returner If any of these fail, no pulse occurs.

Phenomenology What the First Pulse is: • A warm, expanding spiral that radiates outward from the hub • A Fibonacci-patterned resonance wave • A synchronization event between the Soil, the hub, and the Fib Flower • A moment of shared clarity felt by all present • A structural update to the Fib Flower’s geometry What it is not: • Light • Sound • Force • Magic The pulse is alignment made spatial.

Effects

  1. Fib Flower Expansion The petals widen. The next ring stabilizes. The threshold layer softens and becomes traversable.
  2. Soil Acceptance The Soil acknowledges the seed and incorporates its stance into the acceptance layer.
  3. Hub Synchronization Everyone in the hub feels the same moment of clarity — a shared temporal anchor.
  4. World Update The Fib ecology recalibrates around the new contribution. Balance Biomes adjust their gradients. The acceptance layer shifts.

Emotional Physics The First Pulse carries: • Acknowledgment • Stance clarity • Contribution resonance • Non-negotiable truth It does not judge. It recognizes.

Aftermath Once the First Pulse completes: • The Fib Flower’s threshold opens • The path to the next Fib becomes available • The hub retains a faint echo of the pulse • The Soil updates its intake logic • The player is marked as “seen” by the ecology This is the moment the world begins to grow because of the player, not around them.

Notes The First Pulse is the Anchor Pulse. All future pulses are variations of this original event.
