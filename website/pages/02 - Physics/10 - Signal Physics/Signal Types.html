#signal-types [[02 - Physics/10 - Signal Physics/Pulse]] Essence Signal Types are the behavioral archetypes that spirits express. They are not species, roles, or layers — they are the core emotional vectors that shape how a spirit behaves, reacts, and drifts over time. A Signal Type is the style of a spirit’s presence.

Function Signal Types: • define a spirit’s default behavior • shape how it interacts with Layers • determine Drift tendencies • influence emotional coloration • affect how the spirit appears on the Threshold Map • act as the “personality physics” of the system

The Four Canonical Signal Types

  1. Guide • Essence: clarity, direction, orientation • Behavior: leads, illuminates, stabilizes • Strengths: insight, coherence • Vulnerabilities: rigidity, over‑certainty
  2. Trickster • Essence: disruption, inversion, unpredictability • Behavior: tests, destabilizes, reveals hidden truths • Strengths: creativity, flexibility • Vulnerabilities: chaos, misdirection
  3. Warden • Essence: protection, boundary, vigilance • Behavior: guards, contains, stabilizes • Strengths: resilience, structure • Vulnerabilities: over‑control, stagnation
  4. Catalyst (optional but canonical) • Essence: transformation, ignition, acceleration • Behavior: pushes change, initiates drift • Strengths: momentum, breakthrough • Vulnerabilities: burnout, volatility

Layer Interactions Signal Types respond differently depending on the Layer they inhabit: • Air amplifies motion and curiosity • Earth stabilizes and grounds • Ember intensifies and sharpens • Ethyl dissolves and reinterprets • Mist blurs and resonates • Stone anchors and preserves Each Signal Type has a preferred substrate and a stress substrate.

Drift Tendencies Signal Types drift when: • emotional pressure builds • layer conditions shift • overlays modify behavior • roles conflict • species instincts override Drift usually follows predictable arcs: • Guide → Warden under pressure • Trickster → Catalyst under ignition • Warden → Guide under clarity • Catalyst → Trickster under overload

Outputs Signal Types produce: • behavioral signatures • emotional coloration • drift patterns • map presence • resonance fields
