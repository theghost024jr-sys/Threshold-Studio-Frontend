These are not gradients — they are distinct states with thresholds, behaviors, and consequences.

  1. Stable Soil Condition: Balanced, aligned, metabolizing residue cleanly Behavior: • Environment behaves predictably • NPCs act according to their core logic • Collapse thresholds are high • Recovery is fast Player experience: Everything feels “right,” even if the environment is harsh.

  2. Enriched Soil Condition: Surplus of coherent residue Behavior: • Environment becomes more expressive • Signals are clearer • NPCs gain nuance and depth • Growth accelerates Player experience: The world feels alive, generous, responsive. Cause: Aligned actions, truth, rhythm, patience.

  3. Thin Soil Condition: Not enough residue to support full environmental behavior Behavior: • NPCs become brittle or simplistic • Environment loses detail • Collapse thresholds drop • Recovery slows Player experience: The world feels “hollow,” like something is missing. Cause: Neglect, contradiction, lack of engagement.

  4. Hardened Soil Condition: Accumulated contradictions compacted under pressure Behavior: • Environment becomes rigid • NPCs become inflexible or defensive • Signals distort • Collapse becomes sharp and sudden Player experience: Everything feels tense, resistant, unforgiving. Cause: Lies, incoherence, forced actions, broken geometry.

  5. Fractured Soil Condition: Structural break in the environment’s memory layer Behavior: • NPC behavior splits or desynchronizes • Environment produces glitches or discontinuities • Echoes misalign (in Spiral) • Heat signatures scatter (in Lattice) • Boundaries dissolve unpredictably (in Mist) Player experience: Reality feels unstable but not yet catastrophic. Cause: Overpressure, emotional rupture, unresolved contradictions.

  6. Corrupted Soil Condition: Foreign residue or incompatible physics introduced Behavior: • Environment behaves against its own nature • NPCs inherit impossible traits • Signals invert or misfire • Collapse becomes inevitable Player experience: The world feels “wrong,” uncanny, hostile to its own rules. Cause: Cross‑environment contamination, forced role imposition, broken lineage.

  7. Dead Soil Condition: No memory, no residue, no inheritance Behavior: • Environment cannot grow • NPCs cannot form • Collapse is permanent • Only Null environments can recover from this Player experience: Silence. Absence. A void where an environment used to be. Cause: Total collapse, erasure, or intentional reset.

HOW THESE STATES EXPRESS DIFFERENTLY BY SOIL TYPE A few examples to show the architecture: Compressed Soil (Spiral, Stone Basin) • Hardened → becomes dangerous • Fractured → produces sharp collapse • Corrupted → creates impossible pressure loops Luminous Soil (Branch, Lattice) • Enriched → brightens, reveals truth • Hardened → dims, distorts signals • Corrupted → inverts truth/lie detection Dissolved Soil (Mist, Marsh) • Thin → becomes foggy and disorienting • Hardened → rare but catastrophic (identity freeze) • Fractured → creates identity bleed Inheritance Soil (Ruins, Stone Layer) • Enriched → lineage strengthens • Hardened → old contradictions dominate • Corrupted → ancestral memory becomes hostile Every soil type expresses the same seven states, but the behavior is environment‑specific.
