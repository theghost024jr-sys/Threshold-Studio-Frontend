The structural and dynamic map of spiral drift across Threshold, detailing curvature mechanics, collapse alignment, resonance distortion, and pressure‑driven spiral formation.

Purpose Spiral Drift Topology explains the curved movement patterns that emerge when drift interacts with: • nonlinear pressure • distorted resonance • collapse vectors • boundary inversion • lineage rupture Spiral drift is not a subtype — it is a topological behavior that reshapes environments, identities, signals, and collapse physics.

The Four Spiral Forms Spiral drift appears in four canonical forms, each with distinct curvature and failure modes.

  1. Outward Spiral Drift Curvature expands away from the center. Mechanics: • pressure release • resonance thinning • boundary expansion Effects: • drift dispersal • identity diffusion • collapse suppression Failure Mode: Spiral dissipation → oscillation drift

  2. Inward Spiral Drift Curvature collapses toward a center. Mechanics: • pressure compression • resonance curvature • collapse vector alignment Effects: • drift concentration • identity compression • collapse well formation Failure Mode: Collapse ignition

  3. Lateral Spiral Drift Curvature moves sideways across environments. Mechanics: • pressure shear • resonance bending • drift‑pressure coupling Effects: • corridor deformation • boundary wobble • signal curvature Failure Mode: Recursive drift

  4. Helical Drift Three‑dimensional spiral movement across layers. Mechanics: • vertical drift propagation • layer‑stack curvature • resonance inversion Effects: • identity recursion • collapse identity formation • worldphase instability Failure Mode: Systemic collapse

Spiral Formation Mechanics Spiral drift forms through three primary mechanisms:

  1. Pressure Curvature • compression → inward spirals • release → outward spirals • shear → lateral spirals Pressure is the primary driver of curvature.

  2. Resonance Distortion • distorted resonance → spiral amplification • inversion → helical drift • voids → collapse spirals Resonance determines spiral stability.

  3. Collapse Alignment • collapse wells pull spirals inward • collapse identity creates helical drift • absence wells create catastrophic spirals Collapse determines spiral direction.

Spiral Propagation Patterns Spiral drift propagates through:

  1. Drift Corridors Spirals deform or override existing corridors.
  2. Pressure Fronts Spirals ride pressure gradients.
  3. Collapse Corridors Spirals accelerate toward collapse wells.
  4. Rupture Lines Spirals follow lineage fractures.

Spiral Drift and the Layer Stack Spiral drift moves vertically through layers: • Mythic → helical spirals • Identity → boundary curvature • Role → vector curvature • Signal → harmonic curvature • Species → behavioral curvature • Environment → spatial curvature • Worldstate → global spiral conditions Helical drift is the layer‑stack expression of spiral topology.

Spiral Drift Failure Modes Spiral drift destabilizes the system when: • curvature accelerates • spirals merge • collapse wells deepen • resonance inverts • lineage ruptures propagate This produces: • recursive drift • collapse identity • drift storms • worldphase fracture • long‑cycle instability
