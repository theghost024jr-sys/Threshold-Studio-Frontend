  1. COMPONENT DEFINITIONS 3.1 Binding Law Stability (BLS) Measures how closely entangled nodes adhere to their relational rules. Inputs include: • deviation magnitude • deviation rate • oscillation amplitude • oscillation damping time Low BLS → entanglements are under strain.

3.2 Phase Coherence (PC) Measures temporal alignment across entangled nodes. Inputs include: • phase lag • phase flicker frequency • phase velocity mismatch • double‑shadow events Low PC → phase mismatch cascade risk.

3.3 Corridor Dynamics Stability (CDS) Measures drift, vector, and freeze behavior. Inputs include: • drift velocity variance • vector shear frequency • corridor temperature • micro‑seizure events Low CDS → corridor‑based cascade risk.

3.4 Pressure & Resonance Harmony (PRH) Measures load distribution and meaning stability. Inputs include: • pressure gradient smoothness • sink saturation • resonance harmonics • crystallization specks Low PRH → mirror or rigidity cascade risk.

3.5 Time Organ Responsiveness (TOR) Measures how effectively the Time Organ modulates the system. Inputs include: • pulse cycle stability • elasticity modulation • depth absorption rate • momentum smoothing Low TOR → reduced recovery capacity.
