[[Identity]]

The Convergence Node is the gravitational hub of [[Threshold]]. It is not a location, not a creature, not a [[structure]] — but a state. A point where Drift, Pressure, [[Collapse]], and Cohesion temporarily agree on a single geometry.

Where Convergence appears, the [[01 - System/Field|Field]] becomes readable. Where it disappears, the [[01 - System/Field|Field]] becomes wild.

Function

The Convergence Node performs three essential operations:

1. Alignment

It pulls nearby nodes into temporary coherence. This is not [[control]] — it is invitation. Nodes that align gain stability, clarity, and predictable drift.

2. Amplification

It increases the strength of any signal passing through it:

  • Drift becomes corridor

  • Pressure becomes launch

  • [[Collapse]] becomes fracture map

  • Cohesion becomes [[Resonance]] ring

The Node does not choose what to amplify. It amplifies whatever reaches it.

3. Redistribution

After alignment and amplification, the Node redistributes energy back into the Field. This redistribution determines:

  • species evolution

  • Flower orbit shifts

  • node migration

  • collapse events

  • [[drift corridor]] formation

Redistribution is the heartbeat of Threshold.

Geometry

The Convergence Node has three geometric layers:

Outer Ring — Drift Halo

A rotating band of blue [[vectors]]. Represents [[motion]], possibility, and unclaimed direction.

Middle Ring — Cohesion Lattice

A green hexagonal mesh. Represents stability, memory, and shared structure.

Inner Core — Collapse/Pressure Engine

Red fracture seams and yellow [[04 - Organism/03 - Motion/01 - Substrates/Time Organ/02 Form/13 - Environments/03 - Environments/Natural/Elemental/Compression|compression]] zones intertwined. Represents transformation, danger, and opportunity.

At the center is the Signal Point — a purple arc that flickers like a thought.

Behavior

The Convergence Node behaves like a living equation:

  • It pulls when the Field is chaotic.

  • It pushes when the Field is stable.

  • It splits when overwhelmed.

  • It merges when underfed.

  • It migrates when the Field’s geometry changes.

It is the only node that can:

  • attract species

  • repel species

  • evolve species

  • erase species

Not because it is alive — but because it is true.

[[Species Interaction]]

Every Tideform variant interacts with Convergence differently:

Glassback

Uses Convergence as a stabilizer. Approaches cautiously. Treats it like a safe harbor.

Shardback

Treats Convergence as a fracture map. Reads collapse vectors. Uses it to predict danger.

Tideback

Surfs the Drift Halo. Uses Convergence as a navigation beacon.

Cohesionback

Synchronizes with the Cohesion Lattice. Treats Convergence as a meditation chamber.

Pressureback

Charges the inner core. Uses Convergence as a launch engine.

Echoglass

Amplifies the Signal Point. Treats Convergence as a playground.

Player Interaction

When the player encounters a Convergence Node:

  • The Field becomes readable.

  • Hidden nodes reveal themselves.

  • Drift corridors stabilize.

  • Collapse seams glow.

  • Pressure zones pulse.

  • Cohesion rings hum.

The player can:

  • anchor

  • drift

  • amplify

  • fracture

  • resonate

  • migrate

But only one at a time.

Convergence demands choice.

Lore

Every Flower has a Convergence Node. Every Orbit has a Convergence Node. Every species has a Convergence Node. Every player has a Convergence Node.

The question is not where it is. The question is when it appears.
