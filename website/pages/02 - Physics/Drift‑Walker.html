Drift‑Walker

Category: 06 – Mind Color: #FF4DA6 (Identity Pink) Role: A being who perceives, interprets, and navigates the Drift Layer with zero disturbance, using micro‑pressure, time‑particle behavior, and directional temperament fields.

A Drift‑Walker is not a class, species, or role. It is a state of being — a cognitive‑somatic alignment that allows an entity to:

  • read Drift currents

  • sense micro‑pressure gradients

  • stabilize zones simply by passing through

  • move without generating agitation

  • perceive time‑particle behavior directly

Drift‑Walkers are rare because the state requires:

  • low internal turbulence

  • stable identity core

  • high signal‑clarity

  • non‑reactive boundaries

  • directional neutrality

They are the quietest possible presence inside Threshold.
