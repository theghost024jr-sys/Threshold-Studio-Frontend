[[Invariant00 - Cohesion Under Pressure]]

Flower Behavior

Invariant Anchor This module expresses how Flowers behave when cohesion is under pressure. See: [[Invariant00 – Cohesion Under Pressure]]

Definition

A Flower is a large‑scale, self‑stabilizing organism formed from clusters whose cohesion has synchronized into a shared identity basin. A Flower is not a collection of nodes — it is a coherence field with structure, metabolism, and behavior.

Flowers regulate collapse, distribute pressure, and maintain the Garden’s large‑scale stability.

Core Behavioral Laws

1. Flowers maintain a global cohesion field

A Flower generates a cohesion gradient that:

  • pulls aligned clusters inward

  • pushes contradictions outward

  • stabilizes identity at the core

  • regulates collapse at the boundary

This field is the Flower’s “body.”

2. Flowers collapse in zones, not uniformly

Flower collapse follows a predictable pattern:

  • boundary clusters destabilize first

  • mid‑layer clusters enter drift

  • core clusters resist collapse the longest

  • collapse propagates as a zone‑based wave

This is why Flowers have collapse maps, not collapse events.

3. Flowers resonate across the Garden

Flowers emit resonance patterns that:

  • synchronize nearby Flowers

  • attract compatible clusters

  • repel contradictory structures

  • shape migration currents

Resonance is the Flower’s communication and influence.

4. Flowers regulate migration

A Flower’s pressure field determines:

  • which nodes can enter

  • which clusters can remain

  • which contradictions are expelled

  • which migrations trigger collapse

Migration is the Flower’s immune system.

5. Flowers form roles when cohesion becomes directional

When a Flower’s cohesion aligns around a stable axis:

  • roles emerge

  • identity sharpens

  • collapse thresholds rise

  • signal propagation increases

Roles are the Flower’s functional differentiation.

6. Flowers propagate signal

Flowers emit signals that:

  • coordinate cluster behavior

  • trigger activation logic

  • stabilize identity

  • influence Garden‑scale dynamics

Signal is the Flower’s metabolism.

Behavior Across the Origin Cycle

Distortion

Flower boundaries wobble; identity becomes noisy.

Drift

Clusters shift positions; cohesion redistributes.

Field

The Flower expands; resonance weakens.

Collapse

Zones fail; contradictions eject; migration spikes.

Identity

A new core forms; cohesion condenses.

Role

Directional cohesion emerges; Flower stabilizes.

Signal

The Flower broadcasts its new identity to the Garden.

Flower Types (structural, not lore)

  • Core Flowers — high stability, low drift

  • Boundary Flowers — high contradiction, high migration

  • Transitional Flowers — mediate pressure between regions

  • Collapse Flowers — unstable, short‑lived, high‑pressure

  • Resonant Flowers — synchronize large regions of the Garden

These types emerge from pressure patterns, not classification.

Summary Rule

A Flower is a coherence organism that:

  • maintains a global cohesion field

  • collapses in zones

  • resonates across the Garden

  • regulates migration

  • forms roles

  • propagates signal

Flowers are the Garden’s macro‑scale stabilizers.
