Initiation Engine — The Ignition Protocol of Threshold

The Initiation Engine is the protocol that governs how any entity in Threshold transitions from:

  • Dormant → Active

  • Undefined → Defined

  • Potential → Behavior

It is the first mechanical layer of every node, Flower, cluster, and system. The Initiation Engine ensures that activation is structured, predictable, and aligned with the system’s resonance and pressure profile.

Core Components

1. Pre‑Ignition Scan

The system evaluates:

  • local resonance

  • pressure load

  • drift vectors

  • polarity (Crown/Seam)

  • environmental cues

Links: [[Node Dynamics]] • [[Pressure System]] • [[Drift System]]

2. Identity Lock

The entity’s identity signature is locked into place.

Links: [[01 - System/Scaffolding/01 - Roles/Archetypal Roles/Echo]] • [[EchoNode State Machine]]

3. Resonance Alignment

The entity aligns with its immediate environment.

Links: [[Harmony Emergence]] • [[Echo Protocol]]

4. Activation Pulse

A short, high‑intensity pulse that transitions the entity into active state.

Links: [[Core Pulse]] • [[02 - Physics/Signal Types]]

5. Drift Stabilization

Initial drift vectors are dampened or redirected.

Links: [[Drift System]] • [[Cross-Flower Migration]]

Initiation Engine State Machine

markdown

stateDiagram-v2
    [*] --> PreIgnition

    PreIgnition --> IdentityLock: scan_complete()
    PreIgnition --> Abort: instability_detected()

    IdentityLock --> ResonanceAlign: identity_fixed()
    IdentityLock --> Abort: signature_conflict()

    ResonanceAlign --> ActivationPulse: alignment_success()
    ResonanceAlign --> Abort: alignment_fail()

    ActivationPulse --> DriftStabilize: pulse_emitted()
    ActivationPulse --> Abort: pulse_failure()

    DriftStabilize --> Active: drift_resolved()
    DriftStabilize --> Abort: drift_unstable()

    Abort --> PreIgnition: retry_possible()
    Abort --> [*]: failure_terminal()
