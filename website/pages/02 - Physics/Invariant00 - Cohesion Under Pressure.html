Invariant00 — Cohesion Under Pressure

Definition Cohesion is the degree to which a structure remains itself under pressure. Pressure is any force that tests, redistributes, or destabilizes cohesion.

Law All systems, subsystems, entities, processes, and maps in Threshold behave according to how cohesion responds to pressure.

Behavioral Rule When pressure increases, cohesion redistributes toward aligned structure and away from contradiction. When cohesion falls below viability, collapse occurs. When cohesion condenses, identity forms. When cohesion becomes directional, role emerges. When cohesion propagates, signal is produced.

Scope This invariant governs:

  • nodes

  • clusters

  • Flowers

  • migration

  • collapse ecology

  • activation logic

  • spatial physics

  • binding

  • sequencing

  • meta‑systems

  • the Garden itself

Constraint No module may define behavior that contradicts the principle that cohesion under pressure determines stability, identity, collapse, and transformation.
