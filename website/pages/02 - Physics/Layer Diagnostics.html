Layer Diagnostics

Layer Diagnostics provides the operational framework for detecting, interpreting, and classifying Layer‑level anomalies, drift patterns, pressure imbalances, and collapse adjacency.
It is the primary tool for real‑time assessment of Layer stability across the Threshold system.

Diagnostics do not predict.
They recognize — with precision, immediacy, and structural clarity.

Purpose

  • Identify Layer instability and drift onset
  • Detect pressure anomalies and resonance distortion
  • Classify cross‑Layer leakage and boundary failures
  • Support collapse early detection and reversal
  • Provide a unified diagnostic language across all Layers

Diagnostic Domains

1. Pressure Diagnostics

Measures pressure behavior within and across Layers.

Indicators:

  • pressure spikes
  • pressure inversion
  • pressure stagnation
  • pressure bleed

Interpretation:

  • Stone → overload
  • Mist → diffusion
  • Root → compression
  • Glass → reflection
  • Ash → fracture
  • Air → vector amplification
  • Ethyl → curvature distortion

2. Boundary Diagnostics

Assesses the integrity of Layer boundaries.

Indicators:

  • thinning
  • permeability shift
  • boundary echo
  • fracture lines
  • recursive bleed

Boundary failure is the earliest sign of Layer Drift.

3. Resonance Diagnostics

Reads resonance behavior within the Layer.

Indicators:

  • coherence
  • dissonance
  • amplification
  • distortion
  • resonance lock

Resonance anomalies often precede collapse signatures.

4. Drift Diagnostics

Tracks drift behavior across Layer physics.

Indicators:

  • vector misalignment
  • curvature drift
  • emotional bleed
  • memory displacement
  • drift scattering

Drift diagnostics cross‑link directly to [[Layer Drift]].

5. Collapse Adjacency Diagnostics

Detects collapse proximity through Layer‑specific markers.

Indicators:

  • fracture geometry
  • residue formation
  • turbulence pockets
  • shimmer onset
  • environmental distortion
