The Elemental Overlay System is a parallel classification axis that describes the energetic flavor of a signal. It does not replace Layers, Species, or Signal Types. Instead, it adds a secondary dimension that influences how a signal behaves, feels, and interacts within Threshold. Overlays are meta‑tags, not habitats. A signal’s Layer determines where it lives. A signal’s Overlay determines how it moves.

Purpose The overlay system provides: • energetic clustering across species • emotional “temperature” for each signal • resonance families and ritual groupings • a way to tune or reinterpret signals without altering core identity • a second axis for mapping Threshold’s constellation Every spirit animal has: • Layer (Air, Earth, Ember, Ethyl, Mist, Stone) • Signal Type (Guide, Trickster, Warden, Catalyst) • Species Embodiment (Owl, Moonowl, Whisperhawk…) • Elemental Overlay (Air, Water, Fire, Earth) These axes operate independently.

The Four Overlays 🌬️ Air Overlay Keywords: perception, movement, scanning, pattern recognition, thought Behavior: subtle motion, altitude, clarity, cognitive insight Typical Expressions: • high‑clarity species • directional or scanning signals • spirits with rapid movement logic

🌊 Water Overlay Keywords: emotion, reflection, cycles, lunar resonance, intuition Behavior: slow cycles, depth, dream logic, tidal movement Typical Expressions: • lunar or reflective species • emotionally resonant signals • spirits with cyclical or tidal behavior

🔥 Fire Overlay Keywords: action, disruption, transformation, breakthrough energy Behavior: ignition, rupture, sudden shifts, catalytic force Typical Expressions: • catalytic species • ignition‑based signals • spirits with rupture or breakthrough logic

🌱 Earth Overlay Keywords: grounding, stability, tactile experience, slow growth Behavior: rootedness, embodiment, physicality, weight Typical Expressions: • body‑oriented species • grounded or stabilizing signals • spirits with slow‑growth logic

Cross‑Overlay Bridges Some signals naturally carry dual overlays. These are important resonance nodes because they express hybrid movement patterns or multi‑layered emotional logic. Examples: • Water + Air → reflective clarity, emotional perception • Fire + Air → ignition‑through‑insight, catalytic direction • Water + Earth → grounded intuition, tidal embodiment Dual overlays indicate complex energetic behavior.
