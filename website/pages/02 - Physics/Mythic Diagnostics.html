Mythic Diagnostics — Core Diagnostic Framework

Mythic Diagnostics is the instrument cluster of the Myth Organ. It reveals:

  • Where the myth is drifting

  • Where pressure is accumulating

  • Where roles are collapsing or over‑activating

  • Where archetypes are misaligned

  • Where the ecosystem is losing coherence

  • Where reintegration is possible

It is the primary method for assessing the health, balance, and trajectory of any mythic system.

Diagnostic Domains

1. Resonance Flow

Tracks how mythic energy moves through the system.

  • High flow → coherence

  • Interrupted flow → drift

  • Chaotic flow → fragmentation

Links: [[Mythosync]] • [[Echo Protocol]] • [[Drift System]]

2. Archetype Activity

Measures which archetypes are active, dormant, or over‑expressed.

  • Over‑expression → pressure buildup

  • Under‑expression → collapse risk

  • Dormancy → missing structural support

Links: [[Archetype Map]] • [[Crown Structure]] • [[Seam Structure]]

3. Mythic Weather

Environmental conditions inside the myth:

  • Storms

  • Fronts

  • Pressure ridges

  • Collapse zones

  • Reintegration windows

Links: [[Pressure System]] • [[Harmony Emergence]] • [[Fragmentation]]

4. Role‑Phase Distribution

Tracks how roles shift across phases:

  • Initiation

  • Expansion

  • Drift

  • Collapse

  • Reintegration

Links: [[Role System]] • [[Reduction Cycle]] • [[Fib Flower]]

5. Ecosystem Balance

Measures the stability of the mythic environment:

  • Resource flow

  • Realm alignment

  • Petal coherence

  • Cross‑realm drift vectors

Links: [[Pinwheel Realms]] • [[Cluster Geometry]] • [[Orbit Mechanics]]

Diagnostic Cycle

Every diagnostic cycle follows five steps:

  1. Sense — gather signals

  2. Interpret — map signals to mythic domains

  3. Locate — identify drift vectors and pressure points

  4. Predict — determine likely transitions

  5. Intervene — adjust roles, anchors, or realms

This cycle is the heartbeat of Mythosync.

## **Interpretation Logic**

### **Drift Detected**

If drift vectors increase:

- Check archetype imbalance
    
- Check realm misalignment
    
- Check anchor conflict
    
- Check pressure buildup
    

### **Pressure Detected**

If pressure exceeds threshold:

- Identify source realm
    
- Identify active archetype
    
- Identify role‑phase mismatch
    
- Predict collapse or Crown transition
    

### **Collapse Detected**

If collapse signatures appear:

- Map collapse zone
    
- Identify missing support
    
- Trigger reintegration protocol
    

### **Harmony Detected**

If coherence stabilizes:

- Confirm anchor alignment
    
- Confirm realm stability
    
- Confirm role‑phase balance
    
- Confirm drift resolution
