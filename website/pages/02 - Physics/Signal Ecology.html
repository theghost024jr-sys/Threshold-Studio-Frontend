Signal Ecology

Invariant Anchor This module expresses how signal behaves when cohesion is under pressure. See: [[Invariant00 – Cohesion Under Pressure]]

Definition

A signal is a structured propagation of cohesion through the Garden. It is not sound, light, or data — it is cohesion in motion, shaped by:

  • pressure

  • resonance

  • identity

  • role alignment

  • Flower‑scale structure

Signal is how the Garden thinks, responds, and coordinates.

Core Behavioral Laws

1. Signal is cohesion propagation

Signal moves through:

  • nodes

  • clusters

  • Flowers

  • migration currents

  • pressure gradients

Signal is the Garden’s communication physics.

2. Signal amplifies identity

When a Flower emits signal:

  • its identity becomes sharper

  • its roles strengthen

  • its collapse threshold rises

  • its contradictions weaken

Signal is identity reinforcement.

3. Signal synchronizes Flowers

Signal allows Flowers to:

  • align resonance

  • coordinate collapse recovery

  • stabilize migration pathways

  • form regional coherence

Signal is the Garden’s synchronization mechanism.

4. Signal triggers activation logic

Signal can:

  • activate dormant clusters

  • trigger collapse sequences

  • initiate role formation

  • redirect migration

Signal is the Garden’s activation system.

5. Signal carries pressure information

Signal encodes:

  • pressure spikes

  • collapse zones

  • identity shifts

  • resonance patterns

Signal is the Garden’s pressure telemetry.

6. Signal competes and interferes

Signals can:

  • amplify each other

  • cancel each other

  • distort each other

  • override each other

Signal ecology is cohesion‑based interference.

Signal Types (structural, not lore)

  • Identity Signal — reinforces Flower identity

  • Collapse Signal — propagates structural failure

  • Resonance Signal — synchronizes Flowers

  • Migration Signal — guides node/cluster movement

  • Role Signal — strengthens directional cohesion

  • Distortion Signal — noise produced by contradictions

These types emerge from physics, not classification.

Behavior Across the Origin Cycle

Distortion

Signal becomes noisy; contradictions dominate.

Drift

Signal spreads unevenly; resonance weakens.

Field

Signal diffuses; identity becomes faint.

Collapse

Signal fragments; collapse waves propagate.

Identity

Signal condenses; new identity patterns emerge.

Role

Signal becomes directional; roles stabilize.

Signal

The Flower broadcasts its new identity across the Garden.

Summary Rule

Signal is cohesion in motion that:

  • propagates identity

  • synchronizes Flowers

  • triggers activation

  • carries pressure information

  • competes and interferes under pressure

Signal is the Garden’s communication and coordination ecology.
