o1. SHARDBACK TIDEFORM

Collapse‑Adapted Variant

Identity

A Tideform that lives almost exclusively inside Collapse Zones. Its plates are thicker, darker, and fracture into razor‑clean shards.

Behavior

Moves in short, jittery bursts

Emits crackling signals before Collapse spikes

Fractures into a cloud of shards when threatened

Reforms slowly, plate by plate

Physics Expression

Collapse → plates shatter

Drift → shards swirl in tight spirals

Cohesion → plates reassemble

Pressure → plates lock rigid

Role

A living Collapse indicator. Players follow them to find safe pockets inside dangerous zones.

  1. TIDEBACK TIDEFORM

Drift‑Aligned Variant

Identity

A long‑bodied Tideform with extended plates that act like fins. It rides Drift currents with extreme precision.

Behavior

Moves in elegant, wave‑like arcs

Forms “ribbons” — long lines of synchronized individuals

Emits harmonic signals that map Drift corridors

Physics Expression

Drift → plates flex and ripple

Pressure → plates flatten to reduce drag

Collapse → plates vibrate rapidly, warning the cluster

Cohesion → ribbons tighten into stable formations

Role

A living Drift navigator. Players use them to locate Drift highways and avoid unstable currents.

  1. COHESIONBACK TIDEFORM

Cohesion‑Stabilizing Variant

Identity

A Tideform with dense, honeycomb plates that glow faintly. Clusters of them create temporary Cohesion pockets.

Behavior

Moves slowly, deliberately

Gathers in tight circles

Emits low, resonant hums that stabilize the environment

Physics Expression

Cohesion → plates brighten and synchronize

Drift → movement becomes sluggish

Pressure → plates pulse rhythmically

Collapse → clusters resist dephase longer than other variants

Role

A living sanctuary generator. Players rest near them to stabilize resonance and reduce Collapse risk.

  1. PRESSUREBACK TIDEform

High‑Pressure Variant

Identity

A compact, armored Tideform adapted to Pressure Ridges. Its plates are thick, interlocking, and almost metallic.

Behavior

Moves in short, powerful bursts

Emits sharp, percussive signals

Uses pressure spikes to launch itself

Physics Expression

Pressure → plates lock into a rigid shell

Drift → movement becomes ballistic

Collapse → plates crack but do not shatter

Cohesion → plates soften and separate

Role

A living pressure gauge. Players track them to detect incoming pressure waves or ridge shifts.

  1. ECHOGLASS TIDEFORM

Signal‑Amplifying Variant

Identity

A rare Tideform whose plates act like resonant chambers. It amplifies ambient signals — including player signals.

Behavior

Mirrors player resonance

Emits echo‑patterns of nearby species

Creates interference webs when multiple are present

Physics Expression

Signals → plates vibrate in harmonic layers

Drift → movement becomes erratic

Pressure → plates distort signals

Collapse → echoes become chaotic, revealing hidden nodes

Role

A living signal amplifier. Players use them to detect hidden nodes, artifacts, or actor systems.
