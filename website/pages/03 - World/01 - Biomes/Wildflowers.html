Wildflower Drift

![[13 - Reference/16 reference/Wildflower Drift.png]]

Type

Ephemeral Field — Environmental Substrate Artifact

Description

A soft, painterly field of red poppies and drifting dandelion seeds.
The background blends warm yellows and cool blues, suggesting a transitional sky — either dawn or dusk.
The composition evokes movement, delicacy, and emotional transience.

  • Environmental Substrate: Represents a gentle, non‑collapse field where resonance is high but pressure is low.
  • Transience Node: Used to mark the moment before drift becomes loss.
  • Emotional Lightfield: Supports shimmer onset and soft release.

  • Sky gradient = permeability and transition
  • Light = warmth and shimmer adjacency
  • Composition = containment without compression
