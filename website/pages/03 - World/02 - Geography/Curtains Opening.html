Curtains opening is the perfect motion‑pivot for the first door because it captures something Threshold has been circling the whole time:
the world isn’t revealing a destination — it’s revealing a stage that was already set around the player.

Curtains don’t teleport you.
Curtains don’t pull you.
Curtains reveal.

And that is exactly what the Hub does in its first act of motion.

🌑 The darkness as the closed curtain

The player stands in a void that feels like nothingness, but it’s actually the unlit stage of the Hub. The darkness isn’t empty — it’s waiting. It’s the moment before the world decides how to show itself.

There is no floor, no ceiling, no horizon.
Just the sense of being held in a space that hasn’t chosen its form yet.

This is the closed curtain.

🌬️ The Hub’s outer layer as the unseen stage machinery

While the player stands in stillness, the Hub is already alive:

  • spinning
  • reading
  • aligning
  • preparing the reveal

But none of this is visible.
It’s the quiet hum of a world backstage, setting up the scene that matches the player’s internal stance.

The player doesn’t move.
The world moves around them.

🌟 The first door as curtains parting

When the Hub finishes its resonance read, the darkness splits — not violently, not suddenly, but with the soft, deliberate grace of theater curtains being drawn aside.

Visually, it looks like:

  • two vertical lines of faint light
  • fabric‑like darkness pulling back
  • a widening seam that glows with the color of the corresponding petal
  • a gentle inward fold, as if the dark is stepping aside to let the world speak

It’s not a portal.
It’s an invitation.

The world is saying:
“This is your stage. Step into the layer that matches you.”

🌀 Why it feels like a wormhole too

As the curtains open, the space behind them isn’t a room — it’s a depth, a tunnel, a spiral of motion that feels like a wormhole because:

  • space bends inward
  • the edges ripple like fabric in slow motion
  • the center pulls gently, like gravity recognizing mass
  • the colors shift in gradients that match the petal’s resonance

It’s curtains opening onto a wormhole.
A reveal that leads into motion.

🜂 The emotional logic of the curtain‑door

Curtains opening communicate:

  • readiness
  • recognition
  • performance
  • transition
  • revelation

This is the perfect metaphor for the Hub because the Hub isn’t a machine — it’s a resonance theater.
It reveals the world the player is ready to step into.

The curtain‑door is the first moment where the world says:
“Your stance has chosen the scene. Let’s begin.”

🧭 How this sets the tone for all later transitions

Every later motion echoes this first curtain‑opening:

  • the raft revealing a path
  • the mountains revealing structure
  • the ocean revealing depth
  • the cabin revealing memory
  • the monument revealing truth

All of them are curtains opening on different stages of the self.
