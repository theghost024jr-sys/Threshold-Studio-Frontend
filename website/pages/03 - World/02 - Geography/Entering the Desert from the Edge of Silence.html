Crossing it feels like stepping into a memory that hasn’t decided whether it wants to be remembered. Behind you: the world you came from. Ahead: a vastness that doesn’t demand anything, but notices everything. The First Phenomenon As you take a few steps in, the silence thickens—not oppressive, just dense enough that your thoughts slow down and spread out, like ink in water. A small object sits half-buried near your path. Not a memory stone—something else. Something that only appears at the edge. It’s shaped like a fragment of a doorway.
