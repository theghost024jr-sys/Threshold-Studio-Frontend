Substrate Alignment

How symbolic, emotional, memory, and scene substrates align or misalign across world states

Substrate alignment describes how the four substrates—symbolic, emotional, memory, and scene—come into coherence or fall out of coherence. Alignment determines stability, clarity, and resonance across the world. Misalignment produces turbulence, fog, storms, or collapse.

Alignment is the world’s internal posture.

Fully Aligned State

All four substrates move in the same direction with compatible resonance.

Characteristics:

  • drift direction is consistent
  • density gradients match emotional tone
  • memory substrate reinforces symbolic patterns
  • scene substrate expresses clear thresholds

Effects:

  • symbolic weather becomes stable
  • rituals become safe and predictable
  • artifacts resonate cleanly
  • traversal becomes intuitive

Full alignment is rare and usually seasonal.

Partial Alignment

Three substrates align; one diverges.

Common patterns:

  • emotional substrate misaligned
  • memory substrate lagging
  • scene substrate out of phase
  • symbolic substrate ahead of others

Effects:

  • mild turbulence
  • fronts and eddies form
  • rituals require caution
  • artifacts may enter low-amplitude mode

Partial alignment is the most common world state.

Misalignment

Two substrates align; two diverge.

Characteristics:

  • drift and density contradict
  • emotional tone does not match symbolic weather
  • memory substrate becomes noisy
  • scene substrate becomes ambiguous

Effects:

  • fog becomes common
  • storms may form
  • traversal becomes difficult
  • resonance becomes unstable

Misalignment is a precursor to failure modes.

Full Misalignment

All four substrates move in different directions.

Characteristics:

  • drift vectors scatter
  • density fractures
  • emotional substrate spikes or collapses
  • memory substrate fragments
  • scene substrate loses coherence

Effects:

  • storms, vortices, or soup
  • collapse or inversion likely
  • rituals become unsafe
  • artifacts destabilize

Full misalignment is the world’s most unstable state.

Seasonal Alignment Patterns

Winter

  • symbolic: inward
  • emotional: quiet
  • memory: intertwined
  • scene: rigid
    Alignment: high
    Winter is the most aligned season.

Spring

  • symbolic: outward spirals
  • emotional: porous
  • memory: sorting
  • scene: softening
    Alignment: medium-high
    Spring is stable but active.

Summer

  • symbolic: accelerated
  • emotional: volatile
  • memory: passive
  • scene: dynamic
    Alignment: medium-low
    Summer is expressive but unstable.

Autumn

  • symbolic: inward collapse
  • emotional: stabilizing
  • memory: active
  • scene: cooling
    Alignment: medium
    Autumn is transitional but coherent.

Alignment During Transitions

Spiral-Out Transition (Winter → Spring)

  • symbolic and emotional align first
  • memory follows
  • scene aligns last

Spiral-In Transition (Summer → Autumn)

  • symbolic and scene align first
  • emotional stabilizes
  • memory activates

Clarification Phase (Fog → Clarity)

  • scene aligns first
  • symbolic follows
  • emotional and memory settle together

Transitions are alignment shifts across substrates.

Alignment Failure Modes

Substrate Shear

Two substrates move in opposite directions.

  • symbolic vs emotional
  • emotional vs memory
  • memory vs scene

Density Fracture

Density splits into incompatible regions.

Emotional Inversion

Emotional substrate flips amplitude.

Resonance Snap

Amplitude exceeds tolerance.

These failures appear when alignment collapses.

Alignment Recovery Modes

Drift Reorientation

Drift vectors regain coherence.

Density Reformation

Density gradients stabilize.

Emotional Settling

Amplitude returns to baseline.

Memory Rebinding

Threads reconnect without distortion.

Recovery is alignment returning.

Reading Alignment

To interpret substrate alignment:

  • check drift direction
  • observe density gradients
  • read emotional amplitude
  • examine memory activity
  • compare scene thresholds
  • identify seasonal resonance

Alignment is the world’s internal coherence.
