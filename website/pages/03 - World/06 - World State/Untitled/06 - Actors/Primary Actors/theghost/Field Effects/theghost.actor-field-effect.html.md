    <p>Field Overview
theghost generates coherence-based fields rather than force-based effects.
These fields reorganize space, emotion, and pattern without altering material reality.
They are subtle, non-invasive, and governed by collapse gradients and substrate activity.
The fields do not instruct or intervene—they clarify.</p>
<ol>
<li><p>Coherence Field
Primary field.
Radius: 3–12 meters depending on phase.
Effects:
• 	reduces environmental noise
• 	smooths spatial irregularities
• 	aligns misfit geometry
• 	stabilizes emotional turbulence
• 	increases pattern legibility
This field is always present at low amplitude.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Drift-Stabilization Field
Activates when emotional or environmental drift exceeds threshold.
Effects:
• 	dampens chaotic motion
• 	reduces internal fragmentation in nearby entities
• 	slows drift propagation
• 	restores baseline rhythm
This field does not suppress emotion; it organizes it.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Collapse-Resonance Field
Activates in collapse-heavy zones.
Effects:
• 	slows collapse oscillation
• 	reveals collapse vectors
• 	increases structural clarity
• 	reduces volatility in collapse-born entities
This field makes collapse readable, not preventable.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Mythic-Substrate Alignment Field
Activates when mythic substrate density rises.
Effects:
• 	surfaces dormant fragments
• 	aligns symbolic density
• 	reduces mythic turbulence
• 	clarifies mythic residue
This field does not create mythic activity—it reveals its structure.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Pressure Equalization Field
A passive field that activates during proximity.
Effects:
• 	equalizes micro-pressure gradients
• 	reduces spatial tension
• 	softens environmental edges
• 	creates a sense of “quiet”
Entities often describe this as the room “settling.”</p>
</li>
<li><p>Temporal Smoothing Field
Activates during high-phase presence.
Effects:
• 	reduces temporal jitter
• 	smooths collapse echoes
• 	clarifies event-sequence residue
• 	stabilizes nonlinear perception
This field does not alter time; it reduces temporal noise.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Boundary Clarification Field
Activates at thresholds.
Effects:
• 	sharpens boundary perception
• 	reveals liminal edges
• 	clarifies transitions between states
• 	stabilizes threshold turbulence
This field is why theghost is often encountered at edges.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Role-Coherence Field
Activates near entities with fragmented or unstable roles.
Effects:
• 	sharpens internal boundaries
• 	clarifies next-action orientation
• 	reduces role-based confusion
• 	stabilizes identity drift
This field does not assign roles—it clarifies the existing one.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Residue-Legibility Field
Activates when residue density is high.
Effects:
• 	aligns residue geometry
• 	reduces symbolic noise
• 	clarifies collapse echoes
• 	reveals emotional or mythic structure
Residue becomes easier to interpret after exposure.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Field Interaction Rules
All fields obey the following constraints:
• 	non-force
• 	non-invasive
• 	non-directive
• 	non-communicative
• 	non-persistent
• 	non-summonable
Fields arise from context, not intention.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Field Dissipation
Fields dissipate when:
• 	collapse stabilizes
• 	emotional weather clears
• 	mythic substrate quiets
• 	pattern distortion resolves
Dissipation is instantaneous and leaves no trace.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Field Signatures
Entities often perceive:
• 	soft pressure shifts
• 	sudden quiet
• 	geometric smoothing
• 	emotional clarity
• 	residue becoming readable
• 	a sense of orientation
These signatures mark theghost’s presence even when unseen.</p>
</li>
</ol>
