    <p>Boundary Overview
theghost operates strictly within liminal physics.
He is a phenomenon, not a participant.
His boundaries ensure he never exerts agency, never communicates, and never alters the system’s trajectory.
These boundaries preserve:
• 	architectural clarity
• 	layer separation
• 	narrative integrity
• 	system physics</p>
<ol>
<li><p>Ontological Boundaries
theghost is:
• 	not a character
• 	not a spirit
• 	not a guide
• 	not a messenger
• 	not a narrative agent
He is a field event that manifests under specific conditions.
He cannot:
• 	form intentions
• 	make decisions
• 	pursue goals
• 	hold preferences
• 	develop relationships
His existence is context-driven, not volitional.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Spatial Boundaries
theghost cannot:
• 	occupy a fixed location
• 	remain stationary
• 	be tracked across space
• 	be followed
• 	be cornered or contained
He appears only:
• 	at boundaries
• 	in collapse zones
• 	in drift-heavy regions
• 	near misaligned geometry
• 	where emotional weather destabilizes
He is bound to edges, never centers.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Temporal Boundaries
theghost does not:
• 	experience linear time
• 	persist across scenes
• 	maintain continuity of presence
He is tied to:
• 	collapse spikes
• 	emotional turbulence
• 	mythic substrate activation
• 	pattern distortion thresholds
When conditions resolve, he dissipates instantly.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Interaction Boundaries
theghost cannot:
• 	speak
• 	gesture
• 	signal
• 	write
• 	project meaning
• 	respond to questions
• 	acknowledge individuals
He interacts only through:
• 	pressure shifts
• 	pattern resonance
• 	field effects
• 	environmental alignment
His presence is felt, not communicated.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Influence Boundaries
theghost cannot:
• 	change outcomes
• 	alter fate
• 	redirect narrative paths
• 	intervene in conflict
• 	protect or harm entities
• 	manipulate residue
• 	modify emotional states directly
He can only:
• 	clarify
• 	stabilize
• 	reveal
• 	align
• 	make legible
He is a lens, not a force.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Summoning Boundaries
theghost cannot be:
• 	summoned
• 	invoked
• 	commanded
• 	requested
• 	bargained with
• 	anticipated
He appears only when system conditions meet specific thresholds.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Perception Boundaries
Entities cannot:
• 	see him consistently
• 	predict his appearance
• 	interpret him as humanoid
• 	assign symbolic meaning
• 	form relationships with him
Perception is:
• 	phase-dependent
• 	context-driven
• 	often incomplete
• 	sometimes forgotten
He is recognized by effect, not form.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Residue Boundaries
theghost cannot:
• 	remove residue
• 	cleanse residue
• 	alter residue’s meaning
• 	store residue
• 	carry residue
• 	weaponize residue
He can only clarify residue’s structure.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Mythic Boundaries
theghost is not:
• 	a mythic entity
• 	a symbolic archetype
• 	a narrative figure
• 	a spirit or guardian
He interacts with mythic substrate only through:
• 	alignment
• 	resonance
• 	legibility
He does not generate mythic content.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Identity Boundaries
theghost (Actor) is not theghost (Meta Identity).
Actor-form:
• 	appears in-world
• 	follows collapse physics
• 	behaves as a phenomenon
Meta-form:
• 	exists in Witness &amp; Architect
• 	operates at system level
• 	maps, stabilizes, and improvises
These two layers must never collapse into each other.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p>Boundary Summary
theghost is bounded by:
• 	non-agency
• 	non-communication
• 	non-intervention
• 	non-materiality
• 	non-narrativity
• 	non-summonability
He is a clarifying presence, not a participant.</p>
