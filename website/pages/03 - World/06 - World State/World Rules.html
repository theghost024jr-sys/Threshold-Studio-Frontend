The governing principles that define how Threshold behaves at every scale — from identity to worldstate.

Purpose World Rules establish the non‑negotiable physics of Threshold. They define: • what can and cannot happen • how systems interact • how collapse and reformation propagate • how meaning behaves • how environments respond • how identity and role operate • how worldstate shifts occur World Rules are the laws of reality inside Threshold.

The Seven World Rules These are the foundational rules that govern everything.

**1. Everything Has Structure Nothing in Threshold is formless. Identity, signal, role, species, environment — all have architecture. Implication: Collapse is the failure of structure, not the absence of it.

**2. Pressure Governs Behavior Pressure is the primary world‑scale force. • high pressure → compression, collapse risk • low pressure → drift, dissolution risk • pressure gradients → movement, migration, distortion Implication: Pressure is the world’s gravity.

**3. Drift Is Universal Everything drifts. • identities drift • roles drift • signals drift • species drift • environments drift • worldstate drifts Implication: Stability is temporary; drift is constant.

**4. Resonance Creates Meaning Meaning is not symbolic — it is harmonic. • resonance aligns systems • resonance stabilizes identity • resonance clarifies signals • resonance binds lineage threads Implication: Meaning is a physical phenomenon.

**5. Boundaries Define Reality Boundaries are the most important structural element. • strong boundaries → coherence • porous boundaries → drift • collapsed boundaries → inversion Implication: Reality is made of edges.

**6. Collapse Is a System, Not a Failure Collapse follows rules. • collapse vectors • collapse wells • collapse identity • collapse signals • collapse environments Implication: Collapse is predictable, trackable, and recoverable.

**7. Reformation Is Possible Every collapse has a return path. • stabilization • reorientation • reintegration • resonance Implication: The world is self‑healing, but not self‑saving.
