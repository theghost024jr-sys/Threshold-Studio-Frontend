Ruins of Velin

The Ruins of Velin are an ancient collapse‑adjacent environment where memory, stone, and resonance intermingle.
They are not dead structures.
They are active remnants — holding pressure, echo, and symbolic residue.

Velin is a site where collapse never fully resolved.
It lingers.
