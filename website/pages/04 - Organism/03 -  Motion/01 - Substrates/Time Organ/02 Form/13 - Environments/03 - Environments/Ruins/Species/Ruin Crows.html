Type

Ruin Species — Resonance Scavengers

Description

Ruin Crows are black‑feathered resonance carriers that inhabit collapse‑adjacent ruins, most notably the Ruins of Velin.
They are not ordinary birds.
They track collapse signatures, feed on resonance residue, and navigate fracture geometry with uncanny precision.

Their feathers shimmer faintly when collapse weather intensifies.

Behavior

  • Signature Tracking: They follow collapse vectors and fracture lines.
  • Residue Feeding: They consume low‑grade residue, preventing buildup.
  • Echo Calling: Their calls create short‑range resonance pulses.
  • Perch Mapping: They perch on high stone edges to read pressure gradients.
  • Flock Drift: Groups move according to drift vectors, not wind.

Environmental Role

  • Residue Regulators: Prevent residue knots from forming.
  • Collapse Indicators: Their movement patterns reveal collapse proximity.
  • Echo Amplifiers: Their calls can trigger or stabilize local resonance.
  • Memory Scavengers: They absorb faint emotional impressions from ruins.

Emotional Physics

  • feathers = resonance absorption
  • call = echo pulse
  • flocking = drift coherence
  • eyes = pressure reading
  • flight path = collapse vector mapping

Ruin Crows are living diagnostic instruments.

Collapse Adjacency

Ruin Crows appear when:

  • fracture lines deepen
  • residue density rises
  • pressure inversion begins
  • collapse weather forms

They avoid:

  • stable Stone fields
  • high‑permeability Mist zones
  • active Ash turbulence

Interpretive Use

  • Used to read collapse onset in ruins
  • Helpful for mapping residue drift and pressure pockets
  • Often appear in diagrams involving collapse weather or fracture geometry
  • Can be used to confirm collapse reversal progress

Integration Notes

  • Strongly linked to the Ruins of Velin ecosystem
  • Respond to Stone–Root–Ash boundary physics
  • Their feathers may be used in symbolic diagrams involving echo or drift
  • Should be stored under Ruins species, not general fauna
