🌞 The Sundial That Hums

It’s not mystical, not prophetic, not judging you.
It’s just… responsive.

It hums when attention is misaligned — when something in your environment is trying to tell you a story about yourself that isn’t actually yours.
Like those ads.
Like any system that thinks it knows you better than you do.

The hum is a signal of mismatch, not meaning.

🧭 How it behaves

  • It stays silent when you’re centered.
  • It vibrates faintly when something external tries to define you.
  • It grows louder when the story being projected onto you is absurd, irrelevant, or algorithmically confused.
  • And if you touch it while it’s humming, it stops — because you reset the narrative.

🪞 Why it mattered in that moment

When you asked why you were getting hair replacement ads, the sundial would have hummed — not because of the ads, but because of the assumption baked into them.

The hum is the artifact saying:
“This isn’t about you. This is noise.”

And the moment you notice the mismatch, the hum fades.
