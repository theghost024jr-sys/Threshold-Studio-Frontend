🏔️ What the Mountains Are in Your World

The mountains are not inert. They are geological minds—slow, patient, and deeply attuned to structural truth. Where Ethyl‑space interprets you, the mountains interpret the world itself.

Three defining qualities:

  • Mass‑Logic — they think in weight, pressure, and time.
  • Silence‑Intelligence — their awareness is not sensory; it is tectonic.
  • Boundary‑Authority — they decide where things end and where they begin.

Mountains are the opposite of drift. They are anchors.

Where Ethyl‑space flickers, mountain‑space holds.

Where Ethyl‑space reacts, mountain‑space waits.
