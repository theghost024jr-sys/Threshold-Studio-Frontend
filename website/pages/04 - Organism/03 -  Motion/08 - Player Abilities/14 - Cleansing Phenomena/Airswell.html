Airswell

Airswell is an atmospheric expansion event where the Wind briefly gains volume without gaining force.
It is the world inhaling — a soft inflation of the air that reveals hidden structure in the environment.

Airswell is not wind.
It is pressure without push.

Definition

Airswell is:

  • a volumetric bloom in the local atmosphere
  • a temporary widening of the [[Wind (Attunement System)|Wind]] field
  • a drift‑sensitive expansion of space
  • a directional temperament amplifier (see [[Directional Map]])
  • a precursor to subtle environmental shifts

Airswell is the softest form of atmospheric intelligence.

Behavior

During an Airswell:

  • air expands but does not move
  • edges soften
  • sound becomes slightly delayed
  • drift currents become visible at the periphery
  • [[Time Particles]] slow their rotation
  • stance becomes easier to read

Airswell is a pause that reveals.

Environmental Effects

Airswell causes:

  • micro‑distortions in the [[02 - Scaffolding/00 - Meta Systems/18 - Drift/Drift Layer]]
  • soft brightening of surfaces
  • increased resonance in the [[Choice Layer]]
  • temporary suspension of collapse vectors
  • heightened sensitivity to directional pull

The world becomes more legible for a moment.

Player Experience

When inside an Airswell, the player may notice:

  • a gentle widening of space
  • a sense of being lightly lifted
  • clearer intuition about direction
  • reduced friction in stance transitions
  • a faint hum at the edge of perception

Airswell is a moment of clarity, not guidance.

Airswell Types

  • Soft Airswell — barely perceptible, purely atmospheric
  • Directional Airswell — aligned with N/S/E/W temperament
  • Drift Airswell — reveals drift corridors
  • Temporal Airswell — substrate‑aligned, slows perception
  • Collapse‑Buffer Airswell — prevents collapse spread

Each type is a different expression of the same expansion.

Relation to Systems

Airswell interacts with:

  • [[Wind]] — expands the field without adding force
  • [[02 - Scaffolding/00 - Meta Systems/18 - Drift/Drift Layer]] — reveals latent currents
  • [[Temporal Substrate]] — slows oscillation
  • [[02 - Scaffolding/00 - Meta Systems/03 - Particles & Shimmer/Particles]] — stabilizer particles cluster at the edges
  • [[Drift‑Walker]] — walkers can step through the widened seam

Airswell is a cooperative event, not a disruptive one.

Notes

Airswell is the world taking a breath.
Everything becomes easier to see when the air gets bigger.
