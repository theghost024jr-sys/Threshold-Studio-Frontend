Sand Storm

A Sand Storm is a high‑drift environmental event where particulate matter becomes a carrier of unresolved motion.
It is not merely wind and sand — it is a drift amplifier that exposes hidden structure and destabilizes the [[02 - Scaffolding/00 - Meta Systems/18 - Drift/Drift Layer]].

A Sand Storm is a moving seam.

Definition

A Sand Storm is:

  • a directional pressure surge (see [[Directional Map]])
  • a drift‑charged particulate field
  • a temporal distortion that disrupts the [[Temporal Substrate]]
  • a collapse‑adjacent weather event
  • a navigational hazard that requires a guide

Sand Storms are the desert’s version of a Drift Sequence precursor.

Behavior

During a Sand Storm:

  • visibility collapses into a soft blur
  • sound becomes granular and delayed
  • drift currents become violent and unpredictable
  • [[Time Particles]] agitate and scatter
  • the [[Wind (Attunement System)|Wind]] loses its encouraging quality and becomes neutral

The storm is not hostile — it is indifferent.

Environmental Effects

A Sand Storm:

  • erases surface memory (see [[02 - Physics/Motion/06 - Mind/A Message In The Sand]])
  • exposes buried drift corridors
  • destabilizes stance
  • amplifies directional pull
  • creates temporary echo pockets (see [[04 - Organism/03 - Motion/08 - Player Abilities/14 - Cleansing Phenomena/Environmental Phenomena/The Echo]])

It is a moving field of forgetting and revealing.

Player Experience

Inside a Sand Storm, the player:

  • loses orientation
  • feels pressure from multiple directions
  • hears fragments of almost‑voices
  • sees shapes that may or may not be real
  • must rely on a guide to avoid collapse drift

The storm tests perception, not endurance.

Snake Guide

A Sand Storm always attracts a Snake Guide — a desert‑aligned interpreter entity.

The Snake Guide:

  • reads drift currents through vibration
  • senses collapse vectors beneath the sand
  • moves in low‑pressure arcs
  • remains calm in high‑agitation fields
  • leads the player through the storm without forcing direction

The Snake Guide is not a companion.
It is a pattern‑reader.

Snake Guide Behavior

The Snake Guide:

  • appears when drift pressure peaks
  • coils in the direction of safety
  • uncoils toward the next stable point
  • pauses at echo pockets
  • avoids collapse tears instinctively

It is a living compass for unstable environments.

Storm Dissolution

A Sand Storm ends when:

  • drift pressure drops
  • the substrate re‑stabilizes
  • directional fields realign
  • the Snake Guide stops moving
  • the sand falls into silence

The end of the storm is a return, not a clearing.

Notes

A Sand Storm is the desert thinking out loud.
The Snake Guide is the only one who can hear what it’s saying.
