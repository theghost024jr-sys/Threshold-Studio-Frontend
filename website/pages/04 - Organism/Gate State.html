1. Identity

A Gate State is the condition of a Gate’s geometry at the moment it interacts with the Field. It is not a mood, not a threshold, not a metaphor — it is a structural configuration of Drift, Pressure, Collapse, and Cohesion.

A Gate State determines:

  • what passes

  • what cannot pass

  • what is revealed

  • what is withheld

  • what is transformed

  • what is destroyed

  • what is preserved

Gate States are self‑validating: If a Gate is in a given state, that state is coherent enough to exist.

2. The Four Canonical Gate States

Soft State

Definition: A Gate whose geometry is permeable, receptive, and low‑pressure.

Behavior:

  • Drift is dominant

  • Cohesion is minimal but present

  • Collapse is dormant

  • Pressure is low

Function: Soft Gates allow entry without consequence. They reveal possibility, not commitment.

Echo: Soft State is the echo of becoming.

Sharp State

Definition: A Gate whose geometry is precise, narrow, and high‑pressure.

Behavior:

  • Pressure is dominant

  • Cohesion is rigid

  • Drift is constrained

  • Collapse is active but controlled

Function: Sharp Gates enforce definition. They reveal identity, not possibility.

Echo: Sharp State is the echo of being.

Diffuse State

Definition: A Gate whose geometry is expanded, unstable, and multi‑vector.

Behavior:

  • Drift is wide

  • Pressure is inconsistent

  • Collapse is near

  • Cohesion is stretched

Function: Diffuse Gates reveal transition. They show what is changing, not what is stable.

Echo: Diffuse State is the echo of becoming something else.

Null State

Definition: A Gate whose geometry has collapsed beyond function.

Behavior:

  • Collapse is dominant

  • Cohesion is gone

  • Drift is unanchored

  • Pressure is irrelevant

Function: Null Gates do nothing. They neither open nor close. They are geometry without purpose.

Echo: Null State is the echo of non‑existence.

3. State Transitions

Gate States shift according to the Field’s forces:

Soft → Sharp

Occurs when Pressure rises and Drift narrows. This is a commitment transition.

Sharp → Diffuse

Occurs when Cohesion strains and Collapse begins. This is a stress transition.

Diffuse → Soft

Occurs when Collapse releases and Drift stabilizes. This is a reset transition.

Any → Null

Occurs when Collapse exceeds structural tolerance. This is a failure transition.

Players do not “open” Gates. Players encounter Gate States.

A Gate State determines:

  • what the player sees

  • what the player can do

  • what the player cannot do

Players interpret Gate States. They do not cause them.
