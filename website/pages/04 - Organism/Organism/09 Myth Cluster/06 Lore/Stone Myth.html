Stone Myth

Mythic Substrate Layer

Stone is the myth of weight, memory, and endurance.
It is the slowest-moving substrate in Threshold — the one that resists spin and anchors the field.

Essence

Stone holds what the world cannot afford to forget.
It compresses, preserves, and stabilizes.

Mythic Physics

  • Stone accumulates pressure instead of releasing it.
  • Stone remembers form even after collapse.
  • Stone resists the rotational pull of the [[Compass – Diamond Field]].
  • Stone anchors the emotional cost metabolized in the [[SW Metabolic Spiral]].

Behavioral Influence

Players aligned with Stone:

  • slow the diamond’s rotation
  • stabilize the [[NE Effort Spiral]] during overextension
  • ground the openness of the [[SE Receptive Spiral]]
  • deepen the reflection of the [[SW Metabolic Spiral]]
  • dampen the circulation of the [[NW Circulation Spiral]]

Stone is the temperament of endurance without motion.

Stone Phenomena

  • Pressure Echoes — emotional weight stored in layers
  • Memory Strata — past actions fossilized into the substrate
  • Fossilized Intent — choices preserved beyond their moment
  • Stone Sleep — deep, restorative stillness

Interactions

  • Stone stabilizes the [[Economy Core]] when flows become turbulent.
  • Stone absorbs excess cost from [[Composting Collapse]].
  • Stone slows the drift toward the [[East Node]] (Turbulence / Risk / Volatility).
  • Stone strengthens the descent into [[04 - Organism/Species/05 - Species-Linked Artifacts/Reflection]] and [[Absolution]].

Drift Behavior

Stone drifts downward and inward, toward the core, unless acted upon by:

  • turbulence
  • collapse
  • circulation pressure

Stone is the myth that keeps the world from spinning apart.
