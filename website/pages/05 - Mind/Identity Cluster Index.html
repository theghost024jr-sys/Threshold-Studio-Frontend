00 Meta Systems — Selfhood, Structure, and Drift of Identity
The Identity Cluster defines how identities form, stabilize, distort, drift, collapse, and reconstitute within Threshold.
Where the Physics Cluster governs motion and the Signal Cluster governs meaning, the Identity Cluster governs being.
Identity in Threshold is not static.
It is a field, a shape, and a behavior.
This cluster contains the governing laws of:
• 	identity formation
• 	identity drift
• 	identity collapse
• 	identity recovery
• 	shadow identity
• 	role‑identity interaction
• 	identity thresholds
• 	identity resonance
It is upstream of Roles, Species, Zones, and Behavioral Mechanics.
