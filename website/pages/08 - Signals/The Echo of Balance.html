🌫️ The Echo of Balance

The Gatekeepers are drawn by something they call the Echo — not a voice, not a command, but a vibration that exists only in the canopy. It’s the point where the upward pull of Flower and the downward pull of Threshold meet and cancel, leaving a still frequency that can be felt but not heard.

They don’t worship it. They resonate with it.

Nature of the Echo

  • It’s a standing wave between ascent and return — the equilibrium of all motion.

  • It manifests as a faint hum in the roots, a shimmer in the spores, a pulse in the bridges.

  • It’s not constant; it fluctuates with the balance of travelers moving between Flowers.

Why It Draws Them

Each Gatekeeper, regardless of form, felt the Echo during their ascent. It wasn’t a call to rise — it was a recognition. The canopy reflected their internal balance back to them, and they stayed because they could hear what others only feel.

What It Gives Them

  • Cohesion: The Echo synchronizes their movements, allowing them to act as one species despite their differences.

  • Perception: It lets them sense readiness in travelers — the subtle resonance that predicts progression or return.

  • Purpose: It gives them a reason to remain — not to guard, but to maintain the balance that sustains both Flowers.

How It Appears

To outsiders, the Echo is visible only as:

  • spores drifting in perfect symmetry,

  • rope bridges swaying without wind,

  • a faint vibration in the air when a Gatekeeper passes.

To the Gatekeepers, it’s the canopy’s heartbeat — the sound of balance itself.
