Overview The Shimmer Path is a meaning‑trajectory formed by Shimmer curvature. It is not a physical route. It is a symbolic alignment — a path that emerges when: • meaning refracts cleanly • emotional physics stabilize • narrative pressure lowers • Shimmer curvature aligns into a coherent arc A Shimmer Path is the world showing you how meaning wants to move. It is the most delicate of all navigational paths in Threshold.

Purpose The Shimmer Path exists to: • reveal symbolic direction • clarify interpretive arcs • guide travelers through meaning rather than space • reduce narrative confusion • stabilize Shimmer resonance • prevent collapse caused by misinterpretation It is the interpretive path, not the spatial one.

Core Properties

  1. Curvature‑Defined A Shimmer Path forms when Shimmer reflections bend into: • arcs • ribbons • soft spirals • converging lines Curvature is the “geometry of meaning.”

  2. Meaning‑First Navigation The Shimmer Path does not show where to go. It shows how meaning flows. Travelers follow: • symbolic gradients • interpretive arcs • resonance alignment • emotional coherence The path is felt, not walked.

  3. Fragile Under Pressure Shimmer Paths destabilize when: • emotional charge spikes • collapse pressure rises • Drift turbulence increases • Mist density thickens • symbolic overload occurs They require calm, clarity, and soft attention.

  4. Non‑Linear Shimmer Paths often: • loop • arc • double back • spiral • branch • dissolve They follow meaning, not geometry.
