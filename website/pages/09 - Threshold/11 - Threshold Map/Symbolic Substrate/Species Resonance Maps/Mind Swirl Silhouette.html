[[04 - Organism/Organism/09 Myth Cluster/06 Lore/The Lantern's First Fire ( Mythic Version)]] Symbolic Substrate Diagram Essence The Mind Swirl Silhouette represents the internal motion of thought, identity resonance, and drift‑patterning inside a person. It is not a character or an entity — it is a shape of mind, a diagram of how attention moves when it is unanchored, overloaded, or reorganizing itself. Core Function • Maps the internal swirl of cognition • Reveals where attention is pooling or leaking • Shows the relationship between thought, emotion, and drift • Acts as a diagnostic tool for internal coherence The silhouette doesn’t tell you what someone is thinking — it shows how their thinking is moving. Structure • A central void representing unarticulated thought • Spiral vectors indicating drift, recursion, or looping • Outer contours showing identity boundaries • Light‑density regions marking clarity • Dark‑density regions marking noise or overwhelm The silhouette changes shape depending on emotional physics. Interpretive Use • Tight spirals → overfocus, rumination, pressure buildup • Loose spirals → drift, dissociation, soft attention • Fragmented edges → boundary instability • Bright inner ring → clarity emerging • Dense outer ring → external pressure or collapse weather influence It is a snapshot of internal motion. Environmental Interaction The silhouette reacts to: • Collapse weather • Drift fields • Environmental entities (especially Sapheen) • Saturation states • Hub proximity When the environment stabilizes, the silhouette smooths. When the environment destabilizes, the silhouette distorts. Role in the System • Used to read internal states without narrative exposition • Helps determine when a player should be pushed back to the hub • Acts as a visual cue for emotional physics • Serves as a bridge between internal and environmental logic It is the internal counterpart to environmental drift. Symbolic Markers • A faint glow around the head • A swirling pattern behind the eyes • A soft distortion in the air around the silhouette • A sense of motion without movement These markers appear in environments where internal states matter. Cross‑System Links • Substrate Layer → internal drift mapping • Environmental Entities → clarity modulation (Sapheen) • Collapse Weather → pressure signatures • Hub Logic → return thresholds • Petal 7 (Clarity) → stabilization • Petal 2 (Drift) → swirl amplification The silhouette is the internal map that mirrors the external world.
