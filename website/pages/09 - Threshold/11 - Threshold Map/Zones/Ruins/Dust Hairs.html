Overview Dust Hairs are micro‑faunal drift‑sensitive creatures found throughout Ruins environments. They are not mammals, not insects, not rodents — they are lightweight particulate organisms that resemble tiny drifting hairs or filaments. They move in response to: • Drift currents • Mist density • collapse residue • emotional gradients • Shimmer curvature Dust Hairs are one of the earliest indicators of environmental stability or instability.

Purpose in the Ecosystem Dust Hairs exist to: • reveal Drift turbulence • signal collapse residue • map micro‑currents in Ruins • soften emotional fields • distribute symbolic dust • maintain low‑level environmental coherence They are the canaries of Ruins ecology.

Core Properties

  1. Filament Bodies Dust Hairs are composed of: • ultra‑fine particulate strands • soft Shimmer‑responsive fibers • trace Stone dust • low‑density Mist residue Their bodies are almost weightless.

  2. Drift‑Responsive Movement They move only when Drift moves. Patterns include: • soft lifts • spirals • gentle arcs • sudden scatter • collapse recoil Their motion is a direct reading of Drift behavior.

  3. Collapse Sensitivity Dust Hairs react strongly to collapse residue: • they flatten • they cling to surfaces • they lose buoyancy • they stop drifting entirely This makes them excellent collapse detectors.

  4. Emotional Field Reactivity Dust Hairs respond to emotional physics: • calm → slow, graceful drift • fear → sudden scatter • grief → sinking motion • anticipation → upward lift • overwhelm → chaotic flutter They are emotional barometers.

Behavior Baseline Behavior Slow drifting, gentle spirals, soft arcs. Indicates stable Ruins. Alert Behavior Triggered by: • collapse pressure • emotional spikes • Shimmer fracture Dust Hairs scatter or cling to surfaces. Dormant Behavior Occurs in: • collapse scars • Stone‑heavy zones • deep Ruins Dust Hairs lie flat and still.

Environmental Interactions Drift Primary driver of movement. Dust Hairs reveal micro‑currents invisible to the eye. Mist Mist thickening slows Dust Hairs and makes them clump. Shimmer Shimmer curvature causes Dust Hairs to arc or align. Stone Stone resonance grounds them, reducing drift. Ember Heat causes Dust Hairs to rise sharply, then collapse. Ethyl Ethyl flow distributes Dust Hairs evenly across surfaces.

Species Interaction Dust Hairs are harmless and non‑sentient. Species use them as: • environmental indicators • collapse detectors • emotional field readers • navigation cues in Ruins Curators and Witnesses read Dust Hairs instinctively.

Symbolic Role Dust Hairs represent: • fragility • early warning • micro‑movement • subtle truth • the smallest readable sign They are the whisper‑level indicators of Ruins.
