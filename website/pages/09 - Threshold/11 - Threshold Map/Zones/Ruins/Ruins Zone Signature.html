Ruins is the zone of fractured memory, exposed structure, and unfinished resonance. It is not decay; it is revelation. The world shows its scaffolding here—sentences without endings, architectures without purpose, symbols without their original referents. Everything feels half‑remembered and half‑erased. The felt sense of Ruins is a hollow, echoing incompleteness, as if something important almost happened but never resolved.
