Overview The Ruin Species Index catalogs all known species native to, shaped by, or dependent on Ruins environments. These species are defined not by biology but by collapse ecology, drift‑movement, stone resonance, and symbolic dust behavior. Ruins species fall into three broad classes: • Drift‑Aligned Species — movement indicators, micro‑current readers • Stone‑Aligned Species — stabilizers, memory anchors • Shimmer/Mist‑Responsive Species — soft‑signal organisms, emotional field readers This index provides a structural overview of each.
