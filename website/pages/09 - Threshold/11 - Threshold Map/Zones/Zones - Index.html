Overview Zones are the primary spatial‑symbolic organs of the Threshold Map. They are not locations but states of space, each defined by its own emotional physics, environmental behavior, symbolic density, and narrative pressure. A zone expresses how the world arranges itself under specific resonance conditions. Zones form the stable regions of the map’s geometry. Transitions, Gates, and environmental fields shape how the player moves between them.

Purpose of Zones Zones exist to: • express distinct emotional and environmental states • stabilize or destabilize Roles • shape Drift, Shimmer, Mist, and Collapse behavior • anchor species ecologies • define symbolic and narrative density • provide the structural backbone for navigation • reveal the world’s internal physics through felt experience Each zone is a self‑contained phenomenological organ.

Zone Structure Every zone follows a shared structural pattern: • Zone Signature — the felt sense and atmospheric identity • Environmental Physics — how the zone interacts with the eight fields • Role Gravity — which roles stabilize or destabilize • Species Ecology — which species cluster here and why • Transition Behavior — how the zone connects to others • Mythic Imprint — what accumulates over time • Cross‑Links — relevant organs in the system This structure ensures consistency across all zones.

Current Zones These are the zones currently defined in the system: • Deep Ice — zone of frozen time, pressure density, and crystalline resonance • Ruins — zone of fractured memory, exposed structure, and symbolic residue Additional zones will be added as the world expands.

Zone Taxonomy Zones can be classified by their dominant behaviors: • Stable Zones — inward, reflective, low‑motion resonance • Volatile Zones — high motion, heat, or expressive expansion • Fracture Zones — collapse‑prone, symbolically unstable • Dream Zones — low‑density, narrative‑fluid states • Shadow Zones — zones shaped by absence, erasure, or inversion Deep Ice and Ruins currently occupy: • Deep Ice → Stable / Pressure‑Dense • Ruins → Fracture / Symbolically Volatile This taxonomy expands as more zones are added.

Environmental Interaction Zones are shaped by the eight environmental fields: • Drift • Mist • Shimmer • Stone • Ember • Ethyl • Heat • Collapse Each zone expresses a unique combination of these forces, creating its own physics and navigational behavior.

Role Interaction Roles interact with zones through: • stabilization • destabilization • clarity • shadow expression Zones reveal how a role behaves under specific emotional and environmental conditions.

Species Ecology Species cluster in zones where: • their role tendencies stabilize • their layer affinities align • their drift sensitivity is minimized • their symbolic resonance matches the zone Zones define the ecological map of Threshold.

Transitions and Gates Zones are connected by: • stable transitions • volatile transitions • collapse‑bent transitions • shimmer‑curved transitions • mist‑veiled transitions Gates regulate movement between zones based on resonance, role alignment, and environmental state.

Mythic Layer Zones accumulate: • scars • residues • trails • echoes • imprints This forms the mythic geography that overlays the map.
