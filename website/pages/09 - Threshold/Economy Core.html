Economy Core

Category: 04 – Systems / 01 – Economy Color: #FFD93D (Sunflower Gold) Role: Defines the central economic organ of Threshold — the substrate that governs all flows, costs, exchanges, unlocks, and pressures across the world.

The Economy Core is not a currency system. It is a metabolic engine.

It determines:

  • what moves

  • why it moves

  • what it costs

  • what it unlocks

  • what pressures accumulate

  • what collapses

  • what regenerates

Every subsystem — Player, Spirit, Alignment, Time, Fib Zones — plugs into this organ.

1. Core Structure

The Economy Core is composed of three primary nodes:

1. Flows

Movement of resources, alignment, time, spirit, contribution, or pressure.

Flows determine:

  • direction

  • velocity

  • friction

  • drift

2. Economic Forces

The Economy Core is driven by four forces:

1. Contribution

The act of giving energy, alignment, or action into the system.

In Fib 5–8, contribution becomes mandatory for survival.

Link:

  • [[Contribution Economy]]

2. Progress

Forward movement through zones, layers, or thresholds.

Progress is not linear — it is pressure‑based.

Link:

  • [[Progress Engine]]

3. Scarcity

The natural limit that creates tension, choice, and meaning.

Scarcity is not punitive — it is structural.

Link:

  • [[Scarcity Patterns]]

4. Unlocks

The release of new abilities, spirits, zones, or pathways.

Unlocks are earned, not given.

Link:

  • [[Unlock Economy]]

3. Economic Currencies

The Economy Core uses non‑material currencies:

1. Time

Measured in pressure, not seconds. → Player Five’s mechanic.

2. Alignment

Moral, emotional, or directional coherence.

3. Spirit

Animal resonance, environmental attunement.

4. Contribution

Energy given to the system.

5. Attention

Where the player focuses determines what grows.

4. Economic Zones

The Economy Core behaves differently across zones:

Fib 1–4

Low pressure Low cost High flow Unlocks are gentle

Fib 5–8

High pressure High cost Contribution becomes survival Unlocks require alignment + spirit

Fib 9–13

Extreme pressure Collapse risk Time behaves differently Economy becomes non‑linear

5. Economy Core Behaviors

1. Pressure Accumulation

Every action generates pressure. Pressure creates cost. Cost creates meaning.

2. Drift Interaction

Economy interacts with Drift:

  • high drift → high cost

  • low drift → high flow

Link:

  • [[Drift Layer]]

3. Collapse & Recovery

Economy collapses when:

  • contribution stops

  • alignment fractures

  • pressure exceeds threshold

Recovery requires:

  • re‑alignment

  • re‑contribution

  • re‑flow
