System — The Fundamental Organism of Threshold

A System in Threshold is a living, dynamic, multi‑layer organism composed of:

Nodes

Fib Flowers

Clusters

Signals

Drift fields

Pressure gradients

Resonance structures

A System is not static. It breathes, drifts, collapses, reintegrates, and reforms according to the internal physics of Threshold.

Systems exist at multiple scales:

Node‑scale systems

Flower‑scale systems

Cluster‑scale systems

Realm‑scale systems

Garden‑scale systems

Each scale follows the same underlying laws.

Core System Properties

  1. Coherence

The degree to which the system’s nodes and Flowers align harmonically.

Links: [[Harmony Emergence]] • [[Echo Protocol]]

  1. Pressure

Accumulated structural tension inside the system.

Links: [[Pressure System]] • [[Reduction Cycle]]

  1. Drift

Directional movement caused by imbalance.

Links: [[Drift System]] • [[Cross-Flower Migration]]

  1. Polarity

Crown/Seam alignment determines the system’s stability phase.

Links: [[Crown Structure]] • [[Seam Structure]]

  1. Reintegration

The system’s ability to recover from collapse.

Links: [[Fragmentation]] • [[Reintegration]]

System State Engine

Every System cycles through five macro‑states:

Stable — coherent, low pressure

Resonant — active, aligned, signal‑rich

Drifting — imbalance detected

Collapsing — pressure spike, structural failure

Reintegrating — recovery and reformation
