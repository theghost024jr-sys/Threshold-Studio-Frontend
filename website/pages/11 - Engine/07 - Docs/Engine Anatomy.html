Engine Anatomy

The Engine is organized into five operational layers:

Layer Responsibility
Forces Canonical laws governing node behavior.
Profiles Organ-specific force configuration.
Systems Runtime evaluation and force interaction.
Chambers Local anatomical submodules.
Blueprints and Maps Visual specifications and spatial outputs.

Organs provide Pressure, Resonance, Collapse, Navigation, and Role Gravity profiles. Chambers provide their corresponding local values and paths.
