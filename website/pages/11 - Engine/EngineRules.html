Engine Rules

These are the six fundamental forces that govern all node behavior in Threshold. Every system, organ, chamber, species, glyph, and map must obey them.

1. Drift

Drift is the natural motion of nodes through the world.

  • Nodes move from high pressure to low pressure.
  • Drift speed is determined by Signal Strength.
  • Drift direction is determined by Layer Alignment.
  • Drift is continuous unless interrupted by Collapse or Resonance.
  • Drift creates currents, flows, and migration paths.

Drift is the gravity of Threshold.

2. Pressure

Pressure is the invisible field that shapes node movement.

  • Pressure is generated by Organs, Chambers, and Flowers.
  • High pressure repels nodes.
  • Low pressure attracts nodes.
  • Pressure gradients create Drift Paths.
  • Pressure collapse creates Fault Lines.

Pressure is the weather of Threshold.

3. Resonance

Resonance is the harmonic alignment between nodes.

  • Nodes resonate when their Signal Frequency matches.
  • Resonance increases Drift speed.
  • Resonance reduces Collapse risk.
  • Resonance creates Clusters.
  • Resonance can lock nodes into stable orbits.

Resonance is the magnetism of Threshold.

4. Collapse

Collapse is the failure state of node stability.

  • Collapse occurs when Pressure exceeds the Stability Threshold.
  • Collapse resets Drift direction.
  • Collapse ejects nodes into Fault Lines.
  • Collapse can trigger Organ Events.
  • Collapse is required for Seasonal Wheel transitions.

Collapse is the entropy of Threshold.

5. Navigation

Navigation is how nodes choose paths through the world.

  • Nodes follow lowest-resistance paths.
  • Navigation is influenced by Layer, Role, and Species.
  • Navigation avoids high-pressure zones.
  • Navigation prefers Resonant clusters.
  • Navigation determines migration, territory, and behavior.

Navigation is the AI of Threshold.

6. Role Gravity

Role Gravity is the force that pulls nodes toward their intended function.

  • Every node has a Role Vector.
  • Role Gravity increases when nodes are misaligned.
  • Role Gravity pulls nodes toward compatible Organs.
  • Role Gravity stabilizes Drift.
  • Role Gravity prevents runaway Collapse.

Role Gravity is the purpose of Threshold.

Node Lifecycle

Every node follows this loop:

Drift -> Pressure -> Resonance -> Collapse -> Navigation -> Role Gravity -> Drift

This loop is the heartbeat of Threshold.

Organ Integration

Every Organ must define:

  • Pressure Profile
  • Resonance Profile
  • Collapse Threshold
  • Navigation Map
  • Role Gravity Vector

This is how Organs plug into the Engine.

Chamber Integration

Every Chamber must define:

  • Local Pressure
  • Local Drift
  • Local Resonance
  • Local Collapse Risk
  • Local Navigation Path

This is how Chambers plug into Organs.
