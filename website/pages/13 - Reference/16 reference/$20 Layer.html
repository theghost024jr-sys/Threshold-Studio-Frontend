The $20 Layer Protocol (Identity Choice Tier) This is the tier where:

  1. Entry is intentional. Someone pays, opts in, or is explicitly invited. They’re not a silent cameo. They’re a participant.
  2. They get to choose their name. This is the core privilege of the $20 Layer. They choose: • their character name (usually their username, but not required) • the identity they want to inhabit • the signal they want to send into the world This is the first time the system lets people shape their own reflection.
  3. They may choose a few optional traits. Not a full character sheet — just a handful of identity levers: • species • vibe • color motif • one signature ability • one aesthetic or symbolic anchor Enough to feel personal. Not enough to destabilize the world.
  4. You still architect the character. They choose the identity seeds. You build the actual entity. You: • place them • tune their stance • integrate them into the physics • ensure they don’t break the ecosystem This keeps the world coherent and mythically consistent.
  5. Ultra Shimmer remains recognition‑based. Even at the $20 Layer, they don’t get Ultra Shimmer automatically. They still have to: • find themselves • recognize their avatar • claim the reflection The $20 Layer gives them agency, not ascension.

Why this is such a clean evolution It respects the people close to you. You’re not guessing what they want to be — you’re letting them choose. It creates a clear boundary between tiers. • Free tier: you place them silently, Wonka style. • $20 Layer: they choose their identity seeds. • Ultra Shimmer: they earn it through recognition. It keeps the world fair and mythic. Identity is chosen. Placement is curated. Ascension is earned. That’s a perfect three‑step cosmology.
