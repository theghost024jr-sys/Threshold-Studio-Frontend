1. THE_STAR_THAT_KEPT_CHANGING_ITS_MIND.md

Designation: Variable.
The star cannot decide what it wants to be.

Observed states:

  • briefly a red giant
  • then a white dwarf
  • then something unclassified
  • then nothing at all
  • then back again

Astronomers report the star emits a faint, frustrated sigh every time it shifts.

The current theory:
It is experiencing a “cosmic midlife crisis.”

2. THE_GALAXY_THAT_SPUN_BACKWARDS.md

A spiral galaxy rotating in the wrong direction.

Consequences:

  • time inside the galaxy feels “slightly embarrassed”
  • gravitational arms curl inward like it’s trying to hide
  • stars drift out of formation as if unsure where to stand
  • the central black hole refuses to comment

The galaxy is not dangerous.
It is simply confused.

3. THE_PLANET_THAT_WASN’T_SURE_IF_IT_EXISTED.md

Mass: fluctuating

Atmosphere: optional

Geological scans reveal the crust is made of:

The planet has asked not to be perceived until it figures itself out.
