That alone is enough to scare certain archetypes.

The Threat‑Assessors They want clean binaries: safe/dangerous, good/bad, on/off. Triangles introduce a third force, which breaks their mental model. They fear the loss of control more than the shape itself. C. The Avoiders Triangles demand participation. You can’t sit outside a triangle — you’re either in one of the roles or you’re not part of the system. Avoiders fear being pulled into the geometry.
