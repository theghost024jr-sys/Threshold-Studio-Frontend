  2. The Triad in Motion The triad is not static — it’s a rotating system. Any node can be the initiator: If the Player initiates: • player drift → NPC strain → environmental distortion If the NPC initiates: • NPC collapse → environmental rupture → player identity shift If the Environment initiates: • zone pressure → player behavioral threshold → NPC role inversion The triad is recursive — pressure moves until it finds resolution.

  3. The Governing Law Pressure moves along the triad until someone or something crosses a threshold. That crossing: • transforms the node • reshapes the edges • reconfigures the entire triad This is why the world feels alive.
