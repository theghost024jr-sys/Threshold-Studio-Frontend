  • originates at the hub
  • travels through the petals
  • stretches past the outer edge
  • enters the inter‑flower void
  • dissipates unless it meets another pulse

This outward stretch is the world “testing the boundary.”
It’s not enough to open a path — but it’s enough to touch.

When two flowers pulse at the same time (or close enough in world‑time), their echoes can:

  • meet

  • resonate
  • cancel
  • amplify

This is the first hint that the flowers are not isolated. They are neighbors in a larger topology.
