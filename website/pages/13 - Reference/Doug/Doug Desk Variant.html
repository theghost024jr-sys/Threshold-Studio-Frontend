Doug (Desk Variant) Municipal Shape Safety Technician, Office‑Bound Edition Doug with a desk is a whole different flavor of municipal energy. He’s not out in the streets yelling at chalk art. He’s in a small, windowless office doing paperwork that absolutely no one asked for. His Workspace • A beige metal desk from 1979 • A drawer that sticks unless you jiggle it just right • A stack of “Shape Incident Reports” he created himself • A mug that says “#1 Technician” (he bought it for himself) • A dying plant he waters with the same seriousness he applies to geometry enforcement His Daily Routine • Arrives early because “shapes don’t take days off” • Logs every triangle sighting in a spreadsheet titled “Acute Threat Index” • Sends emails to the town council that begin with “Per my last 14 messages…” • Draws diagrams that are technically wrong but passionately labeled • Eats lunch at his desk: ham sandwich, no crusts (too pointy) His Personality at the Desk • Extremely earnest • Mildly overwhelmed • Proud of his filing system • Thinks the copier is “plotting something” • Believes paperwork is the first line of defense against geometric chaos How he sees his role Doug thinks of himself as the administrative backbone of shape safety. He believes that without his reports, the town would be overrun by rogue angles. No one else believes this. But Doug does. And honestly? That’s enough to keep him going.
