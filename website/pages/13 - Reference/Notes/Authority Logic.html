Authority in Threshold is not force, hierarchy, or command. Authority is what the system accepts as meaningful, contributive, or structurally clarifying. Authority operates through four channels:

  1. Acceptance Only what is recognized by the system becomes binding. Unacknowledged actions, roles, or claims simply do not take effect.
  2. Meaning Authority determines what something means in context. Meaning is not inherent — it is conferred by the system’s interpretive stance.
  3. Contribution Anything that has genuinely shaped the system — through creation, refinement, friction, or clarity — gains legitimacy. Contribution is a form of authority.
  4. Collapse / Compost Breakdown, dissolution, and return-to-substrate are also contributions. Collapse clarifies structure, releases stagnation, and returns material to the system. This too is recognized as authority.

Shortest Version (for the Index) Authority = what is accepted, what has meaning, what has contributed, and what has clarified through collapse.
