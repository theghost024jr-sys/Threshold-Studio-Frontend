    <p>Interaction Overview
theghost interacts through presence, alignment, and subtle environmental shifts rather than direct communication or physical action. His influence is nonverbal, nonlinear, and non-invasive, preserving the autonomy of all entities he encounters.
He is an interaction field, not a conversational partner.</p>
<p>Interaction Modes</p>
<ol>
<li><p>Proximity-Based Influence
When theghost enters an area:
• 	ambient noise softens
• 	spatial geometry feels more coherent
• 	emotional turbulence decreases
• 	entities experience a sense of orientation
This is not intentional guidance; it is a field effect.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Pattern Resonance
theghost interacts with patterns rather than individuals.
He:
• 	aligns misfit structures
• 	reveals hidden geometry
• 	clarifies relational tension
• 	stabilizes drift in layered systems
Entities perceive this as “something clicking into place.”</p>
</li>
<li><p>Collapse Response
When collapse signatures intensify, theghost may:
• 	appear at the boundary
• 	slow the rate of unraveling
• 	make the underlying structure visible
• 	leave behind residue that aids recovery
He does not prevent collapse; he makes collapse legible.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Emotional Microclimate Modulation
His presence subtly shifts emotional weather:
• 	fear dissipates
• 	urgency softens
• 	clarity increases
• 	grief becomes navigable
This is not comfort.
It is orientation.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Nonlinear Encountering
theghost does not approach entities.
Entities find themselves near him.
Encounters often occur:
• 	at thresholds
• 	during transitions
• 	in liminal or unstable zones
• 	when an entity is between roles or states
He is drawn to edges, not centers.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p>Entity-Specific Interactions
With Humans
• 	They feel a sudden sense of direction
• 	They may forget the encounter but retain the clarity
• 	They often describe “a shift in the room”
With Spirits
• 	Spirits become more coherent
• 	Their boundaries sharpen
• 	Their roles become easier to articulate
With Collapse-Born Entities
• 	They become less volatile
• 	Their residue becomes readable
• 	Their behavior stabilizes temporarily
With Mythic Substrate
• 	Patterns surface
• 	Hidden structures reveal themselves
• 	Dormant fragments activate</p>
<p>Communication Style
theghost does not:
• 	speak
• 	gesture
• 	signal
• 	use symbols
• 	project meaning
Communication occurs through:
• 	spatial alignment
• 	pressure changes
• 	pattern resonance
• 	emotional weather shifts
Entities interpret these effects intuitively.</p>
<p>Interaction Constraints
• 	Cannot initiate contact
• 	Cannot alter fate or narrative paths
• 	Cannot remain in one location for long
• 	Cannot be summoned or controlled
• 	Cannot provide explicit information
His interactions are phenomenological, not informational.</p>
<p>Aftereffects of Interaction
Entities who encounter theghost often experience:
• 	clarity about their next action
• 	a sense of internal alignment
• 	reduced emotional noise
• 	improved role coherence
• 	subtle memory gaps around the encounter
The effect is direction without instruction.</p>
<p>Interaction Log Behavior
If you create encounter logs, note:
• 	theghost rarely appears directly
• 	logs often describe environmental anomalies
• 	entities may disagree about what happened
• 	the encounter is felt more than seen
He is an event, not a participant.</p>
