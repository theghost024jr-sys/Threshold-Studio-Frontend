  2. THE FIVE PRIMARY NAVIGATION MODES Each phase requires a distinct navigation strategy.

A. Coherence Navigation (Circle) Move with the gathering Primary strategy: Follow inward drift and radial patterns. Movement principles: • stay within coherence pockets • use radial paths to conserve energy • align with harmonic resonance Hazards: • saturation zones (over‑stability) • rigidity traps Best actions: • stabilize • gather resources • map the environment Transition preparation: Watch for thickening warmth → Tension forming.

B. Tension Navigation (Gate) Move with precision and restraint Primary strategy: Minimize resistance; avoid pressure corridors. Movement principles: • stay out of vertical drift funnels • reduce noise and movement • maintain structural awareness Hazards: • overpressure fractures • resonance spikes • compression corridors Best actions: • hold position • brace intelligently • prepare for rapid change Transition preparation: Monitor for resonance instability → Collapse imminent.

C. Collapse Navigation (Crown) Move with the release, not against it Primary strategy: Ride scatter drift; avoid turbulence pockets. Movement principles: • move diagonally across collapse fronts • avoid outward pressure bursts • use dissolution to escape rigid structures Hazards: • turbulence • debris shedding • resonance disorientation Best actions: • release unnecessary load • stay flexible • move lightly and unpredictably Transition preparation: Watch for slowing turbulence → Void forming.

D. Void Navigation (Tree) Move minimally; conserve energy Primary strategy: Stay still unless movement is necessary. Movement principles: • follow downward drift only if required • avoid disturbing stillness • reduce metabolic and cognitive load Hazards: • deep void saturation • disorientation from low resonance • energy depletion Best actions: • rest • observe • allow residue to settle Transition preparation: Sense for warming pockets → Renewal awakening.

E. Renewal Navigation (Lantern) Move with curiosity and upward drift Primary strategy: Follow rising warmth and flickering resonance. Movement principles: • take exploratory routes • follow spark drift spirals • move lightly but consistently Hazards: • premature ignition zones • unstable early forms • fragile structures Best actions: • initiate • prototype • reconnect with coherence pathways Transition preparation: Look for clustering sprouts → Coherence returning.
