Garden Visual Specification

Garden is a world-state renderer, not a decorative mark. Its six animated layers map one-to-one to the canonical Garden feeds.

Layers

  • #garden-growth-core breathes with growth and chamber seeding.
  • #garden-adjacency-ring rotates according to environmental adjacency density.
  • #garden-drift-arcs accelerate and brighten as drifting nodes are captured.
  • #garden-pressure-ring thickens and intensifies as pressure spreads across biomes.
  • #garden-biome-petals contains the Fib Flower's 13 outer petals and carries biome transition color.
  • #garden-signal-halo pulses or bursts when the environment communicates state changes.

Geometry

The glyph uses a centered 512 by 512 field. Thirteen petals form the outer Fib Flower ring. The center remains rooted while the environmental rings can rotate, breathe, tighten, and change state independently.

Motion

All motion is looped, bounded, and frame-smoothed by garden-feed-calibration.json. Reduced-motion clients receive a stable rendered state with animation disabled.
