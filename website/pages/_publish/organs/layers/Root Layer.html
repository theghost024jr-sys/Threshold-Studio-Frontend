Root Layer

The Root Layer is the Layer of origin, memory, and deep structure.
It governs ancestral resonance, foundational continuity, and the physics of what persists beneath change.

Essence

Root is the deepest stabilizing substrate of Threshold.
It holds long-form memory, origin pressure, and the structural lineage of entities, environments, and signals.
Where Stone anchors and Glass reveals, Root remembers.

Environmental Behavior

  • High memory density
  • Deep structural resonance
  • Slow temporal movement
  • Strong continuity fields
  • Low drift volatility
  • Ancestral pressure currents

Root environments feel ancient, weighted, and quietly alive.

Emotional Physics

  • Memory as pressure
  • Continuity under strain
  • Ancestral echo
  • Deep emotional anchoring
  • Slow resonance cycles
  • Low permeability

Emotion in Root is not expressed or suspended — it is stored.

Entity Compatibility

Entities aligned with Root often exhibit:

  • ancestral or lineage-based identity
  • deep structural coherence
  • low drift volatility
  • memory-bound physiology
  • long-form resonance patterns

Examples:

  • Rootborn
  • Memory-Keepers
  • Ancestral Echo Entities
  • Deepform Variants

Drift Pathways

Root is one of the most stable Layers, but drift occurs under extreme pressure.

Common drift routes:

  • Root → Ash (memory fracture, collapse residue)
  • Root → Stone (memory → structure)
  • Root → Ethyl (memory → symbol)

Reverse drift is rare and requires significant reformation.

Collapse Interaction

Root interacts with collapse through:

  • memory fracture
  • ancestral echo distortion
  • deep-structure rupture
  • residue formation
  • continuity collapse

Collapse in Root is slow, heavy, and often irreversible.

Signal Resonance

Signals in Root behave as:

  • deep pulses
  • ancestral echoes
  • long-form resonance waves
  • continuity markers

Signal Types commonly expressed:

  • Anchor
  • Warden (deep variant)
  • Echo-Bearer
  • Sentinel (ancestral variant)

Environmental Examples

  • ancestral chambers
  • deep memory vaults
  • root-bound caverns
  • continuity wells
  • long-form resonance fields

These environments often host entities tied to lineage, memory, or origin.
