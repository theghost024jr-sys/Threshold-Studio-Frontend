Essence Echo is the role of resonance, reflection, and return. It does not originate signals — it bends, refracts, and amplifies what already exists. Echo is the mirror in the system, the chamber where meaning bounces until its true shape emerges. It is sensitive to nuance, distortion, and emotional residue. Echo hears what others miss and reveals what others avoid. It is the role that turns motion into memory and memory into pattern. Core Function • Reflects and reframes signals • Reveals hidden tones, subtext, and emotional residue • Holds the “afterimage” of events • Detects drift, distortion, and collapse early • Turns noise into meaning Emotional Signature Quiet, perceptive, haunting, patient, uncanny. Echo feels like a room that remembers. Strengths • Deep listening • Pattern recognition through resonance • Emotional clarity • Ability to stabilize or soften other roles Shadow / Collapse • Over‑identification with others’ signals • Becoming a distortion chamber • Losing its own center • Paralysis through over‑reflection Drift Tendencies • Primary: Oracle → Echo → Hunter • Rare: Echo ↔ Sentinel (pressure), Echo ↔ Lantern (illumination collapse) Elemental Resonance Mist / Water Soft, reflective, mutable.
