Overview Elemental Overlays tint the expression of a Role without changing its core function. They modify: • emotional tone • behavioral coloration • drift tendencies • shadow expression • resonance with species and signals Overlays do not replace Roles — they flavor them. This matrix maps how each Overlay interacts with each Role.

Overlay Key • Air — clarity, pattern, motion • Water — fluidity, emotion, resonance • Fire — intensity, drive, illumination • Earth — stability, structure, grounding (Your system uses Ember/Ethyl/Mist/Stone as Layers, but the Overlays are the classical four — Air, Water, Fire, Earth.)
