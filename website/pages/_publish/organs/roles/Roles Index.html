  1. Overview Roles are archetypal patterns that move through Threshold. Each role expresses: • a home zone (outer map) • a home node (inner engine) • a behavioral signature (stable, stress, break) • a movement pattern (preferred pathways) • a narrative arc (how the role transforms) Roles are not characters — they are forces, stances, or modes of being. This index defines the architecture of the roles layer.

Collapse Behavior How the role breaks. Elemental Resonance Optional symbolic layer. Zone Alignment Which world zone the role belongs to. Node Alignment Which inner node the role expresses. Pathway Pattern How the role moves through the system. Mythic Resonance The narrative or archetypal echo. Links • Home zone • Home node • Related roles • Pathways Index • Behaviors Index This template ensures every role is structurally identical.
