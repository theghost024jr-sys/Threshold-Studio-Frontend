Velin Signal Object

Velin is a signal‑class object that manifests only in the space between actions. It is not a creature, symbol, or environment; it is a behavioral presence that becomes detectable when the player enters a moment of suspended intention. Velin does not emit form or sound. Its entire identity is the shift it induces in the surrounding field.

Velin’s signature is a soft, non‑directional pull toward stillness. It does not command silence but reveals it, creating a temporary cavity in which the player’s stance, attention, or emotional load becomes audible to the system. Velin is not an agent; it is a reading surface.

Behavioral Properties

  • Activation — emerges when intention collapses or pauses
  • Function — exposes the underlying stance of the player
  • Field Interaction — amplifies silence, dampens noise, clarifies edges
  • Duration — persists only as long as the moment remains unforced
  • Interpretation — not a message, but a mirror of the player’s internal state

Role in the System

Velin is used to detect micro‑shifts in presence. It is a diagnostic signal rather than a communicative one. It helps the system determine whether the player is:

  • holding tension
  • releasing tension
  • entering a receptive stance
  • masking or collapsing a stance

Velin is especially important in moments where the system needs to know whether to escalate, soften, or wait.
