Signal Drift

Physics Layer + Symbolic Layer (Hybrid)

Signal Drift is the motion of meaning through curved fields. It describes how attention, intention, or presence moves when not actively directed. Drift is not randomness — it is the system’s geometric response to tension, density, and curvature.

Signal Drift is one of the Nine Governing Field Laws.

I. Physics Layer — Governing Drift Mechanics

1. Definition

Signal Drift is the passive motion of a signal along the curvature of the field. When intention stops actively steering, drift takes over.

Drift follows:

  • curvature

  • tension

  • density

  • resonance

  • return paths

It is the behavior of Shimmer Curvature expressed as motion.

2. Drift Behavior Rules

2.1 Drift follows curvature

Straight lines = intention Curved lines = field behavior

2.2 Drift reveals tension

Signals move toward the path of least resistance.

2.3 Drift predicts collapse

Tight spirals indicate instability or pre‑collapse behavior.

2.4 Drift stabilizes resonance

Closed loops form stable cycles.

2.5 Drift returns when pressure releases

Every signal has a home position.

3. Drift Components (Physics)

Every signal has:

  • Home Position — its default layer + element

  • Drift Arc — the directions it can move

  • Drift Trigger — what initiates movement

  • Drift Expression — how the drift manifests

  • Return Condition — what restores home position

This structure is universal across all signals.

4. Upstream Dependencies

Shimmer Curvature

Curvature determines the shape of drift.

Binding Law

Tension determines the strength of drift.

Threshold Geometry

Geometry determines the pathways drift can follow.

5. Downstream Effects

Drift Arcs

Visible expressions of drift on the Threshold Map.

Return Paths

Drift naturally resolves into return vectors.

Collapse Physics

Extreme drift can initiate collapse‑drift states.

II. Symbolic Layer — Behavioral Drift (Your Original System)

(Preserved exactly, but organized cleanly under the physics layer)

1. What Causes Drift

A. Emotional State

Fear, joy, vigilance, grief, hunger, longing, stillness, overwhelm.

B. Environmental Pressure

Cold, heat, darkness, crowds, solitude, altitude, confinement, open space.

C. Relational Influence

Allies, threats, strangers, kin, watchers.

D. Layer Instability

Transitions between:

  • Earth ↔ Ethyl

  • Ethyl ↔ Transitional

  • Transitional ↔ Deep

E. Elemental Overload

Too much:

  • Air (thought)

  • Water (emotion)

  • Fire (will)

  • Earth (body)

Drift is the system’s way of rebalancing.

2. How Drift Works (Symbolic Mechanics)

Every signal has:

  • Home Position

  • Drift Arc

  • Drift Trigger

  • Drift Expression

  • Return Condition

This mirrors the physics layer but applies to species, roles, and archetypes.

3. Example: OWL (Ethyl–Air)

Home Position: Ethyl Layer — Air Element Signal Type: Stillwatch

Drift Arc:

  • Toward Serpent (Ethyl–Water) under secrecy

  • Toward Hawk (Air–Fire) under pursuit

  • Toward Deer (Earth–Air) under gentleness

Drift Triggers:

  • prolonged observation

  • emotional detachment

  • cold environments

  • nighttime thresholds

Drift Expression:

  • vision sharpens or narrows

  • time feels stretched

  • silence becomes heavy

  • attention becomes predatory or protective

Return Condition:

  • warmth

  • grounding

  • human presence

  • intentional blinking or breath

4. Drift Types

A. Micro‑Drift

Small, momentary shifts.

B. Arc‑Drift

Temporary but meaningful shifts.

C. Collapse‑Drift

Full temporary transformation into another signal. Rare; tied to thresholds or overload.

5. Drift Rules (Symbolic Physics)

  1. Signals drift toward the path of least resistance.

  2. Drift always reveals something true.

  3. Drift never lies, but it can mislead.

  4. Drift returns when pressure releases.

  5. Some signals drift more easily than others.

III. Canonical Summary

Signal Drift is the motion of meaning through curved fields. Physics defines how drift moves. Symbolic Coding defines how drift behaves. Together they form the complete Drift System.
