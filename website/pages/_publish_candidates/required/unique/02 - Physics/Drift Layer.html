Drift Layer

Category: 01 – Physics Color: #FF6B4A (Thermal Orange) Role: The motion‑emotion substrate of Threshold — a field where movement, feeling, pressure, and meaning interact as a single physics.

The Drift Layer is the first layer above Environment and the foundation for all higher systems. It is the world’s motion field, emotional field, and resonance field combined.

Everything that moves, feels, or shifts passes through Drift.

1. Definition

The Drift Layer is:

  • a continuous motion field

  • shaped by emotional gradients

  • responsive to pressure

  • sensitive to identity

  • readable as weather

  • navigable as terrain

  • distortable by collapse

It is the softest, most expressive, and most reactive layer in the vertical stack.

2. Core Properties

1. Motion–Emotion Coupling

Movement and emotion are not separate. A change in one produces a change in the other.

2. Pressure Sensitivity

Drift thickens or thins based on:

  • emotional charge

  • narrative tension

  • environmental density

  • collapse residue

3. Resonance Patterns

Drift forms patterns:

  • spirals

  • waves

  • pulses

  • hollows

  • arcs

These patterns are readable by Drift‑aligned species and Drift‑Walkers.

4. Layer Responsiveness

Drift reacts instantly to:

  • fear

  • anticipation

  • curiosity

  • symbolic density

  • collapse pressure

3. Drift States

The Drift Layer expresses four canonical states:

1. Baseline Drift

Calm, directional, readable. Used for navigation and orientation.

2. Pulse Drift

Rhythmic motion triggered by emotional charge.

3. Ripple Drift

Oscillations caused by symbolic density or meaning‑pressure.

4. Turbulent Drift

Chaotic motion caused by:

  • collapse residue

  • high emotional charge

  • narrative pressure

  • environmental instability

5. Drift Behaviors

1. Drift Pull

Soft directional force that suggests movement.

2. Drift Lean

Environmental tilt that warns or guides.

3. Drift Bloom

Sudden expansion of emotional‑motion density.

4. Drift Hollow

A low‑pressure pocket left by collapse or trauma.

5. Drift Spiral

A self‑reinforcing motion loop — precursor to collapse.
