Glass Layer

The Glass Layer is the Layer of clarity, reflection, and precise revelation.
It governs transparency, sharp boundaries, truth exposure, and the physics of seeing without distortion.

Essence

Glass reveals what other Layers obscure.
It sharpens edges, defines form, and exposes the underlying structure of meaning.
Where Ethyl drifts and Mist suspends, Glass clarifies.

Environmental Behavior

  • High visual and conceptual clarity
  • Sharp boundary formation
  • Reflective surfaces that reveal hidden states
  • Low emotional permeability
  • High structural precision
  • Stable resonance fields

Glass environments often feel still, bright, and exact.

Emotional Physics

  • Revelation through transparency
  • Precision of feeling
  • Boundary definition
  • Emotional distillation
  • Low drift volatility
  • High interpretive clarity

Emotion in Glass is not fluid or suspended — it is seen.

Entity Compatibility

Entities aligned with Glass often exhibit:

  • sharply defined physiology
  • reflective or prismatic features
  • high interpretive capacity
  • low symbolic drift
  • strong boundary presence

Examples:

  • Seers
  • Reflectors
  • Truth-Bearers
  • Glassborn Variants

Drift Pathways

Glass is a stabilizing but revelation-driven Layer.

Common drift routes:

  • Glass → Mist (clarity → permeability)
  • Glass → Ethyl (precision → intuition)
  • Glass → Stone (revelation → structure)

Reverse drift occurs when clarity becomes overwhelming or collapses into ambiguity.

Collapse Interaction

Glass interacts with collapse through:

  • shattering events
  • boundary fragmentation
  • reflective distortion
  • truth overload
  • prismatic residue

Collapse in Glass is sudden and often violent in its clarity.

Signal Resonance

Signals in Glass behave as:

  • sharp pulses
  • reflective echoes
  • truth exposures
  • boundary markers

Signal Types commonly expressed:

  • Seer
  • Anchor
  • Reflector
  • Warden (clarity variant)

Environmental Examples

  • mirrored halls
  • prismatic chambers
  • truth-bound corridors
  • reflective plains
  • clarity wells

These environments often host entities that reveal, interpret, or expose.
