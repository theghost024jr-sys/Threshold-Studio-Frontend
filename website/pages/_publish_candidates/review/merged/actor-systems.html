Actor Systems

Category: 06 – Actors Color: #FFD93D (Sunflower Gold) Role: The eight structural subsystems that allow an Actor to maintain coherence, express agency, metabolize drift, and interact with Threshold.

Actor Systems are not behaviors and not identities. They are the mechanical substrate that makes an Actor function.

Every Actor — primary, secondary, emergent, environmental — is built from these eight systems.

1. Boundary System

Defines the Actor’s shape, membrane, and interaction permissions.

Functions:

  • maintains structural integrity

  • regulates pressure exchange

  • prevents drift‑bleed

  • defines inside/outside

Functions:
