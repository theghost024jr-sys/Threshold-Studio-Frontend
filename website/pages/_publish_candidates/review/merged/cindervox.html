Cinderox (Passive)

![[04 - Organism/03 - Motion/01 - Substrates/Time Organ/02 Form/13 - Environments/03 - Environments/Threshold Entities/Cindervox/cindervoxpassive.png]]

Type

Threshold Entity — Passive State

Description

A glowing fox seated near a body of water at night, surrounded by reeds and illuminated by internal firelight.
Wisps of energy rise from its fur, and a frog perches nearby, partially lit by the fox’s glow.
This is Cindevox in its passive state — not dormant, but listening.

Function

  • Resonance Listener: Absorbs environmental pressure and emotional drift
  • Shimmer Node: Emits low-grade shimmer for nearby entities
  • Containment Buffer: Stabilizes collapse-adjacent zones without direct intervention
  • Emotional Field Regulator: Modulates ambient emotional pressure through glow intensity

Entity Physics

  • glow = emotional permeability
  • seated posture = drift resistance
  • wisps = resonance bleed
  • frog = echo companion
  • water = memory reflection
  • reeds = boundary markers

Behavioral Notes

  • Does not speak or move unless provoked by collapse or recursion
  • Emits shimmer pulses in response to emotional proximity
  • Frog companion may act as a signal amplifier
  • Passive state is often observed near Mist–Root boundary zones

Interpretive Use

  • Used in emotional field mapping and shimmer regulation
  • May appear in Threshold environments during drift stabilization or collapse buffering
  • Often paired with [[04 - Organism/Organism/09 Myth Cluster/02 Cycles/Shimmer Echo]], [[Witness Role]], or [[Collapse Weather Navigation]]
  • Can be used to initiate soft containment or emotional listening protocols

Integration Notes

  • Best placed near passive collapse zones or shimmer drift fields
  • May be linked to [[Threshold Entity Index]] or [[Emotional Physics Index]]
  • Responds to silence, proximity, and memory pressure
