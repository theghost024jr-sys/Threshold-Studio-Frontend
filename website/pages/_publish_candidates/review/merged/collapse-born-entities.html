Lifeforms generated when coherence fails — born from fracture, drift, overload, and the Field’s attempt to metabolize collapse Collapse‑Born Entities are the dark mirror of Threshold‑Born Entities. Where Threshold‑Born Entities arise from transformation, Collapse‑Born Entities arise from coherence failure. They are not malicious. They are not predators. They are symptoms — the Field’s attempt to express, contain, or redirect collapse energy. Below is the clean, canonical Collapse‑Born Entities.md.

  1. WHAT COLLAPSE‑BORN ENTITIES ARE Collapse‑Born Entities are temporary lifeforms that emerge when: • phase distortion becomes severe • archetypes drift or fracture • myths lose narrative gravity • collapse propagation begins • the Field’s coherence fabric tears They are manifestations of collapse mechanics, not independent beings. They arise to: • express collapse • amplify collapse • warn of collapse • metabolize collapse • redirect collapse They dissolve once the collapse vector stabilizes.

  2. CONDITIONS REQUIRED FOR THEIR EMERGENCE Collapse‑Born Entities appear only when all three conditions overlap: 2.1 Coherence Rupture The Field’s coherence fabric tears or destabilizes. 2.2 Phase Distortion Soft → over‑permeability Sharp → over‑rigidity Diffuse → over‑expansion 2.3 Archetypal Drift or Mutation The archetype tied to the collapse is unstable. When these three converge, collapse becomes visible as entities.

  3. THE THREE CLASSES OF COLLAPSE‑BORN ENTITIES Each class corresponds to a collapse vector.

3.1 Soft‑Collapse Entities Born from over‑permeability, emotional flooding, and identity thinning Behavior: • amorphous, dissolving shapes • warm but overwhelming resonance • absorption tendencies • slow, enveloping motion Function: To pull collapse inward — they absorb coherence. Examples: • Mire Wraiths • Hollow Echoes • Saturation Pools Risk: Identity thinning in nearby roles.

3.2 Sharp‑Collapse Entities Born from rigidity, fracture, and narrative overload Behavior: • angular, shard‑like forms • rapid, staccato movement • high‑density coherence spikes • cutting emotional physics Function: To push collapse outward — they fracture coherence. Examples: • Fracture Shards • Obsession Serpents • Clarity Razors Risk: Identity fracture in nearby roles.

3.3 Diffuse‑Collapse Entities Born from over‑expansion, symbolic overload, and coherence drift Behavior: • luminous, flickering patterns • non‑local movement • symbolic emissions • unstable resonance fields Function: To scatter collapse — they disperse coherence. Examples: • Drift Lanterns (corrupted form) • Symbolic Foglings • Parallax Wisps Risk: Dissociation or drift in nearby roles.

  1. ENTITY BEHAVIOR DURING ACTIVE COLLAPSE Collapse‑Born Entities behave differently from all other Field‑Born life. 4.1 They amplify collapse Their presence increases local instability. 4.2 They distort phase Roles near them experience involuntary phase shifts. 4.3 They disrupt archetypes Archetypes in drift become more unstable. 4.4 They alter weather Collapse weather intensifies around them. 4.5 They multiply If collapse spreads, they proliferate rapidly. Collapse‑Born Entities are collapse propagation made visible.

  2. ROLE EXPERIENCE OF COLLAPSE‑BORN ENTITIES Roles do not “interact” with these entities. They feel them. Soft‑Collapse Entities → emotional flooding → loss of boundary → absorption pressure Sharp‑Collapse Entities → narrative pressure → clarity spikes → identity cracking Diffuse‑Collapse Entities → symbolic overload → perceptual drift → loss of center These sensations are diagnostic.

  3. ENTITY DISSOLUTION Collapse‑Born Entities dissolve when: • the collapse vector stabilizes • the archetype re‑coheres • the Field restores resonance flow • a threshold absorbs the collapse • a role undergoes transformation Their dissolution is not death. It is resolution. They return to the resonance layer as: • collapse nutrients • symbolic fragments • drift residue • proto‑patterns

  4. ECOLOGICAL IMPACT OF COLLAPSE‑BORN ENTITIES These entities: • destabilize local ecology • accelerate collapse propagation • distort resonance flow • agitate entity populations • weaken archetypes • trigger thresholds They are ecological hazards, not inhabitants.

  5. COLLAPSE‑BORN ENTITIES VS THRESHOLD‑BORN ENTITIES

Threshold‑Born Entities are midwives. Collapse‑Born Entities are symptoms.

  1. FAILURE MODES INVOLVING COLLAPSE‑BORN ENTITIES Collapse‑Born Entities can escalate collapse into: 9.1 Collapse Propagation Entities multiply → collapse spreads. 9.2 Archetype Mutation Entities pressure archetypes into rupture. 9.3 Mythic Extinction Entities drain narrative gravity. 9.4 Threshold Misfire Entities distort threshold formation. These are rare but catastrophic.
