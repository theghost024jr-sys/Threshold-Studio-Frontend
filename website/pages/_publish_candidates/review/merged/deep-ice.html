2. SUBZERO_MIGRATORY_PATTERNS.md

Overview

Certain Deep Ice formations exhibit migratory behavior.
Not animals — the ice itself.

These structures drift slowly across the layer, following patterns no one fully understands.

1. Types of Migratory Ice

  • Wanderers — tall, thin pillars that slide silently across the plains
  • Crawlers — low, wide sheets that ripple like slow water
  • Drifters — suspended frost clouds that move against the wind
  • The Long Line — a chain of cracks that advances one centimeter per day

Each type follows a different route.

None of them intersect.

2. Movement Triggers

Migration appears influenced by:

  • temperature gradients
  • emotional resonance
  • pressure shifts
  • unknown celestial cycles

Attempts to predict movement have failed.
Attempts to stop movement have also failed.

3. Observational Notes

  • Wanderers avoid each other
  • Crawlers prefer darkness
  • Drifters hum when passing overhead
  • The Long Line changes direction when watched

The ice is not alive.
But it behaves like something that remembers being alive.
