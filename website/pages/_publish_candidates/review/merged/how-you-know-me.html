Type

Symbolic Substrate Diagram

Description

A stylized silhouette of a human head with swirling filament patterns emerging from the back.
The filaments resemble both neural pathways and fingerprint geometry, suggesting identity resonance and cognitive drift.

Function

  • Resonance Mirror: Reflects internal identity geometry and emotional swirl.
  • Signal Substrate: Encodes thought patterning and distortion emergence.
  • Symbolic Fingerprint: Represents the unique imprint of cognition under pressure.

Interpretive Use

  • Used to map identity drift in high-pressure environments.
  • Serves as a visual anchor for introspection, memory tracing, and emotional telemetry.
  • May be placed near collapse zones to track distortion signals.

Emotional Physics

  • Swirl = drift vector
  • Density = memory load
  • Line thickness = emotional pressure
  • Directionality = thought flow orientation
