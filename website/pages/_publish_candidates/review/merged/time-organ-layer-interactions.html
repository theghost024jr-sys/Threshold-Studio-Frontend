Overview

The Time Organ is anchored in the Shimmer Layer but interacts with all seven layers. Each layer influences a different aspect of temporal behavior.

Layer‑by‑Layer Interaction Map

  • High Mist density increases temporal ambiguity
  • Organ compensates by increasing compression

Behavioral effect:

  • perception slows
  • time feels “fogged” or softened

02 Stone — Stability & Structure

  • Prevents collapse during overload

  • reduced drift

03 Ember — Activation & Catalysis

  • Ember accelerates temporal flow
  • Enhances insight and symbolic interpretation
  • Can cause overexpansion if unregulated

Behavioral effect:

  • heightened anticipation
  • risk of temporal overshoot

04 Ethyl — Flow & Coherence

  • Ethyl smooths temporal transitions
  • Supports phase realignment
  • Reduces oscillation

Behavioral effect:

  • improved recovery

05 Drift — Motion & Helical Dynamics

  • Drift directly feeds the Drift Nerve
  • Controls phase drift and temporal deviation

Behavioral effect:

06 Shimmer — Symbolic Density
