Random Topic: The Town That Banned Triangles

There’s a fictional town called Greeley’s Bend where triangles are illegal.

Not dangerous.
Not cursed.
Just… banned.

Why?

Because in 1974, a geometry teacher claimed triangles were “too pointy for civic harmony,” and the town council — exhausted, underfunded, and three hours into a meeting — voted yes without reading the proposal.

Consequences:

  • Pizza must be served as squares.
  • Road signs are circles or rectangles only.
  • Kids learn geometry with “the shape that must not be named.”
  • Every year, the mayor inspects roofs to ensure they are “compliance quadrilaterals.”

The weird part:

People swear they still see triangles sometimes.
In shadows.
In reflections.
In the way birds fly overhead.

The town calls these sightings Acute Incidents.

There is a hotline.

It is always busy.
