THE_BENT_BUT_NOT_BROKEN.md

It did not begin as strength.
It began as strain — the slow, steady kind that accumulates in the joints of a day, in the hinge of a choice, in the soft places where pressure settles.

Something leaned.
Something bowed.
Something gave way just enough to feel the shift.

But it did not snap.

Throughout the moment:

  • the weight increased
  • the angle deepened
  • the structure creaked
  • the world pressed harder than it should

And still — the line held.

Not out of defiance.
Not out of pride.
Simply because breaking was not the next step.

When the pressure finally eased, the shape remained altered — a curve where a straight line used to be, a reminder of what it endured.

But the integrity stayed intact.

Bent, yes.
Marked, yes.
Changed, absolutely.

Broken?
Not today.

The world will try again.
It always does.

But so will you.
