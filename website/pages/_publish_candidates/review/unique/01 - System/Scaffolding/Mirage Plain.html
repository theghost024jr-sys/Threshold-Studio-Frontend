Essence The Mirage Plain is a wide, shimmering expanse where perception drifts ahead of reality. It is not a place of lies, but of premature truths — things that appear before they fully exist, possibilities that manifest as if they were already real. It is the environment where the world shows you what you’re leaning toward. Environmental Physics • Visibility: Distorted at distance; edges ripple • Light Behavior: Refracts around emotional pressure • Sound Behavior: Arrives slightly before the source • Stability: Low; shifts with internal states of travelers • Temperature: Warm at the horizon, cool underfoot Field Dynamics • Objects appear closer or farther depending on emotional clarity • Shadows behave independently for brief moments • Movement feels faster than it is • The horizon bends inward, creating a sense of approach Phenomena

  1. Echo Shapes Faint outlines of possible futures appear at the edge of sight. They vanish when approached, but leave emotional residue.
  2. Horizon Drift The horizon moves subtly, creating the sensation of being drawn forward. Often mistaken for destiny.
  3. Split Image A traveler may see two versions of themselves: • the one they are • the one they are becoming The images merge when a decision is made.
  4. Mirage Bloom A sudden intensification of visual distortion triggered by: • Doubt • Desire • Unresolved tension • Role shifts Flora & Fauna • Glassgrass: Thin, translucent blades that hum in the wind • Shimmer Hares: Appear in multiples; only one is real • Mirage Crows: Cast shadows that fly in different directions • Dust Serpents: Move beneath the surface, creating ripples Emotional Physics • Vector: Forward–Outward • Resonance: Anticipatory drift • Effect: Reveals latent intentions; amplifies possibility • Instability: Over-identification with projections • Signature: A tug in the chest, like leaning toward something unseen Role Interaction • Guide Roles: Use mirages to test directionality • Trickster Roles: Thrive; can hide inside projected possibilities • Healer Roles: Anchor travelers to their present form • Wanderer Roles: Risk chasing illusions until exhausted Drift Behavior • Early Drift: Traveler sees faint doubles of objects • Mid Drift: Perception outruns reality; decisions feel pre-made • Late Drift: Identity splits into projected and present versions • Collapse Mode: Mirage inversion — projections become heavier than truth Mythic Fragment

They say the Mirage Plain remembers every path you almost took.

Walk long enough, and the horizon will show you the version of yourself that still waits there.

Integration Notes • Use Mirage Plain for scenes of choice, doubt, or emerging identity • Ideal for testing characters’ internal alignment • Pairs naturally with Spiral Field (curvature) and Drift Zones (instability) • Excellent for foreshadowing without revealing outcomes • Works as a counterpoint to Warm Pocket (comfort vs. possibility)
