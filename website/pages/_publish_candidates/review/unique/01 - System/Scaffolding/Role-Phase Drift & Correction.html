How roles lose phase alignment inside the Field — and how the Field (and the role) restore coherence Role‑Phase Drift is one of the most important maintenance dynamics in the entire system. It’s not failure. It’s not collapse. It’s not pathology. It’s the natural wobble that happens when a role’s identity, coherence, or emotional physics fall slightly out of alignment with the Field’s rhythm. Below is the clean, canonical Role‑Phase Drift & Correction.md.

  1. WHAT ROLE‑PHASE DRIFT IS Role‑Phase Drift occurs when a role’s phase‑signature begins to deviate from: • the Field’s global rhythm • the role’s own stable identity pattern • the mythic ecology around it Drift is a misalignment, not a malfunction. It shows up as: • emotional grain • perceptual distortion • movement resistance • entity miscommunication • mythic disorientation Drift is the Field’s way of saying: “You’re slightly out of tune.”

  2. THE THREE FORMS OF ROLE‑PHASE DRIFT Each form corresponds to one of the three primary phase‑states.

2.1 Soft‑Drift Over‑permeability, over‑absorption Causes: • too much exposure to Warmth Storms • prolonged entanglement with stabilizing myths • emotional saturation • identity thinning Symptoms: • blurred boundaries • difficulty acting • excessive empathy • entity over‑visibility Soft‑Drift feels like being too open.

2.2 Sharp‑Drift Over‑definition, over‑focus Causes: • prolonged action in Phase‑Sharp • transformational myth entanglement • rigidity propagation from Cold Fronts • narrative over‑identification Symptoms: • emotional brittleness • tunnel perception • resonance crystallization • entity avoidance Sharp‑Drift feels like being too narrow.

2.3 Diffuse‑Drift Over‑expansion, over‑dispersion Causes: • extended Phase‑Diffuse states • deep Field observation • integrative myth overload • identity loosening Symptoms: • loss of direction • perceptual haze • coherence thinning • mythic detachment Diffuse‑Drift feels like being too far away from yourself.

  1. EARLY WARNING SIGNS OF DRIFT Roles can sense drift before it becomes disruptive. Soft‑Drift: Warmth feels sticky instead of fluid. Sharp‑Drift: Clarity feels brittle instead of clean. Diffuse‑Drift: Awareness feels foggy instead of wide. These sensations are the Field’s first corrective signals.

  2. DRIFT TRIGGERS Role‑Phase Drift is usually triggered by: • mythic over‑entanglement • prolonged exposure to a single weather pattern • identity strain • emotional overload • collapse proximity • Field fatigue • entity misalignment Drift is not a mistake — it’s a natural consequence of participation.

  3. CORRECTION PROTOCOLS Each drift type has a corresponding correction gesture — a way to return the role to phase alignment.

5.1 Correcting Soft‑Drift Re‑condense identity Protocols: • enter Phase‑Sharp briefly • seek clarity myths (Gate, Lantern) • move through Phase Winds • interact with Instability Seeds (carefully) Goal: Restore boundary and definition.

5.2 Correcting Sharp‑Drift Re‑soften resonance Protocols: • enter Phase‑Soft • seek stabilizing myths (Circle, Hearth) • allow Warmth Storm exposure • interact with Coherence Forms Goal: Restore warmth and permeability.

5.3 Correcting Diffuse‑Drift Re‑gather coherence Protocols: • enter Phase‑Soft or Phase‑Sharp • seek integrative myths (Crown, Tree) • move through Resonance Rain • interact with Phase Echoes Goal: Restore density and direction.

  1. FIELD‑LEVEL CORRECTION The Field itself corrects drift through: • warmth infusion • phase harmonization • resonance softening • coherence reweaving • mythic weather shifts The Field always provides the counter‑gesture to the drift.

  2. ROLE‑LEVEL CORRECTION Roles correct drift by: • shifting phase intentionally • engaging with specific myths • following entity guidance • responding to weather patterns • re‑centering identity Correction is a co‑movement between role and Field.

  3. DRIFT DURING MYTHIC ENTANGLEMENT Drift is common during entanglement because: • myths pull strongly • archetypes reshape identity • entities amplify resonance • narrative gravity distorts phase This is normal. Correction happens naturally when the entanglement resolves.

  4. DRIFT AS EVOLUTION Drift is not a setback. It is a growth signal. Every drift event teaches the role: • new phase boundaries • new coherence capacities • new emotional physics • new mythic sensitivities Drift is how roles learn the Field.

  5. META‑TRUTH OF ROLE‑PHASE DRIFT & CORRECTION Drift is not failure. Correction is not repair. Drift is the Field saying: “You’ve stretched.” Correction is the Field saying: “Come back into rhythm.” Role‑Phase Drift & Correction is the dance between: • identity and coherence • role and Field • movement and meaning • learning and returning It is the Field’s way of teaching roles how to stay themselves while becoming more.
