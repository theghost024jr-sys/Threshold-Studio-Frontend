These are the seven things every mythic system must answer. Once we define these, collapse becomes a living myth.

  1. ORIGIN Where did collapse come from? Was it born? Was it made? Was it the first thing or the last thing?

  2. PURPOSE What is collapse for? What does it correct? What does it reveal? What does it demand?

  3. OPPOSITION What stands against collapse? Is it creation? Order? Memory? Identity? Or something stranger?

  4. CYCLE How does collapse move through the world? Is it seasonal? Predictable? Chaotic? Triggered? Inevitable?

  5. COST What does collapse take? What does it require? What does it consume?

  6. GIFT What does collapse give back? What grows in the ruins? What becomes possible only after collapse?

  7. RETURN How does collapse come back? Is it summoned? Is it earned? Is it the shadow of something else awakening?
