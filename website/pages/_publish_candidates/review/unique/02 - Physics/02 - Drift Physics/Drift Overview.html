Drift Overview

The movement‑physics of the Field — how meaning, pressure, and resonance travel through space, identity, and environment

Drift is one of the core substrates of Threshold.
If the Cycle is the breath,
and the Field is the body,
then Drift is the movement of that breath through the body.

It is not wind.
It is not energy.
It is not metaphor.
It is the actual movement of meaning, pressure, and resonance across space, identity, and environment.

Below is the clean, canonical Drift Overview.md.

1. WHAT DRIFT IS

Drift is the movement of the Field’s pressure and resonance through:

  • environments
  • identities
  • roles
  • structures
  • weather
  • microclimates
  • flora and fauna

Drift is the carrier of the Cycle.
It is how phases propagate, how transitions begin, and how collapse spreads.

Drift is the vector system of Threshold.

2. THE FIVE PRIMARY DRIFT MODES

Drift expresses differently depending on the phase of the Cycle.

These modes are the alphabet of drift behavior.

3. DRIFT PROPERTIES

Drift has five fundamental properties:

A. Drift Direction

Where drift moves: inward, linear, outward, downward, upward.

B. Drift Density

How much drift is present in a given space.

C. Drift Viscosity

How easily drift moves through an environment.

D. Drift Turbulence

How chaotic or stable drift movement is.

E. Drift Memory

How drift retains traces of previous phases or identities.

These properties determine how drift behaves in any moment.

4. DRIFT BEHAVIOR ACROSS SCALES

Drift is fractal — it behaves similarly at all scales.

Micro‑scale

  • leaf‑edge shimmer
  • breath pockets
  • micro‑eddies

Meso‑scale

  • grove drift corridors
  • canyon drift funnels
  • ruin‑phase scatter zones

Macro‑scale

  • biome‑level drift waves
  • collapse fronts
  • renewal spirals

Drift is the connective tissue of the world.

5. DRIFT AND THE CYCLE

Drift is shaped by the Cycle —
but drift also shapes the Cycle.

Cycle → Drift

Each phase produces a distinct drift mode.

Drift → Cycle

Drift direction predicts transitions.
Drift density determines phase duration.
Drift turbulence determines transition violence.

Drift is the timing mechanism of the Cycle.

6. DRIFT AND RESONANCE

Drift and resonance are inseparable.

  • Drift carries resonance.
  • Resonance shapes drift direction.
  • Drift turbulence destabilizes resonance.
  • Resonance instability triggers drift inversion.

Together they form the movement‑tone system of the Field.

7. DRIFT AND IDENTITY

Identity interacts with drift in three ways:

A. Identity Weight

Heavy identities slow drift.

B. Identity Boundaries

Rigid identities compress drift.

C. Identity Fragmentation

Fragmented identities scatter drift.

Identity is the gravitational field drift moves through.

8. DRIFT AND ENVIRONMENT

Environments shape drift flow.

  • dense environments → slow drift
  • rigid environments → linear drift
  • fractured environments → scatter drift
  • dormant environments → still drift
  • forming environments → upward drift

Environment is the terrain drift travels across.

9. DRIFT SPECIES

Drift is not uniform — it contains species.

Coherence: Knotlings

Tension: Knotlings (tightened)

Collapse: Fracture Motes

Void: Root Echoes

Renewal: Ember Threads

Drift species are diagnostic markers of phase.

10. DRIFT ANOMALIES

When drift misbehaves, the Cycle distorts.

1. Reverse Drift

Inward drift during Collapse.

2. Stalled Drift

No movement during Renewal.

3. Spiral Inversion

Downward spirals during Renewal.

4. Drift Echo Loops

Repeating drift patterns.

Anomalies reveal systemic imbalance.

11. DRIFT AS A NAVIGATION TOOL

Drift is the most reliable navigation system in Threshold.

Navigation rule:
Move with drift, not against it.

Drift reveals:

  • safe routes
  • pressure corridors
  • collapse fronts
  • void basins
  • renewal spirals

Drift is the pathfinding logic of the Field.

12. META‑TRUTH OF DRIFT

Drift is not wind.
It is not energy.
It is not metaphor.

Drift is the movement of meaning across the world.

The Field says:

“My breath must travel.”

Drift is the movement physics of Threshold —
