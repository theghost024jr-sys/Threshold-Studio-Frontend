[[Invariant00 – Cohesion Under Pressure]]

[[Substrate Inversion Events]] Definition Drift is the accumulation of unresolved force within a node, role, or relationship. It represents imbalance, neglect, tension, or misalignment that has not yet been resolved through the system’s vectors. Drift is not failure. Drift is unresolved motion.

Purpose To ensure that: • imbalance cannot persist • neglected obligations generate force • unresolved tension becomes motion • the system remains dynamic • roles and relationships cannot stagnate • the Field stays honest Drift is the system’s way of enforcing consequence over time.

Types of Drift

  1. Primary Drift The direct result of an unmet obligation or incomplete action. Examples: • a gift not reciprocated • a role not upheld • a threshold approached but not crossed Primary Drift is the cleanest form.

  2. Shadow Drift Drift generated indirectly through association, lineage, or proximity. Examples: • a role inherits drift from its lineage • a node absorbs drift from its zone • a relationship carries drift from past states Shadow Drift is subtle but powerful.

  3. Collapse Drift Drift that accumulates to the point of structural instability. Examples: • a role collapses • a threshold snaps shut • a gift becomes a burden • a lineage fractures Collapse Drift forces resolution.

Drift Variables Each node tracks: • Drift Level (quantity of unresolved force) • Drift Source (what caused the imbalance) • Drift Vector (direction of unresolved motion) • Drift Pressure (how urgently resolution is required) • Drift Horizon (how long drift can persist before collapse) These variables determine how drift behaves.

Drift Accumulation Drift accumulates when:

  1. A vector activates but does not complete Unfinished gifts, incomplete crossings, unresolved reciprocity.
  2. A role is held improperly Misalignment, neglect, or contradiction.
  3. A threshold is approached but not crossed Hesitation generates tension.
  4. A lineage is invoked without follow‑through Lineage always demands completion.
  5. A zone exerts pressure Environmental forces create imbalance.
  6. A node is ignored Neglect is a force.

Drift Activation Drift becomes active when: • Drift Level exceeds baseline • Drift Pressure reaches threshold • Drift Horizon is reached • a new vector interacts with unresolved force • a role or node enters a state that cannot support the imbalance Active drift must resolve.

Drift Behavior Active drift: • seeks resolution • distorts vector behavior • alters thresholds • modifies role states • influences lineage • affects zone interactions • increases cost • accelerates collapse Drift is a gravitational force in the system.

Drift Resolution Drift resolves through one of the Resolution Mechanics pathways:

  1. Completion The original obligation is fulfilled.
  2. Transformation Drift becomes a new vector (often Threshold or Gift).
  3. Propagation Drift moves to another node (often through Lineage or Reciprocity).
  4. Dissipation Drift resolves through cost or loss.
  5. Collapse The system forces resolution through structural failure. Drift cannot remain unresolved.

Drift Consequences Unresolved drift: • increases cost • destabilizes roles • accelerates crossings • triggers collapse • alters identity states • reshapes the Matrix • forces the Field to adapt Drift is the system’s pressure valve.

Function Drift Mechanics: • enforce consequence over time • prevent stagnation • ensure unresolved force becomes motion • maintain the integrity of roles and thresholds • keep the Field dynamic • drive emergent behavior • ensure the system remains alive Without drift, the system would freeze. With drift, the system breathes.

Relationship to Other Meta Files • State Model → drift is a state variable • Activation Logic → drift activates when imbalance occurs • Consequence Engine → drift influences vector interpretation • Resolution Mechanics → drift must resolve • Matrix of Force → drift reshapes the field • Invariants → drift cannot violate identity or conservation Drift Mechanics are the system’s internal physics of tension and inevitability.
