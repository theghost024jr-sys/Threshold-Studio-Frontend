Purpose Resonance Pairs define the seven fundamental identity couplings in Threshold. Each pair is a dual‑pole system that expresses: • stable behavior • stress behavior • break behavior These pairs are the core behavioral physics of the world. Every role, species, signal, and collapse event expresses one or more resonance pairs. This file defines the structure of the pairs. The detailed behaviors live in Behaviors Index.md.

  1. Structure of a Resonance Pair Each pair contains three layers: 1.1 Stable State The natural, balanced expression of the pair. Identity is coherent. Drift is linear or rhythmic. Collapse risk is low. 1.2 Stress State The pair is under pressure. Identity stretches. Drift accelerates or curves. Collapse becomes possible. 1.3 Break State The pair collapses. Identity fractures or inverts. Drift becomes turbulent or spiral. Collapse becomes active. Every pair follows this tri‑state pattern.

  2. The Seven Resonance Pairs These are the canonical pairs in Threshold. Each pair is a dual polarity — not opposites, but counterweights.

2.1 Stillness ↔ Motion Identity Axis: Presence vs. Momentum Drift Axis: Compression vs. Acceleration Collapse Style: Brittle or Sudden This pair governs: • Stillwatch • Hunter/Anchor role tension • Drift speed • Collapse onset timing

2.2 Pattern ↔ Noise Identity Axis: Meaning vs. Overload Drift Axis: Clarity vs. Turbulence Collapse Style: Fragmentation This pair governs: • Scholar/Listener dynamics • Signal interpretation • Distortion environments • Cognitive collapse

2.3 Connection ↔ Isolation Identity Axis: Bond vs. Boundary Drift Axis: Synchronization vs. Divergence Collapse Style: Overconnection or Severance This pair governs: • Weaver role • Species flocking/herding • Social drift • Emotional collapse

2.4 Order ↔ Chaos Identity Axis: Structure vs. Disruption Drift Axis: Linear vs. Spiral Collapse Style: Spiral collapse This pair governs: • Trickster role • Spiral environments • Collapse contagion • Drift curvature

2.5 Expansion ↔ Contraction Identity Axis: Reach vs. Containment Drift Axis: Diffusion vs. Compression Collapse Style: Implosive or Dispersive This pair governs: • Ember signals • Environmental density • Identity pressure • Collapse magnitude

2.6 Memory ↔ Forgetting Identity Axis: Continuity vs. Release Drift Axis: Stabilization vs. Drift Loss Collapse Style: Recursive collapse This pair governs: • Ruins • Old forests • Identity retention • Collapse recursion

2.7 Vulnerability ↔ Protection Identity Axis: Exposure vs. Guarding Drift Axis: Openness vs. Resistance Collapse Style: Defensive collapse This pair governs: • Guardian role • Shelter environments • Species defensive behaviors • Collapse shielding

  1. Resonance Pair Behavior Model Each pair expresses through: 3.1 Identity Expression How the self behaves under the pair. 3.2 Drift Expression How motion behaves under the pair. 3.3 Collapse Expression How failure behaves under the pair. 3.4 Signal Expression How signals amplify or distort the pair. 3.5 Role Expression How roles embody or resist the pair. This is the universal behavioral grammar of Threshold.
