Purpose The Resonance Pocket Map identifies and tracks harmonic conditions across Threshold. It shows: • where resonance stabilizes identity • where resonance distorts signals • where resonance inverts into collapse • where resonance goes silent due to lineage rupture • how resonance flows through drift corridors and pressure fields This map is the harmonic layer of the worldstate.

The Five Resonance Pocket Types Resonance pockets appear in five canonical forms:

  1. Clear Resonance Pockets Zones of harmonic alignment. Effects: • identity stabilization • boundary strengthening • lineage activation • collapse suppression These are the safest harmonic zones.

  2. Mixed Resonance Pockets Zones where multiple harmonics overlap. Effects: • partial clarity • drift‑sensitive alignment • unstable identity • variable collapse risk These pockets are transitional.

  3. Distorted Resonance Pockets Zones where resonance is warped by pressure or drift. Effects: • signal distortion • boundary thinning • lineage interference • collapse vector activation These pockets precede collapse.

  4. Inversion Pockets Zones where resonance flips polarity. Effects: • resonance inversion • identity inversion • collapse identity formation • recursive drift These pockets are collapse‑aligned.

  5. Resonance Voids Zones where resonance is absent. Effects: • mythic silence • lineage rupture • collapse wells • worldphase instability These are the signature of the Lost Myth.

  3. Break — inversion or silence occurs
  4. Reformation — resonance returns or remains void This is the harmonic cycle.

Resonance Pocket Interactions Pockets interact through three mechanisms:

  1. Drift Coupling Drift corridors carry resonance between pockets. • drift amplifies distortion • drift destabilizes clear pockets • drift can trigger inversion

  2. Pressure Coupling Pressure fields warp resonance. • high pressure → distortion • nonlinear pressure → inversion • low pressure → clarity

  3. Lineage Coupling Lineage threads stabilize or destabilize resonance. • active lineage → clarity • ruptured lineage → voids • mythic silence → inversion
