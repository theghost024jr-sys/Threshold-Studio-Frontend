00 Meta Systems — Meaning, Transmission, and Interpretation The Signal Cluster defines how meaning is generated, transmitted, distorted, interpreted, and stabilized within Threshold. Where the Physics Cluster governs motion, the Signal Cluster governs semantics. Signals are not messages. They are events, states, and expressions that reveal the condition of a system. This cluster contains the governing laws of: • signal generation • signal drift • signal distortion • signal thresholds • signal interpretation • signal collapse • signal resonance It is upstream of Roles, Species, Zones, and Behavioral Mechanics.
