[[Global Motion Logic]]

Overview Global Sensory Logic defines how all sensory channels behave across the entire Threshold world. It ensures that every environment — regardless of phase, triad, or collapse state — feels like it belongs to the same underlying physics. This system governs: • color • light • sound • texture • density • motion • temperature • spatial pressure • perceptual clarity It is the sensory backbone of Threshold.

  2. Color

Every environment must define its state across all nine.

  1. Global Sensory States Each channel has three global states: Stable State The environment is aligned with its phase. Stress State The environment is approaching collapse. Collapse State The environment has shifted into its Shadow Phase. These states apply to all sensory channels.

  2. Global Sensory Behaviors Each sensory channel behaves according to the same global rules. Color Behavior • Stable: coherent, readable • Stress: heightened contrast or fading • Collapse: chaotic, misleading, or oppressive Light Behavior • Stable: phase‑appropriate illumination • Stress: flicker, dimming, or over‑brightening • Collapse: broken, frozen, or violent light Sound Behavior • Stable: ambient cues match phase • Stress: distortion, reverb, or irregular pulses • Collapse: noise, silence, or broken loops Texture Behavior • Stable: expresses phase geometry • Stress: roughening or smoothing unpredictably • Collapse: disappearing, glitching, or locking Density Behavior • Stable: matches phase weight • Stress: fluctuates • Collapse: spikes or collapses entirely Spatial Motion Behavior • Stable: subtle movement or stillness • Stress: jitter, drift, or oscillation • Collapse: violent motion or frozen space Temperature Behavior • Stable: phase‑appropriate warmth or coolness • Stress: inconsistent temperature • Collapse: extreme or absent temperature Pressure Behavior • Stable: gentle environmental pressure • Stress: rising tension • Collapse: crushing or hollow pressure Perceptual Clarity Behavior • Stable: readable environment • Stress: blur, noise, or distortion • Collapse: illegibility or sensory void
