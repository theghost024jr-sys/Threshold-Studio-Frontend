The foundational narrative‑resonance field that anchors lineage, meaning, memory, and symbolic physics across Threshold.

Purpose The Mythic Substrate is the deepest layer of Threshold, preceding: • identity • role • signal • species • environment • worldstate • even the Layer stack itself It defines the origin logic of meaning, the threads of lineage, and the symbolic patterns that the Integration Engine uses to stabilize or destabilize the world. The Mythic Substrate is not a story. It is the field from which stories arise.

The Four Components of the Mythic Substrate The Substrate is built from four interwoven elements:

  1. Threads The persistent memory‑lines that carry lineage. Properties: • continuity • inheritance • resonance • activation Threads are the memory architecture of the world.

  2. Echoes Residual patterns left by past events, cycles, or collapses. Properties: • distortion or clarity • harmonic persistence • drift sensitivity • collapse imprinting Echoes are the afterimages of world history.

  3. Archetypes Stable symbolic patterns that shape identity and role. Properties: • boundary templates • vector templates • resonance signatures • collapse tendencies Archetypes are the blueprints of behavior and meaning.

  4. Absences Structural gaps where meaning should exist but doesn’t. Properties: • collapse wells • lineage ruptures • mythic silence • distortion amplification Absences are the negative architecture of the Substrate.

Substrate Dynamics The Mythic Substrate behaves according to four dynamic principles:

  1. Resonance Primacy Resonance determines which threads activate, which echoes persist, and which archetypes dominate.
  2. Memory Persistence Threads endure unless ruptured by collapse or lineage inversion.
  3. Symbolic Gravity Archetypes exert pressure on identity, role, and signal.
  4. Collapse Imprint Absences attract collapse vectors and distortion fields.

Mythic Substrate and the Layer Stack The Substrate sits beneath the Layer stack and influences every Layer: • Mythic Layer → direct expression • Identity Layer → boundary templates • Role Layer → vector templates • Signal Layer → harmonic patterns • Species Layer → expression patterns • Environment Layer → resonance pockets • Worldstate Layer → era conditions The Substrate is the root logic of the entire system.

Mythic Substrate and Collapse Collapse interacts with the Substrate in three ways:

  1. Collapse Ruptures Threads Lineage breaks, memory fragments.
  2. Collapse Imprints Echoes Distortion becomes part of the substrate.
  3. Collapse Creates Absences Structural gaps form where meaning once lived. Collapse is not just a failure — it is a rewriting of the Substrate.

Mythic Substrate and Reformation Reformation restores: • thread continuity • archetypal alignment • echo clarity • absence reintegration Reformation is the healing of the Substrate.

Mythic Substrate and Myth Myths are surface‑level expressions of deep substrate patterns: • Lantern → boundary + resonance • Whisper → drift + signal • Stone → memory + structure • Ember → pressure + ignition • Lost Myth → absence + rupture Myths are not stories — they are Substrate signatures.

Failure Modes of the Mythic Substrate The Substrate destabilizes when: • threads rupture • echoes distort • archetypes invert • absences expand This produces: • lineage storms • mythic silence • collapse identity • worldphase fracture • long‑cycle instability
