[[Field Dynamics]]

How the Bound Grid computes itself The Field Equations describe how four forces interact: • Vector (direction) • Pressure (weight) • Resonance (meaning) • Drift (state change) Each node in the grid is a point where these four forces meet. The equations define how a change in one propagates through the others. Let’s build them cleanly.

  1. VECTOR EQUATION How direction updates

Where: • = vector (directional tendency of the node) • = pressure (downward, inward, outward, etc.) • = resonance (quiet, cold, warm, linear, recursive…) • = drift (state instability pulling the vector off course) Meaning: A node’s direction is shaped by its weight and meaning, and distorted by drift. This is why: • High Branch vectors sharpen under wind • Deep Ice vectors collapse inward under pressure • Recursive Hollow vectors loop under resonance

  1. PRESSURE EQUATION How weight distributes

Where: • = gradient of vectors around the node • = resonance (which can absorb or amplify pressure) Meaning: Pressure increases when surrounding vectors converge, and decreases when resonance absorbs it. This is why: • Stone Basins accumulate weight • Plains disperse it • Quiet Groves absorb it • Brinicle Spirals amplify it

  1. RESONANCE EQUATION How meaning propagates

Where: • = pressure interacting with drift (emotional turbulence) • = pressure gradient (how weight shifts across the grid) Meaning: Resonance grows when pressure meets instability, and weakens when pressure equalizes. This is why: • Ruins hum with fractured resonance • Old Forests deepen resonance through temporal weight • Wind Channels flatten resonance through pressure dispersal

  1. DRIFT EQUATION How instability spreads

Where: • = resonance (meaning that destabilizes identity) • = vector aligned with pressure (stabilizing force) Meaning: Drift increases when resonance overwhelms the node, and decreases when direction and weight align. This is why: • Recursive Hollows loop • Shatter Zones break • Deep Snowfields sink • Stone Basins stabilize

Where Interactions are: • vector–pressure coupling • resonance–drift amplification • pressure–resonance absorption • vector–drift distortion This is the Field Body updating itself.

This is the equilibrium condition. It means: • every upward vector is balanced by a downward one • every inward pressure is balanced by an outward one • every resonance cluster has a counter‑cluster • every drift corridor has a recovery corridor This is why the grid doesn’t tear itself apart.
