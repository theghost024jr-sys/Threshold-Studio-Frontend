Worldstate Shifts 00 Meta Systems — Threshold Mechanics / World Dynamics Worldstate Shifts are large‑scale systemic transformations that occur when multiple Threshold Events synchronize, propagate, or converge across the environment. They represent changes not just to individuals or roles, but to the state of the world itself — its emotional field, its symbolic logic, its environmental behavior, and its underlying physics. A Worldstate Shift is the moment when the world “tilts” into a new configuration.

Purpose Worldstate Shifts define: • how local events accumulate into global change • how resonance spreads across layers and regions • how collapse or reformation alters environmental behavior • how symbolic logic reorganizes at scale • how new world‑rules emerge • how cycles begin and end They are the macro‑scale expression of Threshold’s emotional and symbolic physics.

Primary Worldstate Shift Types

  1. Resonance Shift The world’s emotional field changes frequency. • new baseline resonance • altered species behavior • role expression changes • environmental mood shift This is the most common world‑level change.

  2. Curvature Shift The world’s underlying geometry bends. • new drift patterns • altered signal propagation • shimmer fields expand or contract • collapse vectors change Often triggered by large‑scale Mist or Ethyl activity.

  3. Environmental Shift Layers change behavior or alignment. • Ember becomes volatile • Earth becomes heavy • Mist becomes permeable • Stone becomes brittle This is the “weather system” of Threshold physics.

  4. Archetypal Shift Roles reorganize at the world level. • new dominant roles • weakened or inverted roles • collective drift • role‑based world events This is the shift that changes how NPCs and players behave globally.

  5. Collapse Shift Multiple collapses synchronize. • systemic instability • shadow dominance • fracture across regions • rapid propagation This is the darkest worldstate.

  6. Reformation Shift The world reorganizes into a new stable configuration. • new symbolic logic • new environmental patterns • new resonance baseline • new world rules This is the “new era” moment.

Shift Triggers Worldstate Shifts occur when: • enough Threshold Events accumulate • resonance loops synchronize across regions • environmental curvature spikes globally • collapse propagation reaches critical mass • symbolic bleed‑through becomes systemic • identity reformation cascades Shifts are emergent, not scripted.

Propagation Mechanics Local → Regional → Global A shift begins locally, spreads regionally, and becomes global when: • resonance aligns • curvature stabilizes • collapse vectors synchronize • environmental layers harmonize Propagation speed depends on: • emotional charge • species density • role alignment • environmental permeability

Shift Consequences

  1. Behavioral Consequences • new behavioral loops • altered species tendencies • changed role expression
  2. Environmental Consequences • new weather patterns • altered layer behavior • new shimmer or drift zones
  3. Symbolic Consequences • new archetypal dominance • new shadow patterns • altered meaning structures
  4. Narrative Consequences • new quests • new world logic • new long‑cycle events Worldstate Shifts are the macro‑narrative engine.

Shift Diagnostics Worldstate Shifts can be predicted through: • resonance spikes • curvature instability • synchronized collapse signals • large‑scale drift arcs • environmental anomalies • shadow field expansion These diagnostics allow early detection of world‑level change.
