New Node: Boundary Tension

(System Node — Cohesion / Drift Interface)

Boundary Tension “The force that emerges where two subsystems meet, defining how much energy is required for a node to cross from one domain into another.”

Purpose

Boundary Tension measures the resistance at the edge of a biome, system, or conceptual cluster. It determines:

  • how easily nodes migrate

  • how brittle or elastic a chain becomes at its edges

  • whether orphans can re‑link

  • how collapse propagates across Flowers

Behavior

  • High Boundary Tension → rigid borders, brittle chains, low permeability

  • Low Boundary Tension → fluid borders, high drift, increased cross‑linking

  • Dynamic Tension → oscillation between stability and collapse

It’s small, clean, and plugs directly into:

…and it gives you a missing “bridge concept” that explains why some clusters stay intact while others fragment.

It’s also a perfect node for light work: no heavy lore, no deep system rewrite, just a structural concept that stabilizes the graph.
