Comet Vector — Directional Drift Engine

The Comet Vector (CV) is the primary directional mechanic of the Shatter Wing Comet. It expresses intent, trajectory, and momentum, and determines how the creature interacts with:

  • Drift fields

  • Pressure gradients

  • Cluster geometry

  • Reintegration pathways

  • Echo signals

The CV is the spine of the creature’s motion system.

Core Components

1. Directional Intent (DI)

The vector’s “decision” about where to go.

  • Derived from core pulse rhythm

  • Influenced by shard alignment

  • Modulated by local drift fields

  • Can invert under pressure spikes

Links: [[Shatter Wing Comet Signal]] • [[Drift System]] • [[Environmental Cues]]

2. Acceleration Profile (AP)

Defines how quickly the creature commits to its chosen direction.

  • Smooth acceleration → stable CV

  • Erratic acceleration → drift instability

  • Over‑acceleration → shard scatter risk

Links: [[Signal Distortion]] • [[Shard Bloom]] • [[Fragmentation]]

3. Harmonic Tail Coupling (HTC)

The tail resonance acts as a stabilizer.

  • Strong HTC → coherent trajectory

  • Weak HTC → wobble, drift, tail break

  • Zero HTC → imminent fragmentation

Links: [[Tail Resonance]] • [[Harmony Emergence]]

4. Shard Influence (SI)

Shards exert micro‑forces on the vector.

  • Aligned shards → directional boost

  • Scattered shards → multi‑vector drift

  • Orbiting shards → unpredictable curvature

Links: [[Shard Bloom]] • [[Shard Scatter]]

Vector States

Stable Vector

  • DI consistent

  • AP smooth

  • HTC strong

  • SI aligned

Creature moves like a comet with a clean tail.

Distorted Vector

  • DI oscillates

  • AP spikes

  • HTC weakens

  • SI uneven

Trajectory becomes jagged or looping.

Fragmented Vector

  • DI collapses

  • AP stalls

  • HTC breaks

  • SI chaotic

Creature enters Fragment state.

Links: [[Fragmentation]] • [[Reintegration]]

Mechanic Rules

Rule 1 — CV determines shard behavior

Shards follow the vector unless distortion exceeds threshold.

Rule 2 — CV collapses under pressure gradients

High pressure → DI inversion → tail break → fragmentation.

Rule 3 — CV stabilizes during reintegration

Core pulse resets → shards re‑align → tail resonance returns → CV reforms.
