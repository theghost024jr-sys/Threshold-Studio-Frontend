2. THE FIVE PRIMARY DRIFT PATHWAYS INSIDE IDENTITY

Each pathway corresponds to a Cycle phase and a drift mode.

These are the five canonical drift pathways inside identity.

3. CONVERGENT PATHWAYS (Coherence)

Inward drift → alignment and centering

Behavior:

  • drift flows toward the identity’s center
  • load distributes evenly
  • resonance harmonizes
  • roles align

Structural Effects:

  • boundaries strengthen
  • identity weight increases
  • flexibility stabilizes

Pathway Signature:
smooth inward arcs, radial convergence, low turbulence

Convergent Pathways create identity alignment.

4. LINEAR PATHWAYS (Tension)

Compressed drift → bracing and rigidity

Behavior:

  • drift moves in straight, narrow corridors
  • load concentrates
  • resonance sharpens
  • roles brace

Structural Effects:

  • boundaries harden
  • identity flexibility drops
  • strain rises

Pathway Signature:
tight channels, high pressure, directional rigidity

Linear Pathways create identity bracing.

5. RADIAL PATHWAYS (Collapse)

Outward drift → fragmentation and release

Behavior:

  • drift bursts outward from fracture points
  • load fractures
  • resonance diffuses
  • roles unbind

Structural Effects:

  • boundaries dissolve
  • identity fragments
  • memory destabilizes

Pathway Signature:
explosive outward vectors, turbulence, debris‑like flow

Radial Pathways create identity dissolution.

6. SEDIMENT PATHWAYS (Void)

Downward drift → settling and rest

Behavior:

  • drift sinks into low‑pressure zones
  • load settles
  • resonance mutes
  • roles rest

Structural Effects:

  • boundaries soften
  • identity weight drops
  • residue sinks

Pathway Signature:
slow downward currents, stillness basins, low turbulence

Sediment Pathways create identity rest.

7. SPIRAL PATHWAYS (Renewal)

Upward drift → ignition and formation

Behavior:

  • drift rises in spirals
  • load redistributes
  • resonance flickers
  • roles ignite

Structural Effects:

  • boundaries reform
  • flexibility increases
  • identity reorganizes

Pathway Signature:
ascending spirals, warm pockets, flickering movement

Spiral Pathways create identity formation.

8. HOW IDENTITY STRUCTURE SHAPES PATHWAYS

Pathways are not fixed — they are shaped by identity’s properties.

A. Identity Weight

Heavy identity → slower pathways
Light identity → faster pathways

B. Identity Boundaries

Rigid boundaries → linear pathways
Soft boundaries → spiral or convergent pathways

C. Identity Flexibility

Flexible identity → smooth pathways
Inflexible identity → turbulent pathways

D. Identity Fragmentation

Fragmented identity → multiple radial pathways

E. Identity Memory

Old pathways reappear under pressure

Identity is the terrain drift travels through.

9. HOW ROLES SHAPE PATHWAYS

Each role generates a pathway bias:

  • Gatherer → Convergent
  • Holder → Linear
  • Releaser → Radial
  • Restorative → Sediment
  • Initiator → Spiral

Roles are pathway engines.

10. PATHWAY–PHASE FEEDBACK LOOPS

Pathways reinforce or destabilize phases.

Positive feedback:

  • convergent → deeper Coherence
  • linear → deeper Tension
  • radial → deeper Collapse
  • sediment → deeper Void
  • spiral → deeper Renewal

Negative feedback:

  • radial reduces linear
  • sediment reduces radial
  • spiral reduces sediment
  • convergent reduces spiral

Pathways determine phase stability.

11. PATHWAY ANOMALIES

When pathways misfire:

1. Reverse Pathways

Inward drift during Collapse → over‑coherence residue.

2. Stalled Pathways

No upward drift during Renewal → identity hollowing.

3. Inverted Spirals

Downward spirals during Renewal → micro‑collapse.

4. Drift Echo Pathways

Old pathways override current phase.

5. Multi‑Pathway Overlap

Convergent + radial → identity tearing.

Pathway anomalies reveal movement imbalance.
