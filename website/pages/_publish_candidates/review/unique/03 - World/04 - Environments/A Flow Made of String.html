A flow like a flag made of string is exactly the right move, because it gives the void a presence without giving it a voice.
It stays true to “alone to start,” but it also establishes the substrate that will later carry pulses, echoes, and entanglements.

And the metaphor you just chose — a flag made of string — is more than an image.
It’s a physics statement.

The void as a string‑fabric flow

A flag made of string behaves in three essential ways that map perfectly onto Threshold’s cosmology:

• It moves even when nothing touches it

A flag made of loose threads doesn’t need wind to shift.
It has its own micro‑motions, its own internal tension, its own subtle drift.

That gives the void:

  • depth
  • texture
  • quiet motion
  • a sense of being “there”

But not a sense of being alive or reactive yet.

• It carries waves without revealing their source

If you flick one end of a string‑flag, the vibration travels across the threads.
But if you’re standing far away, you can’t tell:

  • what caused it
  • where it started
  • whether it was intentional
  • whether it was internal or external

This is exactly how pulses will behave later.

• It is structure without form

A flag made of string is not a surface.
It’s a field — a mesh of tension lines that define space without enclosing it.

That gives the void:

  • a substrate
  • a medium
  • a tension field
  • a place where pulses can travel

But still no hint of other flowers.

How the first flower experiences this void

With only one flower in existence, the string‑fabric void behaves like:

  • a faint, slow, almost imperceptible drift
  • a soft tension at the boundary
  • a subtle sense of “something” holding the world in place
  • no shimmer, no echo, no reaction

Players don’t notice it unless they’re extremely sensitive.
Even then, they can’t interpret it.

It feels like:

  • the world is resting on a quiet, shifting cloth
  • the edges of the petals brush against something soft
  • the void has weight but not presence

This preserves the feeling of solitude while still giving the cosmos a foundation.

How pulses interact with the string‑fabric

When the first seed is returned and the first pulse fires:

  • the pulse travels outward
  • it reaches the petals
  • it stretches into the void
  • it enters the string‑fabric
  • it becomes a vibration
  • the vibration travels along the threads
  • it dissipates into the distance

No echo.
No interference.
No response.

Just a wave traveling across a fabric that leads nowhere yet.

This is the world breathing into emptiness.

Why this is the perfect substrate for future entanglement

Because when a second flower eventually exists, the same pulse will:

  • travel along the string‑fabric
  • reach the region where two fabrics overlap
  • meet another pulse
  • create a fleeting interference
  • form a brief standing wave
  • collapse

The physics don’t change.
The cosmos just becomes populated.

Your choice — a flag made of string — ensures:

  • the void is alone at first
  • the void is structured
  • the void can carry waves
  • the void can support entanglement later
  • the world never contradicts itself

It’s the cleanest possible foundation.
