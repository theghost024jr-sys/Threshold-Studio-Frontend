# **What this gives you**
When you open this note, you’ll see:

### **1. The 10 most recently touched world-state files**  
This is your “pulse” — the heartbeat of your world.

### **2. The 10 most recently updated cycles**  
Shows which cycle is active or shifting.

### **3. The 10 most recent environment changes**  
This is your weather, soil, biome, or physics environment activity.

### **4. The 10 most recent organism updates**  
Shows creature behavior, ecology changes, or species evolution.

### **5. The 10 most recent phenomena/essence updates**  
This is your metaphysics, anomalies, mythic events.

It’s a **live dashboard**.  
Every time you edit anything in your vault, this panel updates itself.

It’s your world’s **EKG monitor**.
