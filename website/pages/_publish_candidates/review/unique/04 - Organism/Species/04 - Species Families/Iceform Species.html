Purpose Iceform Species embody frozen identity states — beings defined by clarity, stillness, and the tension between stability and fracture. They are the custodians of cold logic, preserved memory, and the physics of brittle transformation. They are not lifeless. They are alive in slow motion. Core Identity • Layer: Subsurface / Cold Strata • Signal Type: Fracture Resonance • Elemental Overlay: Water–Air (frozen fluidity + sharp clarity) • Archetypal Role: The Keeper of Still Truths Primary Traits • Movement defined by precision and minimal waste • Emotional expression muted but not absent • Memory stored in crystalline layers within the body • High resistance to emotional turbulence • Vulnerable to sudden heat or rapid change Variants
