Sensitivity Levels • Low — minimal behavioral or signal response • Moderate — consistent but manageable influence • High — strong behavioral or signal shift • Critical — destabilizing; can trigger drift or collapse

Signal Type • Stillness (Owl) destabilizes under Flame • Interruption (Crow) destabilizes under Flame and Strider • Resonance (Treeform) destabilizes under Harbor • Drift (Mistborne) destabilizes under Seer and Strider • Weight (Stonekin) destabilizes under Anchor • Wave (Tidecall) destabilizes under Bridge and Harbor

  3. Role Pressure Roles exert directional pressure that can stabilize or destabilize species.
