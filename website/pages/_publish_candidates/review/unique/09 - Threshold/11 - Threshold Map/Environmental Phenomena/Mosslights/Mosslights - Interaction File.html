[[03 - World/01 - Biomes/Old Forest]] Mosslights — Interaction File Overview Mosslights are low‑intensity bioluminescent environmental organisms that stabilize Drift‑Shimmer boundary zones. Their interactions are subtle but foundational: they smooth turbulence, diffuse symbolic density, and provide soft navigational cues. They do not “act” in the way species or substrates do — they respond and influence. This file maps their interactions across Threshold’s systems.
