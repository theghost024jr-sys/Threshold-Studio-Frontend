It is not sleep, not consciousness, not intention. It is the ambient dreaming of the world, expressed through: • soft resonance • symbolic drift • emotional echoing • narrative murmuring • low‑density pattern formation The Field Dreaming State is the world’s way of processing, cooling, and redistributing meaning.
