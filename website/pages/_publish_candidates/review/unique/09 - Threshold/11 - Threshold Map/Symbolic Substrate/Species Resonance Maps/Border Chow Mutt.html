(Settlement Companion Species) Essence The Border Chow is a settlement‑zone companion species created from the blend of: • Border Collie → attention‑reader, motion‑tracker, emotionally attuned • Chow → grounded, loyal, protective, slow to trust but deep when they do The result is a dog that fits perfectly into Fib 3–5: calm enough for settlement life, alert enough to read the world’s emotional physics. Appearance • Medium size, sturdy frame • Thick, slightly curled tail • Semi‑fluffy coat with a soft mane around the neck • Ears that perk like a collie but soften like a chow • Eyes that track movement and mood with equal precision • Colors: muted browns, blacks, and smoky grays They look like they belong in a world that’s half‑wild, half‑settled. Temperament • Reads emotional fields like a Border Collie • Holds steady like a Chow • Protective without aggression • Curious without chaos • Bonds slowly but permanently • Sensitive to drift, but not overwhelmed by it They’re the first animal in Threshold that can “feel” collapse weather before it forms. Behavior in the Settlement Zone • Patrols the perimeter in slow arcs • Sits near players who are overwhelmed • Nudges them toward calmer areas • Avoids high‑drift pockets • Sleeps near water or warm stone • Watches the horizon more than the settlement They’re not pets — they’re companions with agency. Role in the System • Early emotional stabilizer • Drift‑field indicator • Companion for players who linger in Fib 3–5 • A soft introduction to relational beings in Threshold • A sign that the world is becoming habitable They’re the first hint that the world can support relationships, not just survival. Symbolic Markers • A faint halo of attention around their head when sensing drift • A low rumble when collapse weather is near • A softening of posture when someone is overwhelmed • A stillness that spreads outward like calm water They’re a living barometer of emotional physics.
