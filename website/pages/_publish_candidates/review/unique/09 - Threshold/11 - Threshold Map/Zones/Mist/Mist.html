Zone Signature Mist is the zone of ambiguity, soft perception, and dissolving boundaries. It is not fog; it is unresolved meaning. Edges blur, distances stretch, and identity becomes permeable. Sound is muffled, light diffused, and intention becomes slippery. The felt sense of Mist is a gentle, uncertain openness, like standing inside a question that hasn’t decided what it means yet.
