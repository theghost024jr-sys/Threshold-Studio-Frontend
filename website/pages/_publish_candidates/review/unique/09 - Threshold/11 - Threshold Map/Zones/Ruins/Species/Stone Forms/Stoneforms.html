Overview Stone Forms are semi‑sentient resonance constructs found throughout Ruins environments. They are not statues, not fossils, not petrified creatures — they are stone‑anchored memory‑resonance organisms shaped by: • collapse residue • emotional compression • Shimmer curvature • Stone resonance • symbolic density Stone Forms are the slowest, heaviest, and most stable species‑class in Ruins.

Purpose in the Ecosystem Stone Forms exist to: • anchor collapse‑damaged zones • stabilize emotional fields • store and compress symbolic residue • prevent Drift from becoming chaotic • provide long‑term environmental memory • act as “gravity wells” for Ruins coherence They are the structural stabilizers of Ruins ecology.

Core Properties

  1. Resonant Stone Bodies Stone Forms are composed of: • layered stone strata • collapse‑infused mineral veins • Shimmer‑responsive fractures • Mist‑softened surfaces • embedded symbolic residue Their bodies are geological, not biological.

  2. Slow, Intentional Movement Stone Forms move rarely and slowly. Movement patterns include: • micro‑shifts • slow rotations • pressure realignments • resonance pulses Movement is a response to field pressure, not locomotion.

  3. Collapse Absorption Stone Forms absorb collapse residue into their bodies. Effects include: • darkening of stone • increased density • deeper resonance hum • temporary immobility They are collapse “sponges.”

  4. Emotional Compression Stone Forms compress emotional fields: • grief → becomes weight • fear → becomes tension • overwhelm → becomes fracture lines • hope → becomes warmth in the stone They metabolize emotion into structure.

Behavior Baseline Behavior Stillness, low resonance hum, slow micro‑adjustments. Indicates stable Ruins. Symbolic Role Stone Forms represent: • memory under pressure • emotional weight • collapse endurance • slow truth • structural resilience They are the deep memory stones of Ruins.
