THE FOUR–FIVE CARD SYSTEM

Asymmetric abilities + self‑organized sequencing + graph‑based world expansion

You don’t want a turn order.
You want a social mechanic:

“People figure out who goes next.”

That means the cards must:

  • define what a player can do
  • define when they’re allowed to act
  • define how they interact with others
  • create tension and opportunity
  • prevent chaos without enforcing order

This is a beautiful design space.

Let’s build the card structure.

THE CARD MODEL (4–5 cards, each with multiple aspects)

Each card has three layers:

1. A Permission

What you’re allowed to do in the graph.

2. A Constraint

What you’re not allowed to do.

3. A Trigger

When you’re allowed to act — this replaces turn order.

This is the key.
The trigger is what makes the game self‑sequencing.

THE FOUR CORE CARDS (canonical set)

Here’s a clean, powerful set of four cards that create a complete emergent system.

1. THE LINKER

Permission:
You may create a link between any two existing nodes.

Constraint:
You may not create new nodes.

Trigger:
You act whenever someone creates a node that has fewer than 2 links.

This makes the Linker a stabilizer — they fill gaps.

2. THE SCRIBE

Permission:
You may fill in any blank .md file with up to 3 sentences.

Constraint:
You may not create links.

Trigger:
You act whenever someone creates a node with no content.

This makes the Scribe a meaning‑maker — they give shape to raw structure.

3. THE CARTOGRAPHER

Permission:
You may create a new region/biome/layer and assign up to 5 nodes to it.

Constraint:
You may not modify existing regions.

Trigger:
You act whenever the graph gains 5 new nodes since your last action.

This makes the Cartographer a world‑shaper — they define topology.

4. THE SUMMONER

Permission:
You may create a new node of any type (character, item, biome, event).

Constraint:
You may not link it to anything.

Trigger:
You act whenever someone links two nodes that were previously unconnected.

This makes the Summoner a chaos‑injector — they add raw material.

OPTIONAL FIFTH CARD (if you want 5 players)

5. THE WEAVER

Permission:
You may add a link that changes meaning — a portal, inversion, or contradiction.

Constraint:
You may only act on nodes that already have 3+ links.

Trigger:
You act whenever someone creates a region or biome.

This makes the Weaver a mythic force — they bend the world.

WHY THIS WORKS

1. No turns needed

The triggers are the turn order.
Players act when the world conditions match their card.

2. The graph becomes a living system

Every action creates a ripple that enables someone else.

3. Asymmetry creates emergent narrative

Each player pushes the world in a different direction.

4. Constraints prevent chaos

No one can do everything.
Everyone can do something.

5. It scales beautifully

4–5 players is perfect.
But you could run 20 if you wanted.
