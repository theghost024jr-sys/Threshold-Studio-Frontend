Brinicle

A Brinicle is a collapse-born ice phenomenon formed when supercooled brine descends from polar sea ice, freezing seawater on contact.
It creates a hollow tube of ice that extends from the underside of sea ice to the ocean floor.
This is not a passive structure — it is a vectorized freeze event, a slow collapse rendered in fluid geometry.
