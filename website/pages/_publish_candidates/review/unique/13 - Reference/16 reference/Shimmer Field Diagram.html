Overview The Shimmer Field Diagram visualizes Shimmer as a meaning‑bearing field composed of curvature, resonance gradients, refraction arcs, and fracture zones. It is not a map of a location — it is a map of how meaning behaves in a given moment or environment. The diagram shows: • how meaning bends • where interpretation becomes unstable • where symbolic density accumulates • how collapse pressure distorts curvature • how Drift and Mist alter Shimmer behavior It is the primary tool for reading meaning‑physics.

Core Components

  1. Curvature Lines These are the arcs that define how meaning bends. They represent: • interpretive flow • symbolic direction • resonance alignment • narrative pull Curvature is the “shape” of meaning.

  2. Resonance Gradient Zones Soft color or density shifts that show: • where meaning is strong • where meaning is thin • where interpretation is easy • where interpretation is dangerous Gradients reveal meaning pressure.

  3. Refraction Arcs Bright arcs that appear when Shimmer bends sharply. They indicate: • symbolic overload • emotional spikes • collapse adjacency • interpretive distortion Refraction arcs are the “warning lights” of Shimmer.

  4. Fracture Lines Sharp breaks in curvature. They represent: • collapse vectors • emotional rupture • symbolic fracture • narrative instability Fractures are the most dangerous Shimmer features.

  5. Still Zones Areas of low curvature and low resonance. They indicate: • interpretive calm • emotional stability • low symbolic density Still Zones are safe for Witness work.

  6. Shimmer Core The center of the diagram — the point of highest symbolic density. Represents: • core meaning • narrative anchor • interpretive truth The Shimmer Core is the “heart” of the field.

Behavioral Functions

  2. Gradient Expansion Resonance zones expand when meaning intensifies.
  3. Arc Brightening Refraction arcs brighten when symbolic overload increases.
  4. Fracture Propagation Fracture lines spread when collapse vectors activate.
  5. Core Migration The Shimmer Core moves when the meaning of the moment shifts.
