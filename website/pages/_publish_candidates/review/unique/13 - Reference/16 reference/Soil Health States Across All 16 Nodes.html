This document maps the seven canonical soil health states—Stable, Enriched, Thin, Hardened, Fractured, Corrupted, and Dead—across all sixteen environment nodes in Threshold. Each node expresses these states through its unique soil type and environmental physics.

1. Spiral — Compressed Soil

  • Stable: Pressure cycles cleanly; echoes align.

  • Enriched: Resonance deepens; spiral memory strengthens.

  • Thin: Echoes fade; pressure loops weaken.

  • Hardened: Pressure locks; movement becomes brittle.

  • Fractured: Spiral breaks; echoes misalign sharply.

  • Corrupted: Impossible pressure loops form; inversion of motion.

  • Dead: No resonance; spiral collapses into stillness.

2. Lattice — Luminous Soil

  • Stable: Light channels remain coherent.

  • Enriched: Signals brighten; truth becomes vivid.

  • Thin: Light dims; pathways blur.

  • Hardened: Light distorts; signals bend.

  • Fractured: Lattice cracks; beams scatter.

  • Corrupted: Truth inverts; false signals appear true.

  • Dead: No illumination; lattice becomes inert.

3. Mist — Dissolved Soil

  • Stable: Boundaries soft but consistent.

  • Enriched: Mist becomes clarifying; identity reveals.

  • Thin: Fog thickens; disorientation rises.

  • Hardened: Mist freezes; identity locks.

  • Fractured: Identity bleed; forms overlap.

  • Corrupted: Mist becomes predatory; false identities emerge.

  • Dead: No dissolution; environment becomes static void.

4. Marsh — Dissolved Soil

  • Stable: Absorbs residue evenly.

  • Enriched: Regenerative; supports complex life.

  • Thin: Waterlogged memory; sluggish response.

  • Hardened: Rare; creates identity freeze pockets.

  • Fractured: Memory pools split; paths shift.

  • Corrupted: Toxic seep; hostile residue.

  • Dead: Stagnant; no regeneration.

5. Stone Basin — Compressed Soil

  • Stable: Holds weight; channels pressure.

  • Enriched: Stone warms; resonance amplifies.

  • Thin: Crumbling edges; weak pressure retention.

  • Hardened: Overcompression; brittle stone.

  • Fractured: Sharp breaks; collapse cascades.

  • Corrupted: Impossible pressure pockets.

  • Dead: Dust; no structural memory.

6. Branch — Luminous Soil

  • Stable: Light flows through growth channels.
