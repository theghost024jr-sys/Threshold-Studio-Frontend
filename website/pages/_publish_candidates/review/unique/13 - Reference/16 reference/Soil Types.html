1. Residual Soil Keyword: Aftermath What it holds: What remains after actions complete Behavior: • Stores impressions, heat, pressure, echoes • Thickens with repetition • Thins with contradiction • Feeds future patterns Environments that use it heavily: Mist, Ember Lattice, Grove, Ruins NPC effect: Characters grown from residual soil are shaped by what has happened, not what is happening.

  2. Structural Soil Keyword: Geometry What it holds: The environment’s underlying pattern Behavior: • Determines movement logic • Sets constraints and permissions • Governs collapse modes • Defines “grain” of the world Environments that use it heavily: Brinicle Spiral, Stone Basin, High Branch NPC effect: Characters grown from structural soil have rigid logic, predictable collapse, and strong alignment requirements.

  3. Inheritance Soil Keyword: Lineage What it holds: What the environment passes forward Behavior: • Stores generational memory • Carries old physics into new cycles • Determines what species or roles can reappear • Holds unresolved contradictions Environments that use it heavily: Ruins, Grove, Spiral Field NPC effect: Characters grown from inheritance soil carry “echoes of ancestors” — not literally, but structurally.

  4. Volatile Soil Keyword: Instability What it holds: Unresolved energy Behavior: • Reacts violently to contradiction • Changes state quickly • Produces rare species and unstable roles • Collapses fast but regenerates fast Environments that use it heavily: Brinicle Spiral (deep tiers), Distortion Fields, Collapse-aligned zones NPC effect: Characters grown from volatile soil have unpredictable behavior and sharp collapse thresholds.

  5. Absorptive Soil Keyword: Intake What it holds: Everything Behavior: • Takes in heat, sound, motion, emotion • Stores it without judgment • Slowly metabolizes it into residue • Can become overloaded Environments that use it heavily: Deep Snow, Mist, Recovery environments NPC effect: Characters grown from absorptive soil are quiet, reflective, and slow to react — until they’re not.

  6. Reflective Soil Keyword: Mirror What it holds: Player behavior Behavior: • Echoes back motion, emotion, or intent • Creates feedback loops • Can distort if the player is incoherent • Rewards alignment with clarity Environments that use it heavily: High Branch, Spiral Field, Ember Lattice NPC effect: Characters grown from reflective soil adapt to the player’s rhythm, pace, and emotional coherence.

  7. Null Soil Keyword: Absence What it holds: Nothing Behavior: • No memory • No residue • No inheritance • Pure potential • Extremely fragile Environments that use it heavily: Threshold edges, newborn environments, collapse resets NPC effect: Characters grown from null soil are rare and unstable — they have no past, only present.

  8. Compressed Soil Keyword: Pressure What it holds: Everything tightly Behavior: • Stores contradictions until they rupture • Produces dense, slow environments • Generates high-stakes collapse modes • Rewards patience and precision Environments that use it heavily: Stone Basin, Brinicle Spiral, Deep Ruins NPC effect: Characters grown from compressed soil are deliberate, heavy, and extremely sensitive to fracture.

  9. Dissolved Soil Keyword: Boundary-loss What it holds: Blurred memory Behavior: • No clear edges • Everything mixes • Growth is chaotic but fertile • Collapse is soft, not catastrophic Environments that use it heavily: Mist, Marsh, Fog Basin NPC effect: Characters grown from dissolved soil have fluid identity, shifting roles, and soft collapse states.

  10. Luminous Soil Keyword: Signal What it holds: Truth Behavior: • Brightens with coherence • Dims with contradiction • Stores emotional clarity • Shapes species by honesty Environments that use it heavily: Ember Lattice, High Branch, Grove NPC effect: Characters grown from luminous soil are sensitive to truth, alignment, and emotional integrity.
