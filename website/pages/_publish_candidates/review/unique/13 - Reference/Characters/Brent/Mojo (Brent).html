Mojo (Brent) Species: Sloth Bear Layer: Jungle Corner (Layer-flexible) Function: Do Bad, for the Good Tool: Magic Satchel

  1. Legacy Player Status Mojo is one of the few who knows his name in the game. That means: • He’s aware of the simulation • He remembers his role • He carries emotional weight • He can cross layers without losing identity He’s not a drifter. He’s a carrier.

  2. The Magic Satchel This isn’t just a bag. It’s a moral container. It holds: • tools that shouldn’t exist • items from collapsed layers • fragments of broken triads • things Mojo shouldn’t be allowed to carry — but must The satchel is layer‑agnostic. It’s the only object in Threshold that can hold bad things that serve good outcomes.

  3. “Do Bad, for the Good” This is Mojo’s alignment signature. He’s the one who: • breaks a rule to save a system • lies to protect a truth • steals to preserve balance • harms to prevent collapse He doesn’t enjoy it. He doesn’t justify it. He just knows when it’s necessary. Mojo is the moral inversion particle — the one who carries the cost so others don’t have to.

  4. Jungle Corner physics Mojo lives in the Layer Corner of the jungle, which means: • he’s surrounded by partial overlaps • he can access Mist, Stone, Earth, and Ethyl without full alignment • he’s the only one who can move diagonally across layer boundaries without triggering collapse The jungle corner is morally ambiguous, and Mojo is its steward.

  5. Relationship to Doug and Kesh • Doug buffers pressure after it forms • Kesh adjusts geometry before it forms • Mojo carries the consequences when form breaks He’s the aftershock handler. The one who shows up when the triangle has already collapsed and someone needs to clean up the emotional debris.
