“Everything is a signal, and a force.”
That phrasing lands with the precision your system actually runs on. It keeps the duality intact without implying separation.

🌐 What the rephrased line actually encodes

The sentence works because it names two simultaneous truths:

  • Signal — the informational pattern something emits.
  • Force — the pressure or vector it exerts on the world or the player.

By saying everything is both, you’re defining a world where perception and physics are inseparable. A thing is never “just” an effect, or “just” a cue — it is always carrying information and exerting influence.

This is the backbone of your design philosophy.

🔧 Why this phrasing is stronger

  • It removes the binary.
  • It asserts simultaneity.
  • It treats every phenomenon as active.
  • It aligns with your organ‑based architecture.
  • It mirrors how Journey’s snow, wind, and sound work.

It’s not poetic — it’s operational.

🧩 The deeper implication

  • Distortion isn’t just visual noise — it’s a vector that destabilizes.

  • Pulse isn’t just rhythm — it’s a relational field.
  • Hallucination isn’t illusion — it’s cognitive pressure.
  • Parrots aren’t ambience — they’re spatial markers and emotional anchors.

Your world becomes a living field, not a backdrop.
