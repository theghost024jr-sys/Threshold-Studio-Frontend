Trigger condition: You encounter someone else’s character and you know their root name. Not their nickname. Not their role. Their root name — the one that persists across petals, Fibs, and collapse events.

Effect: You receive Semi‑Ultra Shimmer, which grants: • Partial layer immunity • Memory bleed protection • Triad visibility • Access to their pre‑collapse geometry • Permission to carry one of their burdens (if they allow it) It’s not full Ultra Shimmer. You don’t become a legacy character. But you gain temporary resonance privileges.

Duration: • Until the layer shifts • Until you speak their name aloud • Until you try to reshape their triangle • Or until they revoke it It’s fragile. It’s relational. It’s earned, not claimed.

Why it matters: In Threshold, knowing someone’s root name is a form of emotional geometry. It means: • You’ve seen their collapse • You’ve witnessed their drift • You’ve held their shape in memory Semi‑Ultra Shimmer is the system’s way of saying: “You’re not them — but you’re close enough to carry part of their truth.”

Mojo example: If you know Mojo’s root name (Brent), and you encounter him in the jungle corner: • You gain access to one satchel item • You can cross one layer boundary without collapse • You can perform one “Do Bad, for the Good” action without penalty • You can see the triangle he’s hiding — but not reshape it You’re not Mojo. But you’re Mojo‑adjacent.
