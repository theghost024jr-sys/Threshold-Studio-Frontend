[[The Twins of Chaos Form]] They aren’t opposites. They’re phase-locked misalignment. Two expressions of destabilization that share a root but diverge in how they rupture a space. They show up together, but never in sync.

Travis the Loud Essence: Volume-as-presence. A pressure front in human form. Signature: He doesn’t speak — he collides with the air. Environmental Effect: Raises the ambient amplitude of a room. Even silence feels louder after he leaves. Emotional Physics: • Overstimulates the surface layer • Disrupts pacing • Forces premature reactions • Creates false urgency Role in a Scene: He is the accelerant. The one who pushes everything three steps ahead before anyone is ready. He doesn’t mean harm — he just has no concept of “ambient load.” Symbolic Animal: A crow that thinks it’s a thunderclap.

Dave the Inconsistent Energy Essence: Flicker-state embodiment. Signature: He oscillates between hyper-engaged and ghosted-out with no transitional frames. Environmental Effect: Breaks rhythm. Introduces micro-desynchronizations that ripple outward. Emotional Physics: • Unpredictable availability • Fragmented attention • Sudden surges followed by collapse • Creates instability in group coherence Role in a Scene: He is the disruptor of continuity. Not malicious — just governed by a different internal clock that never aligns with the room. Symbolic Animal: A firefly that blinks in the wrong pattern.
