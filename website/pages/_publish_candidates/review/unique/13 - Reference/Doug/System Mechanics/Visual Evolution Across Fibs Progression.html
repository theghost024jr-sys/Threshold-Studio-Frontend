[[Visual Language Index]]

(Threshold — Visual Architecture Progression) Purpose This document defines how Threshold’s visual identity evolves across the Fibonacci layers. Each Fib band introduces a new level of visual, emotional, and symbolic complexity. The world becomes richer as the player becomes more aligned, more capable, and more attuned to the emotional physics of Threshold. This evolution also creates a natural open‑source contribution ladder, allowing creators to enter at simple tiers and grow upward.

  1. Overview of Visual Progression Threshold’s visuals evolve in three major tiers:

Each tier builds on the previous one, expanding the world’s expressive capacity.

  1. Fib 1–3 — Basic 2.5D (Tunic / Zelda Tier) Visual Traits • Low‑poly, soft shapes • Pastel or lightly saturated palette • Gentle gradients • Rounded, toy‑like proportions • Minimal detail • Clean silhouettes • Soft lighting and bloom • No symbolic geometry yet Motion & Interaction • Simple drift • Soft turning • Basic idle loops (bobbing, blinking) • Proximity‑based interactions • No complex timing or personality Puzzle Structure • Tunic/Zelda‑style environmental puzzles • Spatial clarity • Discoverable, not forced • Gentle symbolic hints • No layered logic yet Emotional Tone • Safe • Dreamlike • Curious • Non‑threatening Purpose This tier teaches the player: • how to notice • how to drift • how to align • how to read emotional cues It is the onboarding band.

  2. Fib 3–5 — Expressive 2.5D (Sinker Swim Tier) Visual Traits • More defined silhouettes • Brighter accent colors • Slightly richer palette • More expressive shapes • Early symbolic geometry begins to appear • More environmental motion • Slightly sharper lighting Motion & Interaction • Rhythmic idle loops • Snappy interactions • Personality‑driven motion • Early emotional states (curious, alert, aligned) • Abzû‑style proximity behavior becomes more dynamic Puzzle Structure • More expressive environmental puzzles • Rhythm‑based interactions • Early symbolic patterns • Multi‑step but still readable • Playful, personality‑driven mechanics Emotional Tone • Awakening • Playful • Curious • Expressive Purpose This tier introduces: • personality • rhythm • early symbolic logic • cooperative emotional physics It is the emergence band.

  3. Fib 5–8 — Full Threshold Identity (Symbolic, Layered, Alive) Visual Traits • Layered motion • Symbolic geometry fully active • Dynamic color transitions (Journey‑style) • Environmental participation (Abzû‑style) • Soft glow and emotional presence (Sky‑style) • High contrast emotional palettes (HLD‑style) • Calm, elegant silhouettes (Sable‑style) Motion & Interaction • Complex emotional states • Group motion • Alignment dances • Symbolic gestures • Environmental resonance • Motion tied to emotional vectors Puzzle Structure • Symbolic puzzles • Layered emotional logic • Environmental resonance puzzles • Multi‑phase interactions • Threshold State transformations Emotional Tone • Alive • Resonant • Frontier • Transformative Purpose This tier reveals: • the full symbolic ecology • emotional physics • the living architecture of Threshold It is the frontier band, where contribution becomes necessary.

  4. Why the Fibonacci Structure Works

  5. Natural Skill Ladder • Fib 1–3: beginners • Fib 3–5: intermediate contributors • Fib 5–8: advanced artists and animators

  6. Emotional Onboarding The world becomes more complex as the player becomes more aligned.

  7. Visual Metaphor for Growth The world “awakens” as the player progresses.

  8. Modular Development You can ship Fib 1–3 first and expand over time.

  9. Open‑Source Friendly Each Fib band is a self‑contained visual tier.

  10. Canonical Summary Threshold’s visual evolution follows the Fibonacci sequence: • Fib 1–3: soft, simple, dreamlike • Fib 3–5: expressive, rhythmic, awakening • Fib 5–8: symbolic, layered, alive This progression mirrors the player’s emotional growth and the world’s unfolding complexity.
