Intention as the trigger is exactly right, and it gives both reveals their shape and their physics. When intention is the key, the shimmer isn’t a door or a barrier — it’s a sentient threshold that waits for the player to mean it. That’s what makes the reveal feel earned, personal, and mythic.

Below is how intention shapes both reveals, how the world responds, and how the player feels the shift in their body.

Fib 1–3 → Fib 3–5: the liquid‑shimmer that waits for intention

This reveal works because the membrane doesn’t open automatically. It reacts to:

  • proximity
  • hesitation
  • emotional stance
  • commitment

The player approaches the surface. It looks like:

  • liquid glass
  • a Stargate‑like ripple
  • a soft, breathing membrane
  • something alive but not threatening

How intention shapes the reveal

The shimmer doesn’t open when touched.
It opens when the player decides.

That moment of decision is the emotional hook:

  • a pause
  • a breath
  • a flicker of apprehension
  • a sense of “I’m crossing into something real”

Then the membrane yields.

How the reveal feels

  • The surface ripples outward.
  • The world muffles, like sound underwater.
  • The camera eases into drift physics.
  • Colors shift into the 3–5 palette.
  • The environment gains agency.
  • Movement becomes buoyant and conversational.

The player realizes instantly:
“This world has been hiding its true physics.”

This is the first moment someone will bring back to the hub — the first myth.

Fib 3–5 → Fib 5–8: the electrical‑storm that responds to resolve

This reveal is harsher, older, and more dangerous.
It doesn’t wait for curiosity — it waits for resolve.

The boundary looks like:

  • charged air
  • static snow
  • streaks of light
  • a cosmic storm
  • a blizzard lit by high beams

How intention shapes the reveal

The membrane doesn’t open because the player is near it.
It opens because the player chooses to endure it.

This is not apprehension.
This is commitment.

The world tests the player:

  • pressure rises
  • drift intensifies
  • visibility fractures
  • the environment pushes back

Only when the player holds their intention does the storm part.

How the reveal feels

  • Light cracks like electricity.
  • The camera tightens or pulls back dramatically.
  • The world becomes vast and mythic.
  • Movement becomes slow, intentional, survival‑based.
  • Collapse ladders and scarcity activate.
  • Entities become rare but powerful.

The player realizes:
“This world is ancient, alive, and indifferent. I am small.”

This is the second myth — the one only a few will ever bring back to the hub.
