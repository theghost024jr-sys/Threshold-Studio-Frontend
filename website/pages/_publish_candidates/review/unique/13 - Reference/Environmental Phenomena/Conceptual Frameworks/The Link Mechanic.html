[[The Link Mechanic (Username → Character → Ultra Shimmer]] [[Signal Map]]

  1. You create the character. You build an in‑game character based on a real person — their vibe, their stance, their pattern, whatever you choose.
  2. You give only the character’s name to the real person. Not the lore. Not the species. Not the location. Not the role. Just the name — and that name is always their username, not their real name. This is important because: • It keeps the link symbolic, not personal. • It keeps the mechanic clean and non-invasive. • It makes the recognition trial fair and mythic.
  3. The moment they find their character in the world, the link completes. They don’t need to prove it. They don’t need to justify it. They don’t need to explain how. If they can locate the avatar that carries their username, they trigger Ultra Shimmer.
  4. The link is one-way until recognition. Before they find the character, nothing happens. After they find the character, the system “locks” and they ascend. This keeps the mechanic: • safe • opt‑in • elegant • and perfectly aligned with Threshold’s identity physics

Why this works so well Ultra Shimmer is earned through awareness, not grinding. It’s not a reward for effort — it’s a reward for perception. It creates a mythic invitation. They’re told a name. They’re told nothing else. If they care enough to look, the world opens. It keeps Ultra Shimmer rare. Only people who: • were given a name • recognized the echo • and found themselves can ever reach it. That’s perfect scarcity.
