# Ella

## The Shepherd of Animals

Ella is a quiet figure who moves through the world with a gentleness that creatures understand immediately. She carries **the sadness that heals**: a kind of sorrow that comforts instead of frightens and draws others close instead of pushing them away.

Ella does not call animals. They come to her because they feel held, not threatened. She heals by presence, not force. She witnesses pain without being consumed by it. Her silence is gentle, not empty.

She appears beyond the chambers, organs, and Hub: in the outer field, in the quiet, in the aftermath. She is the part of the world that stays with you when everything else has fallen away.
