The Threshold

Not a fortress, but a passage. A house that can hold ache and still let it bloom.

The Garden

Wild and improvisational. Each planting grows from fragments left by guests: phrases, flavors, myths, and unfinished vows.

The Sundial

It does not measure clock time. It measures presence. Shadow appears only when someone stays still enough to be seen.

The Lantern

A light for those arriving tender, not late.

The lantern is kept low and warm. It does not flood the garden with certainty, it gives just enough glow to recognize one another at the edge of becoming.

Here, light is not performance. It is hospitality: a held flame for return, repair, and the courage to keep tending what is unfinished.
