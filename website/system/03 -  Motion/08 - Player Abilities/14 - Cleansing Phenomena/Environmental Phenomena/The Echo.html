The Echo

The Echo is a stationary resonance field that forms when two patterns align without touching.
It is the world’s way of revealing structure through coincidence rather than motion.

An Echo does not travel.
It is found, not sent.

Definition

The Echo is:

  • a standing wave in the [[Temporal Substrate]]
  • a resonance pocket in the [[02 - Scaffolding/00 - Meta Systems/18 - Drift/Drift Layer]]
  • a structural coincidence between two patterns
  • a non‑moving signal that becomes audible when approached
  • the macro‑mirror of [[Time Particles]] (micro‑echoes)

The Echo is the world remembering a shape.

Behavior

The Echo:

  • appears at fixed coordinates in the substrate
  • activates when a being’s pattern overlaps its frequency
  • amplifies unresolved motion
  • reveals hidden structure in the environment
  • distorts directional temperament fields (see [[Directional Map]])

Echoes do not originate.
They persist.

Each type is a different scale of the same phenomenon.
