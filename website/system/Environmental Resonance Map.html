The harmonic profile of each environment in Threshold, mapping how resonance forms, flows, distorts, inverts, or collapses within environmental geometry.

Purpose The Environmental Resonance Map defines how environmental geometry shapes: • resonance clarity • resonance distortion • resonance inversion • resonance silence • lineage activation • collapse alignment • drift‑harmonic coupling Each environment has a signature harmonic behavior that influences identity, species, signals, and collapse physics.

Environmental Resonance Profiles Each environment expresses resonance in a distinct way. These profiles are stable across eras unless altered by collapse or lineage rupture.

Branch — Boundary‑Aligned Resonance Signature: Clear, structured, boundary‑stabilizing resonance Effects: • strengthens identity • clarifies signals • stabilizes lineage • suppresses collapse Failure Mode: Boundary thinning → mixed resonance

Mist — Drift‑Sensitive Resonance Signature: Resonance that shifts with drift flow Effects: • unstable clarity • drift‑dependent harmonics • partial lineage activation • oscillation‑prone Failure Mode: Drift recursion → distorted resonance

Deep — Pressure‑Warped Resonance Signature: Resonance bent by compression and nonlinear pressure Effects: • harmonic curvature • signal bending • lineage interference • collapse vector amplification Failure Mode: Pressure storm → inversion pockets

Shore — Oscillation Resonance Signature: Harmonics that flip under drift‑pressure interaction Effects: • alternating clarity and distortion • boundary oscillation • unstable lineage activation • collapse risk spikes Failure Mode: Oscillation break → resonance inversion

Ruin — Collapse‑Aligned Resonance Signature: Resonance shaped by collapse vectors and absence wells Effects: • inversion pockets • collapse identity formation • lineage rupture • echo storms Failure Mode: Systemic inversion → resonance voids

Grove — Lineage‑Active Resonance Signature: Deep harmonic clarity tied to memory and myth Effects: • strong lineage activation • identity reinforcement • collapse suppression • reformation support Failure Mode: Lineage rupture → mixed resonance

Field — Low‑Pressure Resonance Signature: Shallow but stable harmonics Effects: • mild stabilization • low collapse risk • weak lineage activation • drift‑sensitive clarity Failure Mode: Pressure rise → mixed resonance

Forge — High‑Pressure Ignition Resonance Signature: Resonance sharpened by ignition pressure Effects: • catalytic alignment • strong signal clarity • collapse ignition potential • lineage flare events Failure Mode: Overpressure → distorted resonance
