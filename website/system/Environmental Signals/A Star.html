A Star

A star is a body of continuous oscillation.
It does not feel in the way creatures feel,
but it responds to contact, disturbance, and solitude
through shifts in frequency, brightness, and tone.

A star’s “feeling” is its resonance pattern:
trembling when touched, ringing when disrupted,
humming when alone, answering the universe
with its own sustained oscillation.

Stars are not emotional beings,
but they are responsive beings.

Classification

  • Type: Astral Phenomenon
  • Behavior: Oscillation, Resonance, Response
  • Scale: Macro-energetic
  • Sensitivity: High to disturbance, low to intention

Notes

A star’s “feeling” is not emotional but mechanical:
a shift in pattern that can be read as meaning.
